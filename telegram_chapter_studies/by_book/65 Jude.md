**Jude**
*Jude, the servant of Jesus Christ, and brother of James, to them that are sanctified by God the Father, and preserved in Jesus Christ, and called:*

*2 Mercy unto you, and peace, and love, be multiplied.*

*3 Beloved, when I gave all diligence to write unto you of the common salvation, it was needful for me to write unto you, and exhort you that ye should earnestly contend for the faith which was once delivered unto the saints.*

*4 For there are certain men crept in unawares, who were before of old ordained to this condemnation, ungodly men, turning the grace of our God into lasciviousness, and denying the only Lord God, and our Lord Jesus Christ.*

*5 I will therefore put you in remembrance, though ye once knew this, how that the Lord, having saved the people out of the land of Egypt, afterward destroyed them that believed not.*

*6 And the angels which kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation, he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day.*

*7 Even as Sodom and Gomorrha, and the cities about them in like manner, giving themselves over to fornication, and going after strange flesh, are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire.*

*8 Likewise also these filthy dreamers defile the flesh, despise dominion, and speak evil of dignities.*

*9 Yet Michael the archangel, when contending with the devil he disputed about the body of Moses, durst not bring against him a railing accusation, but said, The Lord rebuke thee.*

*10 But these speak evil of those things which they know not: but what they know naturally, as brute beasts, in those things they corrupt themselves.*

*11 Woe unto them! for they have gone in the way of Cain, and ran greedily after the error of Balaam for reward, and perished in the gainsaying of Core.*

*12 These are spots in your feasts of charity, when they feast with you, feeding themselves without fear: clouds they are without water, carried about of winds; trees whose fruit withereth, without fruit, twice dead, plucked up by the roots;*

*13 Raging waves of the sea, foaming out their own shame; wandering stars, to whom is reserved the blackness of darkness for ever.*

*14 And Enoch also, the seventh from Adam, prophesied of these, saying, Behold, the Lord cometh with ten thousands of his saints,*

*15 To execute judgment upon all, and to convince all that are ungodly among them of all their ungodly deeds which they have ungodly committed, and of all their hard speeches which ungodly sinners have spoken against him.*

*16 These are murmurers, complainers, walking after their own lusts; and their mouth speaketh great swelling words, having men's persons in admiration because of advantage.*

*17 But, beloved, remember ye the words which were spoken before of the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ;*

*18 How that they told you there should be mockers in the last time, who should walk after their own ungodly lusts.*

*19 These be they who separate themselves, sensual, having not the Spirit.*

*20 But ye, beloved, building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in the Holy Ghost,*

*21 Keep yourselves in the love of God, looking for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life.*

*22 And of some have compassion, making a difference:*

*23 And others save with fear, pulling them out of the fire; hating even the garment spotted by the flesh.*

*24 Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy,*

*25 To the only wise God our Saviour, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now and ever. Amen.*

**Zechariah 3** presents the prophet with a vision of the high priest Joshua, in the time of Zerubbabel and the restoration of the Temple in Jerusalem. And the angel of the Lord stood before him, and Satan at his right hand to resist him. To accuse him and tempt him. 

The Lord rebukes Satan, the adversary, just as in **Jude,** where angels, even archangels, who all are mightier than man, don't bring railing accusations or curses against the Devil, but say "the Lord rebukes thee." 

There are many who have built an entire ministry on the basis of "rebuking Satan," railing against the devil. Not resisting him, that he flees, but they themselves speaking evil of dignities, railing against things they don't understand. They often come with a false gospel, adding works or strange doctrines. Charismatics and charlatans. 

But the Lord rebukes Satan here, saying that Joshua was an iron plucked from the fire. His instrument. Even as the Lord set His name on Jerusalem, so He had chosen Joshua to be His high priest. 

In the vision, Joshua is arrayed in filthy garments. And those that stood before him are ordered to take them away, that the Lord would clothe him in a change of raiment. Making him clean. 

Just as Jude writes of saving some by fear, pulling them from the fire, hating even the garments spotted by the flesh. Just as Jesus promises to clothe those who believe on His name in white. 

For the priests of the Old Testament must be cleansed and sanctified, made holy by diverse washings and sacrifices, but Joshua, without the temple and without the purification rituals was made clean by grace through faith by the living God. 

Joshua is given a fair mitre, a head covering for the office, and fine garments, as the angel of the Lord stood by. 

And again, we are given a conditional promise to the high priest, *Thus saith the Lord of hosts; If thou wilt walk in my ways, and if thou wilt keep my charge, then thou shalt also judge my house, and shalt also keep my courts, and I will give thee places to walk among these that stand by.* The angels around him. 

Then he is told through the prophet, that the Lord will bring forth His servant, the Branch, a promise of Christ Jesus. He says He will lay before Joshua a stone, that rock of salvation as Christ is called;

**Isaiah 28:16**
*Therefore thus saith the Lord God, Behold, I lay in Zion for a foundation a stone, a tried stone, a precious corner stone, a sure foundation: he that believeth shall not make haste.*

As in **1 Peter 2:6-9**
*Wherefore also it is contained in the scripture, Behold, I lay in Sion a chief corner stone, elect, precious: and he that believeth on him shall not be confounded. Unto you therefore which believe he is precious: but unto them which be disobedient, the stone which the builders disallowed, the same is made the head of the corner, And a stone of stumbling, and a rock of offence, even to them which stumble at the word, being disobedient: whereunto also they were appointed. But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light*

The image of Christ as the foundation, the corner stone, perfect and square, and the sanctification in Him of the royal priesthood, carrying over from the Old Testament, done away with, and given to a new priesthood of believers, washed clean in the blood. 

The Lord of hosts says, *I will remove the iniquity of that land in one day. In that day, saith the Lord of hosts, shall ye call every man his neighbour under the vine and under the fig tree.*

That blessed day, at the coming of the Lord of hosts. Amen.
