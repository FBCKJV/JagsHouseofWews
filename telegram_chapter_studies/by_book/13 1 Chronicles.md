Here in **1 Chronicles 29**, the final chapter, we come to the transition of power between King David and his son Solomon. David has, in obedience to the Lord, organized the Kingdom, set up the leaders of men and the chain of command in the various roles and responsibilities. He has given Solomon the plans for the house of God, the pattern given by the Spirit, and laid up treasure and provisions to see it built. 

And the people all willingly gave treasure to build up the house of the Lord and to do His work. Free will offerings. 

David calls them to prayer and to bless the Lord God of Israel, to give Him all glory and honor. David's prayer reminds us that whatever we give to the service of God is His to begin with. 

Tithing, the practice of giving the first of the flock, the tenth of the wealth and harvest, etc...these are commands in the Old Testament, that we honor today in the New. 

But the free will offerings here are a reminder that all we have *cometh of Thine hand, and is all Thine own.* Not just 10%, pretax income, itemized and listed on our taxes. All of it. Everything we have is His because it is by His grace we have it at all. 

For all things come of God. And when we give, it is of God's own. 

When we approach giving towards the church, or to help the brethren, or missions and church plants and soul winning trips...whatever it may be, give cheerfully, willingly. 

Because riches and honor come from God. It is His power to make us great, and blessed by whatever riches He provides. We can't take it with us; there are none abiding. 

So give joyfully, knowing it's not yours but the Lord's. And rejoice in it, praising Him in thanksgiving, that you have abundance wherewithal to give.
