NameMeaning and verse
ElohimGod as the Creator. A plural name, showing God’s majesty and power
I AM THAT I AMGod’s self-existence and eternal nature . The Hebrew here is ’ehyeh ’asher ’ehyeh; the name YHWH (the Tetragrammaton) is given in
Almighty God (El Shaddai)God as the all-powerful and all-sufficient One
The LORD (Jehovah)God as our provider, covenant keeper, and shepherd
Jealous (Jehovah Qanna)God’s intolerance for competition and demand for exclusive loyalty
A consuming fireGod’s holiness and judgment against sin
