The cross I take up daily

We then that are strong ought to bear the infirmities of the weak, and not to please ourselves.Romans 15:1

The turning from sin and giving up worldly pleasures and habits is actually the easiest part. Living for God, and loving others as ourselves, magnifying the patience and love He has shown us, that's the challenge. That's the cross I take up daily.

With those who become a burden, it becomes so difficult. But in it there's a lesson for those of us willing to see, how much worse it must be for God dealing with us.

Charity

The more excellent way

But covet earnestly the best gifts: and yet shew I unto you a more excellent way.1 Corinthians 12:31

It is the unfeigned love, given for no personal gain, but genuine and selfless, that is above all else, and is a faint reflection of the love God showed us by giving His Son that we may be saved.

The charity is caring for the others in the church, sorrow for each members' sorrow, and joy for their individual success. Not coveting the gifts each may have. Edifying one another.

Charity

The motive, not the gift

And though I bestow all my goods to feed the poor, and though I give my body to be burned, and have not charity, it profiteth me nothing.1 Corinthians 13:3

We usually use the basic definition of charity to mean giving something away to help others, alms. That can be a form of expression for charity, but it is not itself charity. So many do so much as a form of “charity” but for selfish purpose, or taking pride or accolades for it.

Helping people who cannot help you back out of love, is love unfeigned. The spirit behind it is everything. We can know all things in scripture, and if we aren't edifying others and given to love people, it's for nothing. It's the motive not the gift.

Charity

Faith, hope and charity abide

And now abideth faith, hope, charity, these three; but the greatest of these is charity.1 Corinthians 13:13

Charity, the unconditional love that is God's love, magnified through us, that cares for others and seeks to build them up, even at our own expense. These are what endure in the Kingdom of God.

Not tongues, various languages or soaring rhetoric, not preaching and prophecy, that fails and fades. Not knowledge, that is imperfect at best in our current state, seeing through the glass darkly.

Charity

Love without dissimulation

Let love be without dissimulation. Abhor that which is evil; cleave to that which is good.Romans 12:9

Dissimulation is deceit, dishonesty regarding intent. Concealing motives. Speaking the truth in love. There is no love in manipulation or in hidden agendas.

What men call love is often manipulative and controlling, and with this false love comes the lack of judgment, calling nothing evil. That isn't love. God's love is without manipulation, freely given. And God hates evil. It is impossible to love without hate.

Pure religion

A mission statement

The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised,Luke 4:18

This reading of Isaiah by Christ Jesus, a prophecy of Him fulfilled before those in the synagogue that day, serves as a mission statement for Christians and our purpose as servants of God.

Jesus moved with compassion for the widow of Nain. A woman he doesn't know. No one has sought him to intervene. He does what He can to comfort the brokenhearted ().

Pure religion

To visit the fatherless and widows

Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father is this, To visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep himself unspotted from the world.James 1:27

Religion is the outward practice of the faith we have inwardly. We are meant to serve. To help. To feed and clothe the needy, both physically and spiritually. Not just pray “be ye warmed and filled,” but to genuinely care about others.

A lot of people seem to think that Jesus introduced these concepts in the New Testament. But again and again we see that this is how God wants us to be. Self-sacrifice for others, not the selfish sacrifice of the works-based religions of the world (; ).

Pure religion

Workers of Heaven

Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye pay tithe of mint and anise and cummin, and have omitted the weightier matters of the law, judgment, mercy, and faith: these ought ye to have done, and not to leave the other undone.Matthew 23:23

We aren't supposed to be working for Heaven, we are supposed to be workers of Heaven.

That the Kingdom of Heaven draw nigh them, when we do the charity and humble service to others we are called to. When we give the Gospel to the poor, and heal the broken hearted. Clothe the naked and feed those in need.

We didn't find Jesus, He found us, and set us to labor for Him, as we were bought with a price.

Pure religion

Call the poor to the feast

But when thou makest a feast, call the poor, the maimed, the lame, the blind: And thou shalt be blessed; for they cannot recompense thee: for thou shalt be recompensed at the resurrection of the just.Luke 14:13-14

If you're invited to a party, don't go in assuming you're asked to sit in the high places of status, instead be seated in the lowest room ().

If you have the means to call a feast, humbly seek to serve those who cannot do anything for you. Who are weak. Honor them without thought for reward in this life and be rewarded for it at the judgment.

Pure religion

Leave the gleanings

And thou shalt not glean thy vineyard, neither shalt thou gather every grape of thy vineyard; thou shalt leave them for the poor and stranger: I am the Lord your God.Leviticus 19:10

The biblical way of caring for the poor and the sojourners among the people. Not going back again and again to make sure you got all the crops that God had blessed us with. Our bounty, freely given as God so freely gave to us. In charity and mercy. Hospitality.

Right judgment doesn't mean weak judgment. Provide the opportunity for gleaning our fields. Be generous and hospitable, while maintaining the biblical truth; that if any would not work, neither should he eat ().

Pure religion

Personal and local

A Godly nation takes care of the poor and widows indeed, providing sound judgement and aid to the fatherless, through the actions of individual Christians, organized locally by their church.

When that lacks, then inequities abound, and the beast system will use the opportunity to provide as a means of control and punishment.

Feeding and clothing the needy is an opportunity to do unto them and by doing so, doing it unto God. Therefore, it should be personal and local. Acts of charity and alms done discreetly, or by our churches. Not given off to the government to do for us. Biblical Christianity, not “social justice.”

Pure religion

Lending unto the Lord

He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the Lord; and that which he hath given will he pay him again.Proverbs 19:17
  • Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me.
  • It is human nature to be respecters of persons. To favor the wealthy for what they could do for you, while the poor is separated from their neighbors.
  • Blessed is he that considereth the poor: the LORD will deliver him in time of trouble.

Not blindly enabling sloth and rewarding scoffers and fools, but having mercy on the poor. We are lending to the Lord. He will repay.

Pure religion

Comfort the broken hearted

O that ye would altogether hold your peace! and it should be your wisdom.Job 13:5

“I know you're going through a lot, so I don't want to offend you, but I can't help myself...” What follows it rarely is a help. Eliphaz was quoting scripture, and some of what he said is true. But he's misapplying it ().

When dealing with someone in grief and inconsolably miserable, it is better to sit in silence and love them, than seek to explain away their feelings, and lecture them what to do.

When bad things happen to friends, it is an opportunity given by God to show His love. Not make it about ourselves.

Pure religion

A father to the poor

I was eyes to the blind, and feet was I to the lame. I was a father to the poor: and the cause which I knew not I searched out.Job 29:15-16

Why did Job have everyone's respect? He delivered the poor and the fatherless. Those that had no man. He blessed the dying and gave to the widow in her need. Because he clothed himself in righteousness and wore judgment like a crown ().

Job not only was as a father to the poor, but further seeking out causes to take up that he hadn't known yet. Going out of his way to help others.

Forgive

Seventy times seven

Then came Peter to him, and said, Lord, how oft shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? till seven times? Jesus saith unto him, I say not unto thee, Until seven times: but, Until seventy times seven.Matthew 18:21-22

Jesus replies, 70 times 7, indicating an order of magnitude greater than the letter of the law. To essentially always forgive those seeking it.

The servant beseeching the king to be forgiven a massive debt then refuses to release a fellow servant a relatively minor one (). Holding a grudge against one who has sincerely asked to be forgiven is evidence of pride, and a sign one has no understanding of the humility of receiving grace from God.

We forgive as we have been forgiven. There but for the grace of God go I.

Forgive

If God forgives so much

For if ye forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you: But if ye forgive not men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.Matthew 6:14-15

Jesus says that all manner of sin and blasphemy, including blasphemies against him and God the Father, shall be forgiven of men (). If our creator and savior can forgive great offense directed at them, how can we not do likewise for the petty squabbles of this life?

It can be hard to not lose sight of the mercy of God that has been given to us. To forget the pattern of prayer Jesus taught us, asking the Father to forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors.

Forgive

Put that on mine account

Not now as a servant, but above a servant, a brother beloved, specially to me, but how much more unto thee, both in the flesh, and in the Lord? If thou count me therefore a partner, receive him as myself. If he hath wronged thee, or oweth thee ought, put that on mine account;Philemon 16-18

Paul's delicate appeal to take back the servant, not only as a servant, but forgiven as a brother in Christ is a model for us to follow. Paul even offers to pay whatever debt is owed, though he gets in a not-so-subtle reminder that Philemon owes even more so ().

We should also make overtures to bring brethren together, to help broker peace and forgiveness. Being peacemakers.

Forgive

Correct in love, rejoice over the lost

It was meet that we should make merry, and be glad: for this thy brother was dead, and is alive again; and was lost, and is found.Luke 15:32

This is not to say we ignore sin, or compromise what is right. There is church discipline for a reason. Right is right.

But we correct in love. We deal with the beam in our eye so that we may help others with the mote that is in theirs. We forgive when sincere apology is made. We forget, so that there is no resentment or grudge. We celebrate the return of the lost, rejoicing in their salvation.

Forgive

A glory to pass over a transgression

The discretion of a man deferreth his anger; and it is his glory to pass over a transgression.Proverbs 19:11

Let the small stuff go, let go of a slight. Our pride and anger at being treated poorly or stolen from can be difficult to resist. But it is a glory to pass over such things.

David's kind remembrance of Saul, someone who had tried to kill him multiple times, shows the forgiveness Christians should have (). In the death of those who've hurt us, we should let go of the animosity, because we know that God is just and He will repay.

It was Job's forgiveness of others who had wronged him that led God to restore him ().

Love your enemies

Love your enemies, in context

But love ye your enemies, and do good, and lend, hoping for nothing again; and your reward shall be great, and ye shall be the children of the Highest: for he is kind unto the unthankful and to the evil. Be ye therefore merciful, as your Father also is merciful.Luke 6:35-36

This is the part of the preaching of Christ that those who don't understand call “weak.” They remove the context. But He's just condemned the very masses He is serving, for their fickleness and worldly lusts ().

Pity your enemies. They don't have what you got. We were that unthankful and evil. Sure we love those who love us. But sinners do all that too. That's easy.

He isn't saying to simply stand for evil, not judging the actions of others, but to put in perspective their condition with the understanding of our own. Vengeance is His.

Love your enemies

Coals of fire on his head

Therefore if thine enemy hunger, feed him; if he thirst, give him drink: for in so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire on his head. Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good.Romans 12:20-21

Not in the sense our detractors claim; the weakness that they perceive from this. But in truth, its strength. 9 times out of 10 when someone offends you and you turn the other cheek, which isn't an act of submission but of defiance, most people back down. Making an enemy into a friend.

Note the spiritual application. Your enemy hungers and thirsts. For what? Food and water? Or truth? Will you withhold that bread that came down from Heaven? Don't be as Jonah ().

Love your enemies

David spares Saul

The Lord judge between me and thee, and the Lord avenge me of thee: but mine hand shall not be upon thee.1 Samuel 24:12

When David has the opportunity to end this one-sided feud with King Saul, he chooses to let God judge. By trusting in the Lord, without a single blow, David has been delivered and his enemy made weak.

David's men saw the opportunity to exact vengeance as a sign from God (). It can be hard to do the right thing in the presence of that kind of peer pressure. Do what's right in His eyes. The rest takes care of itself.

Love your enemies

Blessed are the peacemakers

If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men.Romans 12:18
  • There is an honor in being a peacemaker, regardless of the bitterness of the disagreement. Especially between brothers.
  • Lead with an offer of peace. It isn't a show of weakness. It is a show of strength. It is a mercy.
  • Love our enemies, certainly, but don't go out of your way to make one.

You don't seek vengeance because you know that belongs to God, and He is not slack when it comes to delivering just punishment on the wicked.

Servant of all

Whosoever will be great

But so shall it not be among you: but whosoever will be great among you, shall be your minister: And whosoever of you will be the chiefest, shall be servant of all. For even the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many.Mark 10:43-45

It is not about being lifted up in greatness like the rulers of this world, who are served by many, but being a lowly servant to many. Just as He, the Son of God was for us, a sacrifice.

And Jesus promises us, as good and trusty servants, that in His return He will again serve us (). If He, the King of Kings, can humble Himself to serve us, how can we do anything less to those we meet every day?

Servant of all

The king as servant

Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves. Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others.Philippians 2:3-4

David strove to rule as a God fearing judge, with mercy and faith, putting the profit of others before himself. David is a picture of the king as servant. A picture of Christ, and an example for us who follow after Jesus.

We can do great things for the Lord, even strive with one another to out do each other. But first we should seek to serve. A good judge for the children, the fatherless, the widows. A servant to our brethren.

Servant of all

The one or the nine

So likewise ye, when ye shall have done all those things which are commanded you, say, We are unprofitable servants: we have done that which was our duty to do.Luke 17:10

All ten lepers were healed. All who believe are given eternal life. But it is the rare few who follow it up with this level of devotion as pictured by the Samaritan ().

Turn from sin, read your Bible, say your prayers, love God, love others. Bare minimum Christianity. We grow our faith not by doing the bare minimum but realizing that doing it makes us unprofitable servants.

Are we like the 9? Saved, for certain, but doing what we ought to by obligation, and little else. This hits hard. I fall short. But it's the lesson that is being taught.

Servant of all

Not what is in it for me

And he made the laver of brass, and the foot of it of brass, of the lookingglasses of the women assembling, which assembled at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.Exodus 38:8

The women gave their mirrors that they be crafted into the laver. Turning from self, as these faithful women did by giving up the objects of their vanity. We are asked to turn from ourselves towards service to others.

We Christians often approach the Bible with a “what is in it for me” attitude. We dread work, family obligations, being hospitable and neighborly. Instead we should submit to serving God in everything, as glad servants to Him.

Servant of all

The gift of helps

And God hath set some in the church, first apostles, secondarily prophets, thirdly teachers, after that miracles, then gifts of healings, helps, governments, diversities of tongues.1 Corinthians 12:28

Though it is the easiest of the gifts to be manifest in a believer, it is often the most neglected, the least coveted, the one responsibility so many shirk. (Of which I am chief...)

Show up to church, not to feed our own needs, but looking whereby we can be a blessing. Asking what needs be done, with cheer. Rejoicing in our liberty that we have the opportunity to be a help to the Kingdom of God itself ().

Servant of all

All things come of thee

But who am I, and what is my people, that we should be able to offer so willingly after this sort? for all things come of thee, and of thine own have we given thee.1 Chronicles 29:14

All we have cometh of thine hand, and is all thine own. Not just 10%, pretax income. All of it. Everything we have is His because it is by His grace we have it at all.

We who have are expected to share with brethren who lack. Our liberality leads to our receiving abundance of the spiritual. It's an even trade, not a burden (; ).

So give joyfully, knowing it's not yours but the Lord's.

Servant of all

Love thy neighbour

And the people asked him, saying, What shall we do then? He answereth and saith unto them, He that hath two coats, let him impart to him that hath none; and he that hath meat, let him do likewise.Luke 3:10-11

In your daily life, don't defraud people. Don't steal. Don't abuse whatever authority you have, at work or at home. Don't lie. Love others the way you want to be loved.

Do we pull over to help a person with a flat tire? Do we find someone's wallet and return it, intact ()?

Be a friend to others. Be as a brother, a help in trouble. Born for adversity (). Jonathan loves David more than his own earthly reward. We should love our brothers in Christ this much ().

A good witness

Would they want to follow?

Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.1 Timothy 4:12

A common lament of the believer is that their friends and family are perishing. But do we live in a way that these people who we care about can emulate? Would they want to? What advantage do they see in accepting salvation, in how you live?

Why would someone believe someone telling them of the peace that passes all understanding found in Christ when we are as miserable and fearful as everyone else?

A good witness

An excellent spirit

Then the presidents and princes sought to find occasion against Daniel concerning the kingdom; but they could find none occasion nor fault; forasmuch as he was faithful, neither was there any error or fault found in him.Daniel 6:4

Daniel was blameless in his work. He did more, meekly and humbly, in God's name. They couldn't find anything to accuse Daniel of, except for his faith.

Make them come at us for our faith having nothing else with which to attack you. Being blameless and excellent. Going that extra mile. And when you are thrown in the lions den, believe that God will deliver you.

A good witness

As to the Lord, and not unto men

And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men; Knowing that of the Lord ye shall receive the reward of the inheritance: for ye serve the Lord Christ.Colossians 3:23-24

Be the best employee at your job. Even if your boss is terrible. We work, we don't rob our employers, because we work for God. We are bought with a price already, by the blood of Christ ().

That the name of God and his doctrine be not blasphemed (). We represent Christ to the world. This isn't to say if a manager calls on you to do something contrary to God's word that you have to submit. But we do it peaceably, with humility and meekness.

A good witness

No occasion to blaspheme

Howbeit, because by this deed thou hast given great occasion to the enemies of the Lord to blaspheme, the child also that is born unto thee shall surely die.2 Samuel 12:14

We are at liberty in Christ. We aren't going to be judged by our works for justification before God, but we are judged of man. By our sin as believers, we give the accuser cause to blaspheme God.

Blameless. Not sinless, which is an impossible standard while we live in the flesh, but blameless. Trying to do the right thing, being a good example.

In all things shewing thyself a pattern of good works: in doctrine shewing uncorruptness, gravity, sincerity, Sound speech, that cannot be condemned; that he that is of the contrary part may be ashamed, having no evil thing to say of you.Titus 2:7-8
A good witness

Pray for all men

I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men; For kings, and for all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty. For this is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Saviour; Who will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth.1 Timothy 2:1-4

This is our instruction for the rulers of this world as much as the beggars, that we may live peaceably. Not given to fear or wrath. Doubt and wrath show a lack of faith and offends others from the truth.

The Bible says the will of God our savior that ALL MEN come to believe. It isn't a select few, and it is our calling to help make God's will manifest.

A good witness

Rejoicing in hope

Rejoicing in hope; patient in tribulation; continuing instant in prayer;Romans 12:12

Be cheerful. Not a doomer. You have the hope of Christ, act like it. Let that joy come off of you. It is contagious. Try not to let the petty frustrations of this world lead you to wrath, being a bad report to the Gospel we live by.

The Philippian jailer was saved because, despite being in chains, Paul and Silas sang hymns and cheerfully held themselves out as content even in tribulation ().

For fear of the Jews

Howbeit no man spake openly of him for fear of the Jews.John 7:13

When the unsaved come to the leaders of churches and say “tell this Christian to shut up,” they comply. We can't preach against sin. Against sodomites and Jews. They will even say, like Billy Graham or various Popes, that sincere Jews, Muslims and Hindus will find Heaven, knowing the only path to the Father is through the Son ().

How much dispensationalism and Zionism is preached by pastors who know better, for fear of the Jews? But by fearing only God, we will be delivered. So we speak boldly. We give the Gospel to every creature, even if it means we are mocked ().

Moses' face shone

And it came to pass, when Moses came down from mount Sinai with the two tables of testimony in Moses’ hand, when he came down from the mount, that Moses wist not that the skin of his face shone while he talked with him. And when Aaron and all the children of Israel saw Moses, behold, the skin of his face shone; and they were afraid to come nigh him.Exodus 34:29-30

And sinful man can't bear to look at us. They call for a veil to cover our faith. And sadly, many Christians today hide our light behind a bushel. But Jesus said to let our light shine.
