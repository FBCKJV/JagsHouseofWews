Repentance and Assurance

Godly sorrow, confession, and the believer kept by God.

That ye may know

These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life, and that ye may believe on the name of the Son of God.1 John 5:13

God Who cannot lie promised before the foundation of the world that whosoever believes on Christ Jesus, calling upon His name for salvation, has eternal life, and are sealed and regenerated by the Spirit that you may KNOW you are a child of God.

Eternal security. Once saved, always saved.

Repent: a change of mind

And saying, Repent ye: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.Matthew 3:2

Repent means to change your mind, to accept new information as true and have a change of heart, a conversion.

Not this

He is not saying "get your life together and stop sinning, the Kingdom of Heaven is here."

But this

Repenting in relation to salvation is turning from a false belief in something to justify us before God, or turning from unbelief to believing His word.

Turning from sin follows

That discernment, if the new man created at salvation is fed, leads to a change in attitude towards sin. It becomes less desirable. The lusts of the flesh can lose their appeal.

If that new man isn't fed, and they continue walking in the flesh, they are still saved by the knowledge of the truth, but that discernment is dulled and God may have to chasten the person to work godly sorrow in their heart.

Only faith in the risen Christ and belief in the promise of God that Jesus is the sole propitiation for all sin can save us.

Godly sorrow and the sorrow of the world

For godly sorrow worketh repentance to salvation not to be repented of: but the sorrow of the world worketh death.2 Corinthians 7:10

Cain

The burden Cain couldn't bear was the punishment, but he sorrowed not unto repentance. He never changed his mind about what he had done.

David

David's sorrow is godly, he cannot bear his own iniquity and hates his sin that brought him low, rather than resenting the punishment he'd received.

The work of sorrow

This sincere sorrow for sin is not itself repentance, but rather, repentance is the work of that sorrow.

For behold this selfsame thing, that ye sorrowed after a godly sort, what carefulness it wrought in you, yea, what clearing of yourselves, yea, what indignation, yea, what fear, yea, what vehement desire, yea, what zeal, yea, what revenge! In all things ye have approved yourselves to be clear in this matter.2 Corinthians 7:11

This is a helpful list, both for those of us seeking to get right with God regarding sin in our life, and how we should act, and for judging rightly the sincerity, be it godly or earthly sorrow, of those who have wronged us.

As many receive it in tears, many still receive the truth in joy.

If we confess our sins

If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.1 John 1:9

First John, the epistle, is written to the saved. Here the Bible tells us if we the saved, want fellowship with God and one another, to walk with God, to confess our sins to Him, and be forgiven them all by Christ, washed clean in His blood.

This promise shows not only the faithfulness of God to forgive, but also that our sin in the flesh is not held against our ledger in Heaven.

Restore unto me the joy of thy salvation

Restore unto me the joy of thy salvation; and uphold me with thy free spirit.Psalm 51:12

David is not writing that he is afraid to lose his salvation, but seeking the confidence in it again that has been marred by his transgressions and the fear from the chastening he has received.

God wants to forgive us more than we want to be forgiven.

Not earned by penance

Perfect contrition

It requires the sinner to be 100% honest, 100% contrite, about 100% of their sins, that depends on the sinner alone, not the blood of Christ.

Grace

We confess our sins to God knowing He's forgiven them already, that we have eternal grace that's never lost or taken away, in Christ Jesus.

Penance isn't how we earn forgiveness. Forgiveness is given by grace and mercy. Working to maintain God's grace makes grace no more grace.

New every morning

It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.Lamentations 3:22-23

No matter how backslidden. No matter how snared in sin. No matter how the devil accuses you before God, in Christ Jesus you are forgiven.

For a saved believer who has been sealed by the Spirit, it is never too late to turn back to God and away from your sins. Like the Prodigal son, returning to the Father.

Whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth

For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth, and scourgeth every son whom he receiveth.Hebrews 12:6

We are punished here on earth for our sins. Sometimes very harshly. Sometimes with death.

And so God punished David, yet He put away David's sin, because he had the imputed righteousness of Jesus Christ. His faith in God's word.

Chastened, yet saved

Ananias and Sapphira

Their being in Heaven when they die, justified by faith and Jesus' imputed righteousness didn't keep them from sinning, nor did it stop God from punishing them with their lives.

Delivered unto Satan

God allows Satan to destroy us in the flesh. The hope in church discipline is that the person gets right. But if not, at least in death they are free from the sin and present with the Lord.

We saved are held to a higher standard than the unsaved, and we are punished here in this earth, sometimes more so than the unsaved, who are not God's children.

None shall pluck them out

And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand. My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father’s hand.John 10:28-29

Once you are saved you are always saved, always His, always His sheep.

Once saved always saved is not just a slogan, it is the truth of the Gospel and the crux of the Gospel, without which you cannot claim to have faith.

Sealed unto the day of redemption

And grieve not the holy Spirit of God, whereby ye are sealed unto the day of redemption.Ephesians 4:30

Once we believe we are sealed by the Holy Spirit as the earnest, the down payment as it were, until redemption, where God makes good on His promise, on the day of the Lord.

We who are saved know that we have the Holy Spirit testifying within us that we are the sons of God.

Answering the errors

Falling away

If someone chooses atheism after having full knowledge of salvation, they were never saved. They believed in vain.

Enduring to the end

Too many false teachers assert that perseverance is for our spiritual salvation. It is for grace and protection during tribulation and persecution.

Judas

Not only wasn't Judas saved, he was a reprobate and Jesus knew this.

The answer

Salvation cannot be lost, only refused.

Summary

  1. RepentanceRepent means to change your mind, to accept new information as true and have a change of heart.
  2. Godly sorrowSincere sorrow for sin is not itself repentance, but rather, repentance is the work of that sorrow.
  3. ConfessionIf we are honest we confess our sins, and they are forgiven already in Christ.
  4. MercyIt is never too late to turn back to God and away from your sins.
  5. ChasteningWe are punished here on earth for our sins, yet He put away David's sin.
  6. KeptOnce you are saved you are always saved, always His, always His sheep.
  7. SealedWe are sealed by the Holy Spirit as the earnest until redemption.
