The charge of formalism

This people draweth nigh unto me with their mouth, and honoureth me with their lips; but their heart is far from me.Matthew 15:8

The religious practices are being maintained, the bare minimum of service to keep the covenant. The Lord isn't accusing them of failing this aspect, but in the weightier matters, as Jesus accused the Pharisees ().

As an application for Christians today, the indictment is the same leaven of the Pharisees; hypocrisy. Are we bound by the formalism and outward appearance of being clean, while failing to maintain the good work that God has saved us for?

The heart

What cometh out of the man

There is nothing from without a man, that entering into him can defile him: but the things which come out of him, those are they that defile the man.Mark 7:15

It is what is in our hearts, our old man's mind of the flesh that wars with the new man created of the Spirit within us. Over and over again the Bible tells us that it is with our heart and mind and tongue that we sin. Our intent.

Diverse washings and dietary laws and carnal ordinances of the tradition of men... none of these material things save us or make us more spiritual. The key is to walk with the Spirit, in God's word and in prayer, that the heart be filled with His righteousness and not our own sinful flesh.

The heart

Mercy, and not sacrifice

For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.Hosea 6:6

Today, many Christians worry more about the outward acts of religion over the spiritual things of God. They play church, and make a long pretense of prayer. Dead works and vain traditions, but no mercy or judgment for the lost, the poor, the widow, the fatherless.

You can be the most separated person in the pews, but if you look down on others, and hold yourself as better than, you're separated unto yourself. Not the gospel of God.

The heart

The fast that I have chosen

Is not this the fast that I have chosen? to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke? Is it not to deal thy bread to the hungry, and that thou bring the poor that are cast out to thy house? when thou seest the naked, that thou cover him; and that thou hide not thyself from thine own flesh?Isaiah 58:6-7

They boast about these works, bragging about their fasts. They fast for strife and debate, to lord over others. To place burdens on people grievous to be borne.

The fast the Lord has chosen is to comfort the afflicted, to loose the bands of wickedness, the burden of the law, especially the doctrine of men taught as commandments. To feed the hungry and to clothe the naked. Not doing without to be seen of men, but sacrifice of our time and talents and wealth to help others in need. Without fanfare.

The heart

In secret

But thou, when thou fastest, anoint thine head, and wash thy face; That thou appear not unto men to fast, but unto thy Father which is in secret: and thy Father, which seeth in secret, shall reward thee openly.Matthew 6:17-18

Give alms in secret. Pray in secret, not making a show of it. Don't pray as the heathen do, with vain repetitions. Don't make a public show of fasting and doing without.

Notice He says “when you fast.” Fasting in the Bible wasn't a sign of righteousness or a way to get God to do our will, but usually done at a sign of trouble or turmoil in the person's life. A death in the family, a perilous time. At the beginning of a trial.

The heart

Broad phylacteries

But all their works they do for to be seen of men: they make broad their phylacteries, and enlarge the borders of their garments,Matthew 23:5

Pharisees and scribes exaggerated religious symbols, phylacteries and garment tassels, to appear more pious and draw attention to themselves, rather than truly living God's law. They made the command literal, rather than in the spirit of how it was given.

Being led by the law, on God's paths, is the meaning behind it. That when you sleep, when you wake, His word talks with you ().

The heart

The sabbath was made for man

And he said unto them, The sabbath was made for man, and not man for the sabbath: Therefore the Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath.Mark 2:27-28

Replacing the spiritual application with rites and ceremony, these sacramental ordinances become a religion unto themselves. Just as the Old Testament devolved into the Pharisaical religion practiced in Jesus' day.

The Sabbath was designed to picture the rest we have in Jesus after salvation. It wasn't meant to be a way of showing super righteousness by how closely you followed the tradition of man that sprung up around it. As a pious exercise to be seen of others.

The heart

We are the elder son

And he was angry, and would not go in: therefore came his father out, and intreated him. And he answering said to his father, Lo, these many years do I serve thee, neither transgressed I at any time thy commandment: and yet thou never gavest me a kid, that I might make merry with my friends:Luke 15:28-29

The parable is addressed to those Pharisees and scribes, ostensibly righteous men, who are casting judgment on the sinners who're called by faith to Christ.

How many saved Christians today are like the elder son? Who grew up hearing the Gospel, and seeing new converts, babes in Christ, often scarred and broken, came to resent them? To be holier than thou?

And the publican, standing afar off, would not lift up so much as his eyes unto heaven, but smote upon his breast, saying, God be merciful to me a sinner.Luke 18:13
The heart

Mind not high things

Be of the same mind one toward another. Mind not high things, but condescend to men of low estate. Be not wise in your own conceits.Romans 12:16

If you have wisdom, if you have knowledge, it came from God and isn't yours. Be humble. Don't get caught up in theologies and intellectual pursuit, thinking you're better than the unlearned.

Understand that the more lowly and sinful and broken a person is, there's a strong likelihood that they will be receptive to the Gospel. They aren't beneath you. Be their servants, ministering the Gospel in love.

The heart

A living sacrifice

I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service. And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.Romans 12:1-2

This isn't an appeal to hit the gym and not eat poorly. It is about walking in the spirit that we may not fulfil the lusts of that flesh, and by example bring others to Christ.

For bodily exercise profiteth little: but godliness is profitable unto all things (). We the saved must exercise and grow, spiritually.

The heart

Samson's one line

That he told her all his heart, and said unto her, There hath not come a razor upon mine head; for I have been a Nazarite unto God from my mother’s womb: if I be shaven, then my strength will go from me, and I shall become weak, and be like any other man.Judges 16:17

The lifelong vow of the Nazarite that he violated so often, being unclean with dead bodies, and laying with harlots, included not taking a razor to his hair. And that's where he drew the line.

Many times as Christians, we do the same thing. We set an arbitrary line around one particular thing, that we fool ourselves into thinking we are right with God so long as that one thing isn't violated, even as we sin all around it.

In the end it was faith that delivered Samson. In the end God was the source of his strength.

Doers of the word

As a very lovely song

And, lo, thou art unto them as a very lovely song of one that hath a pleasant voice, and can play well on an instrument: for they hear thy words, but they do them not.Ezekiel 33:32

God compares their vain pleasure in hearing the word of God preached to those listening to a song. It's got a good beat, and the singer is talented, but it has no deeper meaning than that for them.

How many professing Christians, even in solid fundamental churches, are there just to enjoy the show? Who show up to a service, check the box, hear the word, and let it drip off them like water off a duck? Spending time with God's word should change you.

But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.James 1:22
Doers of the word

Built on the rock

And why call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things which I say?Luke 6:46

If we live by His word, which is like a foundation of rock, we will not be moved. If we don't, but lean into our own understanding, we are building on shifting sands ().

What is it your heart treasures? The mercy and grace of God? Or your petty resentments and grudges? For of the abundance of the heart his mouth speaketh ().

It is up to us whether we be the rock or the thorny ground, or if we are fertile soil. We have a choice ().

Doers of the word

Once you begin

Therefore to him that knoweth to do good, and doeth it not, to him it is sin.James 4:17

If you begin reading the Bible every day, you're admitting that you should. If you go to church once, you are admitting you should be going regularly, and now you don't have an excuse. This can be terrifying. It makes us make rationalizations. Or empty promises to “do better tomorrow.”

You will fail sometimes. It's ok. God's mercies are new every morning. You will be making yourself obligated to doing the right thing by acknowledging it is the right thing. That is the Spirit working in you. Accept it.

Doers of the word

Render unto God the things that are God's

They say unto him, Caesar’s. Then saith he unto them, Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar’s; and unto God the things that are God’s.Matthew 22:21

Often the focus here is mistakenly on Jesus saying to render unto Caesar what is Caesar's, which is true. But Jesus is turning the question around on those tempting Him, that their focus should be on the spiritual. They do not render unto God that which is His due.

We may be justified by the blood of Christ before God, but if we hide that faith under a bushel, or we bury that which we've been trusted, our faith is fruitless. Dead ().

Right judgment

Judge not, in context

Judge not, that ye be not judged. For with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged: and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again.Matthew 7:1-2

Jesus' words are often twisted by others to mean a Christian should never judge another person, no matter how wicked they are. But we know the warning is against hypocrisy, not right judgment. Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment ().

Rightly judge, beginning with ourselves that we may purge ourselves. First cast out the beam out of thine own eye ().

Right judgment

Another man's servant

Who art thou that judgest another man’s servant? to his own master he standeth or falleth. Yea, he shall be holden up: for God is able to make him stand.Romans 14:4

We are answerable to God. If one is falling down in some area, weak in the faith, God will correct them. Accusing them accuses Him ().

It isn't an excuse to enable, and standards and church discipline still exist. But encouraging speech, edifying one another with fear and admonition of the Lord, is what we should use. Not accusations of being less holy than thou.

Right judgment

Hear the matter first

He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him.Proverbs 18:13

He that is first in his own cause seemeth just; but his neighbour cometh and searcheth him (). We should not go with the first story you hear.

Phinehas, zealous for the Lord at Peor, shows discernment, judgment and mercy at the altar Ed. Rather than accuse, he asks of them the altar's purpose, and he listens to their answer (). We need to meekly and humbly seek to know, before either grabbing a javelin or declaring peace.

Right judgment

No respect of persons in judgment

These things also belong to the wise. It is not good to have respect of persons in judgment. He that saith unto the wicked, Thou art righteous; him shall the people curse, nations shall abhor him: But to them that rebuke him shall be delight, and a good blessing shall come upon them.Proverbs 24:23-25

The rich or the lords among us should be subject to the same treatment as the lowly and poor. Judge a man by the words they speak, their reputation for wisdom, diligence, the honor they give their parents. Not by appearances.

If you pervert justice and promote the wicked and cast your lot with the rich man over the widow or fatherless, woe unto you (). It is a blessing to call it straight and to rebuke that which is evil.

Words and vows

The window to the heart

But I say unto you, That every idle word that men shall speak, they shall give account thereof in the day of judgment. For by thy words thou shalt be justified, and by thy words thou shalt be condemned.Matthew 12:36-37

Words are the window to the heart of a man. One of the most repeated commands in scripture is to watch what you say.

Corrupt communication isn't simply a naughty word, as if it had power to corrupt. In this context, it's the things we say in wrath and anger, envy, strife, backbiting, tale bearing, railing, false accusations. Is it edifying ()?

Words and vows

Swear not at all

But let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay: for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil.Matthew 5:37

A just man doesn't need to prove by vow what his word is, because by being honest in all things, and keeping it to yes or no, and meaning it, we uphold the spirit of the law. Men will believe us or not ().

We are already called to live consecrated lives in service of the Lord. Separated unto the Gospel. Dedicated to the purposes of God without vow, but as His.

Words and vows

Don't make a vow you cannot keep

Better is it that thou shouldest not vow, than that thou shouldest vow and not pay.Ecclesiastes 5:5

People who “swear to God,” who make promises to God they cannot keep, need to be careful. God is very clear that He will exact the price for your vow ().

All Judah took an oath in their zeal, but the high places were not taken away (). None of us are perfect. Better to rely on God's mercy, than make promises in our excitement, that we in the flesh cannot keep.

Words and vows

Answer a fool

Answer not a fool according to his folly, lest thou also be like unto him. Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he be wise in his own conceit.Proverbs 26:4-5

When a fool is arguing foolishness, bickering back and forth makes you as much a fool, no matter how correct you are. But to not answer leaves him wise in his own conceit. The instruction then is to rebuke, not argue. To correct with authority, from scripture especially.

When we are out soul-winning, never get into arguments with the unreceptive or the mockers. You simply let them oppose themselves and move on. Shake off the dust ().

Words and vows

The Titus 3:10 rule

A man that is an heretick after the first and second admonition reject; Knowing that he that is such is subverted, and sinneth, being condemned of himself.Titus 3:10-11

Debates and silly theological questions about the law help no one. They can be a snare and a stumbling block to those seeking truth (). If you've shown them clear scripture, two times, and they still insist, then shake the dust.

The second they enter into putting words in your mouth, they are in bad faith. Be satisfied, instead of having to prove them wrong. That's your pride, and it helps no one. (I have fallen in to this trap. It's so tempting.)

Words and vows

Meekness as strength

For I bear them record that they have a zeal of God, but not according to knowledge.Romans 10:2

Rather we should correct them with love from the scripture they're misusing. Many of these people are well meaning. Pray that God opens their eyes ().

When we spiral in anger and get drawn into debate, all we do is give them the hook to hang us with. Jesus silenced them by their own hypocrisy ().

Stay out of contentions that don't involve you. It is like one that taketh a dog by the ears ().

Submitting to authority

As Christ submitted

But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men: And being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.Philippians 2:7-8

As Christ, who thought it not robbery to be equal with God, submitted Himself to the will of the Father, so we must submit ourselves to doing what we are created for; giving pleasure to God ().

Husbands submit to Christ. Wives submitting to husbands are submitting to Christ. Children honoring parents, servants working diligently, masters who rule justly, members of a nation submitting to authority, all are serving Christ ().

Submitting to authority

The spirit of rebellion

For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry. Because thou hast rejected the word of the Lord, he hath also rejected thee from being king.1 Samuel 15:23

“But I don't like it. I don't like being told what to do. I am obviously better than my parents, than my boss, than my rulers.” The same spirit of rebellion that Satan loosed into the world.

Many people today hate the idea of submitting to authority. Men refusing to join a church and submit themselves to a pastor. Who slack off at work, constantly disrespecting their bosses. This encourages wives to rebel against husbands, and children against parents. Ultimately, it is rebellion against God.

Submitting to authority

Daniel's meek appeal

But Daniel purposed in his heart that he would not defile himself with the portion of the king’s meat, nor with the wine which he drank: therefore he requested of the prince of the eunuchs that he might not defile himself.Daniel 1:8

But what if authority over you asks you to do something that would offend God? Daniel shows us how it is done. Meek and faithful, he doesn't rebel against the authority in his life, and yet doesn't compromise his faith. He instead asks permission ().

There's never an occasion to partake in another man's sin. There is always a way out, even if that way is to sacrifice yourself, that those who witness understand the right path ().

Submitting to authority

We are all here

But Paul cried with a loud voice, saying, Do thyself no harm: for we are all here.Acts 16:28

When an earthquake came and tare down the walls of the prison, Paul and Silas remained, obedient to the charge. And the Philippian jailer said, Sirs, what must I do to be saved? ()

The church of the Philippians was born and grew, and did mighty things for God, because of the obedience of Paul and Silas. To God and to government, even the government that unjustly persecuted them.

Submitting to authority

Yielding pacifieth great offences

If the spirit of the ruler rise up against thee, leave not thy place; for yielding pacifieth great offences.Ecclesiastes 10:4

If a ruler comes against you, justly or unjustly, it is better to yield than to flee or fight. David, pursued by Saul, had opportunity to kill the king, and refused. Daniel and his friends, Joseph in Egypt.

Mordecai did not bow to Haman (). The Jews in captivity were told to seek the peace of the city (). The entire affair could have been avoided by simply and meekly bowing. His rebellion seems borne of arrogance and pride.

Submitting to authority

Tribute to whom tribute is due

Render therefore to all their dues: tribute to whom tribute is due; custom to whom custom; fear to whom fear; honour to whom honour.Romans 13:7

Rendering to Caesar what is Caesar's is a trifling thing. Paying custom and tribute; taxes, is nothing. God provided us what we have and will again. Even from the mouth of a fish ().

God can use unsaved people, people who are wrong on doctrine but in position of authority. Moses heeded Jethro (). Submit with meekness and faith, always keeping God as the head, that we don't join in another man's sin.

Submitting to authority

Learning to serve that we may rule

And it shall be with him, and he shall read therein all the days of his life: that he may learn to fear the Lord his God, to keep all the words of this law and these statutes, to do them: That his heart be not lifted up above his brethren, and that he turn not aside from the commandment, to the right hand, or to the left: to the end that he may prolong his days in his kingdom, he, and his children, in the midst of Israel.Deuteronomy 17:19-20

We who are saved are heirs to the Kingdom, to rule and reign with Christ. We learn to minister that we may rule justly. How can He trust us with much when we squandered the talents we were given?

God told whomever would be king to make a handwritten copy of the law, that he may know it, and serve with a humble heart. We are not to turn either to the left or the right, but to maintain the straight paths He has set before us.

Submitting to authority

The hoary head

Thou shalt rise up before the hoary head, and honour the face of the old man, and fear thy God: I am the Lord.Leviticus 19:32

Young men, do not be as Elihu (). He waited on the elders, but not out of respect, but to bide his time so he could give his opinion. Even if you have knowledge, it is of the Lord. Have real humility.

Rehoboam forsook the counsel of the old men (), and a kingdom was lost. Young Christian men especially should seek to learn from their elders, not mock them.

Submitting to authority

Gravity before office

And let these also first be proved; then let them use the office of a deacon, being found blameless.1 Timothy 3:10

Respected men of faith in the church don't put on this gravity like a coat when they get named. You must be these things before you are ever even considered for leadership.

If you are a Christian man in your middle age, and aren't mentoring young men in your church, give an honest assessment between you and God. You might never hold a title, but your quiet strength and aged wisdom can still be used mightily by God.
