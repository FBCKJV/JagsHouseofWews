Every one his role

Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as it is fit in the Lord. Husbands, love your wives, and be not bitter against them. Children, obey your parents in all things: for this is well pleasing unto the Lord. Fathers, provoke not your children to anger, lest they be discouraged.Colossians 3:18-21

Each person in the home has a Godly role and responsibility, as both servants and masters. Husband and wife. Parent and child.

Regardless of how well the others do at keeping up their responsibilities, you're commanded by God to keep up yours.

God's order in the home

The order of heads

But I would have you know, that the head of every man is Christ; and the head of the woman is the man; and the head of Christ is God.1 Corinthians 11:3

The “head” in this regard is not the physical body part but the ruler. The higher power each submits to. Submission isn't total helplessness. It is just the order of delegation of authority, God, lords, husband, wife, children, each with a role to play. Each requiring both sacrifice and responsibility.

The first half of 1 Corinthians 11 is about hair length: women are to have long hair, men short (). If a woman thinks she should wear a covering, it's not a big enough deal to argue with her. The order of who is put over whom, with God as the head, is what's important.

God's order in the home

When men abdicate

As for my people, children are their oppressors, and women rule over them. O my people, they which lead thee cause thee to err, and destroy the way of thy paths.Isaiah 3:12

To the extent that women may usurp the authority of men, it is always the fault of men for abdicating their authority first. Adam, knowing the word of God, took the fruit and ate it to please Eve.

It's man's duty to God to love our women as Christ loves us. When we don't do our godly duty, society collapses. Men have been more traumatized and subverted than women. It's still up to us.

Far too many, male and female alike, stop reading at “submit” and take up the “war of the sexes” stances, bringing wrath and strife to what is a unifying principle of God.

God's order in the home

Godly women used mightily

And certain women, which had been healed of evil spirits and infirmities, Mary called Magdalene, out of whom went seven devils, And Joanna the wife of Chuza Herod’s steward, and Susanna, and many others, which ministered unto him of their substance.Luke 8:2-3

Women in the word of God are not weak and incidental. Deborah sat beneath a palm tree, and her God given wisdom brought people to her, in the absence of men stepping up (). Women who ministered with Christ provided from their substance and traveled with the disciples.

Yes, a qualified pastor must be a male, and women are to keep silence in the services (). But women are not subject to every man. They are subject to their father or their husband, in church to the pastor (just as the men are). Christian women are called to modesty and meekness ().

Husbands and wives

Wives submit, husbands love

Therefore as the church is subject unto Christ, so let the wives be to their own husbands in every thing. Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it;Ephesians 5:24-25

Women are shown “submit” and taught to resent it. Men are shown it, and taught entitlement, and none look at the responsibility being the head entails.

Men are called to love their wife as their own flesh. Protecting and providing for them as Jesus has for us. When men shirk this responsibility, women see no profit in submission. And the two who become one flesh, under God, tear themselves asunder ().

Husbands and wives

A virtuous woman

Who can find a virtuous woman? for her price is far above rubies. The heart of her husband doth safely trust in her, so that he shall have no need of spoil. She will do him good and not evil all the days of her life.Proverbs 31:10-12

She is trustworthy, diligent, rising early. A hard worker, who manages a household, starts small businesses, buys land, makes it fruitful. She helps the poor, is generous and hospitable. Her children learn her law and the fear of God at her feet.

She takes care of the house and has a side hustle, to the benefit of her household rather than some megacorp. It's not that women didn't work at all. They built up a reputation based on their own merits and labors. A prudent wife is from the LORD ().

Husbands and wives

Drink from thine own cistern

But I say unto you, That whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery with her already in his heart.Matthew 5:28

Today, how many married men are taken in by pornography and internet catfishing schemes? Can a man take fire in his bosom, and his clothes not be burned? () Delight in the wife of thy youth.

Our nakedness is to be for our spouse. This would extend to pornography, strippers, and every other form of nudity (). God's law of jealousy protected both: the husband from secret adultery, and the wife from an obsessive husband ().

Husbands and wives

Better to marry than to burn

Nevertheless, to avoid fornication, let every man have his own wife, and let every woman have her own husband. Let the husband render unto the wife due benevolence: and likewise also the wife unto the husband.1 Corinthians 7:2-3

To withhold relations with our spouse for reason other than brief periods of fasting, agreed upon, is defrauding our spouse ().

If you are living with or fornicating with your boyfriend or girlfriend, you should stop and get married. And by a man's fornication you don't just make the child, you make the woman a mother. He is now on the hook for providing for both.

Husbands and wives

Trust the Lord to bring your Rebekah

And he said, Blessed be the Lord God of my master Abraham, who hath not left destitute my master of his mercy and his truth: I being in the way, the Lord led me to the house of my master’s brethren.Genesis 24:27

So many young men seek their Rebekah, and go where they should not, not trusting in the Lord to guide them. I am firmly convinced, with the longview of being an old head, of seeing God's angels bringing me where I needed to be.

Don't be discouraged. And young ladies seeking a spouse, live Godly with hospitality, grace and kindness, and the man for you will show himself.

Divorce and remarriage

What God hath joined together

Wherefore they are no more twain, but one flesh. What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder. They say unto him, Why did Moses then command to give a writing of divorcement, and to put her away? He saith unto them, Moses because of the hardness of your hearts suffered you to put away your wives: but from the beginning it was not so.Matthew 19:6-8

Again we see the mistaken impression that the Pharisees were legalistic and strict, when in reality they sought loopholes from God's law, and Jesus teaches a stricter interpretation. Moses' compromise on divorce came from the failures and weaknesses of men, not God's intent.

(Full disclosure, I am divorced and remarried, to a widow. This makes me an adulterer. It doesn't matter the circumstances. It is this because Jesus says it is. But, thankfully we are forgiven in Jesus.)

Divorce and remarriage

How Satan uses divorce

For the Lord, the God of Israel, saith that he hateth putting away: for one covereth violence with his garment, saith the Lord of hosts: therefore take heed to your spirit, that ye deal not treacherously.Malachi 2:16

First he gets us to justify divorce. Then fornication and cohabitation become normalized, because if divorce is inevitable, what's the point of marriage? And if divorce harms children, why have them in the first place? And so birth control and abortion become the norm. And Satan keeps accusing.

Many spouses have one foot out the door, looking for any cause to confirm the distrust in their hearts sown by the wicked culture that we have.

Do the hard thing

Now therefore let us make a covenant with our God to put away all the wives, and such as are born of them, according to the counsel of my lord, and of those that tremble at the commandment of our God; and let it be done according to the law.Ezra 10:3

Ezra's mass divorce was a man's idea, not God's. These men could have been removed from office, having disqualified themselves, as Nehemiah did with the priest (). Two wrongs don't always make a right.

When faced with a hard choice because of our sins, wait on God's will. Do the hard things. Not latch on to man's compromise. It can be hard living with a spouse who doesn't treat you right. God says stick it out.

Honour thy father and mother

The first commandment with promise

Honour thy father and mother; which is the first commandment with promise; That it may be well with thee, and thou mayest live long on the earth.Ephesians 6:2-3

Modern teaching misses the point of this commandment, limiting it almost exclusively to children obeying their parents. Honor in the Bible often means paying a price, providing financial support ().

But if any provide not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel (). Take in your parents in their old age. Provide for them.

Honour thy father and mother

Corban

But ye say, If a man shall say to his father or mother, It is Corban, that is to say, a gift, by whatsoever thou mightest be profited by me; he shall be free. And ye suffer him no more to do ought for his father or his mother; Making the word of God of none effect through your tradition, which ye have delivered: and many such like things do ye.Mark 7:11-13

People used the Corban to get out of honoring, that is financially supporting, their parents in their later years. The letter of the law used to justify corruption.

The refusal of children to take care of their parents, consigning them to nursing homes and the state, came before working moms, daycare and divorce. God put in place a multi-generational family where each generation provides something to the unit, and stability to society.

Honour thy father and mother

Obedience modeled by the Son

And being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.Philippians 2:8

Jesus provided the example of a lifelong commitment to honoring the Father. Loving God with your whole heart begins with fearing your parents. The Rechabites obeyed even their father's own rules, and God blessed their line ().

At the judgment seat of Christ, no one's going to give you rewards for how big a victim you were. You won't receive crowns because of how unfairly your parents treated you. But you will be judged for how well you honored them.

Honour thy father and mother

The rebellious son

And Joash did that which was right in the sight of the Lord all the days of Jehoiada the priest.2 Chronicles 24:2

How often do we see the children of a Christian man, kept in subjection, seeing a crack in their father's character (for all have fallen short), grow to resent him? In that resentment their own sinful desires find a justification, and they tear down the moral authority in their life.

And once that authority is removed from them, they sink further and further into sin (). Many such cases.

Raising children

Teach them diligently

And these words, which I command thee this day, shall be in thine heart: And thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children, and shalt talk of them when thou sittest in thine house, and when thou walkest by the way, and when thou liest down, and when thou risest up.Deuteronomy 6:6-7

Even godly Christian parents can become lax, believing that sharing the gospel with them and bringing them to church is sufficient. Hoping Godliness will be passed on by osmosis from one church service a week.

Our fathers have told us, what work thou didst in their days (). Tell your children of God moving in your life, so that they may learn to trust Him in hard times. We were Pharaoh's bondmen in Egypt; and the LORD brought us out ().

Raising children

The rod of correction

He that spareth his rod hateth his son: but he that loveth him chasteneth him betimes.Proverbs 13:24

A scorner is that way because his father didn't love him enough to chasten him. A wise son listens because his father cared enough to apply the rod (; ). The rod teaches them that chastisement comes from love, and they get a picture of God the Father through you ().

When we discipline, it must be with sound judgment and mercy, longsuffering and with grace as God gave us. And mothers, do not undercut the discipline your husband gives out ().

Raising children

Provoke not your children to wrath

And, ye fathers, provoke not your children to wrath: but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord.Ephesians 6:4

Some Christian men think the only way to protect their children from the world is as a tyrant. Their fear shows their children a lack of faith, and it can drive them away from the Lord. Many parents create a barter system in place of love, forgiving but not forgetting. This builds within a child the seeds of a works-based relationship with God.

Honesty about sin and consequences, how it has affected you personally, can help soften the blow when children realize Mom and Dad aren't perfect. And the gospel lets them know there is One who is perfect.

Raising children

You cannot carry their salvation

Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.Proverbs 22:6

It isn't a guarantee, but it certainly is a great inoculation against the snares most people get caught on. Godly father/wicked son, wicked father/Godly sons are found from Adam and Cain straight through the Old Testament (). We cannot exert our own righteousness and salvation onto our children.

Remove the high places in your heart, and you increase the odds that your children will do the same. Read your Bible, be patient and fair. Rule well. Every kid is different, but start with scripture.

Raising children

An heritage of the LORD

Lo, children are an heritage of the Lord: and the fruit of the womb is his reward. As arrows are in the hand of a mighty man; so are children of the youth. Happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them: they shall not be ashamed, but they shall speak with the enemies in the gate.Psalm 127:3-5

God determines when a woman has a child or not. He opens the wombs and closes them (). Today the world uses chemicals to keep women sterile, and kills children as burdens instead of blessings.

God is our goalie. We are to become one flesh, husband and wife, trusting in Him to provide and prevent. We should be producing multitudes of Godly children. It is the main way we increase the Lord's blessing in the world, other than soul winning. (I've done my part, believe me.)

Raising children

With all gravity

One that ruleth well his own house, having his children in subjection with all gravity;1 Timothy 3:4

God has given married men a great and terrible responsibility. To be meek, longsuffering, merciful and just... and with all gravity, bringing your house into subjection. If you don't understand the choices that come from that, especially with rebellious children, read Deuteronomy 21:18-21 and consider what could lead a parent there ().

One that ruleth well his own house is not a clause that allows rebellious children who don't like how their parents chastised them to exercise a veto on a ministry.
