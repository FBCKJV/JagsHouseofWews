The Comforter

Nevertheless I tell you the truth; It is expedient for you that I go away: for if I go not away, the Comforter will not come unto you; but if I depart, I will send him unto you. And when he is come, he will reprove the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment: Of sin, because they believe not on me; Of righteousness, because I go to my Father, and ye see me no more; Of judgment, because the prince of this world is judged. I have yet many things to say unto you, but ye cannot bear them now.John 16:7-12

He reproves the world

The Spirit of God, who was in the beginning, who inspired the prophets, who fell upon the men of God throughout the Old Testament, has other roles and responsibilities in the New Testament we are under.

He reproves, convicting the world of sin. That is, saved or unsaved people; that little voice is our conscience. He reproves the world of righteousness, silently pointing the lost toward faith in Christ. And of judgment, showing us that the prince of this world, that is, Satan, is wrong and will be destroyed.

The Spirit of truth

He is the Spirit of truth. When we hear the truth, especially from the Word of God, the thing that catches our attention and makes us listen is the Spirit. He moves saved believers into the path of the unsaved that they may hear the gospel: divine appointments. He softens hearts and opens ears. He helps lead people to salvation, if they respond.

Once we believe

Once we believe, He indwells us. He quickens our spirit so that we can know Him; this is regeneration. He sanctifies that new man inside us, sealing us unto redemption. The Bible says He is the earnest of our inheritance, the down payment of God upon us, His purchased possession.

He is the Spirit of adoption that lets us know we are the children of God, crying “Abba!” or “Father.” The Spirit testifies within us that we have eternal life.

He guides, remembers and intercedes

He guides us to all truth and lets us know truth from error. He brings to memory the word of God when we need it. He intercedes in our prayers, even when we don’t know what to pray for. He opens up knowledge to us and understanding when we read the Bible.

When we walk in the Spirit, the Bible says, we don’t fulfill the lusts of the flesh. He keeps us honest. We are His temple.

The Bible uses “Spirit” and “Ghost” interchangeably. The Holy Spirit and the Holy Ghost are one and the same.

The nature and attributes of the Spirit

The Holy Spirit is a distinct person of the Godhead, possessing all the attributes of God.

  • Eternal
  • Omnipotent
  • Omniscient
  • Omnipresent

Gifts and fruit

The gifts and the fruit of the Spirit are often discussed together, but they are different in purpose. Gifts are supernatural abilities given by God to a believer for the benefit of others and the church (teaching, helps, prophecy), given as the Spirit wills; not every believer has the same gifts.

Fruit, on the other hand, is the character produced by the Spirit living in a believer’s life. It is not an ability but a reflection of the Spirit’s work, and all believers are commanded to cultivate it. A person may have a powerful gift, but the true mark of spiritual maturity is the presence of the fruit.
