Immediately after the tribulation

Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens shall be shaken: And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven: and then shall all the tribes of the earth mourn, and they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory. And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other.Matthew 24:29-31

There is no secret rapture before the tribulation. There is a very public and unquestionable rapture before the wrath of God is poured out, after the tribulation. Dispensationalists insist the Great Tribulation occurs after a secret second coming, despite these clear verses giving the order of things.

Behold, I have foretold you all things (). Pre-tribbers hate this. But Jesus was very clear, this is the order of events.

After the tribulation

They came out of great tribulation

And I said unto him, Sir, thou knowest. And he said to me, These are they which came out of great tribulation, and have washed their robes, and made them white in the blood of the Lamb.Revelation 7:14

Note the order. They arrive after the tribulation, after the 6 seals, on the day of the Lord. Before the wrath, which only comes after the 7th seal is opened. It is a post-trib, pre-wrath rapture.

The 144,000 are not modern Jews. They are 12,000 of each of the tribes, Dan swapped for Manasses, sealed on their foreheads, and left on the earth during the time of wrath, sealed against harm ().
