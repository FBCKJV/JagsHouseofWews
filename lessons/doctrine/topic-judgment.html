Remember now thy Creator

Remember now thy Creator in the days of thy youth, while the evil days come not, nor the years draw nigh, when thou shalt say, I have no pleasure in them;Ecclesiastes 12:1

Solomon the king and preacher sums up the end of the matter. Remember thy Creator. Remember Him in your youth, while it is still light. Before the darkness of this life or the time to come, when it is too late.

There is a time it is too late to fear the Lord, to get right with Him, to remember our Creator.

Now is the day of salvation

Seek the LORD while he may be found

Seek ye the Lord while he may be found, call ye upon him while he is near:Isaiah 55:6

Is there any judgment upon a nation as fearsome as their seeking the Lord and not being able to find Him, because He has withdrawn Himself from them? () Reprobate silver shall men call them ().

The false idea that it is never too late with God, that as long as you live there's always a chance for salvation, has led so many to Hell. They put off to tomorrow the gift of grace. Like Pharaoh, they harden their hearts. Wake to Him and seek peace in Christ while you can.

Now is the day of salvation

To day if ye will hear his voice

Again, he limiteth a certain day, saying in David, To day, after so long a time; as it is said, To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts.Hebrews 4:7

Their unbelief in the desert prevented them from entering the promised land; so too fear your unbelief keeping you from entering into the rest in Jesus. For we which have believed do enter into rest (). Note the tense.

God gives every opportunity to repent and believe on Jesus Christ, but it isn't limitless. Just as God was longsuffering as Noah built his ark, and then shut the door, too late to be saved, so too is His offer of grace ().

The judgment seat

Every man according to his works

For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ; that every one may receive the things done in his body, according to that he hath done, whether it be good or bad.2 Corinthians 5:10

You heard me. The Lord Jesus Christ will judge every man according to his works. When we are judged, it isn't for our justification; Jesus paid that price for us. God rewards us for our works in the Kingdom of God. He pays His laborers.

Gift vs wage. We do not earn gifts. A wage is debt owed. In sin, our debt is paid by death and hell, but in Christ we are given grace, a free gift, that we may serve Him, and earn rewards ().

The judgment seat

Gold, silver, or wood, hay and stubble

Now if any man build upon this foundation gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, stubble; Every man’s work shall be made manifest: for the day shall declare it, because it shall be revealed by fire; and the fire shall try every man’s work of what sort it is. If any man’s work abide which he hath built thereupon, he shall receive a reward. If any man’s work shall be burned, he shall suffer loss: but he himself shall be saved; yet so as by fire.1 Corinthians 3:12-15

At the Bema seat, wood, hay and stubble does not mean God pulls out a list of your confessed or covered sins to punish you. It means He evaluates the things you did that were worthless for the Kingdom. Time wasted (arguing online), fleshly ambitions, religious works done for show. Burned up: a loss of reward, not an infliction of punishment.

Winning souls to Christ, helping the poor and the fatherless, the widows, being doers of the word, those things are rewarded by God in the judgment.

The judgment seat

Ruler over many things

His lord said unto him, Well done, thou good and faithful servant: thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.Matthew 25:21

In the millennial reign, we who are saved will be appointed to duties, roles and responsibilities, even judge over matters pertaining to God. There shall be thrones, cities and wards, areas of responsibility.

These are the crowns we strive for. This is why we humble ourselves and serve those in need. This is where the least among us, meek and lowly, become first in the Kingdom of God ().

The judgment seat

Crowns

For what is our hope, or joy, or crown of rejoicing? Are not even ye in the presence of our Lord Jesus Christ at his coming?1 Thessalonians 2:19

Every soul we help bring towards Christ, whether a seed sown, watered or harvested, is our glory and joy in Christ's coming. A crown given us, a precious stone, after the wood, hay and stubble are burned away.

A crown of righteousness... unto all them also that love his appearing (). Failure to deliver them that are drawn unto death by claiming ignorance is not an excuse ().

The end of the wicked

Hell is real

And if thy hand offend thee, cut it off: it is better for thee to enter into life maimed, than having two hands to go into hell, into the fire that never shall be quenched: Where their worm dieth not, and the fire is not quenched.Mark 9:43-44

This isn't allegory. It's not a parable. It isn't a mistranslation of the Greek, or a later doctrine cobbled together by extra-Biblical sources. Depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire... And these shall go away into everlasting punishment ().

The torment of the damned takes place in the presence of the Lamb (). Some use corrupt versions to claim Sheol differs from Hades, or that “Abraham's bosom” is a place rather than a body part. Hell is very real, and God is not slack regarding His promises.

The end of the wicked

Eternal torment, not annihilation

And in hell he lift up his eyes, being in torments, and seeth Abraham afar off, and Lazarus in his bosom. And he cried and said, Father Abraham, have mercy on me, and send Lazarus, that he may dip the tip of his finger in water, and cool my tongue; for I am tormented in this flame.Luke 16:23-24

Many have turned out to declare the false doctrine of “annihilationism,” that there is no eternal torment in store for the unbeliever. While the widow of a believer can take comfort in their rest, the unbeliever is going to eternal torment, their wealth motheaten and dust ().

We shouldn't draw doctrine from parables taken literally. But Heaven and Hell are real, fixed places, and the destination of the dead, saved or unsaved.

The end of the wicked

The great white throne

And I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life: and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works.Revelation 20:12

The Great White Throne judgment is of the dead. The damned, great and small, whose names were blotted out of the Book of Life. Judged by the books of the law, the scriptures of God, written in our hearts, His wrath written in all of creation, that man may have no excuse.

No where in scripture does it say any one gets written into the Book of Life. It was written before creation, and contains every one that lives, who are blotted out if they be reprobate or die without the testimony of Jesus ().

The end of the wicked

Vengeance is mine

Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath: for it is written, Vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord.Romans 12:19

God's love towards us, mercy and forgiveness, His grace, requires His wrath upon the ungodly. They cannot be separated. Why do bad things happen? Men decide to do them. God visits evil upon evil.

Punished with everlasting destruction from the presence of the Lord (). When we despair that the unrighteous prosper, we should praise God that vengeance is His. He will repay.

The end of the wicked

Thou that judgest

Therefore thou art inexcusable, O man, whosoever thou art that judgest: for wherein thou judgest another, thou condemnest thyself; for thou that judgest doest the same things.Romans 2:1

If we judge them in their sin against the law, we need to remember we are judged by the same law with the same end, absent Christ. His wrath and our guilt in the law should bring us to repentance, towards the Gospel.

The wicked try to silence preaching of the word against them, because the word of God shames them (). God shall likewise destroy thee for ever ().

The hope of the resurrection

I know that my redeemer liveth

For I know that my redeemer liveth, and that he shall stand at the latter day upon the earth: And though after my skin worms destroy this body, yet in my flesh shall I see God:Job 19:25-26

Job notes the brevity of life, like a flower cut down. But there is hope of a tree. Even if the stock die in the ground, a little water can bring it to bud again (). Men die, and shall not be raised until the judgment.

The hope of the resurrection

Absent from the body

We are confident, I say, and willing rather to be absent from the body, and to be present with the Lord.2 Corinthians 5:8

Our souls go to Heaven, not sleep or instantly regain consciousness at the resurrection as if no time had passed. The souls of the righteous cry out “how long?” (). We still exist within time as the created.

In the resurrection, our glorified bodies will be like the angels, who neither marry (). And all babies go to Heaven: Job wished he died at birth, to be at rest ().
