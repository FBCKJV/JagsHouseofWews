Adopted Sons and Heirs

We who are saved by grace through faith are adopted as sons of God, heirs to His promise and the Kingdom of Heaven.

The doctrine

Who are the sons of God?

But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:John 1:12

Believers. Those that call upon the name of the Lord.

Those born again into the promise, adopted sons, heirs to the Kingdom of the Lord.

Joint-heirs with Christ

For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God. For ye have not received the spirit of bondage again to fear; but ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father. The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God: And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together.Romans 8:14-17

We are heirs of God, joint-heirs with Christ. Not co-religionists with Jews. Not co-heirs with them. But with Christ. And unbelieving Jews have no claim to the promise.

Adopted after first believing

In verse 9, he makes it clear that this is a conditional state of being. You aren’t adopted without first believing.

But ye are not in the flesh, but in the Spirit, if so be that the Spirit of God dwell in you. Now if any man have not the Spirit of Christ, he is none of his.Romans 8:9

False premises

People get really caught up in free will vs predestination, because they begin with fundamentally false premises, and wrong definitions of terms.

Predestination

The destination of adoption was predetermined for all who enter in through Christ, not predetermining which people would step through the doorway.

Then are the children free

He saith, Yes. And when he was come into the house, Jesus prevented him, saying, What thinkest thou, Simon? of whom do the kings of the earth take custom or tribute? of their own children, or of strangers? Peter saith unto him, Of strangers. Jesus saith unto him, Then are the children free.Matthew 17:25-26

What a wonderful example of the free gift of salvation, without works. The children are free. They don’t pay tribute to the King.

Our entrance to the kingdom is free, for we are at Liberty in Christ. We pay nothing, not our works or labors or deeds. It is a gift of God given to all who believe.

But while we sojourn here on earth, we pay tribute to whom tribute is due. Princes of a better country.

Abba, Father

And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father.Galatians 4:6

That we who believe have the Spirit of the Son within us. That we have that quickened spirit, born again crying “Abba!” (Father) to God.

The Spirit itself bears witness to this adoption. That we are the children of God, and those without Christ are none of His.

Sons of the freewoman

Now we, brethren, as Isaac was, are the children of promise. But as then he that was born after the flesh persecuted him that was born after the Spirit, even so it is now. Nevertheless what saith the scripture? Cast out the bondwoman and her son: for the son of the bondwoman shall not be heir with the son of the freewoman. So then, brethren, we are not children of the bondwoman, but of the free.Galatians 4:28-31

Thus we who are born again in the Spirit become heirs, while those trying to work their way into the kingdom, those under bondage, are denied.

Do ye not hear the law?

Tell me, ye that desire to be under the law, do ye not hear the law?Galatians 4:21

The judaizers

“Yes it’s by faith alone but you have to do the works of the law to be saved. You have to adhere to our vain traditions. To our carnal ordinances. To our dietary restrictions. You don’t expect to just be handed salvation? Do you?”

Sarah

Just as Sarah was barren from birth, and brought to old age, it took a miracle of God, visited upon her, to bring her the promise. Not by anything she did, for she was not capable, but in God all things are possible.

If you’re trusting in your own works, if you’re sewing your own fig leaves, if you’re committing adultery with Hagar to produce your salvation, repent. Hear the law.

Under tutors and governors

Now I say, That the heir, as long as he is a child, differeth nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all; But is under tutors and governors until the time appointed of the father.Galatians 4:1-2

The son can be heir but under a schoolmaster, tutors and governors, yet in the fullness of time, be as the Lord, an inheritor of all.

The study of the word is like unto princes under tutors, until the day of adoption. The resurrection where we come into our power as glorified sons of God.

Waiting for the adoption

We become sons of God when we are born again, but notice the adoption is at the resurrection. At the day of redemption.

And not only they, but ourselves also, which have the firstfruits of the Spirit, even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for the adoption, to wit, the redemption of our body.Romans 8:23

The Greek word Paul uses for ‘adoption,’ huiothesia, carries a heavier legal meaning than our modern idea of adoption. It was the formal ceremony, the “placing as a son,” where an heir reached the age of maturity, the father publicly recognized them as a legal adult, granting them the full authority, a signet ring, and power to manage the estate.

We shall be like Him

Beloved, now are we the sons of God, and it doth not yet appear what we shall be: but we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is.1 John 3:2

At the adoption, when we put on immortality and shed corruption, when we are redeemed, our end will be glorious, as we will be like Him.

Even the least in the Kingdom of Heaven is a prince, who will judge with Him in the millennium. We will judge angels.

All believers are saints

A select few

It is not a select few whose works were so good that they are honored here on Earth with the title. By making a church of man the arbiter, and basing it on works, the Catholic church has cheapened the term.

All who believe

All believers are saints. It’s a title bestowed throughout to all who believe. I am a saint. If you believe you are a saint as well. Even if you’re backslidden.

And as saints we should turn from sin, we should live a righteous life as possible. Live up to it.

What manner of love

Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God: therefore the world knoweth us not, because it knew him not.1 John 3:1

Take comfort in this knowledge, joy in the incomprehensible love God has shown us, that we may be adopted joint heirs with His only begotten Son, Christ Jesus.
