Confession and Forgiveness

God wants to forgive us more than we want to be forgiven.

Faithful and just to forgive

If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.1 John 1:9

First John, the epistle, is written to the saved.

Here the Bible tells us if we the saved, want fellowship with God and one another, to walk with God, to confess our sins to Him, and be forgiven them all by Christ, washed clean in His blood.

We know that in Christ our sins are forgiven, past, present and future. But we are also to faithfully confess our sins to the Father, Who is faithful again to forgive.

Admitting our sin before God

Confession, the act of admitting guilt, of revealing your own faults and iniquities, is a fundamental act of humility in the Christian life.

A soul that sins and doesn't confess their trespass to God, can't avail themselves of the sacrifice. You can't get saved without this acknowledgment. You can't accept salvation without knowing you need to be saved.

And so we must confess we are guilty under the law to be saved, and once saved, we confess our sins that God may work through the Spirit indwelling us to remove those thorns in our hearts.

Pride against confession

Works religion

This pride against confession is what draws people to a works-based false religion. People seek to be absolved in any way possible short of actually acknowledging their sin.

“Perfect contrition”

It requires the sinner to be 100% honest, 100% contrite, about 100% of their sins, that depends on the sinner alone, not the blood of Christ.

The believer

We confess our sins to God knowing He's forgiven them already, that we have eternal grace that's never lost or taken away, in Christ Jesus.

Working to maintain God's grace makes grace no more grace.

If we say that we have no sin

If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us. If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness. If we say that we have not sinned, we make him a liar, and his word is not in us.1 John 1:8-10

Sinless perfection

But some claim sinless perfection, and believe what saves them is their not sinning. According to the Bible they are liars.

Our flesh sins, not the new man

Whosoever is born of God doth not commit sin; for his seed remaineth in him: and he cannot sin, because he is born of God.1 John 3:9

John is not contradicting himself by saying if we say we have no sin, we are liars and simultaneously saying we cannot sin, being born of God.

When we sin, it is not the new man, created in Christ, but the flesh.

This promise shows not only the faithfulness of God to forgive, but also that our sin in the flesh is not held against our ledger in Heaven. It is not imputed against those that are in Christ.

Written to the brethren

My little children, these things write I unto you, that ye sin not. And if any man sin, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous: And he is the propitiation for our sins: and not for ours only, but also for the sins of the whole world.1 John 2:1-2

Note he is writing to brethren. To the children of God, babes in Christ. He is edifying them and encouraging them to walk with God, to avoid sin and not deny it, but understand that if we are honest we confess our sins, and they are forgiven already in Christ.

Because we are sons of God.

Psalm 51 · 2 Samuel 12

David's cry of contrition

Psalm 51 is the clearest and most sober cry of contrition and supplication for forgiveness by a believer, in the face of a judgment by the Almighty God, that we have in Scripture.

And David said unto Nathan, I have sinned against the Lord. And Nathan said unto David, The Lord also hath put away thy sin; thou shalt not die.2 Samuel 12:13

David acknowledges his sin and understands his plight is of his own making, and ultimately accepts God's judgment against him. This is a model for us to follow. This is how we get right with God.

Against thee only have I sinned

For I acknowledge my transgressions: and my sin is ever before me. Against thee, thee only, have I sinned, and done this evil in thy sight: that thou mightest be justified when thou speakest, and be clear when thou judgest.Psalm 51:3-4

Bitterness

These people are destroyed with their bitterness, wracked with guilt, have a broken relationship with the God that bought them, and are often called home early, the result of their being incapable of being honest and appealing to His mercy.

David

David acknowledges his understanding that God's judgments are right and deserved. But David shows us the better way.

Restore unto me the joy of thy salvation

Cast me not away from thy presence; and take not thy holy spirit from me. Restore unto me the joy of thy salvation; and uphold me with thy free spirit.Psalm 51:11-12

David is not writing that he is afraid to lose his salvation, but seeking the confidence in it again that has been marred by his transgressions and the fear from the chastening he has received.

He is seeking to renew his relationship with God through his confession and appeals to mercy.

It is the mercy God shows us as His children that leads us to share His praise to others.

He that covereth his sins

He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy.Proverbs 28:13

Covering sin leads to the chastening of the Lord. Humbling ourselves to admit our failings will have mercy.

Don't deceive yourself when faced with chastisement of the Lord. God is long-suffering and merciful to a humble heart, calling out to Him. But you forsake mercy when you stay in denial.

Confession, telling the truth in our heart about what it is we have done, what we do, is for our sake; that we may know His mercy and forgiveness and be cleaned from all unrighteousness.

Forgiveness is not earned by penance

Penance

God doesn't want sacrifice. He doesn't want our works of penance and vows to change. He takes no delight in the ritual formulas of burnt offerings, and performative acts of contrition.

Grace

Penance isn't how we earn forgiveness. Forgiveness is given by grace and mercy, charitable love extended to a sincere change of heart and contrition, acknowledging what we've done and how it impacted others.

The Father ran

I will arise and go to my father, and will say unto him, Father, I have sinned against heaven, and before thee, And am no more worthy to be called thy son: make me as one of thy hired servants. And he arose, and came to his father. But when he was yet a great way off, his father saw him, and had compassion, and ran, and fell on his neck, and kissed him.Luke 15:18-20

The Father runs to meet his returning son, showering him with love. Before any confession of guilt or offer of penance or anything other than turning back towards God, the Father goes to him in compassion and restitution of their relationship.

God wants to forgive us more than we want to be forgiven.

Proverbs 28:1

Bold as a lion

Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need.Hebrews 4:16

This boldness comes from having a clean conscience towards God by the blood of Christ.

We don't need to keep a guilty conscience, in constant anxiety. We have the boldness of Christ's righteousness and the promise of a merciful Father.

We shouldn't be miserable. We shouldn't cling to guilt for sins and iniquities God has forgiven and remembers no more.

New every morning

It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.Lamentations 3:22-23

No matter how backslidden. No matter how snared in sin. No matter how the devil accuses you before God, in Christ Jesus you are forgiven.

For a saved believer who has been sealed by the Spirit, it is never too late to turn back to God and away from your sins. Like the Prodigal son, returning to the Father.

Confess your faults one to another

Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.James 5:16

Brothers and sisters in Christ should edify one another, being of one mind in Christ.

True confession

True confession is a wonderful tool given by God that we may become separated unto the Gospel and better vessels fitted to His purpose.

False confession

False confession, anything that seeks to be given excuse or justification for our sin that we may remain in it, or that we can work it off in some way of penance, is wicked as Hell and leads people further and further into sin.

In summary

  1. Written to the savedIf we the saved want fellowship with God, we confess our sins to Him, and they are forgiven already in Christ.
  2. Confession is humilityOnce saved, we confess our sins that God may work through the Spirit indwelling us.
  3. The flesh sinsWhen we sin, it is not the new man, created in Christ, but the flesh. It is not imputed against those that are in Christ.
  4. David's wayHe acknowledged his sin and accepted God's judgment. This is how we get right with God.
  5. Covering or confessingCovering sin leads to the chastening of the Lord. Humbling ourselves to admit our failings will have mercy.
  6. Not penanceForgiveness is given by grace and mercy. His mercies are new every morning.
