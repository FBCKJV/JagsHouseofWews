Doctrine · Salvation

Grace, Not Works

Salvation by grace through faith without works has been the Gospel from the beginning.

By grace are ye saved

For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.Ephesians 2:8-9

Grace is unmerited, unearned and unconditional love.

That works are not how we are saved is the clearest, most repeated doctrine in scripture, and yet it continues to be the stumbling block that keeps so many perishing, damned to Hell because they trust in their self righteousness to save them.

Grace cannot be earned

Grace is the unearned, unmerited, undeserved gift given by God from His mercy and love.

It’s not a spiritual, magical “force” like Star Wars. It doesn’t come in portions. You don’t need reups and you cannot earn it. It is the favor of God to redeem you in Christ, despite you deserving death and Hell. It is an expression of His love towards us.

And if by grace, then is it no more of works: otherwise grace is no more grace. But if it be of works, then is it no more grace: otherwise work is no more work.Romans 11:6

Grace, not debt

Now to him that worketh is the reward not reckoned of grace, but of debt. But to him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, his faith is counted for righteousness.Romans 4:4-5

If it were of works, that means God would be indebted to us. To pay us for our works. That we were owed. Debt is deserved. Grace is undeserved.

“I must work to be saved, or to stay saved”

When someone says their salvation or their ability to stay saved is contingent on their works, they are saying they are good enough to please God. That they are owed salvation. That the glory is theirs.

Grace is not transactional

Love is not transactional. Grace is not contingent on conditions, because it is unearned. Undeserved. Unconditional love.

Every mouth stopped

Now we know that what things soever the law saith, it saith to them who are under the law: that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may become guilty before God. Therefore by the deeds of the law there shall no flesh be justified in his sight: for by the law is the knowledge of sin.Romans 3:19-20

That is the purpose of the law, to serve to humble us and reveal the holiness of God, that we may come to call upon His name to be saved.

It is impossible for man to work their way to Heaven. We cannot attain the righteousness of God.

Numbers 5

The ledger of sin

The concept of the ledger and the debt incurred by sin is given that we may know we can never do enough to offset it. Our only hope is in Christ.

God demands payment plus the fifth, which when you consider the sins of man in a given day, if all were to be covered, even a righteous and godly man could be bankrupt by the end of a few days.

If you believe that you have to work for your salvation, that Jesus needs your help to impute his righteousness over your filthy rags, then what must you do to be saved? The Bible makes it clear: Keep the whole of the law. But if you offended one point, Hell awaits you.

Justified freely by his grace

For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God; Being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus: Whom God hath set forth to be a propitiation through faith in his blood, to declare his righteousness for the remission of sins that are past, through the forbearance of God; To declare, I say, at this time his righteousness: that he might be just, and the justifier of him which believeth in Jesus.Romans 3:23-26

We are justified freely by His grace. Even the righteousness of God, that is by faith, upon all them that believe.

That’s the good news. This is the Gospel.

Therefore we conclude

Therefore we conclude that a man is justified by faith without the deeds of the law.Romans 3:28

Read that again. Say it out loud. Three chapters of building argument against the judaizers, the works-based false gospel.

There is no one who reads this and says “yeah, but you have to do works” who is seeking truth.

Fig leaves and Cain’s offering

Fig leaves

The first instinct Adam and Eve had, when faced with this feeling of shame, was to attempt to cover it up with their own works. And like our own works, the fig leaves were insufficient to make them righteous.

Cain’s fruit of the ground

Cain brought his works and was rejected.

Abel’s lamb

The firstling lamb. The sacrifice of blood for atonement. The Bible says Abel by faith brought the sacrifice that was acceptable unto the Lord.

How many of us seeking our own righteousness, poorly sow together fig leaves of our own works, seeking to erase the transgressions ourselves. Hiding from God?

Abraham, before circumcision

Counted unto him for righteousness

And he believed in the Lord; and he counted it to him for righteousness.Genesis 15:6
For what saith the scripture? Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness.Romans 4:3
How was it then reckoned? when he was in circumcision, or in uncircumcision? Not in circumcision, but in uncircumcision. And he received the sign of circumcision, a seal of the righteousness of the faith which he had yet being uncircumcised: that he might be the father of all them that believe, though they be not circumcised; that righteousness might be imputed unto them also:Romans 4:10-11

Abraham did not receive the ordinance of circumcision until decades after his believing God’s promise, and being saved by that faith.

There is no rite or ordinance that is required for salvation. Not baptism. Not any ritual or keeping of tradition. No works of the law.

Saved by the blood before the law

Salvation is front loaded and found only by faith. For the salvation of Israel from bondage and death came by faith alone, by the blood.

1The Passover blood. The salvation out of sin of Egypt had already taken place.
2The sea. The baptism of the waters of the sea followed.
3The law. The understanding of the law given.
4Sanctified. Then they were told to sanctify themselves, for their own good and to better serve God’s will.

Turn from sin first

Those telling others they must turn from their sins first before they can believe are entangling them in bondage. Placing the yoke upon those who should be set free.

The order God gave

The Israelites in Egypt were not told to change their ways first. To become holy first. God is not the author of confusion.

Rest in Christ

For he that is entered into his rest, he also hath ceased from his own works, as God did from his.Hebrews 4:10

The Sabbath has always been a picture of Christ, and the rest we have in Jesus. And the prohibition against working on that day was to illustrate that we contribute nothing to our salvation except to honor His sacrifice by trusting on Him to justify us before God.

Using our own works to enter into His rest is a sign of unbelief that He did it all. We rest in His work, not our own.

Thy faith hath saved thee

And he said to the woman, Thy faith hath saved thee; go in peace.Luke 7:50

Jesus flat out says her love for Him is due to her having been forgiven already. She isn’t saved because she showed Him love and hospitality, with humility and sorrow for the sins she’s done. Her faith saved her. The love she showed Him followed.

Simon the Pharisee

Simon the Pharisee, who believed he was righteous by his works, his keeping the vain traditions and ceremony of men, along with the letter of the law, couldn’t come to be saved, lacking the faith.

What must I do to be saved?

And brought them out, and said, Sirs, what must I do to be saved? And they said, Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house.Acts 16:30-31

What Paul did not say

He doesn’t tell him he must repent of his sins, or surrender his life to the Lord Jesus and have his fruit inspected by others. He doesn’t tell him he must join a certain Church, or attend mass, or receive sacraments.

What Paul did say

Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved. Simple as. We are saved by faith alone, plus nothing minus nothing.

One gospel, no added rite

But we believe that through the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ we shall be saved, even as they.Acts 15:11

This is the Gospel of faith. Peter’s doctrine does not differ from Paul’s and neither is different from what Jesus preached.

Is baptism required?

He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved; but he that believeth not shall be damned.Mark 16:16

This verse is often used to create the false doctrine of baptism being required for salvation, ignoring that the either/or condition is belief.

The condition is belief

He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved; but he that believeth not shall be damned.Mark 16:16
He that believeth on him is not condemned: but he that believeth not is condemned already, because he hath not believed in the name of the only begotten Son of God.John 3:18

Baptism is the outward sign that we may assume someone has professed Christ. Those who believe are saved, those who don’t are damned.

Christ shall profit you nothing

Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage. Behold, I Paul say unto you, that if ye be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing. For I testify again to every man that is circumcised, that he is a debtor to do the whole law. Christ is become of no effect unto you, whosoever of you are justified by the law; ye are fallen from grace.Galatians 5:1-4

If they trust in their circumcision to justify them, even partially, then Christ will profit them nothing. This could be any other work of the flesh you’re trusting to save you, even if it is added to faith.

Added works

Adding baptism, turning from sin, church membership and attendance, rites of man, communion... Christ profits them nothing.

Not justified by the law

Salvation comes only in faith that God’s grace has given us His only begotten Son, through whom is remission of sins. Period.

In our own filthy rags

Lord, Lord

Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven. Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in thy name? and in thy name have cast out devils? and in thy name done many wonderful works? And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity.Matthew 7:21-23

Many unsaved people trapped in false doctrine twist this verse to mean you must do works to be saved.

The will of the Father

And this is the will of him that sent me, that every one which seeth the Son, and believeth on him, may have everlasting life: and I will raise him up at the last day.John 6:40

But what is the will of the Father? Jesus tells us.

Being in proximity to Christ isn’t the same as believing on Christ. Doing works in His name, eating supper, His work done in your streets, in your church or even calling Him Lord, isn’t sufficient to be saved.

You can’t be saved by being the son or daughter of a saved Christian. You can’t be saved by going to a Christian church, partaking in the Lord’s supper, or by trying to show up at the wedding feast in your own attire, clothed in the filthy rags of your own righteousness.

A zeal not according to knowledge

For I bear them record that they have a zeal of God, but not according to knowledge. For they being ignorant of God’s righteousness, and going about to establish their own righteousness, have not submitted themselves unto the righteousness of God. For Christ is the end of the law for righteousness to every one that believeth.Romans 10:2-4

This same prayer and sad refrain could apply to so many zealous Catholics and Orthodox, Lordshippers and Repent-of-your-sins types. They have a zeal for God, but go about to set up their own righteousness to their destruction.

All those who seek to establish their own righteousness, who deny the eternal gift, who make grace into some sort of rewards system in a game, rather than the gift of God given in the blood of His only begotten Son, seek to rob God of His glory.

“Easy believism”

The accusation

People who use the term “easy believism” to derisively mock the simple Gospel of grace and reconciliation, are trying to keep men under the yoke of bondage. They deny that Jesus paid it all. They add burdens grievous to be borne, and lay them on men’s shoulders.

The answer

If believing the Gospel for salvation were “easy,” then why do so many people struggle with trusting in Christ Jesus alone for their salvation? Believing on Christ alone for salvation is the narrow way. The strait gate.

Salvation is easy, from a works standpoint because it doesn’t require any effort from us to believe that Jesus paid it all. But it is hard from a human standpoint, letting go of all the burdens that keep us from passing through.

Careful to maintain good works

Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy he saved us, by the washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost; Which he shed on us abundantly through Jesus Christ our Saviour; That being justified by his grace, we should be made heirs according to the hope of eternal life. This is a faithful saying, and these things I will that thou affirm constantly, that they which have believed in God might be careful to maintain good works. These things are good and profitable unto men.Titus 3:5-8

This is the Biblical Doctrine of good works. That they which have believed, past tense, in the Gospel that was just laid out, might do them and be profitable to others.

And no, they aren’t automatic, as some false prophets preach, for if they were then we wouldn’t need so much encouragement to commit ourselves to them.

Saved as we are

But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.Romans 5:8

We are dead to sin as saved believers. The spirit does work in us the more we grow closer to God, to help remove the thorns in our hearts as we become more sanctified, praise God! But we are saved just as we are, though we still are sinners.

Unable to understand the difference between salvation and sanctification, the faith that saves and the love that shines from the saved.

Plus nothing. Minus nothing.

Call upon the name of the Lord

For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.Romans 10:13

If you believe that God so loved you that he sent His only begotten Son, Jesus Christ, to live, die and be raised again so that all who believe on Him have eternal life, that can never be taken away or lost, never earned or deserved, given in grace, then confess with thy mouth, call upon the name of the Lord and be saved.
