Doctrine · Assurance

Eternal Security

Once saved always saved is not just a slogan, it is the truth of the Gospel.

✦ ✦ ✦

Use the arrow keys or swipe to move through the study. Tap any gold reference to read the verse.

They can never lose their salvation

And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand. My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father’s hand.John 10:28-29

If a person has believed in their heart that Jesus Christ died for their sins, and received the baptism of the holy Spirit, made a child of God, they can NEVER lose their salvation.

You cannot claim to believe on Christ and at the same time maintain that it can be lost.

John 10

None shall pluck them out

Like we have seen repeatedly, Jesus spells it out and explains His parables and analogies in clear, unmistakable language.

Those who hear the Gospel and believe the truth Jesus gives eternal life. They shall never perish. No man can take them, that is make them unbelievers, bring them to repent of their faith, having repented into belief. No man. Not ourselves, not another.

Once you are saved you are always saved, always His, always His sheep.

In no wise cast out

All that the Father giveth me shall come to me; and him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out. For I came down from heaven, not to do mine own will, but the will of him that sent me. And this is the Father’s will which hath sent me, that of all which he hath given me I should lose nothing, but should raise it up again at the last day. And this is the will of him that sent me, that every one which seeth the Son, and believeth on him, may have everlasting life: and I will raise him up at the last day.John 6:37-40

Those who say you can lose your salvation ignore that Jesus says those given Him by God's grace shall in no wise be cast out. That He shall not lose any who believe and are given Him. That they will have everlasting life and be raised up on the last day.

Everlasting. You cannot lose your salvation.

Shall never die

Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live: And whosoever liveth and believeth in me shall never die. Believest thou this?John 11:25-26

Martha confesses the truth of the Gospel Jesus gives, the truth her brother and sister also believe. That those who believe on Him shall never die, but he shall live. This is salvation by faith and eternal security, yet again.

Nothing can separate us

Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword? As it is written, For thy sake we are killed all the day long; we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter. Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him that loved us. For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.Romans 8:35-39

And nothing can separate us from God once we have received the Spirit, which is by faith, through grace.

Let no man tell you you can lose your salvation. Let no man lay charge against you who are in Christ. Let no man lead you back to the yoke of the law, which is death.

Sealed and kept by the power of God

All who believe on Him have eternal life and remission of sin, are sealed by the Spirit unto redemption. To an inheritance incorruptible, that never fades. Kept by the power of God. Eternal security. Once saved, always saved.

To an inheritance incorruptible, and undefiled, and that fadeth not away, reserved in heaven for you, Who are kept by the power of God through faith unto salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.1 Peter 1:4-5

The eternal security of salvation is the purpose of the Spirit indwelling us. That we may KNOW we have eternal life.

The truth dwelleth in us for ever

For the truth’s sake, which dwelleth in us, and shall be with us for ever.2 John 2

They may backslide, become castaway and chastised by God, not produce works or fruits and even forget their own salvation, but they will NEVER believe anything other than the truth of the gospel, because the truth dwells within us and shall be with us forever.

Once you have partaken and drank the water of life, you will never thirst again. How could you? The Spirit of truth does not leave room for the spirit of error.

His sons are always His sons

In dozens of clear verses, and in parable and story, we see how God rates forgiveness, how His sons are always His sons.

That we are heirs to the kingdom through Christ, adopted to the promise. Not servants to work off debt. Not fumbling Esau, trying to emulate Jacob and regain favor.

Planted, not plucked up

For I will set mine eyes upon them for good, and I will bring them again to this land: and I will build them, and not pull them down; and I will plant them, and not pluck them up.Jeremiah 24:6

We are planted in Christ and cannot be plucked up.

Judas was never saved

The claim

There is a false doctrine out there that Judas proves you can lose your salvation, but of course that's untrue.

The answer

Not only wasn't Judas saved, he was a reprobate and Jesus knew this.

One of you is a devil

Jesus answered them, Have not I chosen you twelve, and one of you is a devil?John 6:70

This literally couldn't be any clearer and is completely spelled out.

Hebrews 6

The claim

And so many false prophets teach that Hebrews 6 is a warning that you can lose your salvation.

The answer

If someone chooses atheism after having full knowledge of salvation, they were never saved. They believed in vain. They had head knowledge, but didn't trust in their heart.

They were not of us

They went out from us, but they were not of us; for if they had been of us, they would no doubt have continued with us: but they went out, that they might be made manifest that they were not all of us.1 John 2:19

God has sworn we cannot lose the free gift of eternal life that He has given us in His grace and mercy, purchased by the blood of Christ.

He that endureth to the end

Endure unto the end

But he that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved.Matthew 24:13

Too many false teachers assert that perseverance is for our spiritual salvation, that we could lose the free gift of eternal life, but that is not the context or meaning of it. It is for grace and protection during tribulation and persecution.

Saved from the persecution. Saved out of great tribulation.

Salvation cannot be lost, only refused

The claim

Some use these parables to teach that you can lose your salvation. That the foolish virgins and the unprofitable servant were saved, and having been sinful or disobedient, had lost their shot at Heaven.

The answer

The foolish virgins and unprofitable servant believed falsely, and what had been given them, the chance to enter into the joy of the Lord, is taken from them. Salvation cannot be lost, only refused.

The unforgiving servant wasn't saved and then lost his salvation for being unforgiving. He was never saved because he never accepted that he truly owed that debt.

The candlestick and the book of life

Remove thy candlestick

Aren't those verses where Jesus tells the churches to repent or He will "remove thy candlestick" saying they are going to lose their salvation? The answer is a resounding no.

The candlesticks are the churches

He is literally speaking of their authority, hedge of protection, etc, as His church in fellowship with Him. He doesn't threaten those saints with damnation.

The seven candlesticks

The mystery of the seven stars which thou sawest in my right hand, and the seven golden candlesticks. The seven stars are the angels of the seven churches: and the seven candlesticks which thou sawest are the seven churches.Revelation 1:20

Jesus' promise to us who are saved is that He will confess our names before our Father. No one in the Bible is added to the book of life, only blotted out and removed having already been there.

Castaway, not lost

Saul was castaway. He never stopped believing, never lost his salvation, but he no longer would receive the blessings and benefits.

Obedience to the Lord is not for our spiritual salvation, but rather that it be well with us and we may be His workmen doing great things for the Lord.

The longsuffering of God has limits and He will take from us blessings and eventually even call us home if we no longer can be useful to His works that He has crafted us to do.

Not a license to sin

The slander

People who doubt the gospel will often slanderously report that we teach a license to sin.

The truth

But in truth, we who are saved are held to a higher standard. To whom much is given, much is expected. The chastisement of God is fierce against our sin.

Vessels to honour and dishonour

But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth; and some to honour, and some to dishonour. If a man therefore purge himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honour, sanctified, and meet for the master’s use, and prepared unto every good work.2 Timothy 2:20-21

Some will sanctify themselves to honor, becoming more useful to God. Others won't be more than earthen pots, and dishonored. But all are saved.

The evil report

Probably the biggest stumbling block people get caught on is the idea of eternal security.

The evil report

And they brought up an evil report of the land which they had searched unto the children of Israel, saying, The land, through which we have gone to search it, is a land that eateth up the inhabitants thereof; and all the people that we saw in it are men of a great stature. And there we saw the giants, the sons of Anak, which come of the giants: and we were in our own sight as grasshoppers, and so we were in their sight.Numbers 13:32-33

This is the spirit of fear and doubt that uses the lie that eternal security doesn't exist. That you can lose your salvation. That because you are small and weak, you won't make it.

Let us go up at once

And Caleb stilled the people before Moses, and said, Let us go up at once, and possess it; for we are well able to overcome it.Numbers 13:30

Joshua and Caleb showed them the fruit that comes from trusting in the Lord.

Our blessed hope

Beloved, now are we the sons of God, and it doth not yet appear what we shall be: but we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is.1 John 3:2

We seek the resurrection. To be shed of the sinful flesh and to fully be the new man. That is our hope.

If you aren't trusting God that that's a sure thing for believing Jesus died and rose again, when His word tells us again and again that that is the payoff for that faith, then you don't have a hope in Hell.

Summary

  1. Never lostIf a person has believed in their heart that Jesus Christ died for their sins, they can NEVER lose their salvation.
  2. Held in His handNo man can take them. No man. Not ourselves, not another.
  3. Never cast outHe shall not lose any who believe and are given Him.
  4. Never fall awayThe truth dwells within us and shall be with us forever.
  5. Chastened, not lostObedience to the Lord is not for our spiritual salvation. The chastisement of God is fierce against our sin.
