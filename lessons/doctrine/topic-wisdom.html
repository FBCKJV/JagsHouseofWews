The fear of the Lord

The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge: but fools despise wisdom and instruction.Proverbs 1:7

It is astounding how we, as men, can hear God's word, know His word, study it, hear it preached, and yet still lean on our own understanding.

God's word should lead us to fear Him. To respect His holiness, power, mercy and justice. His wrath and His judgments. This is what makes a man wise. And it is every parent's responsibility to teach it to our children. It is the greatest inheritance we can give them ().

God's wisdom, not man's

Lean not unto thine own understanding

Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.Proverbs 3:5-6

This is the key verse of Proverbs. The foundational understanding. Remember it, sing it, say it. And in everything acknowledge God first.

Don't think to blaze your own trails and beat down your own paths. There is nothing new under the Sun and God's ways are the best ways. The old paths, tried and true.

God's wisdom, not man's

A way that seemeth right

There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death.Proverbs 14:12

Man is able to rationalize any transgression, every petty sin and idle thought. But man's ways are not God's ().

Every way of a man is right in his own eyes: but the LORD pondereth the hearts (). Often we can do the right things for the wrong reasons. A man can do good for bad reasons, he can do evil with good intentions. God knows our hearts.

God's wisdom, not man's

Jesus, the vein of wisdom

In whom are hid all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge.Colossians 2:3

Job describes the relative ease of finding silver and gold, compared to the wisdom of God (). In Christ is all God's wisdom and knowledge. Jesus is the vein of wisdom, through the fear of God.

But we have the mind of Christ (). But our flesh is at enmity with the spirit, and so when we walk in the carnal, we have less access to the wisdom of God. The root of the righteous, that is Christ Jesus, shall not be moved (). All wisdom leads to Christ.

God's wisdom, not man's

The foolishness of God

For the preaching of the cross is to them that perish foolishness; but unto us which are saved it is the power of God.1 Corinthians 1:18

Despite all his knowledge and wisdom, Solomon recognized the ways of God are still far above him (). Not by man's wisdom. But by His Spirit.

We all know people today who are constantly looking for supernatural signs from God before they can commit to believing, or who seek to impose their own logic on matters that are spiritual, and who scoff at the simplicity of the Gospel. What many see as foolishness, or weakness, is actually the wisdom of God.

God's wisdom, not man's

True things, false points

Shall mortal man be more just than God? shall a man be more pure than his maker?Job 4:17

Eliphaz uses true things to make false points. Many times the greatest lies are based on a misrepresentation or misapplication of the greatest truths.

Just because you believe something about the world to be true, doesn't make it Biblical doctrine. Literal creation is a primary doctrine. The shape of the earth isn't Biblical doctrine. Don't project your opinions of the worldly onto scripture. If you think you know for certain anything other than Christ and him crucified, you're a fool ().

God's wisdom, not man's

The whole duty of man

Let us hear the conclusion of the whole matter: Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man.Ecclesiastes 12:13

Solomon the Wise comes to the ultimate conclusion that the purpose of life isn't gathering riches, or pleasure or mirth. It is only to fear God and keep His commandments.

Turn not to the right hand nor to the left (). Don't get caught up in the false binary presented by the beast. The true third position is the path of righteousness and steadfast faith in God alone.

The righteous and the wicked

The fear of God divides them

The transgression of the wicked saith within my heart, that there is no fear of God before his eyes.Psalm 36:1

The contrast between the wicked and righteous is the fear of the Lord. The love of truth and abhorring evil. The prideful versus the humility of falling before God.

A scorner seeketh wisdom, and findeth it not (). How can they, without the fear of God? Ever learning, and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth (). God grants us wisdom by His word and by the rod of reproof ().

The righteous and the wicked

Pride goeth before destruction

Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall.Proverbs 16:18

The things God hates are the things we are called to hate, and pride is one of the things God hates most (). It is why the sodomites use the term. They defy God by announcing their pride, against His judgments.

But wisdom is justified of her children (). What people think to give as their opinion is shown to be true or not by what following it begets. The fruit that wisdom bears.

The righteous and the wicked

The wise son

The proverbs of Solomon. A wise son maketh a glad father: but a foolish son is the heaviness of his mother.Proverbs 10:1

God blesses those that prepare, gathering in the summer against the winter. Sleeping through the harvest is a shame (). The harvest analogy is not just for earthly goals, but the sharing of the gospel. Go soul-winning! He that winneth souls is wise.

The way of the LORD is strength to the upright: but destruction shall be to the workers of iniquity ().

The tongue

Death and life

Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof.Proverbs 18:21

Imagine every foolish or wicked thing you've ever said in anger, read aloud by the holy God for all to hear (). I thank God for His great salvation, that spares me from such utter humiliation.

A soft answer turneth away wrath (). A word spoken in due season, how good is it! () How good it is to hear the joy of a brother when we are feeling down!

The tongue

A bridle on the mouth

I said, I will take heed to my ways, that I sin not with my tongue: I will keep my mouth with a bridle, while the wicked is before me.Psalm 39:1

Parents with adult children, or pastors with a flock, should recognize this feeling. When we see people we love being led away, and we know if we speak out in anger, that we'd do more harm than good. My heart was hot within me ().

A fool uttereth all his mind: but a wise man keepeth it in till afterwards (). Blabbing every idle thought that comes into your head is vain ().

The tongue

Talebearers and evil surmising

Where no wood is, there the fire goeth out: so where there is no talebearer, the strife ceaseth.Proverbs 26:20

Evil surmising is harboring unproven, malicious suspicions, assuming the worst motives about others without evidence (). They take innocent actions and invent malicious intent, spreading rumors to poison others. Don't be one, but also don't fall victim to one by listening to their talebearing.

The words we say are a window to our heart (). The eyes of the LORD are in every place, beholding the evil and the good ().

The strange woman

The strange woman

For the lips of a strange woman drop as an honeycomb, and her mouth is smoother than oil: But her end is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a two-edged sword. Her feet go down to death; her steps take hold on hell.Proverbs 5:3-5

The analogy of adultery serves as both a metaphor and practical application. The strange woman of folly speaks sweet sayings, but her end is bitter. God's paths are straight and knowable, but her ways are moveable. Sin will take you further than you want to go, keep you longer than you want to stay, and cost you more than you want to pay.

She says the goodman is gone a long journey (). There is an allegory here: the temptation that judgment of God is afar off. Stolen waters are sweet (). But her guests are in the depths of Hell.

The strange woman

The wife of thy youth

Let thy fountain be blessed: and rejoice with the wife of thy youth.Proverbs 5:18

King Solomon loved many strange women... and his wives turned away his heart (). The wisest man in history fell prey to the very thing he counseled against. It is often true that the preacher preaches what he needs to hear, more than the audience.

Give not thy strength unto women, nor thy ways to that which destroyeth kings (). Women were not just problems Solomon carried, but a downfall for his father David, as well.

True riches

Riches profit not

Riches profit not in the day of wrath: but righteousness delivereth from death.Proverbs 11:4

Wise men die, likewise the fool and the brutish person perish, and leave their wealth to others (). The man trusting his riches cannot redeem himself or his brother. But the precious redemption of the soul is of God, through faith in Christ.

Give me neither poverty nor riches ().

True riches

Rich yet nothing, poor yet rich

There is that maketh himself rich, yet hath nothing: there is that maketh himself poor, yet hath great riches.Proverbs 13:7

God is not opposed to earthly riches. Job and Abraham and Solomon were incredibly wealthy in their day. It isn't the wealth we gain but what we do with it. It isn't the riches but where your treasure is.

The love of money is the root of all evil (). Be satisfied with what God blessed you with, even if it is subsistence, knowing that if you win souls, your riches in Heaven will be greater.

True riches

Low time preference

Wealth gotten by vanity shall be diminished: but he that gathereth by labour shall increase.Proverbs 13:11

Low time preference is the tendency to delay immediate gratification in favor of future, long-term rewards. It reflects a patient, disciplined, and forward-thinking mindset, and it is a sign of wisdom.

Be thou diligent to know the state of thy flocks (). But he that hasteth to be rich hath an evil eye (). The “Prosperity Gospel” scam artists take as much advantage of this tendency in man as the street corner swindler.

True riches

The borrower is servant

The rich ruleth over the poor, and the borrower is servant to the lender.Proverbs 22:7

We complain about the “unfair” reality of billionaires and the power of usury, while we rack up debt. We will contribute to the rich for things we don't need, while neglecting the brother at church who is struggling to pay for basic necessities.

Boast not thyself of to morrow (). Now is the day of salvation (). Be prudent and plan, without worry. Don't put off till tomorrow what can be done today.

True riches

Rich toward God

And I say unto you, Make to yourselves friends of the mammon of unrighteousness; that, when ye fail, they may receive you into everlasting habitations.Luke 16:9

Make friends with your wealth. Spend it to others' benefit. Forgive them debts, that those you've helped may hear the Gospel. If we can't be just with unrighteous Mammon, how will we expect to be just with others' souls?

You can't take it with you (). But the works we do for the Lord are never in vain (). All good things are of God, and it's okay to enjoy those things. They are a gift.

True riches

Say not the former days were better

Say not thou, What is the cause that the former days were better than these? for thou dost not enquire wisely concerning this.Ecclesiastes 7:10

Appeals to the “better days” of the past prey upon the inclination to see the former days as better, especially given the evil we see all around us. For we all feel like Lot at times.

There is no political solution to a spiritual problem. Revival is a continuous process. The need for revival is never greater than now. No matter when “now” is.
