Chastened as Sons

He chastises those he loves and scourges every son.

Whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth

For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth, and scourgeth every son whom he receiveth.Hebrews 12:6

God chastises His own for our iniquity. He takes the rod to His children when they sin.

We are punished here on earth for our sins. Sometimes very harshly. Sometimes with death.

The Lord hath put away thy sin

And David said unto Nathan, I have sinned against the Lord. And Nathan said unto David, The Lord also hath put away thy sin; thou shalt not die.2 Samuel 12:13

And so God punished David, yet He put away David's sin, because he had the imputed righteousness of Jesus Christ. His faith in God's word.

We who are saved in Christ shall not die. But in this life we will be punished, that we may learn and the glory of God be known, not just by His mercy but His wrath. As an example for others.

Acts 5

Ananias and Sapphira

Were Ananias and Sapphira saved? The Bible says in Acts 4 that the entire church was of one heart and one soul. They were of that church. I think the implication is clear they were.

But their being in Heaven when they die, justified by faith and Jesus' imputed righteousness didn't keep them from sinning, nor did it stop God from punishing them with their lives.

Chastened unto death

The fruit inspectors

Many of the religion of works, who maintain that you must repent of sinning to be saved, and claim that the Gospel of Christ, salvation by faith in grace, is a “license to sin.” And so, being fruit inspectors, take this story as evidence they were not believers.

What it shows

But what it shows is that God chastises His own for our iniquity. He takes the rod to His children when they sin. The Bible says He scourges His adopted sons and daughters. Even unto death.

We saved are held to a higher standard than the unsaved, and we are punished here in this earth, sometimes more so than the unsaved, who are not God's children.

Delivered unto Satan

In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when ye are gathered together, and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ, To deliver such an one unto Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus.1 Corinthians 5:4-5

Paul tells them to church discipline him, that he be given over to Satan for destruction of the flesh, even if his spirit is saved. God allows Satan to destroy us in the flesh.

The hope in church discipline is that the person gets right. Sees their error and comes back. But if not, at least in death they are free from the sin and present with the Lord.

Note this teaches OSAS.

Called home, still saved

If they remain backslidden, God may call them home. But they are still saved.

Thyatira

Backslidden and defiant, Jesus says He will punish her and those who are with her. He chasteneth who He loves. These aren't some sort of reprobates like the Nicolaitanes, these are His servants gone astray.

Just, but not eternal

We know that judgment is just, but not eternal, because once saved always saved. God will take a backslidden Christian home in chastisement.

To whom much is given

But he that knew not, and did commit things worthy of stripes, shall be beaten with few stripes. For unto whomsoever much is given, of him shall be much required: and to whom men have committed much, of him they will ask the more.Luke 12:48

It should also be a warning to us that know better. That our knowledge and understanding means that should we stray from the goodness of God, we should expect a much worse punishment for it.

We do not have a license to sin and disobedience is met with punishment and chastisements from our Father. Many a great Christian has fallen to this, backslidden and ruined in this life. Lacking proper fear of the Lord.

Ezekiel 33

The way of the Lord is not equal

The misreading

Many make the entire message spiritual, and apply this to salvation, that you can lose salvation or that we must turn from our sins to be saved.

The chastening of believers

Again, this applies to chastening of believers. We are saved by grace through faith, not of ourselves. It is a gift of God.

But the blessings of this world come when you are right with God. And He withdraws His protection when those who call themselves by His name don't walk in His ways.

If you've stopped, you aren't right with God. If you aren't right, get right. Life is but a vapour. The time is now.

The proud believer

Now I Nebuchadnezzar praise and extol and honour the King of heaven, all whose works are truth, and his ways judgment: and those that walk in pride he is able to abase.Daniel 4:37

Too many Christians get comfortable in the blessings and protection God provides us. Like Nebuchadnezzar, we not only take things for granted, but begin to pridefully take credit for the things in our life that are by the grace of God.

Don't be surprised if you continue down the road, backslidden, to have this type of punishment. I have both observed it and experienced a fraction of it. Don't let it happen to you!

Genesis 12 · Genesis 20

Abraham's repeated lie

If he, a mighty man of faith, called “a friend of God,” struggled with sin and fear, then we can know the same is true of us.

But we can also see that God saves us regardless of our fear or lack of faith. He chastises those he loves and scourges every son. He often will expose us to embarrassment and humiliation in exposing our sin. But it is to our benefit, as it is here for Abraham.

Secret sin and the Father's rod

Thou hast set our iniquities before thee, our secret sins in the light of thy countenance.Psalm 90:8
For nothing is secret, that shall not be made manifest; neither any thing hid, that shall not be known and come abroad.Luke 8:17

That fearful looking for judgment, the guilt we feel when we disobey God's word, the pangs of our conscience, is a powerful motivation to get right with the Lord.

Especially knowing that, as adopted children, our Father will not spare the rod, and the sins we do in secret will be revealed in the light.

Rehoboam · 2 Chronicles 12

The prodigal chastened

Very familiar to a lot of Christians, who after conversion and with the support of those before them to edify them, do great things for the Lord and are blessed, but soon their passion dies and they turn from the path of righteousness. And God chastens them. But He is merciful to send His word as warning before destruction to get right with Him.

Therefore turn thou to thy God: keep mercy and judgment, and wait on thy God continually.Hosea 12:6

The same promise to the saved, who may backslide and be chastened. Turn back to the Lord and be forgiven.

Always welcome

If you're in rebellion now, repent. You are always welcome in the Father's house, as sons and daughters. He will deliver you from what you deserve, that you may have a chance to serve Him.

Always let your sons and daughters know they are loved no matter what, even in chastisement. That they always have a way back and know it, terms that are fair, and that it's offered in love. Don't spare the rod, but don't forget mercy and grace.

Grace toward the brother

“Works come automatically”

There are quite a few professing Christians and even entire denominations that teach that works come automatically, and without evidence of a changed life, we should doubt the salvation of someone.

Encourage rather than condemn

We should be careful to be understanding rather than judgmental. To encourage rather than condemn. Not to condone sin. But to not be confrontationally judgmental.

If they have repented, and asked to be forgiven, we are to treat them as if they'd never sinned. For we were once bondmen in Egypt.
