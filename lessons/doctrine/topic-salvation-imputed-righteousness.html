Imputed Righteousness

Jesus took on our sin and paid the price for all of it. By believing this, we take on His righteousness.

The doctrine

It is an exchange

For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him.2 Corinthians 5:21

Jesus took on our sin and paid the price for all of it. He was the propitiation. The ransom. The sacrifice.

By believing this, we take on His righteousness. His blood washes us clean. It is an exchange. That is the testament.

The truth of imputed righteousness of God being given the believer is found throughout scripture.

Genesis 15 · Romans 4

Abraham believed God

And he believed in the Lord; and he counted it to him for righteousness.Genesis 15:6

Not by the law. Not by circumcision, for Abraham was saved decades before God gave circumcision as an ordinance. But by believing God.

Abraham believed God’s promise to him, by faith, without knowing the exact means by which He would make good on the promise, only that He would. And it was counted for righteousness.

Not of debt, but of grace

Now to him that worketh is the reward not reckoned of grace, but of debt. But to him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, his faith is counted for righteousness.Romans 4:4-5

Paul quotes this and argues that if it were of works, that means God would be indebted to us. To pay us for our works. That we were owed.

Grace is an unmerited and unearned gift or favor. Debt is deserved. Grace is undeserved.

If it were his willingness to obey God, then Abraham would have the glory. But all the glory is God’s. He did it all. We just have to trust Him as Abraham did.

Psalm 32 · Romans 4

Blessed is the man

Even as David also describeth the blessedness of the man, unto whom God imputeth righteousness without works, Saying, Blessed are they whose iniquities are forgiven, and whose sins are covered. Blessed is the man to whom the Lord will not impute sin.Romans 4:6-8

The truth is that, in Christ, whether it was in David’s day, or Paul’s or our own, God forgives our past transgressions, covers our current sin, and will not hold our future sins and iniquity against us.

The Lord will not impute sin, our ledger is clean, our transgressions forgiven. This is a promise of God.

Transgression, sin and iniquity

While it isn’t entirely wrong to take the words transgression, sin and iniquity, and use them interchangeably, they do carry subtle differences in their meaning.

Transgression

The wilful rebellion against the truth of God and His commands.

Sin

The transgression of the Law; it also carries with it the idea of missing the mark, falling short.

Iniquity

From the Hebrew word, Avon, meaning to twist, bend, or distort. The perverted and crooked nature of fallen man.

Imputed on our ledger

But he was wounded for our transgressions, he was bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace was upon him; and with his stripes we are healed. All we like sheep have gone astray; we have turned every one to his own way; and the Lord hath laid on him the iniquity of us all.Isaiah 53:5-6

He suffered these things for our iniquity and transgressions, that we may be purged. That His righteousness be imputed on our ledger, even as His blood blots away our sins.

On the cross, Jesus carried away our rebellion, covered our failures with His blood, and cleared the ledger of our crooked nature by imputing His own perfect righteousness to us. Past, present and future.

The propitiation for the whole world

  • Christ the propitiation, through faith in His blood
  • For the sins of the whole world
  • Trespasses not imputed
  • The righteousness which is of God by faith
  • The righteous servant bears their iniquities
  • Justified by faith, not by the law

By believing this, we take on His righteousness. His blood washes us clean. We are clothed in White of His perfection, our sins forgotten.

Romans 5

While we were yet sinners

But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us. Much more then, being now justified by his blood, we shall be saved from wrath through him.Romans 5:8-9
For as by one man’s disobedience many were made sinners, so by the obedience of one shall many be made righteous.Romans 5:19

When we were righteous? No, as sinners. Did we earn it through works, the keeping of the law? No, that would be debt, it is the imputed righteousness from Christ that justifies us in faith. It is the law of grace. We don’t deserve it.

Is imputation a lie?

The error

There are false religions that deny the imputation of righteousness by faith in Christ. They say that imputation is not taught in scripture.

The Council of Trent

Canon 11 on Justification anathematized the idea that people are justified “by the sole imputation of the justice of Christ, or by the sole remission of sins.”

The answer

This is a lie. Let God be true, and every man a liar. All who come to the saving knowledge of Christ are made pure, in Him. Righteous, in Him.

Going about to establish their own

For they being ignorant of God’s righteousness, and going about to establish their own righteousness, have not submitted themselves unto the righteousness of God. For Christ is the end of the law for righteousness to every one that believeth.Romans 10:3-4

Our righteousness is as a filthy rag, our works could never justify us before a holy and perfect God.

When believers are faced with those full of zeal for the Lord, but going about to establish their own righteousness rather than the imputed righteousness of Jesus Christ, we sigh in exasperation and ask, “are your hearts still hardened?”

Philippians 3

Counted as dung

Yea doubtless, and I count all things but loss for the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord: for whom I have suffered the loss of all things, and do count them but dung, that I may win Christ, And be found in him, not having mine own righteousness, which is of the law, but that which is through the faith of Christ, the righteousness which is of God by faith:Philippians 3:8-9

If birth, circumcision and zeal for the law saved anyone, it would be Paul, but he counts all his own “righteousness” as dung, so that he may gain Christ.

Despite our having the imputed righteousness of Jesus Christ, we should strive for sanctification. Obeying the law, not for our own righteousness but for the love of God.

Exceeding the scribes and Pharisees

For I say unto you, That except your righteousness shall exceed the righteousness of the scribes and Pharisees, ye shall in no case enter into the kingdom of heaven.Matthew 5:20

Unlike the claimed righteousness of the scribes and Pharisees, who taught loopholes and exceptions to God’s law and accused Jesus of seeking to destroy it, we must have the righteousness of Christ to enter into the Kingdom of Heaven, exceeding those hypocrites’ false idea of righteousness.

Matthew 22

The wedding garment

And when the king came in to see the guests, he saw there a man which had not on a wedding garment: And he saith unto him, Friend, how camest thou in hither not having a wedding garment? And he was speechless. Then said the king to the servants, Bind him hand and foot, and take him away, and cast him into outer darkness; there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.Matthew 22:11-13

His own filthy rags

In his own filthy rags, his own righteousness, representing those who believe they can enter the Kingdom by their own works.

The garment given

The spotless white garment given to believers by the lamb of God. The imputed righteousness of Jesus Christ which covers all our sin.

We have been invited to the wedding party. Everything is ready. He’s provided us with wedding clothes.

Covered by His handiwork

Coats of skins

Unto Adam also and to his wife did the Lord God make coats of skins, and clothed them.Genesis 3:21

Shedding of blood for the remission of sin. God gives them this gift, freely. Of His own handiwork, not ours.

No spot in thee

Thou art all fair, my love; there is no spot in thee.Song of Solomon 4:7

Just as we who are saved and sanctified by faith in Christ are pure when the Lord looks upon us. Clothed in righteousness. No sin imputed upon our new man.

Two tales of our lives

And their sins and iniquities will I remember no more.Hebrews 10:17

As a Christian, we have two tales of our lives. The one, details our sin and the other remembers it no more.

In the Christian life, we are not just our sin and failings. The sin of our flesh and the trespasses we may do are not imputed against us. God remembers them no more.

If God sees our sin as far from us as far as east is from west, we should strive to be as gracious and merciful to our brethren.

Deuteronomy 9 · Romans 11

Not for thy righteousness

Not for thy righteousness, or for the uprightness of thine heart, dost thou go to possess their land: but for the wickedness of these nations the Lord thy God doth drive them out from before thee, and that he may perform the word which the Lord sware unto thy fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.Deuteronomy 9:5

We are not given the kingdom over those who refused to believe because we are better than they are, but because we submitted ourselves to receive the imputed righteousness of Christ. There but for God’s grace go I.

In matters of righteousness we are all in need of a physician, Christ Jesus.

Now is the day of salvation

(For he saith, I have heard thee in a time accepted, and in the day of salvation have I succoured thee: behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation.)2 Corinthians 6:2

There is a time, it is today. There is a day, and it is the present.

If you haven’t accepted the truth that we are sinners in need of salvation, and that it is offered as a free gift to all who believe, through the grace of Almighty God, now is the time.
