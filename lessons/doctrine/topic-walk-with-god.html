Enoch walked with God

And Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him.Genesis 5:24

When we look at these early patriarchs, we can see the pattern for salvation. Adam knows God, his creator, and knows his sin. Abel brings the blood of atonement, given in faith. Seth and Enos, that with the mouth, confession is made, calling upon the name of the Lord. And with Enoch, we have the testimony that he walked with God, that he lived separated, pleasing God.

By faith Enoch was translated that he should not see death; and was not found, because God had translated him: for before his translation he had this testimony, that he pleased God.Hebrews 11:5

A daily choice

The fruits of the Spirit don't come automatically. Just because we are saved doesn't mean that we will do good. Just as it doesn't mean we will stop sinning.

And he said to them all, If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross daily, and follow me.Luke 9:23

It's a daily choice. It is on us to reject the temptations of the flesh by walking in the spirit.

Walking with God daily

Our daily bread

It is our daily walk with God that protects us. We ask Him to provide our daily bread, not just physical need for food, but spiritually, studying the word of God.

We start our day in prayer, talking to God, and reading His word that His will be made known to us. His promises made clear that we may grow in faith. This is how we resist temptation.

This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.Joshua 1:8
Walking with God daily

How to increase your faith

How to increase your faith? The Bible tells us:

  • Read the word and listen to preaching from His word
  • By living the word we begin to rely more on God and less on ourselves
  • Pray for faith
  • Obey His word and see how His blessings reward your faith

If you are feeling rudderless. If you are searching for more faith. If you don't know what you should do. Start with the word.

Walking with God daily

Beginning each day with Him

The first fruits are His. The first sons of Israel. The first of the flock. The first tenth of your earnings. All His. And as we begin each day as His, seeking first the kingdom of God.

A believer makes the conscious decision to make Jesus their companion. To wake early in the morning, starting the day with Christ, and His word. In prayer.

The bride who would not open

I sleep, but my heart waketh: it is the voice of my beloved that knocketh, saying, Open to me, my sister, my love, my dove, my undefiled: for my head is filled with dew, and my locks with the drops of the night. I have put off my coat; how shall I put it on? I have washed my feet; how shall I defile them?Song of Solomon 5:2-3

She makes excuses. Rather than hear His voice and do His will, a believer may see their contentment and undefiled spirit as a bed to rest upon. When God asks so little of us whom He has saved, to open when He knocks, will we respond?

Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me.Revelation 3:20
Walking with God daily

In good times and bad

It is vitally important for a Christian to not wait until there's trouble in their life to take their walk seriously. To turn to the Lord in praise and thanksgiving when times are good.

Remove far from me vanity and lies: give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with food convenient for me: Lest I be full, and deny thee, and say, Who is the Lord? or lest I be poor, and steal, and take the name of my God in vain.Proverbs 30:8-9

Like Paul we need to work on being content (). We are better to stoke our zeal for the Lord as a fire, and keep it going as warm embers for our entire life, than burn out quickly and fade like Jehu ( · ).

The old man and the new

Put off the old man

That ye put off concerning the former conversation the old man, which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts; And be renewed in the spirit of your mind; And that ye put on the new man, which after God is created in righteousness and true holiness.Ephesians 4:22-24

In grace, through Jesus, we have a new man, created by the Spirit, and that Spirit convicts us in our sin. Where the reprobate is past feeling, we are made all the more sensitive to God's will.

If we be saved, children of light, then seek the things of Heaven (). Putting off anger, wrath, malice, blasphemy, filthy communication, the behavior of the unsaved that acts as a bushel to hide our light.

The old man and the new

The spirit willing, the flesh weak

What shall we say then? Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound? God forbid. How shall we, that are dead to sin, live any longer therein?Romans 6:1-2

Paul is really hammering this point home because it is not automatic that we who are saved turn from our sins. Our new man, inside, quickened by the Holy Ghost, sealed unto the day of redemption, knows not sin. But the old man still reigns in our members. Our flesh.

For I know that in me (that is, in my flesh,) dwelleth no good thing: for to will is present with me; but how to perform that which is good I find not. For the good that I would I do not: but the evil which I would not, that I do.Romans 7:18-19

We sin, every day. But unlike the unsaved, which we were and should never forget, we have the Spirit within us, and our own spiritual new man cries out to do good.

The old man and the new

Sanctification is not about salvation

Our sanctification isn't about salvation. It is about becoming acceptable tools in God's shed. To be workmen who aren't ashamed. Good and faithful servants.

If we walk in the spirit we will also learn more about the holiness of God and His law, and in that knowledge come to see more things that are sinful. It is a refining process. One that removes thorns in our flesh we didn't know we had.

It's not about you. We are saved already, and justified by the perfect work of Christ Jesus that we believe on. Rejoice.

The old man and the new

It is written

It wasn't His divine nature that saved His human flesh from falling, but His doing His Father's will, and clinging to the word.

  • Bread: Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word of God
  • Power: Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve
  • Presumption: Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God
There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able; but will with the temptation also make a way to escape, that ye may be able to bear it.1 Corinthians 10:13

That way of escape is found in our knowledge of the word. By how much time we spend in it.

The whole armour of God

Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand. Stand therefore, having your loins girt about with truth, and having on the breastplate of righteousness; And your feet shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace; Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked. And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God:Ephesians 6:13-17

We are equipped with these things, when we walk in them. And if we walk in them, we should have no fear of the wicked. If God be for us, who can be against us? ()

How sin gets back in

How sin compounds

David tarried in bed, rather than in battle. And rather than refresh himself in the Spirit, thinking of the things of the Lord, he wandered.

When we do that, blow off our commitments, “take a day off” from prayer or study, we often wander, too. David goes from a momentary lapse in duty and honor to premeditated murder. This is how Christians backslide. This is how sin compounds.

And it came to pass, after the year was expired, at the time when kings go forth to battle, that David sent Joab, and his servants with him, and all Israel; and they destroyed the children of Ammon, and besieged Rabbah. But David tarried still at Jerusalem. And it came to pass in an eveningtide, that David arose from off his bed, and walked upon the roof of the king’s house: and from the roof he saw a woman washing herself; and the woman was very beautiful to look upon.2 Samuel 11:1-2
How sin gets back in

After a victory

The devil loves to bring a test before us when God is in the process of rewarding our faith in Him. When we are the most comfortable.

Many times in the Christian life, you will find that you've made it through something by walking in the Spirit and trusting in God, only to stumble at the finish line because the relief turns to complacency. Beware of the sin of Achan (). A battle isn't the war.

Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour:1 Peter 5:8
How sin gets back in

Blessing should humble us

A great triumph of the Lord acting miraculously in our lives, followed by a low point where we forget the trouble we were in, and grow comfortable, taking it for granted.

Hezekiah, recovered from his deathbed, showed the envoys of Babylon all his treasures rather than praising the Lord (). Uzziah allowed pride in himself for the victories and blessings given of God to put himself where he ought not be ().

When we are blessed by the Lord, it should humble us. Stay humble.

How sin gets back in

Pottage and quail

Esau's pottage

Christians who live carnally, despising their birthright received through Christ, losing blessings of God for a simple bowl of pottage, are like Esau. How many times do we do the same, for much less?

Quail at the nostrils

If you stumble back into sin, you find it doesn't hold the same appeal. In short order you'll see it as repulsive. You will have it coming out of your nostrils.

We forget where we were. We forget how we escaped. We melt down the treasure God provided, and the people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play ().

How sin gets back in

The Father's rod, and restoration

For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth, and scourgeth every son whom he receiveth.Hebrews 12:6

Especially knowing that, as adopted children, our Father will not spare the rod, and the sins we do in secret will be revealed in the light.

And even if we have made mistakes in our past, there's still reconciliation with God because we are saved. If we turn from our wicked ways, and walk in His paths, we can be restored and do great works for the Lord.

The cost of discipleship

Discipleship, not salvation

If any man come to me, and hate not his father, and mother, and wife, and children, and brethren, and sisters, yea, and his own life also, he cannot be my disciple. And whosoever doth not bear his cross, and come after me, cannot be my disciple.Luke 14:26-27

Many mistakenly think this passage is about salvation, not discipleship. Some believe that He is describing a works-based salvation. But even more common is to think that by belief unto redemption they have picked up that cross. That discipleship comes naturally with being born again.

Jesus doesn't mean that we should hate them, or ourselves, but that we be willing to give them up, and more, our very lives, because we love Him all the more.

The cost of discipleship

Count the cost

For which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and counteth the cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it? Lest haply, after he hath laid the foundation, and is not able to finish it, all that behold it begin to mock him, Saying, This man began to build, and was not able to finish. Or what king, going to make war against another king, sitteth not down first, and consulteth whether he be able with ten thousand to meet him that cometh against him with twenty thousand? Or else, while the other is yet a great way off, he sendeth an ambassage, and desireth conditions of peace. So likewise, whosoever he be of you that forsaketh not all that he hath, he cannot be my disciple. Salt is good: but if the salt have lost his savour, wherewith shall it be seasoned?Luke 14:28-34

Jesus wasn't trying to discourage us from being His disciples, but dividing the chaff from the wheat, being honest with those of us who remain. Every born again believer is called to be in service to Christ. It is time to consider the cost.

The cost of discipleship

Willing to die, but not to be inconvenienced

Most saved Christians will tell you that they would gladly lay down their life for the cause of Christ.

But how many saved Christians are willing to lay down 15-20 minutes of our morning to read the Bible? To go to church once a week, let alone three times a week? To go door knocking and soul winning? How can we expect to seriously be willing to go to our death for professing the name of Christ if we won't be inconvenienced a little bit here and there?

No servant can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon.Luke 16:13
The cost of discipleship

Not peace, but a sword

Think not that I am come to send peace on earth: I came not to send peace, but a sword. For I am come to set a man at variance against his father, and the daughter against her mother, and the daughter in law against her mother in law. And a man’s foes shall be they of his own household. He that loveth father or mother more than me is not worthy of me: and he that loveth son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me.Matthew 10:34-37

If you have been born again for any length of time, you have experienced this. Especially within family. Those that know us best and who refuse the truth, resent and turn on us.

The final excuse many of us have against doing God's will for us is the anger and confrontation we will have with loved ones. As Moses with Zipporah at the inn ().

To endure hardness like a soldier. Not entangled with any other cause or cares, but the one he's been drafted to.

Lovest thou me?

Yes they were saved, yes they knew they had salvation, and yet when left to their own devices, Peter and the others returned to their old ways. Gone a fishing.

So when they had dined, Jesus saith to Simon Peter, Simon, son of Jonas, lovest thou me more than these? He saith unto him, Yea, Lord; thou knowest that I love thee. He saith unto him, Feed my lambs. He saith to him again the second time, Simon, son of Jonas, lovest thou me? He saith unto him, Yea, Lord; thou knowest that I love thee. He saith unto him, Feed my sheep. He saith unto him the third time, Simon, son of Jonas, lovest thou me? Peter was grieved because he said unto him the third time, Lovest thou me? And he said unto him, Lord, thou knowest all things; thou knowest that I love thee. Jesus saith unto him, Feed my sheep.John 21:15-17

This is the direction given to all saved believers. To share the Gospel and win the souls of those lost sheep. Follow thou me.
