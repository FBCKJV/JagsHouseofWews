Three that bear record

For there are three that bear record in heaven, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one.1 John 5:7

The Bible does not demand that we figure out the metaphysical mechanics of how three can be one. It simply states the fact of their heavenly record and their absolute unity, leaving no room for human rationalization.

It identifies them by their revealed biblical names: Father, Word, and Holy Ghost, and the undeniable fact that these three are one.

Great is the mystery of godliness

And without controversy great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifest in the flesh, justified in the Spirit, seen of angels, preached unto the Gentiles, believed on in the world, received up into glory.1 Timothy 3:16

Where the text of scripture presents mystery, the fundamentalist stops, where the philosopher and the theologian continue, pressing on in their vain imagination.

Always search the scripture to see if it is so. And if you cannot draw a clear, direct understanding of a doctrine from plain language and context, then it is ok and in fact wise, to leave it to faith and admit your ignorance as fallible, finite man.

Each member of the Godhead has His own will, are their own person, Father, the Son and the Spirit. But these three are one and obey the will of God the Father.

The fulness of the Godhead bodily

Beware lest any man spoil you through philosophy and vain deceit, after the tradition of men, after the rudiments of the world, and not after Christ. For in him dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily.Colossians 2:8-9

The fulness of the Godhead is stated as a fact, in Christ. The scripture does not seek to define His “essence,” substance, or the mechanics of that fulness. God's word simply declares it, its truth, and then warns against the very methods that later generations of men would use to elaborate it.

There is no need to explain what we know is true. It is just true.

I am the Lord, and there is none else

I am the Lord, and there is none else, there is no God beside me: I girded thee, though thou hast not known me: That they may know from the rising of the sun, and from the west, that there is none beside me. I am the Lord, and there is none else.Isaiah 45:5-6

God says He has named Cyrus as deliverer, more than a century off in the future. And just as sure and specific and certain as this prophecy is for Cyrus, so it is for the Messiah.

God calls all His shots beforehand, and we see the results are true. And if these things are sure and true, then know the promise of salvation and eternal life in Christ Jesus is as well.

There is none else

The true and living God

But the Lord is the true God, he is the living God, and an everlasting king: at his wrath the earth shall tremble, and the nations shall not be able to abide his indignation.Jeremiah 10:10

The Lord is consistently pointing out the vanity, the utter uselessness of false gods. How foolish it is to bow and serve dead things as gods, that cannot save anyone. They are an invention of man's own vain imagination.

We worship the living God, through Whom all things were made. The heathen in their fear worship the sun, the moon, the stars. Idols made of wood and stone and molten metals.

There is none else

God is love, and God is holy

The Bible says God is love. In no uncertain terms He is love.

God so loved the world He gave His only begotten Son, Who so loved us that He gave His life for us. While we were yet sinners, He died for us. And because God loves His children, He hates that which is against us. His is a perfect hatred.

The Lord is separate from sin. Singular in His perfect ways. Where we can love, He is love. Where we are called to be holy, He is holy.

There is none else

Righteous, faithful and just

He is the Rock, his work is perfect: for all his ways are judgment: a God of truth and without iniquity, just and right is he.Deuteronomy 32:4

God is love. God is holy. God is righteous in all His ways. Faithful. True. God is just. God is not a respecter of persons. He shows no favor or bias.

the withholding of what we deserve; Hell and death.

Jealous is His name

And the Lord passed by before him, and proclaimed, The Lord, The Lord God, merciful and gracious, longsuffering, and abundant in goodness and truth, Keeping mercy for thousands, forgiving iniquity and transgression and sin, and that will by no means clear the guilty; visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children, and upon the children’s children, unto the third and to the fourth generation.Exodus 34:6-7

We learn a name for God and it is Jealous. Jealousy is not envy or covetousness. It is fiercely protecting that which one owns. God is saying in this covenant He owns them, they are His people.

In the beginning was the Word

In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. The same was in the beginning with God. All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made. In him was life; and the life was the light of men. And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.John 1:1-5

John hits the ground running. No human genealogies. Not the human birth of Christ, but the divine godhead of Jesus.

5 verses that dispel modalism, oneness heresy. Dispel the “Jesus didn't exist until he was born” heresy. The “Jesus isn't God” heresy. 5 verses that lay a foundation for the faith.

The deity of Christ

The image of the invisible God

Who is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of every creature: For by him were all things created, that are in heaven, and that are in earth, visible and invisible, whether they be thrones, or dominions, or principalities, or powers: all things were created by him, and for him: And he is before all things, and by him all things consist.Colossians 1:15-17

Jesus is the express image of God the Father, and made through Jesus, all that has been created is His, who was before all things.

Jesus forgiving the sins of the man with palsy, and then healing his flesh in opposition to the scribes questioning in their hearts His authority, gives a definitive statement of the divinity of Christ. “Who can forgive sins but God only?” Exactly.

The deity of Christ

Son of David and Son of God

It is these two sonships that Jesus was born into. Fully God. Fully man.

Being the son of Joseph, even adopted, places Jesus in line for the throne of David, fulfilling the prophets' sayings. The Son of David. But to those called by the Spirit, and given understanding of scripture, the savior of their souls and heir to the Heavenly Kingdom. The Son of God.

For David himself said by the Holy Ghost, The Lord said to my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, till I make thine enemies thy footstool. David therefore himself calleth him Lord; and whence is he then his son? And the common people heard him gladly.Mark 12:36-37

Jesus asks how can a physical son be called Lord by David, himself a king at the writing of the Psalm.

The deity of Christ

Made a little lower than the angels

But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels for the suffering of death, crowned with glory and honour; that he by the grace of God should taste death for every man.Hebrews 2:9

That the Creator became as the created that he may bring us to glory. And in all things He was like us, tempted of the flesh that He may know our struggles and understand our pain.

Jesus, being fully man and fully God, was born as we are. He grew as we do. He waxed in spirit and filled with wisdom, meaning He grew into these things. Jesus as man had to, at every step of His life, choose to do the will of the Father.

And so, Jesus wept. And then raised Lazarus from the dead.

The deity of Christ

He that descended is the same also that ascended

Wherefore he saith, When he ascended up on high, he led captivity captive, and gave gifts unto men. (Now that he ascended, what is it but that he also descended first into the lower parts of the earth? He that descended is the same also that ascended up far above all heavens, that he might fill all things.)Ephesians 4:8-10

Jesus died for our sins, went to Hell for our sins, and after 3 days and 3 nights, rose again from the dead in triumph. His soul was not left in Hell, neither did His flesh see corruption.

In a like manner, Jesus who was captive in Hell for 3 days and 3 nights, rose from the dead, bodily, led captivity captive, as He had victory and took power over death and Hell. He freed mankind from the bonds of death and Hell. He took captive His captors.

The Holy Ghost the Comforter

The Comforter shall teach you all things

But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.John 14:26

Jesus tells them the nature of the 3rd person of the trinity, the Holy Ghost. The Comforter. Who, dwelling within us gives us discernment to understand the word, as He teaches us all things and brings to remembrance those doctrines and scripture we've been taught.

In Him is peace, to not be troubled or afraid.

The Holy Ghost the Comforter

He will reprove the world

And when he is come, he will reprove the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment: Of sin, because they believe not on me; Of righteousness, because I go to my Father, and ye see me no more; Of judgment, because the prince of this world is judged.John 16:8-11

All men are called by the Spirit, God the Father having lifted up the Son, because the Spirit of the Comforter works to reprove the world of sin, righteousness and judgment. That they may know they need salvation, and believe.

When the Holy Spirit talks to you, have an ear to listen. That comes from prayer and reading and reflecting on God's word throughout the day.

The Holy Ghost the Comforter

Sealed unto the day of redemption

In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise, Which is the earnest of our inheritance until the redemption of the purchased possession, unto the praise of his glory.Ephesians 1:13-14

Earnest is money, sometimes called a “good faith deposit,” a sum that is included with your offer to purchase something to show your seriousness of intent to go through with the purchase, which is also called redemption. The Spirit is the earnest of God, given in Christ, sealing us as His until the resurrection, whereby we are redeemed.

The Holy Ghost the Comforter

The Spirit maketh intercession

Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought: but the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered.Romans 8:26

The Spirit, if we let Him, works on our infirmities, helps us with removing the thorns in our hearts that otherwise lead us to sin. He heals us as we walk in the Spirit.

But more, the Spirit is an intercessor, bringing our needs before the Father, even that which we don't know we have, that His will be done through us.

The Holy Ghost the Comforter

Spiritually discerned

But the natural man receiveth not the things of the Spirit of God: for they are foolishness unto him: neither can he know them, because they are spiritually discerned.1 Corinthians 2:14

Without first believing the Gospel, preached by the power of the word of God, and the understanding by the Spirit, the things of God are foolishness to man. The Holy Ghost teaches, not the wisdom of men, not of the world, but the spiritual things of God. Comparing spiritual things with spiritual.

As we seek, so too He gives wisdom and understanding freely, through the Spirit within us.

The Holy Ghost the Comforter

Oil and living waters

In scripture, oil is almost always a picture of the holy Spirit.

The widow's oil

Just as we who are saved are filled with the Spirit, and we can fill others, as vessels, infinitely. It never runs out.

The ten virgins

The oil is the Holy Spirit, the lamps, our hearts. It is not the wise's to give or share. Each must get their own from Christ.

Rivers of living water

If you are like the believer in John 7, you are a tree bearing fruit, and from you flows the Spirit, living waters, His word.
