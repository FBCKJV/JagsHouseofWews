So, pray

After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name.Matthew 6:9

Jesus not only taught us the pattern of prayer, but He lived a prayerful life. Often. Earnestly. Seeking God's will and the acceptance to do it. Alone. Privately. Early in the morning. All night if needed. On your knees. In tears and joy. Praising God in thanksgiving. Seeking forgiveness. Asking for favor, always in His will.

Not publicly and loudly to show how holy you are. Not in vain repetition, but in humble conversation, directly with God our Father.

One mediator

Boldly before the throne

For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus;1 Timothy 2:5

We pray to the Father, in the name of the Son, through the power of the Spirit (). Praying directly to God without a need for any other intermediary. Not praying to dead spirits, which is divination and necromancy. Not praying through a “mediatrix;” Jesus didn't say “Our mother...”

Many pray directly to angels, which is wicked (; ). We pray, as David did, to the Living God. He directs His hosts.

Catholics use idols and “Mary,” and “saints” and priests as intermediaries, because they know in their hearts they cannot go to the Father as sons. Very sad. We go boldly before the Father with confidence as sons ().

One mediator

Father and friend

My voice shalt thou hear in the morning, O Lord; in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up.Psalm 5:3

I have conversations with God all day, everyday. He points out things and I come to Him with my problems. We should be reverent and respectful but He's also our closest and dearest friend.

Having adult children, you notice that when your kids are up to something they know you wouldn't approve of, they go silent suddenly. That's how God sees it when we're not praying to Him everyday. Don't let sin in your life stop you from talking to God about it. Don't let embarrassment keep you from Him.

The Psalms as a model

Lord, my God, hear me

I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, Lord, only makest me dwell in safety.Psalm 4:8

We often don't know how to pray, let alone in the midst of trouble. But the Psalms are given as a foundation of prayer. “Lord, my God, hear me” is an excellent start any time you go boldly before the Throne.

Stand in awe, and sin not: commune with your own heart upon your bed, and be still (). Psalm 4 is prayer in the night before sleeping; Psalm 5, prayer to start the morning. Day and night, we should be in prayer to our Father.

The Psalms as a model

Praise, humility, mercy, then judgment

I said, Lord, be merciful unto me: heal my soul; for I have sinned against thee.Psalm 41:4

Before anything else, the Psalmist has prioritized calling out to God, humbling himself, admitting fault and dependency on God. This should be our starting position in prayer. Praise, humility, mercy, then judgment.

Many of us use prayer as a means of complaining, to whine about it being unfair that we are suffering at all. The problem with this is it lacks the self-reflection and humility.

Watch out for the creeping pride that may come from being recognized as a “prayer warrior.” Begin with confession. A contrite heart with love unfeigned can move mountains.

The Psalms as a model

Reminding God of His promises

Return, O Lord, deliver my soul: oh save me for thy mercies’ sake. For in death there is no remembrance of thee: in the grave who shall give thee thanks?Psalm 6:4-5

To remind God of His word shows you actually know it, and trust in it. I think this is pleasing to the Lord. Letting God know how weary it has become is also an effective means of working it through yourself.

If my people... shall humble themselves, and pray, and seek my face (). When we seek the Lord, it is obedience to His command to seek His face ().

The Psalms as a model

Thanksgiving, and to be used

In God have I put my trust: I will not be afraid what man can do unto me.Psalm 56:11

I wish more of the prayer time in a service were of thanksgiving than for asking. God already knows our needs. With all sincere humility, glorify God for answered prayers. People need to hear that. It gives hope to the hopeless.

The most surprising thing you find when you walk in the spirit is that what you start to actually pray for, instead of your fears and needs, is that He use you. That you can be the means of blessing someone else, because you know He's already got you.

How God answers

Ask, believing

Therefore I say unto you, What things soever ye desire, when ye pray, believe that ye receive them, and ye shall have them. And when ye stand praying, forgive, if ye have ought against any: that your Father also which is in heaven may forgive you your trespasses.Mark 11:24-25

This is not simply an affirmation to put on Instagram. It is a promise from God. All He asks is your forgiveness for others as He has forgiven you. To not hold grudges.

How can we humble ourselves to ask for forgiveness for disobeying God when we hold back forgiveness from those who've wronged us? Don't treat it lightly, but as literally true. Grow in faith, living faith to faith.

How God answers

According to His will

And this is the confidence that we have in him, that, if we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us:1 John 5:14

Jabez called on the God of Israel, And God granted him that which he requested (). It will be in His time. It may not look like what you imagined. It may be what you need not what you want.

When I first seek the things of God, and pray for His will to be done, I am blessed beyond measure. This isn't a prosperity gospel. Seeking His will first will find you asking for ways to please Him, to be a blessing to others, rather than riches.

How God answers

Your iniquities have separated you

Behold, the Lord’s hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear: But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you, that he will not hear.Isaiah 59:1-2

It isn't any failure of His that we have trouble. He can still save. The fault lies with us, when our iniquity has separated us from our God (). Many claim to “want to know God's will for their lives,” but flee from hard preaching against their sin.

When we are in sin, and ashamed, our doubts and fears increase. Thankfully, if we turn to Him, and confess our sins, He will forgive us (). Walk in the light that we may have confidence in the Lord to deliver us.

How God answers

When the answer is no

For this thing I besought the Lord thrice, that it might depart from me. And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.2 Corinthians 12:8-9

If a prayer remains “unanswered” by our measure, it very well may be answered in His. We've already been spared, hell and death, and given the glory of eternal life in Christ.

When we have a decision to make, and we consult any other source but God, He may well allow bad advice that tickles your itching ear (). Seek ye first the Kingdom of God. Start in His word and in prayer. Content with whatever His will may be.

How God answers

Prayer and fasting

And Jesus said unto them, Because of your unbelief: for verily I say unto you, If ye have faith as a grain of mustard seed, ye shall say unto this mountain, Remove hence to yonder place; and it shall remove; and nothing shall be impossible unto you. Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.Matthew 17:20-21

Jesus here is telling us that it was a lack of faith, and how to rectify that is by prayer and fasting. If we encounter things we struggle to overcome, it is by prayer and fasting that we can grow in faith enough to overcome. Simple as.

Daniel read the word of God and, believing it to be true, began to plea for mercy, not of their righteousness, but by His mercy (). If we all held to the spirit of Daniel, and prayed like him, those things would work themselves out.

Pray, and not faint

Importunity

And he spake a parable unto them to this end, that men ought always to pray, and not to faint;Luke 18:1

Importunity means asking for something over and over again in a strong, pushy way. It may seem contrary to the fear of the Lord, but scripture shows it is effective. We tend to move on, rather than “bother God.” We have not because we ask not.

Daniel's answer was delayed 21 days, matching the three weeks he had been mourning (). We should not give up in prayer when we aren't seeing them answered immediately.

Settling it in your heart to be glad with whatever God has given you will often lead to Him blessing you with yet a better outcome. I have personally experienced this.

Pray, and not faint

Don't bargain with God

I will give thee thanks in the great congregation: I will praise thee among much people.Psalm 35:18

Some try to bargain with God. “I'll go to church more, I will read my Bible more.” Those are things that are the least we could do, even as unprofitable servants. We have nothing to give but our love and praise in exchange.

When we bargain, we give ourselves a means to boast. We begin to see our efforts as the reason God “owed it to me.” Avoid anything that shares the glory with Almighty God.

Imprecatory prayer

Shall not God avenge his own elect?

And shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him, though he bear long with them? I tell you that he will avenge them speedily. Nevertheless when the Son of man cometh, shall he find faith on the earth?Luke 18:7-8

Notice the woman before the unjust judge is pleading for vengeance against her adversary, just as David does in the psalms, just as the martyred under the throne in Heaven ().

Imprecatory prayers are Biblical. When Jesus teaches us to pray, He says “Thy kingdom come.” Some teachers claim there is no room for this in the New Testament. But they are a mighty tool in our arsenal of faith.

Imprecatory prayer

Love your enemies, yet pray for His vengeance

But as for me, when they were sick, my clothing was sackcloth: I humbled my soul with fasting; and my prayer returned into mine own bosom.Psalm 35:13

When they were sick, David humbled himself in prayer and fasting. He shows them love. That is what Jesus asked of us (). When they are weak, or in need, we are to love them.

But we have no obligation to pray they succeed in the evil they do. We are called to love our personal enemies. But those that oppress the righteous, God will judge, and we can pray that God will repay. What little we can do in our wrath? Better to rejoice in the wrath of God.

Imprecatory prayer

Search me first

Thou hast proved mine heart; thou hast visited me in the night; thou hast tried me, and shalt find nothing; I am purposed that my mouth shall not transgress.Psalm 17:3

It is foolish to cry out for God's perfect justice while you yourself are guilty. Like a thief going to the police because his partners robbed him of his share.

The pattern of imprecatory prayer includes self-examination and willingness for God to judge us the same way (). Have a clean conscience towards God. That we don't fall into a victim mentality.

Imprecatory prayer

Destroy the wicked, spare thine anointed

Save thy people, and bless thine inheritance: feed them also, and lift them up for ever.Psalm 28:9

We have a constant black pilling from the world. But our perspective should be to entreat the Lord, that as He punishes the wicked, He protects His people. We should feel joy rather than fear.

Jeremiah prayed, forgive not their iniquity (). We should preach the Gospel to every creature, but we shouldn't hold out hope for those who reject the truth utterly, and seek our destruction for giving it.

Vows

Better not to vow

When thou vowest a vow unto God, defer not to pay it; for he hath no pleasure in fools: pay that which thou hast vowed. Better is it that thou shouldest not vow, than that thou shouldest vow and not pay.Ecclesiastes 5:4-5

Fools promise God a multitude of promises; turning from this sin or that, an offering for His favor, that is never paid. God takes vows seriously. He doesn't care for excuses, long public prayers, vain repetitive prayers. He wants sincere prayers and humble servants.

Ananias and Sapphira kept back part of the price (). When we haven't been walking with Christ, we will make vows to God if He would provide, like Jacob (). God holds us to the vows we make in His name.

Vows

Jephthah's needless vow

And Jephthah vowed a vow unto the Lord, and said, If thou shalt without fail deliver the children of Ammon into mine hands, Then it shall be, that whatsoever cometh forth of the doors of my house to meet me, when I return in peace from the children of Ammon, shall surely be the Lord’s, and I will offer it up for a burnt offering.Judges 11:30-31

Jephthah would have won victory over the children of Ammon by the power of the Lord. He did not need to add his own works as part of God's plan.

God does not need our vows, our promises to do good, in order to deliver us. He simply asks we trust in Him and let our yea mean yea (). Jephthah paid a price he needn't have, because he placed a portion of his faith in his own hands.

Praise and worship

The glory came with the praise

It came even to pass, as the trumpeters and singers were as one, to make one sound to be heard in praising and thanking the Lord; and when they lifted up their voice with the trumpets and cymbals and instruments of musick, and praised the Lord, saying, For he is good; for his mercy endureth for ever: that then the house was filled with a cloud, even the house of the Lord; So that the priests could not stand to minister by reason of the cloud: for the glory of the Lord had filled the house of God.2 Chronicles 5:13-14

The sacrifices and offerings were not when the cloud of God's Majesty came. It was when the praise and worship of the Lord was made. If you want to feel the power of God in your life, start with this ().

God wants us to sing songs of praise and thanksgiving, continuously. As it is in heaven, as it was in the Tabernacle and the temple. As it is in our hearts today ().

Praise and worship

Sing skilfully

Praise the Lord with harp: sing unto him with the psaltery and an instrument of ten strings. Sing unto him a new song; play skilfully with a loud noise.Psalm 33:2-3

Music is a form of learning and worship, as much as prayer, songs with the doctrine of scripture (). What it is not, is an excuse to be carnal, to satisfy the flesh with worldly beats, mimicking a rock concert.

Many worship services turn into a tantric trance, swaying, captivated by the beats, with songs that are thinly veiled pop love songs, with little to no doctrine. Other churches take the instruments completely out, which seems at odds with the Bible. There is a middle ground, decently and in order.

Praise and worship

The Lord bless thee

The Lord bless thee, and keep thee: The Lord make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee: The Lord lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace.Numbers 6:24-26

The purpose is to put His name upon them. That they understand where and Who the blessings are from.

We should do the same, as a priesthood of believers, especially with our family, but everywhere in our lives. People need to be reminded that the blessings that come to them are of God.
