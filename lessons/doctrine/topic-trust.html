God is our refuge and strength

God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.Psalm 46:1
  • The name of the LORD is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe.
  • Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God.
  • The LORD is good, a strong hold in the day of trouble.

These are not platitudes or empty promises. These are real and observable and heartfelt understandings and expressions of faith, forged in troubled times. And in any Christian life, given enough time, this is proven to us again and again.

Affliction

Counted as sheep for the slaughter

As it is written, For thy sake we are killed all the day long; we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter. Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him that loved us.Romans 8:36-37

What makes Psalm 44 so jarring is that the congregation is stating they are not being punished for apostasy (). Travails come upon the just. Upon the faithful who have placed their trust in the Lord.

Because man is naturally going to complain in the best of times, we need to be prepared for the proper attitude in the worst. To be persuaded that nothing can separate us from the love of Christ. Even when things are bleak.

Affliction

Why art thou cast down?

Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted within me? hope thou in God: for I shall yet praise him, who is the health of my countenance, and my God.Psalm 42:11

It is a comfort to any believer to realize that remembering past deliverances doesn't automatically cure present depression. As Pastor Jack Hyles often preached, you can't have unshakable faith unless your faith has been shaken.

Faith isn't the absence of trouble; it is the stubborn decision to anchor oneself to the Rock when you feel like you are drowning. In the moment, it's okay to say unto God my rock, Why hast thou forgotten me? Just don't take this feeling as an excuse to forget God.

Aaron held his peace, but he was still grieved (). God is understanding that sometimes the truth of His word is bitter to us, in the flesh. He is patient.

Affliction

Neither hath this man sinned

Jesus answered, Neither hath this man sinned, nor his parents: but that the works of God should be made manifest in him.John 9:3

Man has the gift of pattern recognition, and it is hard not to see that the fates that befall others must be in answer to their sin. Jesus rebuked the people doing this (). Some things are just chance ().

Job's friends defend retribution theology rigorously, the idea of karma in God's doings. This line of thought is just as common today. But not God's (). We shouldn't assume every bad thing that happens to another is for their own sin. Lazarus' suffering on earth wasn't in vain ().

Affliction

My grace is sufficient

And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.2 Corinthians 12:9

Shall we receive good at the hand of God, and shall we not receive evil? And Job sinned not with his lips (). Whether we are blessed or cursed, all is to His glory.

This. I cannot tell you what this truth of God's word feels like, you have to experience it. When there is trouble and you think you cannot go on, you turn to the Lord and He carries you there, to where you need to be.

Affliction

Tribulation worketh patience

And not only so, but we glory in tribulations also: knowing that tribulation worketh patience; And patience, experience; and experience, hope:Romans 5:3-4

God left the Canaanites in the land to prove Israel (). The Jews, who say they are but are not and do lie, exist in this world for the same reason, and every evil organization of men (). The harder the times, the more the people turn to the Lord. God uses tribulation to prove us.

Many are the afflictions of the righteous: but the LORD delivereth him out of them all (). We are not promised a carefree life. You cannot be delivered by God from tribulation, without tribulation.

Affliction

Whom the Lord loveth

My son, despise not the chastening of the Lord; neither be weary of his correction: For whom the Lord loveth he correcteth; even as a father the son in whom he delighteth.Proverbs 3:11-12

Many new Christians become frustrated that when they got saved, they still had to endure the pain and hard work of getting up out of the hole their old man had dug for them with their sin. But that hole was a grave. And God has given us the rope to climb out of it.

Where we slip, know it is God's correction, rebuking those sons whom He loves. We must not despise the rod of correction given us by our Father, but take joy in that He loves us ().

Affliction

The more they afflicted them

But the more they afflicted them, the more they multiplied and grew. And they were grieved because of the children of Israel.Exodus 1:12

In the days of the Hebrews in Egypt, of Jesus' ministry on Earth, of the early churches, persecution led to the greatest gains for God. We are given trials because God has considered us worthy to endure ().

In our tribulations we have comfort, given by God's grace, and in that comfort, the ability to give the same comfort to others in need ().

Affliction

A sweet savour of Christ

Yea, and all that will live godly in Christ Jesus shall suffer persecution.2 Timothy 3:12

A younger sister in Christ just won someone to the Lord, and within a few days was being afflicted from all sides. It was good to give her this perspective. To patiently endure it, and see her faith grow.

The elders of Israel said to Moses, ye have made our savour to be abhorred in the eyes of Pharaoh (). To the world, we stink like death, because we remind them that they are perishing, but our obedience to God in Christ is a sweet savour unto the Lord ().

Affliction

Hands held up

But Moses’ hands were heavy; and they took a stone, and put it under him, and he sat thereon; and Aaron and Hur stayed up his hands, the one on the one side, and the other on the other side; and his hands were steady until the going down of the sun.Exodus 17:12

We may grow weary. Our arms may fall. But surrounding ourselves with fellow believers, the brethren who will help hold up our arms, and the rock that is Jesus Christ, to rest on, we will be victorious.

We often face betrayal at the most inopportune times. Jesus knows what it is like to be betrayed by those closest to Him (). There is a friend that sticketh closer than a brother (). That friend is Jesus Christ.

God our refuge

The Lord is my shepherd

Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff they comfort me.Psalm 23:4

What the unsaved world uses as a false comfort in death should be a comfort in this life for saved believers. We may be in a low point in our lives, a valley, and feel the shadow of death, but the strength to fear no evil comes from knowing the good Shepherd is with us.

Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life. Goodness and mercy follow us, today ().

God our refuge

Some trust in chariots

Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will remember the name of the Lord our God.Psalm 20:7

This verse is often quoted, but in practice it can be difficult. We often seek reassurance from our material, wealth and position. In our battles, even if we are seemingly overmatched by our enemy, we are still victorious in God.

The rich man's wealth is his strong city (). Humble yourself to recognize, God is the only shelter you need.

God our refuge

Egypt a broken reed

And all the inhabitants of Egypt shall know that I am the Lord, because they have been a staff of reed to the house of Israel. When they took hold of thee by thy hand, thou didst break, and rend all their shoulder: and when they leaned upon thee, thou brakest, and madest all their loins to be at a stand.Ezekiel 29:6-7

Egypt represents the old, unsaved life, under bondage to sin. We should look at the world's solutions to our problems the same way. Weak and insufficient. Trusting in Egypt is death ().

Asa sought the physicians, not the Lord (). There's nothing wrong with using doctors, medicines, but first go to God in prayer. Those men and things may be the tools with which God heals you, but it is God's mighty hand that we need.

God our refuge

The battle is the Lord's

And all this assembly shall know that the Lord saveth not with sword and spear: for the battle is the Lord’s, and he will give you into our hands.1 Samuel 17:47

It wasn't David who overcame Goliath, but the Lord. Increased faith doesn't mean relying more and more upon yourself, but rather leaning more and more upon Him. No one would think that 300 men blowing trumpets and smashing pots could rout tens of thousands. But they had already been delivered into Gideon's hands ().

Be of good courage, and let us play the men for our people (). Too often today, professing Christian men come off as afraid. That's the secret to winning. To win souls and obey God, live righteously and trust in Him when the battle comes.

God our refuge

The still small voice

And after the earthquake a fire; but the Lord was not in the fire: and after the fire a still small voice.1 Kings 19:12

The triumph that should have led to revival brought depression and fear in the prophet. But the Lord corrects him: Yet I have left me seven thousand in Israel ().

Even after great triumphs of faith, it isn't unusual to feel a sense of letdown. To feel alone. But there is always a righteous remnant. God is not in the wind. He is in the still small voice, heard in our spirit, and He has work for us to do.

God our refuge

Yet I will rejoice

Although the fig tree shall not blossom, neither shall fruit be in the vines; the labour of the olive shall fail, and the fields shall yield no meat; the flock shall be cut off from the fold, and there shall be no herd in the stalls: Yet I will rejoice in the Lord, I will joy in the God of my salvation.Habakkuk 3:17-18

This is a great illustration of the way we, as saved believers, should approach the Great Tribulation should it come in our day, or even individual troubles. In the midst of days, the Lord remembers His mercy.

Jehoshaphat called the people together in fast, prayer and singing songs in praise of God, and the Lord confounded their enemies (). When we are at the end of our rope, the Lord hears us. Even at the depths of the sea ().

God our refuge

Never alone

And Elisha prayed, and said, Lord, I pray thee, open his eyes, that he may see. And the Lord opened the eyes of the young man; and he saw: and, behold, the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire round about Elisha.2 Kings 6:17

Anyone who has been a saved Christian for any meaningful length of time will testify that the Lord places angels over us, protecting us, often from ourselves (; ).

Two are better than one (). With Christ, we are never alone. We have the Comforter (). So, be of good cheer: for I believe God, that it shall be even as it was told me ().

Waiting on the Lord

They that wait upon the LORD

But they that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.Isaiah 40:31

Psalm 13 captures a very real feeling of the Christian life, the impatient waiting for God's perfect timing, turning into an acknowledgement that He has dealt bountifully with us ().

The Lord is not slack concerning his promise, as some men count slackness (). In our patience, God gives us added strength. They that wait on Him shall not be ashamed ().

Waiting on the Lord

New every morning

It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.Lamentations 3:22-23

We are called to pick up our cross every day, so if we stumble and backslide, if we turn to the left or the right, God is merciful in calling us back to the straight paths.

It is good that a man should both hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the Lord ().

Waiting on the Lord

Not forcing the issue

And the Lord said unto me, Say unto them, Go not up, neither fight; for I am not among you; lest ye be smitten before your enemies.Deuteronomy 1:42

Rather than accept the chastisement, they tried to defeat the Amorites themselves, only to be driven out, as bees do. When their children went to war, 38 years later, on God's schedule, they easily defeated them ().

It is entirely about faith, patience and meekness. Not taking matters into our own hands. Accepting that the Lord may deny us something now, that we have something better later.

There is also a fine line between faith and tempting God (; ). Maybe the offer of help itself came from God.

Waiting on the Lord

How long, O Lord?

And they cried with a loud voice, saying, How long, O Lord, holy and true, dost thou not judge and avenge our blood on them that dwell on the earth? And white robes were given unto every one of them; and it was said unto them, that they should rest yet for a little season, until their fellowservants also and their brethren, that should be killed as they were, should be fulfilled.Revelation 6:10-11

Many, frustrated by the world, express impatience with those of us who profess Christ. That we should do something! They complain that God is too long suffering for them.

The saints slain for the word plead with God to pour out His wrath on the wicked, and are told to rest a while more, and they do. We should learn to be a bit more patient with God.

There failed not ought of any good thing which the LORD had spoken ().

Waiting on the Lord

Not sedition, but faith

And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.Romans 8:28

We need not sow sedition or take up arms or anything else but have faith in God. Often Christians get impatient with God, and try to take vengeance into their own hands. Reactionary spirals, further worsening the conditions we face. Dancing to the devil's tune.

There is no liberty outside of Christ. If you truly believed that when they threaten your life, they are threatening you with Heaven, you wouldn't be afraid.

The more you are willing to suffer and yet trust Him, the more you will gain (). I have seen it. I have been so blessed. Let go. Have faith.

Fear is a lack of faith

Be careful for nothing

Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.Philippians 4:6-7

This should be the default state of your Christian life. Joy, not worrying and fearful for the cares of this world but trusting, in prayer and supplication, in thanksgiving.

For me, it was fear and concerns of this world that led me consistently to misery, anger and resentment. Fear for family, work, relationships with others... all things work for good when you love God and trust Him, thankful regardless of outcome. Just have to let go.

Fear is a lack of faith

Fret not

Fret not thyself because of evildoers, neither be thou envious against the workers of iniquity.Psalm 37:1

God has built us in a way that makes our gratitude for what we have the source for being less anxious. It is impossible for those that are truly grateful to God for their station in life, and the protection of His mighty hand, to fret.

In every thing give thanks (). This one exercise will change your life as profoundly as anything short of salvation. I testify to it, God is my witness.

Fear is a lack of faith

Help thou mine unbelief

And straightway the father of the child cried out, and said with tears, Lord, I believe; help thou mine unbelief.Mark 9:24

We often search for faith in our tribulation, not because we doubt God, but we have doubts about ourselves. Gideon wanted a sign, and the Lord said, have not I sent thee? () How often, when we know the right thing to do, but are afraid, do we place the burden on the Lord to show us a sign?

Gideon tore down his father's altar, by night, because he feared his father's household (). Often the only hindrance to doing something great for the Lord is our own fear.

Fear is a lack of faith

Running with the footmen

If thou hast run with the footmen, and they have wearied thee, then how canst thou contend with horses? and if in the land of peace, wherein thou trustedst, they wearied thee, then how wilt thou do in the swelling of Jordan?Jeremiah 12:5

If we, in the land of peace wherein we've trusted, can't handle things, what will we do when the river floods? So many Christians constantly complain about how “persecuted” they feel.

If you are walking the best you can in the spirit, and still find yourself fearful and angry all the time, snapping at others, there's something wrong with your Christian walk. If what you're consuming online leaves you more afraid, stop engaging in it. Fear is a sin.

Fear is a lack of faith

Faith over fear

And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.Revelation 12:11

We may be cut off from buying and selling for refusing the mark of the beast. All our preparing and stockpiling won't save us. But faith can.

It's easy to fall into fear at being cast out into the wilderness. But there in the wilderness is where God provides. Manna from heaven. Fed by ravens. Water from flint (). Faith over fear.

Vengeance is the Lord's

A just man falleth seven times

For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again: but the wicked shall fall into mischief. Rejoice not when thine enemy falleth, and let not thine heart be glad when he stumbleth:Proverbs 24:16-17

We may fall in our Christian walk, multiple times, but we rise up again. Knowing this, we are admonished against taking pleasure in the fall of the wicked. In their fall is revealed His wrath, but it also is the opportunity to show His mercy and give the truth of the gospel, through us.

We should not worry and fret about what evil men do, nor be envious of the wicked. God will repay.

Vengeance is the Lord's

We are the servants of God

And thus they returned us answer, saying, We are the servants of the God of heaven and earth, and build the house that was builded these many years ago, which a great king of Israel builded and set up.Ezra 5:11

When we are accused, we should not be afraid, nor proudly stand up, nor in fear kowtow and cease our efforts. Humbly and honestly stand for what we believe in. When an apartment manager threatens to call the police on soul winners, don't argue. Trust Him to deliver you.

God in His grace and love promises us one more thing; vengeance. He will repay (). We aren't floor mats to be trampled on. God will always deliver.
