The Bible is the word of God

I will worship toward thy holy temple, and praise thy name for thy lovingkindness and for thy truth: for thou hast magnified thy word above all thy name.Psalm 138:2

Jesus Christ is the word of God. The word of God created all things. The word of God is what we place our hope on, and are never ashamed. The word of God is the lamp to our feet, our shield, our strength and our sword.

The word of God is found in the collection of inspired scripture, 66 books known collectively as the Bible. Not paper and cardstock, ink and glue. But the words and their spirit and the truth.

One voice in all of scripture

It is all one word, one testimony, one book made up of 66 smaller books, written over thousands of years, but with one voice, the inspiration of God, through the Spirit, testifying of the Son.

Whether it is Moses, the prophets, the apostles... Christ Himself, all of it has a consistent and clear voice. Not a collection of the works of men, but the word of an eternal and living God.

Nothing in God's word is coincidental, incidental or accidental. If God repeats something, it is for emphasis and our edification.

The preservation of God's word

One jot or one tittle

For verily I say unto you, Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled.Matthew 5:18

Jesus and the Apostles quoted the OT from the Hebrew texts, compiled by Levite scribes. Jots and tittles are explicitly terms from the Hebrew alphabet and written Hebrew.

William Tyndale translated and published, in 1526, the first-ever mechanically-printed New Testament in the English language. More than 90 percent of the King James Version that was published nearly 100 years later matched Tyndale's. He was strangled and then burned at the stake for translating the Bible into English.

It would deny preservation to say that the word of God was not preserved until 1611, as millions were saved from every nation and tongue long before it, by faith. The power is in the word itself, and the Spirit moving in those receiving it. Not in the languages.

Rightly dividing the word

Comparing spiritual things with spiritual

Which things also we speak, not in the words which man’s wisdom teacheth, but which the Holy Ghost teacheth; comparing spiritual things with spiritual.1 Corinthians 2:13

You can't form doctrine around a verse or two and your assumptions and own reasonings, to fit doctrine you've decided on. You have to let God's word tell you what it means directly. God is not an author of confusion.

Like anything else in the Bible, the answer to our confusion is found by comparing scripture with scripture.

Rightly dividing the word

Context tells us what is meant

Most instances of the word saved in the Bible refer to rescue of the flesh, not salvation of the soul.

Just as “saved” in scripture isn't always referring to justification by faith, “faith” isn't always referring to the saving faith that we need to be redeemed. These are important distinctions, and understanding the context of the terms will save you when some try to cherry pick verses.

Unless God or someone in the Spirit speaking for God says it, we have to look at context to see if what a man in scripture is saying is true, or the truth from their perspective, or possibly a lie or rationalization.

Rightly dividing the word

Primary doctrine and pet theories

Just because you believe something about the world to be true, doesn't make it Biblical doctrine.

Other than the Gospel and a few primary doctrines, there's interpretation of secondary and tertiary doctrine that's open for discussion. Literal creation is a primary doctrine. Biblical age as determined by generations is secondary.

No doctrine should stand alone on a parable. The Bible is very clear on doctrine, and things that have to be forced out of the text aren't worthy of being dogmatic.

The Bible is not for our eisegesis to win fights online. Especially about carnal things. It is for the winning of souls.
