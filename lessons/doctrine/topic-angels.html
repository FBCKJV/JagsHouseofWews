The sons of God are believers

Power to become the sons of God

But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:John 1:12

Believers. Those that call upon the name of the Lord. Those born again into the promise, adopted sons, heirs to the Kingdom of the Lord. The only way to become a son of God is by faith in THE Son of God.

Then began men to call upon the name of the LORD (). Abraham rejoiced to see His day (). They were sons of God by faith in God's promise, Christ, the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world, long before the cross.

The sons of God are believers

Unto which of the angels?

For unto which of the angels said he at any time, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee? And again, I will be to him a Father, and he shall be to me a Son?Hebrews 1:5

At no time has God called angels His sons. Angels, while higher created beings than man, are below man in our dominion as sons of God (). They aren't heirs to the promise. They are not partakers with Christ; they but serve God and His heirs, or rebel and tempt and accuse.

Angels are spirit, not flesh, and not made in the image and likeness of God ().

The sons of God are believers

Giants in the earth

There were giants in the earth in those days; and also after that, when the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men, and they bare children to them, the same became mighty men which were of old, men of renown.Genesis 6:4

The sons of God in Genesis 6 are believers from Seth's line, who called on the Lord, marrying ungodly women, leading to wickedness. The giants were already there before these marriages, not produced by them. The children born after became “mighty men,” not hybrids.

God blames the wickedness of man for the Flood (), not angels or their offspring. And angels don't marry ().

The sons of God are believers

Satan came also among them

Now there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the Lord, and Satan came also among them.Job 1:6

The sons of God here are the spirits of those saved by faith in Christ, in heaven, where only the redeemed enter as sons (). Satan is an angel. A rebellious angel, a cherub. And he came among them, as a separate entity than them. Not as one of them.

When the morning stars sang

When the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God shouted for joy?Job 38:7

The “corner stone” is Jesus (), and this is His triumph, seen outside time. The morning stars are angels; the sons of God are the faithful, who rejoice in Christ's day as Abraham did.

What angels are

Ministering spirits

Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of salvation?Hebrews 1:14

Angels are servants of God, ministers to us that are saved in Christ. We are His children, His sons. What love He has for us! Amen.

What angels are

Equal unto the angels

Neither can they die any more: for they are equal unto the angels; and are the children of God, being the children of the resurrection.Luke 20:36

The Sadducees tried to paint a contradiction into the resurrection of believers, with a woman married to seven brothers. Jesus answered that we aren't taken in marriage in the glorified eternal life awaiting us.

Go back and read the verse again. Jesus is describing us. What we will be like. Undying children of God.

What angels are

The angel of the LORD

Then the angel of the Lord went forth, and smote in the camp of the Assyrians a hundred and fourscore and five thousand: and when they arose early in the morning, behold, they were all dead corpses.Isaiah 37:36

Hezekiah and Isaiah tore their clothes and cried out to God for deliverance. God considered His pleasure, promises and plans, and destroyed the army before the gates. For I will defend this city to save it for mine own sake, and for my servant David's sake ().

Many times God delivers us out of mercy and grace. But sometimes it is His pleasure, promises and plans that He responds to.

Satan and devils

Satan was created

Thou wast perfect in thy ways from the day that thou wast created, till iniquity was found in thee.Ezekiel 28:15

Satan is a created being. There was a day where Satan was created. Some believe the devil is some sort of balance to God, or the Mormons say Jesus' brother. These are blasphemous statements. Satan is a creature, Jesus the Creator.

Hell was created for Satan and the others who rebel with him (). Knowing his fate, he sought to bring down man with him. But God already had a plan of salvation for fallen man: the lamb slain before the foundation of the world.

Satan and devils

The accuser of our brethren

And I heard a loud voice saying in heaven, Now is come salvation, and strength, and the kingdom of our God, and the power of his Christ: for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, which accused them before our God day and night.Revelation 12:10

Satan goes to and fro in the earth, and walks up and down in it (). Christians should remember that he continues this practice today. He is our adversary, the god of this world, and our accuser before the Lord.

His resentment is in those sons of God, that God so loved us that He gave His only begotten Son for us.

False exorcisms

Then goeth he, and taketh with himself seven other spirits more wicked than himself, and they enter in and dwell there: and the last state of that man is worse than the first. Even so shall it be also unto this wicked generation.Matthew 12:45

Most Catholic “exorcisms” are fraudulent, performative and made up, like their “apparitions.” The swept house left empty is the problem with any false gospel exorcism (). Real deliverance is a quiet affair, and the goal is to get the person cleaned AND saved.

Possession only affects the unsaved; the Spirit within the saved prevents this.
