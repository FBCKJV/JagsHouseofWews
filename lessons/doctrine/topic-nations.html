Nations by works, individuals by faith

Righteousness exalteth a nation: but sin is a reproach to any people.Proverbs 14:34

God judges nations based on their works. Even as He judges individuals based on their faith. It's an important distinction to keep in mind as we rightly divide the word of God.

All of God's covenants with the Hebrew are if/then (). This is the covenant with nations, with the people on this earth. God judges us as individuals spiritually by faith, but God judges nations on earth by their works.

Judged here on this earth

Seek ye the Lord, all ye meek of the earth, which have wrought his judgment; seek righteousness, seek meekness: it may be ye shall be hid in the day of the Lord’s anger.Zephaniah 2:3

The judgment of man comes after our physical death (and it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment, ). But the judgment of nations is here on this earth, and is a collective judgment of the people in their land.

The prophets are sent by God with warnings and rebuke, calling the lost to repentance, nations to reform, kings to fear the Lord.

God's law for nations

Babel: scattered into nations

So the Lord scattered them abroad from thence upon the face of all the earth: and they left off to build the city.Genesis 11:8

The people imagined a vain thing. A tower to Heaven. That they could work their way to Heaven and be like God.

We are meant to be in nations. Nations by clan. By blood. Not one world government, full of vanity. Not even by a civic nationalism of paper and statutes that erases who we are, making a name for ourselves.

God's commands for the king

Thou shalt in any wise set him king over thee, whom the Lord thy God shall choose: one from among thy brethren shalt thou set king over thee: thou mayest not set a stranger over thee, which is not thy brother.Deuteronomy 17:15

The king shall be one He chooses, not a stranger or foreign influence. A brother. In modern practice, this would preclude putting a non-believer in charge.

He shall not multiply horses to himself, a cavalry that is a false sense of security, emboldening the people to turn from trusting in God's mighty hand to defend them.

God's law for nations

The law of God is perfect

Ye shall therefore keep all my statutes, and all my judgments, and do them: that the land, whither I bring you to dwell therein, spue you not out.Leviticus 20:22

The purpose was to separate the people unto God and away from the error, whoredoms and abominations of the other nations. To be holy because He is holy.

The law of God is perfect. His judgments are righteous. A just nation seeking after the Lord, who have the fear of God, would implement these strictures and punishments, that their nation not be judged. These laws give us insight to the perfect will of God for a people.

God's law for nations

Restitution, and judgment speedily

Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily, therefore the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do evil.Ecclesiastes 8:11

Robbery was punished by restitution plus a fifth part, and a trespass offering. That's it ().

The refusal to judge rightly and swiftly is why crime and perversion are rampant today. It all starts with shielding the wicked from the consequence of their actions. This keeps them from fearing God.

Judgment for innocent blood

Blood defileth the land

So ye shall not pollute the land wherein ye are: for blood it defileth the land: and the land cannot be cleansed of the blood that is shed therein, but by the blood of him that shed it.Numbers 35:33

The foundation of God's law is justice, and the avoidance of shedding innocent blood. His mercy is extended to His people, who if they receive it must make provision for the innocent, and punish those who subvert the system.

If a murderer seeks sanctuary falsely, he is turned over to die the death, because that is how the blood of the innocent is atoned. Whoso sheddeth man’s blood, by man shall his blood be shed ().

Judgment for innocent blood

Sacrifice of children to false gods was a common practice in every age and nation. Today it is done through abortion. In Canaan, they sacrificed to Moloch. Today, it is often Mammon. It is horribly wicked.

Again and again we see God's judgment poured out on nations that shed innocent blood, child sacrifice as part of worshiping false gods being the worst example of this ().

The powers that be are ordained of God

Of course we are to follow the higher law of God when earthly authority contradicts it; we ought to obey God rather than men (). Think of Daniel. He never compromised what was right, yet even under evil powers, he prospered.

Liberty comes from Christ

No liberty outside of Christ

Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.Galatians 5:1

The blessings of liberty come from the liberty found in Christ. There is no liberty outside of Christ. It isn't a concept to be understood and maintained in a secular setting. It has no meaning without the Gospel, the beautiful law of liberty.

Not every man right in his own eyes

The blessings of liberty come from those who believe on the Lord, know His word and follow it. Not from every man doing what is right in their own eyes.

There are Internet theologians who claim the law of Israel was libertarian. That is a lie. God has laws. God puts government in place. The more godly the people, the greater the blessings of liberty. The freer we are. The more moral and just.
