One of the most overlooked doctrines

And even as they did not like to retain God in their knowledge, God gave them over to a reprobate mind, to do those things which are not convenient;Romans 1:28

The teaching on reprobation is one of the most overlooked but relevant doctrines in the Bible.

It is taught throughout both Old and New Testament, which not only gives description of how one becomes a reprobate, but example after example of those that do, and warning after warning of how to handle them, the evil they do, and the signs to watch for to identify them.

Reprobate simply means rejected

Reprobate simply means rejected. Unable to come to saving faith having already rejected God, utterly and finally being given up on by God.

Reprobation is a condition caused by belief, and not wishing to retain God in their knowledge, to no longer be bothered by conscience, unwilling to believe and so being given over, given up by God and made unable to. They are given what they want, and made twice-dead.

It is a free will choice as much as salvation, and isn't determined by level or severity of sin.

The three states of man

The three states of man

Reprobation, the final and utter rejection by God of those who have finally and utterly rejected His overtures of grace, the call to Christ and the prodding and patience of the Holy Ghost, is one of the three spiritual states of man.

The unregenerate sinner

Dead in spirit but with the conscience of God within, who, until death, could hear the call and believe on Jesus Christ and be saved. They are the children of disobedience.

The saved

Those who call upon the name of the Lord, believing God's testimony and, spirit quickened. They are saved, born again believers. Called the sons of God.

The reprobate

Twice-dead. Conscience seared. Hearts hardened, ears dulled. Unable to come to the knowledge of the truth. Rejected of God. Turned over. Given up. The children of the devil.

Normal unsaved sinners are not reprobates

It's important to recognize that normal unsaved sinners are not reprobates. They haven't been turned over by God.

They commit normal sin, common to man, but occasionally have righteous actions and are repulsed by the unnatural lusts of the reprobate, having still a conscience and the law of God written in their hearts.

Most people fall in neither the camp of the saints or the reprobate, they are just unregenerate sinners, with all too common lusts of the flesh. There is hope for them until they die, lest they become reprobate.

Most people understand perishing, death and Hell. Few people understand salvation and eternal life. Even fewer understand the third spiritual state of man, reprobation.

Given over: Romans 1

Not the sin, but the heart toward God

Reprobate isn't a synonym with depravity or degeneracy. It is the cause of such behavior.

Unnatural sin is not the cause of reprobation, but a symptom that it's occurred. It is important to get that right. It's never about the sin itself, but the state of a man's heart towards God.

This is why you may have a 40yo murderer who, overcome with guilt and coming to the realization of what awaits them, accepts the Gospel sincerely, and you may have a 14yo who has hardly left the house, let alone committed grave sin, who willfully blasphemes the Holy Spirit and rejects God utterly, and is given over to reprobation.

Past repentance

The window has closed

It is IMPOSSIBLE for these people to be renewed again unto repentance. They can't believe. Their foolish heart was darkened. Hardened. Their conscience seared as with a hot iron.

Reprobation is real and it happens after God has repeatedly led one to repentance, only to be rebuffed. There is a point where it is too late, even if that point is death.

Past repentance

It is too late for some

“God never gives up on us!” That's a lie. “It's never too late to repent and believe!” That's not what the Bible says.

As longsuffering as God is, He is clear in His word that it is too late for some.

  • The people before the flood. The people of Sodom and Gomorrah.
  • Pharaoh and the Egyptians. The people of Canaan who had committed abominations.
  • The wicked kings and queens who killed the prophets of God.
  • The Pharisees and Sadducees and scribes whom Jesus condemns in their own unbelief.

In the book of Daniel, Babylonian King Belshazzar saw the writing on the wall. Literally. It was too late for him.

Sodom and the reprobate

Sins common to man

We know from scripture that while man's heart is wicked, and left to our own devices, living in the flesh, all manner of natural sin afflicts us if we don't walk in the spirit, daily. Lust, envyings, lying, fearfulness, adultery, fornication, drunkenness, stealing, wrath and railing, greed and vengeance.

Sins all too common to man. We are all sinners, of which I am chief.

But the reprobate, those who the Bible says have been rejected by God, are given over to a reprobate mind to do those things which are inconvenient. Who burn with unnatural lusts and are filled with all unrighteousness.

Sodom and the reprobate

The outward sign

Understanding that these are rejected by God not of blood, or DNA, not by their sin, but by choice.

And so without excuse, they choose. And therefore, as a consequence, they are given up to vile affections. And one of the most outward signs of the reprobate mind is becoming a sodomite. Having pride in their unnatural lusts. Making their abomination their identity.

Now not every reprobate is a sodomite. There are some who, despite being filled with all unrighteousness, choose to not engage in sodomy. Those wolves in sheep's clothing can be recognized by other means, but some are great at hiding it.

Why the doctrine matters

Reprobate Doctrine is God giving you the who, the what and the why. Even the how, as far as creeping into a church unawares.

It explains their behavior and why their choices are made but it also gives us incentive to spread the truth and live in a way that reveals the righteousness of God, that maybe some will get it before “it's too late, reprobate!”

Very often Christians will be disarmed in their judgment by blindly trusting people at church. People professing to be fellow Christians. People who claim to have repented of their evil ways. It starts with denying the Reprobate Doctrine. Teaching that all men are able to be saved at any time, that it's never too late to repent.

They take the mark because they are reprobate

They aren't made reprobate because they took the mark, they take the mark because they are reprobate.

It's a free will choice to utterly reject God and salvation. The same as those who add to or take away from God's word to knowingly teach a false gospel or who blasphemed the Holy Ghost. It's not so much that the sin itself is unpardonable as it is an act that testifies to their reprobate mind. Their hardened heart.

Why the doctrine matters

Struggling faith versus rejecting the truth

If a person's faith falters, fails, or is unfruitful will that person be lost? No. Of course not.

The difference between struggling with faith and having doubt after being saved vs rejection of the truth they understood but never trusted in, to believe a lie. Don't be confused.
