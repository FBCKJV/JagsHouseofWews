In the beginning

In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.Genesis 1:1

The fullness of the Godhead exists when nothing else did, including time, and through Him was created light, substance, life, and mankind. Everything after its own kind, with the seed contained therein propagating that life over time.

From Genesis 1 through Revelation 22, the Bible is the story of Jesus Christ. God revealing His Son to us that we may believe and have eternal life.

The heavens declare the glory of God

The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament sheweth his handywork. Day unto day uttereth speech, and night unto night sheweth knowledge. There is no speech nor language, where their voice is not heard.Psalm 19:1-3

Looking upon the night sky we see His precision and power. The things of creation speak without words, that all man may understand. They don't need a language. They don't need to be explained.

The vain hypothetical of a savage on a remote island, never hearing the gospel, is undone by the understanding that all nature is a parable and a testament of God, showing men truth.

Without excuse

For the invisible things of him from the creation of the world are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made, even his eternal power and Godhead; so that they are without excuse:Romans 1:20

Nature teaches us God's law. His wrath is revealed against those who sin, even without the oracles of God. Man can see the law of God, know it written in their hearts, watch the outcome of their behavior, and know they are guilty.

If the day declares His righteousness and the night His knowledge, and the very nature of things speaks to His wrath, then the oracles of God give us the hope of His mercy.

Job asks why, God shows Who

Doth the hawk fly by thy wisdom, and stretch her wings toward the south?Job 39:26

The wild goat, the wild ass, the unicorn (not a mythical beast, but the wild ox), the ostrich, the horse, the hawk and the eagle. Not by will of man. In all this, man is helpless. It is all the providence of God.

Creation is the parable

Creation is the parable

Which is as a bridegroom coming out of his chamber, and rejoiceth as a strong man to run a race.Psalm 19:5

It's not that parables use creation to explain God's word. Creation is the parable. It exists to illustrate the biblical truth. Not the other way around.

Every seed we sow, every shooting star, bird in flight, or wave crashing on the shore. All creation is a parable to those with ears to hear and eyes to see. The boldness of the sun breaking forth in the morning is as the risen Christ.

Children of the day

Ye are all the children of light, and the children of the day: we are not of the night, nor of darkness.1 Thessalonians 5:5

One of the reasons God created day and night, if not the only reason, is a picture of the end. On the day of the Lord, the damned get darkness. We are of the day and of the light. When we hear God's word say it, we can picture it, and in picturing it understand what God's saying.

We are to be as the firmament, as the rising sun and the crashing wave. New creatures in Christ, brought forth as a testimony to God's greatness.

And out of the ground made the Lord God to grow every tree that is pleasant to the sight, and good for food; the tree of life also in the midst of the garden, and the tree of knowledge of good and evil.Genesis 2:9

From the beginning God gave two options to the man He had created: the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, the law, and the tree of life, which is by faith in the word of God.

The birds were gleaning

Thou shalt not muzzle the ox when he treadeth out the corn.Deuteronomy 25:4

The same birds who all year kept the harmful bugs away from my garden were feasting on my peas. I had set traps for a chipmunk. As I prayed, God said to me, “they're gleaning.” The Spirit bringing all things into remembrance. A laborer gets its reward.

And, just like that, my frustration disappeared. This is God's garden.

While the earth remaineth

While the earth remaineth, seedtime and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease.Genesis 8:22

God vows never to destroy every living thing because of the evil imagination of man's heart. Rather than curse the ground, God vows to preserve the world as is until the end.

Literal creation, a young earth

In six days

For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is, and rested the seventh day: wherefore the Lord blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it.Exodus 20:11

This is a literal story of creation. Not because of fossils found in weird places, or the problems with carbon dating, or the complexity and variance of life. But because God said this is how it happened. The God who cannot lie tells us these things and that they are so.

You don't have to believe in the literal creation in order to believe on Jesus. But it is difficult to express that faith to others while doubting much of His word, or thinking it allegory.

Literal creation, a young earth

The chronogenealogies

Which was the son of Enos, which was the son of Seth, which was the son of Adam, which was the son of God.Luke 3:38

The ages of Genesis 5 are given literally: 1,558 years from Adam to Shem, about 2,008 years from Adam to Abraham, and roughly 2,000 more to Christ. This places the earth at just slightly more than 6,000 years old. Give or take a century or two.

The Septuagint's chronology has Methuselah living years after the Flood. The calendar is not the preserved word of God, but the genealogies are there to mark time.

Primary doctrine and opinions

Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.2 Timothy 2:15

Literal creation is a primary doctrine. Biblical age as determined by generations is secondary. A precise number of years has Biblical basis, but is incidental, and people of good faith can disagree.

Don't project your opinions of the worldly onto scripture and become dogmatic in things that are ultimately not important.
