The Doctrine of Christ

Jesus — promised, pre-incarnate, Creator, eternal Son, fully God, fully man, our only salvation

I.

The Promise of Christ

From the Garden onward, Scripture points forward to one coming Deliverer.

II.

Theophanies / Christophanies

Old Testament appearances of Jesus — the "Angel of the LORD" and other pre-incarnate visitations widely understood as the Son of God, seen before Bethlehem.

III.

Jesus as Creator of All Things

All creation, visible and invisible, was made through Him — and He sustains it still.

IV.

Eternal Sonship

Jesus as the eternal Word, co-existent with the Father before time began.

V.

The Deity of Jesus

The complete essence of God dwells in Christ — He is not merely God-like, but God.

VI.

The Seven "I Am" Statements

In the Gospel of John, Jesus repeatedly says "I am" — not just self-description, but a direct claim to deity, echoing God's revelation to Moses as "I AM THAT I AM" (Exodus 3:14). These statements angered the Pharisees, who understood exactly what He was claiming.

    The Most Direct Claim of All

    "Before Abraham Was, I Am"

    Unlike the seven statements above, which all have a predicate — "bread," "light," "door" — this is an absolute declaration. The Greek behind it, ἐγώ εἰμι (egō eimi), is the same phrase used in the Greek Old Testament for God's name revealed to Moses at the burning bush. The Pharisees understood immediately that Jesus was claiming to be God — and picked up stones to kill Him for blasphemy.

    "Verily, verily, I say unto you, Before Abraham was, I am."

    — Jesus, John 8:58

    VII.

    The Humanity of Jesus

    Fully God, Christ also took on real flesh and blood — tempted as we are, yet without sin.

    VIII.

    Salvation in Jesus

    There is no other name under Heaven given among men whereby we must be saved.
