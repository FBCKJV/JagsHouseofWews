The doctrine

For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.Ephesians 2:8-9

There is no doubt we are saved from our sins and Hell by faith alone, by grace alone, in Christ alone. That God’s grace extends to all men.

This is the Gospel. There is nothing more to it. There can be nothing less.

None righteous, no, not one

As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one: There is none that understandeth, there is none that seeketh after God. They are all gone out of the way, they are together become unprofitable; there is none that doeth good, no, not one.Romans 3:10-12

It serves as a universal appeal to the need for the gospel, and the reconciliation of fallen men with a holy God. There is none good but God. All have gone astray.

1All have sinned.
2All deserve death.
3Jews under the law.
4Gentiles without the law.

What one must understand to be saved

  • If you don’t believe you are a sinner, or that as a sinner you deserve Hell and death, you won’t accept the Gospel.
  • If you don’t believe that salvation from the wages of your sin, Hell and death, is a free gift, given by God’s grace and not of yourself, you cannot turn to believe the Gospel.
  • If you don’t believe that Jesus is the Son of God, Who was made flesh, lived a sinless life, was crucified, buried and risen again, that we may have remission of sin and eternal life, you can’t believe and be saved.
  • If you don’t accept the gift from God in Jesus name, calling upon the name of the Lord to forgive you, a sinner, you cannot be saved.

The gospel of grace and reconciliation by which we believe and are saved, is a concrete understandable and specific thing, spelled out by God hundreds of different ways throughout the Bible.

What must I do to be saved?

And brought them out, and said, Sirs, what must I do to be saved? And they said, Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house.Acts 16:30-31

This is the fundamental question. This is the doctrine of the Gospel being asked for, and Paul answers, not with long-winded theological points or esoteric hints at salvation being some sort of Gnostic journey.

We are saved by faith alone, plus nothing minus nothing.

To him that worketh not

But to him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, his faith is counted for righteousness.Romans 4:5

Works has nothing to do with salvation. God doesn’t owe us salvation. Our works, no matter how good we think they are, can never offset our sin, and the wages thereof.

Grace is the unearned, unmerited, undeserved gift given by God from His mercy and love. Debt is deserved. Grace is undeserved.

Trusting anything but Christ

Anything else

If you are trusting in traditions, rites, your sacrifices, prayers to saints, carnal ordinances and sacraments, doing good works or turning from your sins to save you, you are assuredly perishing.

Even partially

If they trust in their circumcision to justify them, even partially, then Christ will profit them nothing.

The finished work of Christ

The only salvation is by grace of God, the unmerited favor He extends to all, that whosoever believes on His Son… Period. Plus nothing. Minus nothing.

Only the blood atones

None of these

Turning your life around, repenting of sin, joining a church, getting water baptized, making Jesus the Lord of your life, seeing real change, sacraments and good deeds... None of these things satisfy the price of sin.

Whom God hath set forth to be a propitiation through faith in his blood, to declare his righteousness for the remission of sins that are past, through the forbearance of God;Romans 3:25

Only the blood of a holy God offered in our place and sprinkled on the mercy seat can atone for our sin and justify us before God. That is the only propitiation.

While we were yet sinners

But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.Romans 5:8

That when we were still enemies, working iniquities, dead in sin, God reconciled us through the death of His Son. God loved His enemies, and offered the free gift of salvation by the shedding of blood of His only begotten Son.

Think about this, for in it is the righteousness of God.

An exchange

For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him.2 Corinthians 5:21

Jesus took on our sin and paid the price for all of it. He was the propitiation. The ransom. The sacrifice.

By believing this, we take on His righteousness. His blood washes us clean. We are clothed in white of his perfection, our sins forgotten.

It is an exchange. That is the testament.

Abraham believed God

Counted unto him for righteousness

And he believed in the Lord; and he counted it to him for righteousness.Genesis 15:6
For what saith the scripture? Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness.Romans 4:3

If it were of works, that means God would be indebted to us. To pay us for our works. That we were owed.

Abraham did not receive the ordinance of circumcision until decades after his believing God’s promise, and being saved by that faith.

What the Bible means by repent

“Repent of your sins”

And God saw their works, that they turned from their evil way; and God repented of the evil, that he had said that he would do unto them; and he did it not.Jonah 3:10

Here we get the biblical definition of repent, God changing His mind, AND the acknowledgement that “turning from their evil ways” ie “Repenting of sin” = works.

Turning to the Lord

When they heard these things, they held their peace, and glorified God, saying, Then hath God also to the Gentiles granted repentance unto life.Acts 11:18
And the hand of the Lord was with them: and a great number believed, and turned unto the Lord.Acts 11:21

They have identical meaning. There is no implied “...from sin.” It is Biblical repentance, turning to the Lord.

There is no clear verse that spells out a Doctrine that says that we must give up sin or be willing to give up sin in order to believe and be saved. Repentance is a changing of mind or turning from one thing to another.

Biblical repentance in relation to salvation is always this; turning from whatever else you are relying on, or whatever delusion that you hold that conflicts with the good news of Jesus Christ, and turning towards God and acceptance of this truth.

Saved, though still sinners

Saying “real repentance WILL mean you turn from sin, and WILL walk in the Spirit,” is false. We must pick up our cross daily. It is a choice to engage in sanctification. But salvation is of Christ, not ourselves.

The spirit does work in us the more we grow closer to God, to help remove the thorns in our hearts as we become more sanctified, praise God! But we are saved just as we are, though we still are sinners.

Don’t let the misunderstanding about repentance be the stumbling block that keeps you from His grace.

James 2 and Romans 4

Justified by works?

Was not Abraham our father justified by works, when he had offered Isaac his son upon the altar?James 2:21

The misuse of James 2 to teach a false gospel of works is one of the great stumbling blocks to salvation and faith.

Not before God

For if Abraham were justified by works, he hath whereof to glory; but not before God. For what saith the scripture? Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness.Romans 4:2-3

Scripture says it was faith that justified Abraham to God.

By comparing scripture with scripture we see that there is no contradiction, only context. That Paul is writing of justification before God in grace, while James is writing of justification before man, by doing good works in faith.

This is the Biblical Doctrine of good works. That they which have believed, past tense, might do them and be profitable to others.

The sons of God

But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:John 1:12

We who are saved by grace through faith are adopted as sons of God, heirs to His promise and the Kingdom of Heaven.

Our entrance to the kingdom is free, for we are at Liberty in Christ. We pay nothing, not our works or labors or deeds. It is a gift of God given to all who believe.

Passed from death unto life

Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation; but is passed from death unto life.John 5:24

This is why we who believe on Him that is faithful and true may know we have eternal life. That we are sealed unto redemption by His Spirit. That He testifies within us. That we may have that blessed assurance. Eternally adopted sons and daughters of God.

Lose the gift?

If you think that anything you do can justify you, or to lose that free gift, given by God by grace through faith in Christ, you aren’t saved.

Confess and believe

That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation.Romans 10:9-10

If you believe in your heart that God loved you so much, He sent His only begotten son to die for your sin and be raised again, then confess this with your mouth in prayer, calling upon Jesus’ name, and be saved. Knowing that blessed assurance of eternal security, never to be lost or taken away.
