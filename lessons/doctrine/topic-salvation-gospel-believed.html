The Gospel That Must Be Believed

This is the Gospel. This is what one must believe to be saved.

The doctrine

The gospel which I preached

Moreover, brethren, I declare unto you the gospel which I preached unto you, which also ye have received, and wherein ye stand; By which also ye are saved, if ye keep in memory what I preached unto you, unless ye have believed in vain. For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures:1 Corinthians 15:1-4

If you don’t believe that you stand and are saved by the death, burial and resurrection of Christ Jesus, as foretold in scripture and witnessed by many, you aren’t saved.

What must I do to be saved?

When the Philippian Jailer asks Paul and Silas, “Sirs, what must I do to be saved?” And they reply, “Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved,” this is what he had to believe.

And they said, Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house.Acts 16:31

That Jesus is the Christ. That by His death, our sins are forgiven, in His blood, and in His resurrection, the promise of eternal life.

What one must understand

This is what one must understand to repent towards God and salvation in Christ Jesus.

  • If you don’t believe you are a sinner, or that as a sinner you deserve Hell and death, you won’t accept the Gospel.
  • If you don’t believe that salvation from the wages of your sin; Hell and death, is a free gift, given by God’s grace and not of yourself, you cannot turn to believe the Gospel.
  • If you don’t believe that Jesus is the Son of God, Who was made flesh, lived a sinless life, was crucified, buried and risen again, that we may have remission of sin and eternal life, you can’t believe and be saved.
  • If you think that anything you do can justify you, or to lose that free gift, given by God by grace through faith in Christ, you aren’t saved.
  • If you don’t accept the gift from God in Jesus name, calling upon the name of the Lord to forgive you, a sinner, you cannot be saved.

The whole gospel

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.John 3:16

That’s it. That’s the entire good news of the living God by His grace, His love for the entire world, not some.

The Father gave His Son as a gift. Not to some. But to whosoever, without caveat or conditions except one, that believes.

When someone is bringing doctrine to you that sounds ok or a little sus, and you’re just not sure if it’s right or not, compare it to . If it violates any of it, you know immediately it’s a false gospel.

He that believeth is not condemned

For God sent not his Son into the world to condemn the world; but that the world through him might be saved. He that believeth on him is not condemned: but he that believeth not is condemned already, because he hath not believed in the name of the only begotten Son of God.John 3:17-18

He that believes not is condemned. He that believes is not condemned. Repent and believe; Turn from non-believer to believer. That is the Gospel.

If you don’t know for sure you have eternal life, you don’t know the gospel and you aren’t saved.

Faith is trusting God’s word

Faith is trusting someone or something promised. Relying on the hope of an offer or covenant.

So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.Romans 10:17

Faith in God relies on His word being true, His promises being kept. God, who cannot lie tells us His word, and by hearing, we believe the report. That is faith.

Believe means trust to be true. To have faith in. A secure knowledge. A lively hope. It doesn’t mean anything more than that.

It isn’t a mystical force. It isn’t will-to-power sorcery.

The Ethiopian eunuch

Then Philip opened his mouth, and began at the same scripture, and preached unto him Jesus. And as they went on their way, they came unto a certain water: and the eunuch said, See, here is water; what doth hinder me to be baptized? And Philip said, If thou believest with all thine heart, thou mayest. And he answered and said, I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.Acts 8:35-37

The Gospel that was preached to the Ethiopian Eunuch was of Jesus being the Christ as promised in scripture. Not his willingness to turn from sin. Not his making Jesus “boss” of his life. Nothing about himself. Nothing more than accepting the truth about Jesus Christ.

This is the order of things, and baptism is but a public expression of the salvation that came from belief.

Cornelius

To him give all the prophets witness, that through his name whosoever believeth in him shall receive remission of sins. While Peter yet spake these words, the Holy Ghost fell on all them which heard the word.Acts 10:43-44

And, while still preaching Christ, they who heard believed and were not only saved, but bestowed gifts by the Holy Ghost.

Also note that their salvation and gifts came before being baptized. Not after or a result of.

Jesus Christ IS Lord of all. He doesn’t need you to anoint Him. God the Father already has.

We have example after example of those saved by faith on the risen Christ, who believed and it was counted unto them for righteousness. Not of repenting from sin. Not because they tried to avoid sin. Not because of anything of themselves, but the declaration that Jesus is Christ.

One apostle repented

There is one apostle who repented, however.

Judas repented himself

Then Judas, which had betrayed him, when he saw that he was condemned, repented himself, and brought again the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders, Saying, I have sinned in that I have betrayed the innocent blood. And they said, What is that to us? see thou to that. And he cast down the pieces of silver in the temple, and departed, and went and hanged himself.Matthew 27:3-5

And we know how that turned out for that reprobate.

None other name

Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.Acts 4:12

It is in Jesus Christ’s name that we are saved, there is none who come to the Father except through the Son. There is no ecumenical path to heaven.

Not by other faiths

There are none saved by other faiths, trusting in their works, praying in the name of another.

The Son

If you are looking for righteousness and truth, you cannot find it in anyone else or belief in any other power but that of the Son. The matchless name of Jesus Christ.

Calling upon the name of the Lord

For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.Romans 10:13

Not this

Not “turning from your sins.” Calling upon the name of the Lord isn’t “asking Jesus into your heart.” It’s not giving your life to Jesus.

But this

Admitting that you’re a sinner. It’s confessing He is the Christ. It’s knowing He gave His for you.

Plus nothing, minus nothing

Perishing

If you are trusting in anything other than the finished work of Christ to save you from Hell and death, if you are trusting in traditions, rites, your sacrifices, prayers to saints, carnal ordinances and sacraments, doing good works or turning from your sins to save you, you are assuredly perishing.

The only salvation

The only salvation is by grace of God, the unmerited favor He extends to all, that whosoever believes on His Son, who was made flesh, lived a sinless life, died for our transgressions, suffered in our place, and rose again to life after 3 days and nights. Period. Plus nothing. Minus nothing.

Anyone who adds requirements to this belief on Christ Jesus for salvation, in any manner, isn’t saved, and is bringing a false gospel.
