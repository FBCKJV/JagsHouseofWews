The law of the LORD is perfect

The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.Psalm 19:7

The law wasn't done away with, but the old covenant. Not because God's law was unjust but because we are. In His mercy He provided us a new covenant. A better testament.

Those who walk after the Spirit delight in His law. But those living after the flesh hate His judgments, because the law convicts them afresh, daily. So they say “that's Old Testament law. It doesn't count any more.” Despise not the law of God, but meditate on it day and night.

The law our schoolmaster

To bring us unto Christ

Wherefore the law was our schoolmaster to bring us unto Christ, that we might be justified by faith.Galatians 3:24

How can there be revival without the law, when the law is the schoolmaster that brings the lost to repentance? How can someone know they are wrong if they don't know what's right?

It shall be our righteousness, if we observe to do all these commandments (). Knowing it is impossible in our flesh to observe all, we see God's mercy revealed. The lawyer's answer was true; the Good Samaritan revealed that none of us hold to it ().

The law our schoolmaster

If a man use it lawfully

But we know that the law is good, if a man use it lawfully; Knowing this, that the law is not made for a righteous man, but for the lawless and disobedient, for the ungodly and for sinners, for unholy and profane, for murderers of fathers and murderers of mothers, for manslayers,1 Timothy 1:8-9

Paul's admonition is against the legalistic works-based false teachers, not the law itself. Many see no role for God's law today. But as we see from this list of sins, there is a clear need to maintain God's law regardless of what current year is.

Follow the commandments of God that it be well with you. Hold others to standards that it should lead them to understand they are sinners. Advocate for the law because it is good, when used lawfully, to punish evildoers (). This shouldn't be controversial.

The law our schoolmaster

Love thy neighbour came from Leviticus

Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.Leviticus 19:18

Many modernists believe that Jesus invented this commandment, and that it is in variance with the “more wrathful God” of the Old Testament. But they err not knowing scripture.

Jesus simply quoted the first commandment and Leviticus (). And Leviticus 19:18 is in the same teachings that deal with stoning sodomites and adulterers. Both great commandments come from the same God in the same place in scripture.

The law our schoolmaster

The law failed because men failed

Who also hath made us able ministers of the new testament; not of the letter, but of the spirit: for the letter killeth, but the spirit giveth life.2 Corinthians 3:6

The failing of the law to achieve righteousness wasn't God's fault, but man's. The people didn't follow His statutes, and God's wrath abides on a nation that fails to sanctify themselves through them.

I have set before you life and death... therefore choose life (). Because of the fault of the people, the law became a ministration of death. But in the Spirit is liberty ().

The law our schoolmaster

The most complete ritual religion

And every priest standeth daily ministering and offering oftentimes the same sacrifices, which can never take away sins: But this man, after he had offered one sacrifice for sins for ever, sat down on the right hand of God;Hebrews 10:11-12

God gave Israel the most well thought out and complete ritualistic religion the world has ever seen, kept holy, reflecting patterns in Heaven, and symbols of the Christ to come. And it too was a schoolmaster, to show the failings of earthly systems of sacrifices and high offices.

Carnal people see it as instructions for massive chapels and priests in robes and fancy vestments. But these things were not a pattern for us. Ye are they which justify yourselves before men; but God knoweth your hearts ().

God's civil law still stands

No dispensation, no repeal

Jesus Christ the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever.Hebrews 13:8

Not the carnal ordinances and meats and diverse washings, which were a picture of Christ, but the nitty-gritty administration of a godly nation. These stand today. There is no dispensation, there is no repeal.

Some claim the civil laws and commandments are no longer in force, and apply it to sodomy and adultery. Yet they would not claim that murder, stealing or perjury are now ok. They simply want to call evil good. His moral law is eternal, the rules that will be enforced with a rod of iron in the Millennium.

God's civil law still stands

That it may be well with thee

And now, Israel, what doth the Lord thy God require of thee, but to fear the Lord thy God, to walk in all his ways, and to love him, and to serve the Lord thy God with all thy heart and with all thy soul, To keep the commandments of the Lord, and his statutes, which I command thee this day for thy good?Deuteronomy 10:12-13

The commandments of God aren't held over the people as punishment or “just because I say so,” but that it be well with us. Those that make obedience to the law their gospel miss the point. Its real purpose is for a righteous people to know the will of God for their lives.

Christianity is a religion of low-time preference. People who make the commandments the means by which they are saved become impulsive and selfish, judgmental instead of rightly judging.

God's civil law still stands

Usury, the release and the jubilee

And ye shall hallow the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubile unto you; and ye shall return every man unto his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his family.Leviticus 25:10

In the King James Bible, usury is any interest charged on a loan, not just excessive interest (). God's law instituted a release every seven years, and a jubilee every fifty, to restore families to their land and prevent generational poverty ().

The release of debts and the redemption of the servant are pictures of the salvation found in Christ. God hates the accumulation of property to the exclusion of others (). Basically, capitalism. But that is not to say He endorsed communism, either. Fair and righteous trade.

God's civil law still stands

Just weights and true witnesses

One witness shall not rise up against a man for any iniquity, or for any sin, in any sin that he sinneth: at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, shall the matter be established.Deuteronomy 19:15

The purpose of requiring multiple witnesses isn't because 2 or 3 witnesses make an accusation true, but make it worthy to be judged. Quantity isn't truth. People who say “there are enough witnesses, case closed!” have judged the matter before considering anything else.

Divers weights are an abomination (). God hates liars, cheats, bribery and extortion. And His laws protected marriage and virginity, protecting women and men (). The law of God is perfect.

Mercy, not sacrifice

I desired mercy

For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.Hosea 6:6

The sacrifices were always for man, not God. These things were put in place not because He desired them, but because man does. Man needs to see it, touch it, smell it. Every beast is His, the cattle upon a thousand hills ().

God's will for us is that we show the same mercy towards others He extended to us. Even the greatest and only true sacrifice for sin, the blood of Christ Jesus, is for our benefit, not God's.

Mercy, not sacrifice

Do justly and walk humbly

He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth the Lord require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God?Micah 6:8

Did ye at all fast unto me, even to me? () Their fasts were self-centered, remembering their own woes. Not grand acts of fasting and weeping. To do unto others. To care for the fatherless and widows.

The fake believers in their solemn assemblies call for the day of the Lord, as if their offerings justify them. They are the very reason God's wrath is upon them ().

Mercy, not sacrifice

Eye for eye, and the other cheek

Eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot,Exodus 21:24

In 1901 Hammurabi's Code was found with an eye for an eye, and the concept came to be credited to a man, taught as a savage injustice. But it is God's perfect law. Jesus calling us to turn the other cheek () isn't saying the law is unjust. He's talking about our hearts storing up wrath.

Before a magistrate, the eye for an eye would still be justice. But we who are righteous should forgive those who wronged us. These aren't contradictory but complementary. By not walking this line, we have a society wherein there is no justice and there is no mercy. God's law is perfect, we are not.

Ordinances a shadow

A shadow of things to come

Let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink, or in respect of an holyday, or of the new moon, or of the sabbath days: Which are a shadow of things to come; but the body is of Christ.Colossians 2:16-17

These ordinances were given as a picture, to remind the people of the promise of the coming Christ. But man is simple and superstitious, and puts the power in the picture. The brazen serpent, a picture of Christ, became an idol, Nehushtan ().

The dietary laws were a living parable, to make a difference between the unclean and the clean (). Done away in Christ: What God hath cleansed, that call not thou common ().

Ordinances a shadow

The sabbath fulfilled in Christ

There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God. For he that is entered into his rest, he also hath ceased from his own works, as God did from his.Hebrews 4:9-10

A picture of not working on the day of rest: we are entered into the Sabbath of Christ without works, but faith alone. Our works aren't needed, except to do good, to do the will of the Father.

The Lord of the Sabbath calls us to take the time, ceasing from our labors to help others. Not to be a burden, but to lift others from theirs. To rest in Jesus. Call the sabbath a delight ().

Ordinances a shadow

Circumcision is nothing

For in Jesus Christ neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision; but faith which worketh by love.Galatians 5:6

It's not the act of circumcision but the heart of those seeking justification with God through it, being placed in bondage under the yoke of the law. So Titus was not to be circumcised ().

But Paul circumcised Timothy, half-Gentile, raised Jewish, that he would not be hindered by the Jews when giving the Gospel (). In that case circumcision is nothing, because it's not being put forth as a condition of justification.

The better covenant

The fault was with them

For if that first covenant had been faultless, then should no place have been sought for the second. For finding fault with them, he saith, Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, when I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah:Hebrews 8:7-8

The old covenant was ended, not because the law given Moses was flawed. The flaw was in them. Moses was taught a song to teach the children of Israel that God knew they would break the covenant ().

The ten commandments covenant has ended with Christ. The moral components remain, covered by the two great commandments. The law of death replaced with the law of liberty.

The better covenant

A better testament

But now hath he obtained a more excellent ministry, by how much also he is the mediator of a better covenant, which was established upon better promises.Hebrews 8:6

Jesus wasn't there to patch their old garment, or to refill their old, dried and cracked bottles, but to give new wine in bottles fit to hold it (). It doesn't say “except for the Jews.” Hebrews is literally written to them.

No longer a priesthood, we are all priests. No longer a temple, we are each a temple. No longer a human mediator, Jesus is our High Priest. No longer a vail between God and man, Jesus tore it forever. Not Sinai, but mount Sion ().

The better covenant

The bondwoman and the free

Tell me, ye that desire to be under the law, do ye not hear the law? For it is written, that Abraham had two sons, the one by a bondmaid, the other by a freewoman.Galatians 4:21-22

The Galatians were being swayed into another gospel, of bondage to the law. Being dragged back to Hagar, the bondwoman, and rejecting Sarah and the son of the promise. Cast out the bondwoman and her son ().

If you're sewing your own fig leaves, if you're offering the fruit of your garden instead of the firstling of the flock, repent. Hear the law. The law is made null by the death of Christ, just as a marriage by the death of the husband ().

The better covenant

Better promises

And for this cause he is the mediator of the new testament, that by means of death, for the redemption of the transgressions that were under the first testament, they which are called might receive the promise of eternal inheritance.Hebrews 9:15

Spiritual salvation was always by grace through faith for individuals, while the national promises were always contingent on works. Missed opportunities didn't make God a liar when He promised them. He rightly withdrew them when those the promises were made to didn't keep up their end.

From Noah on, blessings, commandments and promises (). By the blood of Christ a new and better testament, built on better promises, that we have been reconciled despite our own transgression of the law.
