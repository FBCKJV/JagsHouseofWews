Saved to save people

The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life; and he that winneth souls is wise.Proverbs 11:30

“Fruit” in the Biblical sense, means people who've been led to the Lord. Those saved by hearing the gospel from someone else who is saved.

It doesn't mean doing good works or following the commandments. We do good works and follow the commandments so that we may bear more fruit.

This is our calling. We are saved to save people.

The harvest is plentiful

The labourers are few

Therefore said he unto them, The harvest truly is great, but the labourers are few: pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he would send forth labourers into his harvest.Luke 10:2

Jesus says that while the harvest is great, there are too few laborers to bring it all in. This remains true, especially today.

If you aren't in a position to go out yourself, pray that you can be. Work towards it. But pray without ceasing for those who are, and that more may come. If Jesus tells us to pray, then pray.

The harvest is plentiful

One soweth, another reapeth

Say not ye, There are yet four months, and then cometh harvest? behold, I say unto you, Lift up your eyes, and look on the fields; for they are white already to harvest. And he that reapeth receiveth wages, and gathereth fruit unto life eternal: that both he that soweth and he that reapeth may rejoice together. And herein is that saying true, One soweth, and another reapeth.John 4:35-37

In our labors for Christ, some of us will sow seeds and never see if they germinate and grow to fruit ripe for harvesting. Some of us water the seeds sown by others. Some are blessed to reap. All will rejoice in Heaven when we meet those we've helped win to the Lord.

The fruit we bear are converted and saved Christians, who like a branch of a vine then produce more fruit, and so on. One long vine back to Him, the true vine ().

The harvest is plentiful

Two by two

After these things the Lord appointed other seventy also, and sent them two and two before his face into every city and place, whither he himself would come.Luke 10:1

We note the pattern of evangelism given throughout the new testament. Going forth in pairs, taking with them no provision other than what they carried on their backs, and the word of God. Preaching that the Kingdom of God is nigh.

And the message to the soul winners to be of good courage remains true. Like the twelve spies sent to bring of the fruit of the land, the first ripe grapes ().

The Christian's mission statement

The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised, To preach the acceptable year of the Lord.Luke 4:18-19

Not only does this proclamation give witness to His ministry, but it also serves as a mission statement for all Christians. In Him, we are filled with the Spirit. We are anointed Priests of the Lord. We are His Ministers, in Christ.

Do we do everything in our meager power to show compassion on the spiritually sick that they may be healed? The best of us fall short. Most of us don't really even try.

Saved men sent to save men

Not angels, but men

The angels and visions and even the glorified appearance of Jesus, none give the gospel but rather send saved men to preach the word and get other men saved.

Saul and Ananias

While Jesus appeared and struck Saul blind, He doesn't give Saul the answer to what he must do, but rather a man must go to him and bring him to Christ.

Cornelius and Peter

An angel tells him to send men to bring Peter to him, that “he shall tell thee what thou oughtest to do.”

It is by man acting as agents for God, a royal priesthood sharing the Gospel, that by hearing man may come to believe.

Saved men sent to save men

How can I, except some man guide me?

And Philip ran thither to him, and heard him read the prophet Esaias, and said, Understandest thou what thou readest? And he said, How can I, except some man should guide me? And he desired Philip that he would come up and sit with him.Acts 8:30-31

Philip asks the eunuch if he understands the scripture he's reading, and the reply is so true; unsaved people need saved people to explain scripture.

This is an excellent example of how God will move heaven and earth to get the gospel to someone earnestly contending for the truth that is written in every man's heart.

Saved men sent to save men

How shall they hear without a preacher?

How then shall they call on him in whom they have not believed? and how shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard? and how shall they hear without a preacher? And how shall they preach, except they be sent? as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!Romans 10:14-15

The Spirit is ploughing the hearts and minds of the world. And so when Jesus says the harvest is plentiful, but the laborers few, he is talking to us.

Saved men sent to save men

Ambassadors for Christ

Now then we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God did beseech you by us: we pray you in Christ’s stead, be ye reconciled to God.2 Corinthians 5:20

We are a new man, contained in the earthen vessels of the old, but reconciled with God in Jesus, and so, given the ministry, being a servant, to spreading that reconciliation to others. Ambassadors.

Every prayer for the lost is a prayer that the Spirit not only softens the heart of the unsaved, but that He also brings soulwinners into their lives to show them the gospel from scripture.

Whosoever will may come

The word is nigh thee

That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. For with the heart man believeth unto righteousness; and with the mouth confession is made unto salvation. For the scripture saith, Whosoever believeth on him shall not be ashamed. For there is no difference between the Jew and the Greek: for the same Lord over all is rich unto all that call upon him. For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.Romans 10:9-13

That's the simplicity of the Gospel. There are no works, no special status, no secret traditions, ordinances, sacraments or other deeds of the law. And knowing this, then we must preach this glad tiding, that others may by the power of God's word, hear, believe and confess unto salvation.

Whosoever will may come

The Romans Road

A short but direct use of scripture, used by evangelists to win souls to the Lord for generations.

  • God so loved the world
  • All have sinned, and come short of the glory of God
  • The wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life
  • While we were yet sinners, Christ died for us
  • Confess with thy mouth, believe in thine heart
  • Whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved

It is not the only means of giving the Gospel, but it is one of the most effective. It covers all the bases.

Whosoever will may come

At the door

“I'm a good person”

Most experienced soulwinners will turn to Revelation 21:8, and highlight “all liars,” making the sincere appeal, “We have all told lies.” This will often break the impasse.

“Have you ever cried out to the Lord for help?”

Nearly all will say “Yes” if they're being honest. From there, it becomes easier to lead them to the truth that they need to call upon the name of the Lord specifically to save them, eternally.

Some will pushback to any gospel presentation with scorn. We shake the dust and move on ().

Whosoever will may come

Compel them to come in

And the lord said unto the servant, Go out into the highways and hedges, and compel them to come in, that my house may be filled.Luke 14:23

Just as with Hezekiah's letters to all Israel, many made excuses, or mocked the invitation. But many were compelled to come, turning their heart to the Lord. Both bad and good.

The leper believes, and calls upon Jesus to heal him, and Jesus, moved with compassion does so. If thou wilt, thou canst make me clean ().

Not ashamed of the gospel

The power of God unto salvation

For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.Romans 1:16

When we share the Gospel to others, we are an instrument that reveals the nature of God to others. His righteousness. We cannot be afraid to share the good news. We can't hide our light under a bushel.

Not ashamed of the gospel

The power is in the word

So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.Romans 10:17

The power to save men's souls is in God's word. Not our logical arguments, not in our boasts or testimonials. But by scripture.

You see Philip with the eunuch expounds on who Christ is from scripture. When Paul and Silas were with the Philippian jailer, they spake unto him the word of the Lord (). The scriptures are a rock. They do not change.

Not ashamed of the gospel

Soul winning is soul warning

But if the watchman see the sword come, and blow not the trumpet, and the people be not warned; if the sword come, and take any person from among them, he is taken away in his iniquity; but his blood will I require at the watchman’s hand.Ezekiel 33:6

Only the person you're warning can take the gospel you give them, the Spirit working on them, and believe. But we can and must warn them.

If you passed the home of strangers, and saw a fire spreading, wouldn't you bang on the door to get them to evacuate? Or would you pass on by, leaving them to burn?

Wherefore I take you to record this day, that I am pure from the blood of all men.Acts 20:26
Not ashamed of the gospel

Excuses

For many of us, for much of our lives, we are like Moses. We have every excuse in the book not to go.

We fear no one will believe us. That we don't have the skills. That we aren't capable. But God has already told us, “lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.” ()

How often are we Jonahs, running from the calling? The wicked who may seek your life cannot do anything but threaten you with Heaven.

Sowing the seed of the word

The seed groweth, he knoweth not how

And he said, So is the kingdom of God, as if a man should cast seed into the ground; And should sleep, and rise night and day, and the seed should spring and grow up, he knoweth not how. For the earth bringeth forth fruit of herself; first the blade, then the ear, after that the full corn in the ear.Mark 4:26-28

The seed is the word, Jesus tells us. God doesn't dictate to the seed that it germinate, that it sprout, when it should flower and bud, that it bear fruit. Only that it will as it will, and in due season be ready for harvest.

We can trace our lineage in terms of believers who led others to Christ, who led still others, who eventually were those who brought us to salvation. Like a family tree, stretching back to the seed that was sown.

Sowing the seed of the word

Cast thy bread upon the waters

Cast thy bread upon the waters: for thou shalt find it after many days.Ecclesiastes 11:1

He that observeth the wind shall not sow; and he that regardeth the clouds shall not reap. Sow your seed in the morning and evening and at every opportunity, and give the rest to God. Be ready at any opportunity to give the gospel.

So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void, but it shall accomplish that which I please, and it shall prosper in the thing whereto I sent it.Isaiah 55:11
Sowing the seed of the word

The way side, and building up

Naaman confesses there is no other God, and in the same breath asks pardon to bow in the house of Rimmon. In many ways, this is the experience many have when given the Gospel. They are like the seed sown on the pathway in the parable of the Sower ().

A reminder that it is not just saving souls that we should do, but then building upon that salvation, encouraging and edifying babes in Christ, as we would our own children, to walk a Christian walk.

Choose ye this day

How long halt ye between two opinions?

And Elijah came unto all the people, and said, How long halt ye between two opinions? if the Lord be God, follow him: but if Baal, then follow him. And the people answered him not a word.1 Kings 18:21

There are saved people in the world. There is a vast majority of those who are caught between, often in indecision. Soul-winning, giving the gospel to the unbelievers, forces them to make this choice. And many receive it gladly.

Choose ye this day

And if it seem evil unto you to serve the Lord, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were on the other side of the flood, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell: but as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord.Joshua 24:15

Our job is to go work the harvest. To make people decide if they choose life or death.

Not peace, but division

Suppose ye that I am come to give peace on earth? I tell you, Nay; but rather division:Luke 12:51

We all have had loved ones, family, friends, who we have shared the gospel with, but we hesitate to push the issue. Sometimes, it's embarrassment at our own sins being evident to those who know us best. But often, it is the understanding that pushing the issue may push them away.

Christians are often so afraid that our loved ones will perish, but at the same time are scared we could be the cause of it.

Go

Revival starts at the door

Revival doesn't start by some magical change of heart among the heathen!

Revival starts with a knock on the door by a saved Christian with the King James Bible in their hand and the love of God in their heart.

Organized door knocking is more profitable, and having a place to send the souls won to be taught all things and be baptized is crucial. If you go to church, and they don't have soul-winning, ask the pastor to start a program. Offer to organize it.

Do the first works

Nevertheless I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love. Remember therefore from whence thou art fallen, and repent, and do the first works; or else I will come unto thee quickly, and will remove thy candlestick out of his place, except thou repent.Revelation 2:4-5

The first love of any church is the winning of souls to Christ. The joy of our salvation when fresh leads to our telling everyone about the good news. Churches grow from giving the Gospel. But many successful churches plateau and settle in with who they have.

Don't wait for persecution

And Saul was consenting unto his death. And at that time there was a great persecution against the church which was at Jerusalem; and they were all scattered abroad throughout the regions of Judaea and Samaria, except the apostles. And devout men carried Stephen to his burial, and made great lamentation over him. As for Saul, he made havock of the church, entering into every house, and haling men and women committed them to prison. Therefore they that were scattered abroad went every where preaching the word.Acts 8:1-4

Jesus told them to go out to the world and spread the Gospel to every creature. And instead they stayed in Jerusalem. So the Lord brought tribulation and persecution, scattering the saints abroad, so that they would preach everywhere. Are you waiting for the tribulation to go out into all the world and preach?

Instead of prepping for doomsday and hiding our light under a bushel in some mountainous retreat, we should be winning souls. Jeremiah isn't in the wilderness far away from the people, but in Jerusalem, the heart of darkness.
