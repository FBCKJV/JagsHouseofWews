It is entirely about Him

And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself.Luke 24:27

Many argue that Jesus wasn't known in the Old Testament, that salvation by faith didn't exist. There is no anachronism when we see Christ in the Old Testament. It is entirely about Him. He is the Lamb slain before the foundation of the world ().

Moses and the prophets all gave witness to His coming. And though they saw it afar off, most dying in faith having not seen Him, but persuaded of Him, many waited, watching for Him ().

Christ foretold

The seed of the woman

And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel.Genesis 3:15

God the Father who loves man offers the first promise of Christ, while laying out the consequences of bringing sin into the world. The seed who bruises Satan's head, and whom Satan bruises the heel, is Christ. And He covered them with coats of skins ().

Revelation 12 is the fulfilment: the woman bringing forth the man child who will crush Satan's head, the dragon seeking to devour Him as soon as He was born (). The seed of Satan are his spawn, reprobates. Sons of Belial. Vipers.

Christ foretold

God will provide himself a lamb

And Abraham said, My son, God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering: so they went both of them together.Genesis 22:8

Take thine only son to the land of Moriah, which is where Jerusalem is (), for a burnt offering. On the third day Abraham lifted up his eyes. The wood, carried up the mount. The thicket of thorns on the ram, offered in place of the son by God.

Jerusalem is where God chose to place His name. Where His name would be killed for our transgressions. God did not require the sacrifice of man, but gave His only begotten Son. Jehovahjireh ().

Christ foretold

A Prophet like Moses, a Son of David

The Lord thy God will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken;Deuteronomy 18:15

God tells David, through Nathan, that his son will build the house, and the son of David will rule forever. This is a clear picture of Christ ().

To the secular, this places Jesus in line to be an earthly king. To those called by the Spirit, He is the savior of their souls and heir to the Heavenly Kingdom. The Son of David, and the Son of God ().

Psalm 22 and the cross

For dogs have compassed me: the assembly of the wicked have inclosed me: they pierced my hands and my feet. I may tell all my bones: they look and stare upon me. They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture.Psalm 22:16-18

In the 9th hour, Jesus cried out from the cross the opening line of a song penned by David centuries earlier (). Compassed about by dogs, the Roman soldiers doing the work of the Jews, piercing Jesus' hands and feet. Splayed out upon the cross, His joints distended.

As a sheep before her shearers is dumb, so he openeth not his mouth (). Pilate could be excused for not knowing the prophecy. Herod, King of the Jews, and the priests and scribes should have.

Pictures of Christ

The LORD appeared

And the Lord appeared unto him in the plains of Mamre: and he sat in the tent door in the heat of the day;Genesis 18:1

This is an Old Testament manifestation of Christ Jesus, appearing with two angels before Abraham on their way to judge Sodom. No man hath seen God at any time, the only begotten Son... he hath declared him ().

Jacob wrestled with a man until the breaking of the day (). The angel sent before Moses was Christ, God with us: My presence shall go with thee, and I will give thee rest ().

Pictures of Christ

The serpent, the rock, the bread

And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:John 3:14

“If God be real, why bad stuff happen?” They are spoiled by their own lack of faith and gratitude. And God gives them the brazen serpent, lifted up, that whoever looked upon it lived ().

The rock smitten once, then only to be spoken to (; ). Manna, the bread which came down from heaven (). Like the entire story of Exodus, a picture of salvation in Christ.

Pictures of Christ

Joseph and his brethren

And his brethren said to him, Shalt thou indeed reign over us? or shalt thou indeed have dominion over us? And they hated him yet the more for his dreams, and for his words.Genesis 37:8

They hate the favoritism Jacob gives Joseph, and that he's tattled on their sin. In parallel to Christ we see the resentment. Like the Jews' resentment of Christians, the older hating the younger because of the prophecy of who will reign.

Daniel saw a glorified Jesus by the river, eyes as lamps of fire, arms and feet as polished brass (; ). Not Gabriel: Daniel had already met that angel.

The tabernacle

A pattern of heavenly things

Who serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things, as Moses was admonished of God when he was about to make the tabernacle: for, See, saith he, that thou make all things according to the pattern shewed to thee in the mount.Hebrews 8:5

Everything in the tabernacle is a picture of Christ. It may seem like an Ikea assembly manual, and in the view of modern theologies that strip Christ from the “Jewish Bible,” the Old Testament becomes meaningless. But every aspect matters. Blue, purple and scarlet: the colors of the bruises and blood of Christ ().

Why did Christ die in this manner? Man by our nature knows sin requires a sacrifice for atonement. Every animal sacrificed, from Adam and Eve to Abel to Noah to Moses, were pictures of the actual sacrifice to come.

The tabernacle

The burnt offering and the scapegoat

If his offering be a burnt sacrifice of the herd, let him offer a male without blemish: he shall offer it of his own voluntary will at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation before the Lord. And he shall put his hand upon the head of the burnt offering; and it shall be accepted for him to make atonement for him.Leviticus 1:3-4

The burnt offering is both of a man's own free will, and is an offering for substitutionary atonement. Not a man chosen before time by God. Not some elect men, drawn and compelled. But by his own voluntary will.

The sin offering a picture of Jesus; the scapegoat a picture of the remission of sin, all their transgressions in all their sins, carried away into the wilderness to be remembered no more ().

The tabernacle

Cedar, scarlet and hyssop

Then shall the priest command to take for him that is to be cleansed two birds alive and clean, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop:Leviticus 14:4

We see the wood of the cross, the scarlet of the blood, and the hyssop, the same used by the Roman guard to give Jesus the vinegar to drink (). Leprosy is a picture of sin, and its miraculous healing, of the saving grace of God.

The red heifer without blemish, slain and burned without the camp with cedar, hyssop and scarlet, is an explicit picture of Christ (; ). The burnt offerings were all a picture of Christ Jesus. Not a prophecy of the Jews today.

The tabernacle

The camp laid out as a cross

How goodly are thy tents, O Jacob, and thy tabernacles, O Israel!Numbers 24:5

The camps surround the Tabernacle, the throne of God: Judah to the East, larger than the others; West a bit smaller; North and South almost equal (). While we cannot know with 100% certainty that the layout was a cross, it certainly is what is described, in my opinion.

Balaam looked down on the camp and marveled. Four standards, as the four faces of the cherubim about the throne of God: a lion, an ox, a man and an eagle (). Nothing in the Bible is incidental, accidental or coincidental.

The tabernacle

He sat down

And every priest standeth daily ministering and offering oftentimes the same sacrifices, which can never take away sins: But this man, after he had offered one sacrifice for sins for ever, sat down on the right hand of God;Hebrews 10:11-12

In the Tabernacle there were no chairs. A priest could never sit down because his job was never done. But Jesus, the author and finisher of our faith, sat down. Not a bone of Him broken, the Passover lamb (; ).

Not a cracker. Not a wafer. The blood of Christ was shed on that hill in Jerusalem; it isn't in a cup of juice or wine. Ordinances are a memorial. They themselves have no power ().

The feasts fulfilled

Christ our passover

Purge out therefore the old leaven, that ye may be a new lump, as ye are unleavened. For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us:1 Corinthians 5:7

Passover and the Feast of Unleavened Bread, First Fruits, and the Feast of Weeks, Pentecost, were given as a picture of Christ's first coming. The Passover lamb, Christ's sacrifice. The purging of the leaven, the remission of sin. First Fruits, His resurrection.

The lamb without blemish brought before the people on the 10th day, slain on the 14th, the blood upon the wooden door posts (). Three days without work, as Jesus does it all. We can do nothing.

The feasts fulfilled

Pentecost and the fall feasts

Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, In the seventh month, in the first day of the month, shall ye have a sabbath, a memorial of blowing of trumpets, an holy convocation.Leviticus 23:24

Pentecost, the Feast of Harvest, the day the gospel was preached and the firstfruits gathered in (). The spring feasts picture His first coming; the fall feasts, trumpets, atonement and tabernacles, His second.

On these sabbaths NO WORK was to be done. The reinforcement of His grace in giving us a free gift of salvation in Jesus, who is the Sabbath. Those who did any work were cut off (). So too will it be in the second coming.

King and High Priest

After the order of Melchisedec

And Melchizedek king of Salem brought forth bread and wine: and he was the priest of the most high God.Genesis 14:18

Melchizedek is seen as an early appearance of Jesus, the king of peace, without beginning or end, made like unto the Son of God; abideth a priest continually ().

Joshua the high priest crowned: The BRANCH... shall be a priest upon his throne (). Both King and Priest. They who were under the law have no need of the human priests given by Moses.

King and High Priest

Aaron's rod, the covenant of salt, the veil

Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need.Hebrews 4:16

Aaron's rod, a dead branch brought to life, is a clear and obvious picture of Christ Jesus (). The covenant of salt is bestowed on a priesthood and a kingdom (; ). Both fulfilled in Christ Jesus.

The high priest entered behind the veil once a year. But when Christ died, that veil was torn (). There is no calling on a “saint,” or “Mary” to intercede. There is only faith in God's word and His perfect work.

King and High Priest

Mephibosheth at the king's table

So Mephibosheth dwelt in Jerusalem: for he did eat continually at the king’s table; and was lame on both his feet.2 Samuel 9:13

Mephibosheth is a picture of a sinner, seeking grace in the eyes of the King. He was crippled in youth, as we are in sin. Custom would have the heirs of the previous king killed, yet David pardons him and sets him up as an adopted son, welcome at his table forever.

Why do the heathen rage? () Kings and rulers, presidents and popes, all seek to be freed from the ways of the Lord. Kiss the Son, lest he be angry ().

Christ and His bride

The kinsman redeemer

And he said, Who art thou? And she answered, I am Ruth thine handmaid: spread therefore thy skirt over thine handmaid; for thou art a near kinsman.Ruth 3:9

The nearer kinsman could redeem the land, but not Ruth, to raise the name of the dead. This is a picture of the Law itself. By the Law the people could keep their inheritance, but the Law is unable to redeem the person, and raise their name from the dead. The law could not redeem. Boaz could ().

Christ and His bride

Hosea buys back the adulteress

And it shall be at that day, saith the Lord, that thou shalt call me Ishi; and shalt call me no more Baali.Hosea 2:16

The Lord instructed the prophet to love a woman who is an adulteress, just as Israel played the harlot to their God. The people wickedly left His protection for wine and idols, as an unfaithful wife ().

And yet, from the wilderness, the valley of Achor for a door of hope (). Why one? That he might seek a godly seed (). The promise was always Christ.

Christ and His bride

Song of Solomon

My beloved spake, and said unto me, Rise up, my love, my fair one, and come away.Song of Solomon 2:10

The clear allegory of Christ and His beloved, the saved. He is the tree of life, over a precious flower among the thorns. She seeks him in the night, as at the empty tomb, and finds him whom my soul loveth ().

Battered by the watchmen, she still sings his praise: This is my beloved, and this is my friend (). Unsaved people who see Christians suffer for Christ and still preach His love are often baffled, but sometimes moved. Set me as a seal upon thine heart ().

Psalm 45 is a doctrine-rich wedding song that speaks clearly of the Lord Jesus, a bride brought to Him. At His right hand stands the queen, decked in gold of Ophir. Her beauty provided by the King, to set apart the bride. The church.
