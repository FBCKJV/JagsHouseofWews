Romans Chapter 3

The Oracles of God Prove That Jew and Gentile Alike Are Guilty Before God

✦ ✦ ✦

Paul’s Question After Romans 2

Romans 2 just finished proving there is NO difference between Jew and Greek (Gentile).

  • God is no respecter of persons.
  • Circumcision and being under the Law profit nothing if you sin.
  • Not having the Law is no excuse if you do right by nature.
What advantage then hath the Jew? or what profit is there of circumcision?Romans 3:1

Paul’s Answer

Much every way! Chiefly, because unto them were committed the oracles of God, the inspired Old Testament Scriptures ().

Paul now uses those very oracles of God (the Law and the Prophets) to prove his point to both Jew and Gentile.

This silences proud Jews and shows believing Gentiles that grace for all nations was always God’s plan.

Romans 3:4: Quoting David

“God forbid: yea, let God be true, but every man a liar…” is a direct quote from David after his sin with Bathsheba:

Against thee, thee only, have I sinned, and done this evil in thy sight: that thou mightest be justified when thou speakest, and be clear when thou judgest.Psalm 51:4

Paul compiles a devastating chain of Old Testament passages to prove ALL (Jew and Gentile) are guilty before God.

God’s Plan for the Heathen

The Lesson of Jonah

The heathen are not a lost cause and should not be written off as inevitably doomed.

Jonah 1

A ship full of pagan sailors feared the LORD exceedingly, offered a sacrifice unto the LORD, and made vows after seeing His power.

Jonah 3

The entire city of Nineveh repented on a scale unparalleled elsewhere in Scripture.

The Word of God is powerful. Its ability to change hearts and minds should never be underestimated.

Every Creature

Christians must not pick and choose whom to evangelize. The command is clear: “Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature.”

If we sow the seed, God promises souls will be saved: “He that goeth forth and weepeth, bearing precious seed, shall doubtless come again with rejoicing, bringing his sheaves with him.”

God Always Planned to Save the Gentiles

  • The Mosaic Law said other nations would hear of Israel’s righteous laws and say, “Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people.”
  • The Law allowed foreigners to be circumcised and keep the Passover, becoming full Israelites.
  • This naturalization of strangers is spelled out again in Ezekiel.

God Always Planned to Save the Gentiles (continued)

  • Solomon prayed that foreigners from far countries would come, pray toward the temple, and know the name of the LORD.
  • God’s house shall be called “a house of prayer for all people.”
  • Peter confirmed in the New Testament: “In every nation he that feareth him, and worketh righteousness, is accepted with him.”

Distinct Roles in the Godhead

And when he is come, he will reprove the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment: Of sin, because they believe not on me; Of righteousness, because I go to my Father, and ye see me no more; Of judgment, because the prince of this world is judged.John 16:8-11

Note: The Holy Spirit is never said to “draw” men to Christ; His role is conviction of the world concerning sin (unbelief), righteousness (now in Christ), and judgment (already executed on Satan, unbelievers in the time to come), alongside regeneration and sealing.

How Faith Comes, Justification, and Imputation of Righteousness

II. Justification and Imputation of Righteousness

Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ: By whom also we have access by faith into this grace wherein we stand, and rejoice in hope of the glory of God.Romans 5:1-2
Much more then, being now justified by his blood, we shall be saved from wrath through him.Romans 5:9
For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him.2 Corinthians 5:21

At the moment of faith, God declares the believer righteous (justification) by imputing Christ’s perfect righteousness to us and our sin to Him. This is the Father’s judicial act based on the Son’s work at the cross.

Reconciled by His Death, Saved by His Life

Who was delivered for our offences, and was raised again for our justification.Romans 4:25
The Full Scope of Salvation

III. Reconciled by His Death, Saved by His Life

The redemptive price and atonement were fully paid and finished at the cross: “It is finished.” Christ still has future acts to do, the rapture/resurrection, the rewards of the faithful, the wrath on the unbelieving, Millennial rule. But reconciliation was finished by His death on the cross.

Reconciliation is by His death alone. But the ongoing and ultimate salvation, deliverance from sin’s power, wrath, and presence, is by His resurrection, by His life, the living union with the risen Christ who ever lives.

At the Moment of Faith

IV. Regeneration, Renewing, and Baptism by the Holy Spirit

(This is the Spirit’s baptizing us into Christ, placing us into the body of Christ, and the new birth.)

Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy he saved us, by the washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost;Titus 3:5
Jesus answered, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God. That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit.John 3:5-6
At the Moment of Faith

IV. Regeneration, Renewing, and Baptism (continued)

For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we be Jews or Gentiles, whether we be bond or free; and have been all made to drink into one Spirit.1 Corinthians 12:13
In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise,Ephesians 1:13

All this happens the instant we believe: the Holy Spirit regenerates us (new birth), renews us, baptizes us into vital union with the living Christ, and seals us forever as God’s possession as the earnest towards the purchased possession. Electing us to be predestinated to be conformed to His image and glorified, adopted as children and made joint-heirs with Christ to the Kingdom.

The payment was finished at the cross, but the full possession of the purchased inheritance, including the redemption of our body at the rapture, is the blessed hope we eagerly await. This is when the living Christ personally takes His church to Himself.

Conclusion: Eternal Security

And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand. My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father’s hand.John 10:28-29

Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, which according to his abundant mercy hath begotten us again unto a lively hope by the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead, To an inheritance incorruptible, and undefiled, and that fadeth not away, reserved in heaven for you, Who are kept by the power of God through faith unto salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.1 Peter 1:3-5
Reconciled by His Death, Saved by His Life, Awaiting the Blessed Hope

Conclusion: Eternal Security (continued)

The payment and legal basis were perfectly finished at the cross (“It is finished”), reconciling all of creation to God, giving us access to His grace the moment we believe. But the Scripture plainly teaches the “much more” salvation, eternal life, deliverance, and final redemption, is by the life of the risen Christ who ever lives. We have eternal life now because He lives, we are kept by His life now, that we shall be openly glorified when He appears: that blessed hope wherein the full victory purchased at the cross is manifested for all to see, raised up incorruptible, and we reign with Him as joint-heirs forever.
