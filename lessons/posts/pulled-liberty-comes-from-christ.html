Deuteronomy 12

Instruction to us

This is not only instruction to the children of Israel, but to us as saved Christians.

Ye shall not do after all the things that we do here this day, every man whatsoever is right in his own eyes. For ye are not as yet come to the rest and to the inheritance, which the Lord your God giveth you.Deuteronomy 12:8-9

The blessings of liberty come from those who believe on the Lord, know His word and follow it. Not from every man doing what is right in their own eyes.

Freedom comes from Christ

Freedom comes from Christ, and the blessings that follow come from a people made righteous in the blood of the Son, obeying the will of the Father, filled with the Spirit.

A people who know the judgment of God, His mercy and longsuffering, loving good and abhorring evil.

To their own destruction

Removing God from this, or worse, turning Jesus into a false version of Him, inventing a Christ that pleases their itching ears, has been a disaster.

And now that we are a few generations away from the bedrock of faith the people built upon, they seek to "conserve" not that which is God's, but the lusts of their own hearts. To do that which is right in their own eyes. And they do it to their own destruction.

Not libertarian

There are Internet theologians who claim that the OT law of the newly founded Israel was libertarian. That we should as Christians be libertarian. But that is a lie.

God has laws. God puts government in place. The leaders may follow the god of this world. They may be wicked, as their people are wicked. But they exist to enact judgment.

As the people, so the rulers

Weak

when God seeks to overthrow a people.

Tyrants

when the people turn from God.

Less authoritarian

when the people can govern themselves, because they seek first the things of God.

Regardless of the state

That is the biblical truth, given repeatedly in Scripture. We as believers are to bring others to believe, regardless of the nature of the state. Regardless of economic systems or repression. Rejoicing in tribulation. Winning souls.

Because the more godly the people, the greater the blessings of liberty. The freer we are. The more moral and just.

Turn not to the right hand nor to the left

Let thine eyes look right on, and let thine eyelids look straight before thee. Ponder the path of thy feet, and let all thy ways be established. Turn not to the right hand nor to the left: remove thy foot from evil.Proverbs 4:25-27

Everything is downstream

Everything is downstream from a people's relationship with God. That is the lesson of the Bible.

Individuals

God judges individuals by His grace and their faith. One is the individual soul liberty we have as believers.

Nations

And nations by their works. The other by how godly a people are.

Free speech and gun rights

Free speech in a society that is Christian enough that non-believing members feel uncomfortable challenging basic decency and morality, works. Free speech where Christians are a looked down upon remnant, doesn't.

Gun rights in a world where most people are God-fearing is a good thing. It keeps the bad guys afraid.

Do that which is good

Let every soul be subject unto the higher powers. For there is no power but of God: the powers that be are ordained of God. Whosoever therefore resisteth the power, resisteth the ordinance of God: and they that resist shall receive to themselves damnation. For rulers are not a terror to good works, but to the evil. Wilt thou then not be afraid of the power? do that which is good, and thou shalt have praise of the same: For he is the minister of God to thee for good. But if thou do that which is evil, be afraid; for he beareth not the sword in vain: for he is the minister of God, a revenger to execute wrath upon him that doeth evil. Wherefore ye must needs be subject, not only for wrath, but also for conscience sake. For for this cause pay ye tribute also: for they are God’s ministers, attending continually upon this very thing. Render therefore to all their dues: tribute to whom tribute is due; custom to whom custom; fear to whom fear; honour to whom honour.Romans 13:1-7

The blessings that allow these freedoms come from liberty in Christ. Do that which is good, and you get them. Simple as.

Deuteronomy 28

Deuteronomy 28

14verses blessing them for obeying
40 moreverses cursing them if they depart from God's commands

40 more verses cursing the children of Israel if they depart from God's commands than the 14 blessing them for obeying.

The same promise

The conditions placed on the people in the law were that it be well with them, and the same promise applies to us today.

We have the advantage of the total knowledge of God in His scriptures, the earnest of His promise in our hearts, the Spirit testifying within that we are saved, sealed unto redemption. We have the one sacrifice of Christ Jesus, and the one additional command to go out into the world and bring people to Christ with the word of truth.

The blessings of liberty

A nation that calls itself His, where a majority are faithful brethren, receives the abundance of His blessings. The blessings of liberty.

America was one such nation for a time. And that is the excellence that people speak of, it came from God to a humble and separated people. And in just a few generations that was squandered as we turned to the left and the right and allowed Idolatry to take root in the land.

All those curses have come to pass

And now all those curses have come to pass. Strangers in the land eat of our stores. They are lifted up over us. All that we set to do are destroyed and perish quickly. We have a people smitten with madness, and blindness and astonishment of heart. Our sons and daughters given to another people as fodder for their wars.

We have become an astonishment, a proverb, and a byword among all nations.

Would not obey

And it shall come to pass, that as the Lord rejoiced over you to do you good, and to multiply you; so the Lord will rejoice over you to destroy you, and to bring you to nought; and ye shall be plucked from off the land whither thou goest to possess it.Deuteronomy 28:63

And the Lord that blessed America is rejoicing over our destruction because we who call ourselves by His name would not obey the voice of the Lord our God.

1 Samuel 12

Incidental to the hearts of the people

One of the takeaways to the story of Israel, from the patriarchs, Moses and Joshua, the judges and kings, is that the means of government is incidental to the hearts of the people.

That how they are ruled is not as important as their faith in God and their not turning aside to vanity, to the gods of this world. Not falling into Idolatry. Not rejecting His promise or the inheritance He has established for us. Obeying the commandments and law He has given us that it be well with us and we live long in the land He has appointed for us.

None of it matters

The same is true for us today. For all the political discourse on the proper means of government, the economic system that's used, the means of deciding leadership, the false binary choices we are given...none of it matters if we are not a godly people.

And if we are, it will not be a terror for us, but against those who would do evil.

Men of God

We need men of God who stand on His word, preaching against sin. Who say;

Moreover as for me, God forbid that I should sin against the Lord in ceasing to pray for you: but I will teach you the good and the right way:1 Samuel 12:23

preaching the whole word of God. In season or out of season. And the people who call themselves by His name to follow Him in all things.

Calling for a king

1Calling for a king.
2Bringing the ark to battle without the Lord being present.
3Offering our own sacrifices and not accepting His.

But the solutions offered by so many boil down to calling for a king. For bringing the ark to battle without the Lord being present. For offering our own sacrifices and not accepting His. Not repenting to the Lord that He may deliver us.

The old paths

We have the government we deserve.

The evil done to us is the result of turning away from the straight paths, the old paths that God has made for us. Turning to the left or to the right.

If my people, which are called by my name, shall humble themselves, and pray, and seek my face, and turn from their wicked ways; then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin, and will heal their land.2 Chronicles 7:14

The answer to the problem

Winning souls and living Godly, raising Godly children, and assembling together to edify one another, training up those who can go out and do likewise, studying to be approved by God, able to rightly judge, is the answer to the problem.

Our judging the fatherless and taking care of our widows indeed. Bringing hope to the hopeless. Staying spotless from the sins of the world.

To set at liberty

The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised, To preach the acceptable year of the Lord.Luke 4:18-19
Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father is this, To visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep himself unspotted from the world.James 1:27

No political solution

There is no political solution to a spiritual problem.
