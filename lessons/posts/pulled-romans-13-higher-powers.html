Romans 13

Let every soul be subject

Let every soul be subject unto the higher powers. For there is no power but of God: the powers that be are ordained of God.Romans 13:1

God puts the people in power over us, to His purpose. Even the wicked.

Whosoever resisteth the power

Whosoever therefore resisteth the power, resisteth the ordinance of God: and they that resist shall receive to themselves damnation.Romans 13:2

Damnation in this sense for the saved is physical, but in the broader sense it is a sin to revolt against things that aren't contradicted by the higher power, the highest being God.

Think of Daniel

For rulers are not a terror to good works, but to the evil. Wilt thou then not be afraid of the power? do that which is good, and thou shalt have praise of the same:Romans 13:3

Think of Daniel, given us for an example. He never compromised what was right, but did well in that framework that even under evil powers, he prospered.

The minister of God

For he is the minister of God to thee for good. But if thou do that which is evil, be afraid; for he beareth not the sword in vain: for he is the minister of God, a revenger to execute wrath upon him that doeth evil. Wherefore ye must needs be subject, not only for wrath, but also for conscience sake.Romans 13:4-5

Liberty itself as a god

ACAB. FAAFO. Molon labe. Don't tread on me.

The twisting of our liberty in Christ and the blessings that follow, into a cult of individuality that worships Liberty itself as a god leads to entitlement, pride, justification for wickedness and an abandonment of fear of God, and an adoption of fear of EVERYTHING that may infringe on our "freedoms."

The meek shall inherit the earth

But we know, the meek shall inherit the earth. We know that we, as peacemakers are blessed. That as much as possible if it be within us, we should live peaceably with all men.

Liberty comes from faith

Jews, who are in open rebellion against God, who killed the prophets and Christ Jesus, are contrary to all men and God's wrath abides upon them to the uttermost.

The shift from understanding that liberty comes from faith through Christ by grace, to it being something we deserve in itself, is a Jewish trick.

Tocqueville

Tocqueville, the Frenchman who toured early America concluded that the preservation of America’s traditional religion, the Puritan and Baptist faith in Christ, and the understanding that our liberty is from sin, through Him, is one of the most important tasks of democratic statesmanship.

He wrote that religion “should be considered the first” of America’s “political institutions” and even that it is necessary for Americans to “maintain Christianity…at all cost.”

Mark 12

Tempting Jesus

The Pharisees who have decided they need to kill Jesus come tempting Him to say something rebellious against Roman occupation. They flatter Jesus and then ask if the Jews should pay taxes to the empire.

Jesus, in a verse even the godless heathens know, shows that Caesar is on the coin of the realm, and declares;

And Jesus answering said unto them, Render to Caesar the things that are Caesar’s, and to God the things that are God’s. And they marvelled at him.Mark 12:17

Render to God what is His

The amount of discomfort and twisting of this doctrine by those whose love of money, the material, has kept them from rendering to God what is His for generations, in every Christian culture.

There is always the religious equivalent of Sovereign Citizens who balk at the idea of giving "their money" to the beast system. Here Jesus says it's not yours. It's not God's. It's the beast's and the beast exacts tribute.

The point isn't even to pay your taxes, though you shouldn't care if you have to, but to RENDER TO GOD what is His.

God judges nations

If we as a nation, a people, willingly did that, the tax thing would take care of itself, because we get the government we deserve as a people. God judges nations.

The nation of Judea was 40yrs from its final destiny. God vowed to destroy it, and Jesus' prophecy came true and Judea was destroyed, just like the unfaithful servants at the vineyard.

This is how a people die

And it was in a fit of rebellion that the last of the Jews were crushed, following a false messianic figure in the Bar Kokhba revolt, 70 years after that.

What never occurred to these seditious and adulterous Jews was repentance to God and acceptance of Jesus as Christ.

They followed man and wrath and fear instead. They made it their idol. They worshipped themselves and despised God's longsuffering mercy on them.

This is how a people die. This is how a nation is judged.

Tribute to whom tribute is due

For for this cause pay ye tribute also: for they are God’s ministers, attending continually upon this very thing. Render therefore to all their dues: tribute to whom tribute is due; custom to whom custom; fear to whom fear; honour to whom honour.Romans 13:6-7

We are instructed by the Bible to not riot in the daytime or take vengeance for ourselves. To render custom to who is due custom and tribute to who is due tribute.

To render unto Caesar that which is Caesar's. To pay our taxes and obey the laws so long as they do not contradict the authority of the Lord and His word.

Every one in his place

Pastors to subject themselves to Jesus, servants to their masters, husbands to loving their wives as Christ loves His brethren, wives to be subject to their husbands and children to honor their parents.

The irony

I was just struck with a realization about the irony of those who call Christianity "cucked," and who attempt to discredit the writings of the Apostle Paul based on his Epistles regarding the behavior of Christians, especially relative to the powers that be.

To the government. Being subject to those who have power over us, given by God. To, as much as possible, live at peace with all men. Romans 13, etc.

Bound for the gospel

The irony being that Paul was subject to constant persecution, was stoned to death at one point, had to be snuck out of cities in baskets lest the mobs led by Jews tear him apart, and who wrote a majority of his epistles from chains. Literally bound for preaching the word of God and the gospel of Christ crucified.

The only way we win

To have faith that God will save us from tribulation and persecution. To allow God to exact vengeance for us. To glory in our persecution for His name's sake.

This isn't being "cucked," it's literally the only way we win.

And Paul, who wrote much of this from a jail cell, under house arrest, bound to a centurion by chains 24 hours a day for the cause of Christ, saw no contradiction in any of that.

Glory in your affliction

As long as you're preaching the word of God, you shouldn't worry about things like hate crime laws or censorship, or any other persecution led by the Jews. Glory in your affliction and speak the truth.

In Acts, Peter, James, Paul and others are openly condemned and set upon for vocally pointing out that the Jews were antichrists who killed Christ Jesus. Stephen was martyred for "hate speech" against the Jews.

They turned the other cheek, loved their enemies, heaping coals upon the heads of those who hated them by their charity and humble conversation. Living peaceably.

But when it came to the preaching of the word and the truth of those who hate Christ, they boldly spoke out, despite threats of jail, and even unto death.

Would that we are blessed by God with so strong a faith.

For he is the minister of God to thee for good. But if thou do that which is evil, be afraid; for he beareth not the sword in vain: for he is the minister of God, a revenger to execute wrath upon him that doeth evil.Romans 13:4
