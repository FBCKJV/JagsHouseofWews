Never once quoted scripture

I just listened to a 4-minute audio clip titled "How do you actually get saved according to the Word of God?" that never once quoted scripture, used unbiblical terms like "repent of your sins," "admit you're desperately wicked," "make Jesus your boss," "make Jesus your Lord," that finished with strawman and ad hominem in defense of this damnable heresy.

No one can get saved by someone telling them this.

Saved by this truth

They are saved by this truth;

He that believeth on the Son hath everlasting life: and he that believeth not the Son shall not see life; but the wrath of God abideth on him.John 3:36

He did it all

  • It is not how bad we feel about being a sinner.
  • It isn't how well we do walking in the Spirit.
  • It isn't how willing we are to enter into discipleship.

It is believing in your heart that Jesus Christ is the Son of God, as the eunuch professed, period.

And Philip said, If thou believest with all thine heart, thou mayest. And he answered and said, I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.Acts 8:37

He did it all.

Led astray by Judaizers

The foolish Galatians had been being led astray by Judaizers who snuck in to spy out their liberty, and to falsely teach that they were not saved unless they did certain things, and made an outward change that was visible to men.

Starting with circumcision but adding other Judaized concepts to the faith, and making it to none effect.

Made perfect by the flesh?

O foolish Galatians, who hath bewitched you, that ye should not obey the truth, before whose eyes Jesus Christ hath been evidently set forth, crucified among you? This only would I learn of you, Received ye the Spirit by the works of the law, or by the hearing of faith? Are ye so foolish? having begun in the Spirit, are ye now made perfect by the flesh?Galatians 3:1-3

Exactly the same thing

"Repent-of-your-sins" heresy is exactly the same thing.

That while you may begin in the Spirit, you are made perfect in the flesh. That a change must come, and outward signs observable to these hypocritical fruit inspectors, or else you must doubt your salvation. You must be made complete by the flesh to satisfy their own judgment.

It is as wicked now as it was then.

Christ shall profit you nothing

Behold, I Jag say unto you, that if you are trusting your own turning from sins, Christ shall profit you nothing.

Just as Paul warned the Galatians about circumcision. It is the same spirit of placing your trust in things you do rather than Christ.

For I testify again to every man that is circumcised, that he is a debtor to do the whole law. Christ is become of no effect unto you, whosoever of you are justified by the law; ye are fallen from grace.Galatians 5:3-4
For I testify again to every man that [TRUSTING IN SOMETHING THEY DO], that he is a debtor to do the whole law.Galatians 5:3, applied

"This is different!"

No! Circumcision was part of the law, this is different! I am saying you have to stop sinning!The objection
Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law.1 John 3:4

"But James 2!"

But...but...James 2!The objection

Yes. Let us look at James 2:8-13.

If ye fulfil the royal law according to the scripture, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself, ye do well: But if ye have respect to persons, ye commit sin, and are convinced of the law as transgressors. For whosoever shall keep the whole law, and yet offend in one point, he is guilty of all. For he that said, Do not commit adultery, said also, Do not kill. Now if thou commit no adultery, yet if thou kill, thou art become a transgressor of the law. So speak ye, and so do, as they that shall be judged by the law of liberty. For he shall have judgment without mercy, that hath shewed no mercy; and mercy rejoiceth against judgment.James 2:8-13

To whom?

But the fruit of the Spirit should be OBVIOUS!The objection

To whom? To you? Who the Hell are you, respecter of persons?

  • You who show no mercy on judgment?
  • You who think the work begun in the Spirit now must be finished in the flesh?
  • You who have fallen from grace?

Who are you to demand evidence observable to you, and your standards, of another who has believed unto righteousness the gospel of God?

Hypocrite!

For they bind heavy burdens and grievous to be borne, and lay them on men’s shoulders; but they themselves will not move them with one of their fingers. But all their works they do for to be seen of men: they make broad their phylacteries, and enlarge the borders of their garments,Matthew 23:4-5
But woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye shut up the kingdom of heaven against men: for ye neither go in yourselves, neither suffer ye them that are entering to go in.Matthew 23:13

About men

And here's the thing. These unsaved fools who are pushing this nonsense want to make it an argument about individuals. About men. Because they feel they can win an argument about men and convince people that because so and so did something, real or slanderously reported, then the doctrine of the Gospel is no good.

They don't make it about scripture because they can't win that argument.

Not of ourselves

I've just shown conclusively from scripture again and again and again how we are saved and it is by believing on the Lord Jesus Christ, through the grace of God unto eternal life.

Not of ourselves. It is a gift.

For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.Ephesians 2:8-9

And I can confidently go throughout the Bible and show exactly that salvation is not of us but of Christ.
