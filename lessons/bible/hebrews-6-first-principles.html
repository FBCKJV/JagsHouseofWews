Milk, and not strong meat

For when for the time ye ought to be teachers, ye have need that one teach you again which be the first principles of the oracles of God; and are become such as have need of milk, and not of strong meat. For every one that useth milk is unskilful in the word of righteousness: for he is a babe. But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil.Hebrews 5:12-14

Let us go on unto perfection

Therefore leaving the principles of the doctrine of Christ, let us go on unto perfection; not laying again the foundation of repentance from dead works, and of faith toward God, Of the doctrine of baptisms, and of laying on of hands, and of resurrection of the dead, and of eternal judgment. And this will we do, if God permit.Hebrews 6:1-3

The gospel by which you are saved

Part of being a saved believer is knowing these basic doctrines, because they are the gospel by which you are saved.

That foundational knowledge is the understanding with which we first trusted, milk to babes.

The 6 Foundational Blocks

  1. Repentance from Dead WorksTurning away from self-righteousness, religion, and human effort to save oneself.
  2. Faith Toward GodPlacing absolute trust in the finished work of Jesus Christ alone.
  3. Doctrine of BaptismsUnderstanding spiritual baptism (into Christ by the Spirit) and the subsequent water ordinance as a public testimony to join the local church.

The 6 Foundational Blocks, continued

  1. Laying on of HandsThe order and structure of the local church in setting apart qualified men for ministry.
  2. Resurrection of the DeadThe literal, bodily resurrection of believers to eternal life.
  3. Eternal JudgmentThe absolute reality of a final reckoning, the lake of fire for the lost, and the escape from hell for the saved.

The bare minimum

✦ ✦ ✦

Knowledge of these things and faith above all, and for all, is bare minimum.

And these fundamental truths are also the surest way to test those who claim to be teachers, and who through vain words subvert the faith.

Ye are of God, little children

Ye are of God, little children, and have overcome them: because greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world. They are of the world: therefore speak they of the world, and the world heareth them. We are of God: he that knoweth God heareth us; he that is not of God heareth not us. Hereby know we the spirit of truth, and the spirit of error.1 John 4:4-6

Even babes

Even babes are able to exercise discernment, and come to maturity through trying the spirits to see if men are from God or not.

Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world.1 John 4:1

By the gospel

It is by the gospel that we can identify those who teach vain jangling to itching ears.

Made merchandise of

Ironically, it is often longtime Christian believers who get fooled by these men, not losing their salvation, but never moving on to the meat of the word, and so being manipulated to be made merchandise of out of laziness and pride.

A litmus test

✦ ✦ ✦

Sometimes it is a babe in Christ who has the good news fresh before them, that resists these tempters, when others follow after them, puffed up by a little knowledge and not much else.

These truths act as a litmus test.
