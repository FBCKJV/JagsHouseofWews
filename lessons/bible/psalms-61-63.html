Psalm 61

The rock that is higher than I

✦ ✦ ✦
Psalm 61 · Hear my cry, O God

From the end of the earth

Hear my cry, O God; attend unto my prayer. From the end of the earth will I cry unto thee, when my heart is overwhelmed: lead me to the rock that is higher than I.Psalm 61:1-2

What a prayer of salvation and assurance. Psalm 61 is a heartfelt and earnest song of deliverance and praise for the Christ. The King that reigns forever.

No matter where we are, from the ends of the earth physically or spiritually, God hears our cries to Him. When our heart is overwhelmed, there is a rock that is higher, that we can be placed upon by God.

Psalm 61 · The rock

Higher ground

Lord, lift me up, and let me stand / By faith on heaven’s table land; / A higher plane than I have found, / Lord, plant my feet on higher ground.Higher Ground

That higher ground is a plane, a flat and steady surface built upon the rock that is Jesus. The foundation laid in Him. The cornerstone of our faith.

Psalm 61 · A shelter

A strong tower

For thou hast been a shelter for me, and a strong tower from the enemy.Psalm 61:3

We know He hears us and will bring us to that higher rock, because He has been a shelter and a strong tower, shielding those that trust in His holy name.

I will abide in thy tabernacle for ever: I will trust in the covert of thy wings. Selah.Psalm 61:4
Psalm 61 · My vows

The heritage of those that fear thy name

For thou, O God, hast heard my vows: thou hast given me the heritage of those that fear thy name.Psalm 61:5

David says that he knows God has heard his vows. The trust he has placed in the Lord, kept in his heart, means that his dedication to be a doer of the word and keep proper judgement and mercy, will be met.

We have been given the heritage of those that fear His name, if we have kept in mind that truth of the gospel. The patriarchs and prophets, David himself, the hall of the faithful who called out to God for salvation is that godly heritage, that great cloud of witnesses.

Psalm 61 · The King

He shall abide before God for ever

Thou wilt prolong the king’s life: and his years as many generations. He shall abide before God for ever: O prepare mercy and truth, which may preserve him.Psalm 61:6-7

The King's life, prolonged and his years spanning generations, is both a prayer for David, but also an acknowledgement of the King of kings. That Jesus shall reign eternally.

Being forever before God, kept by mercy and truth. Everlasting.

We have the same assurance, and by keeping that in mind, we can do great works for the Lord and sing His praises. Not to obtain salvation, but because of it.

Psalm 61 · Praise

Daily perform my vows

So will I sing praise unto thy name for ever, that I may daily perform my vows.Psalm 61:8

Amen

Psalm 62

He only is my rock and my salvation

✦ ✦ ✦
Psalm 62 · Waiting upon God

From him cometh my salvation

Truly my soul waiteth upon God: from him cometh my salvation.Psalm 62:1

David's Psalm 62 is a meditation of faith as applicable to the Christian today waiting for our Savior, the rock of our salvation, as David was 3,000 years ago. It's the same faith and the same promise.

Jesus Christ.

Psalm 62 · He only is my rock

I shall not be greatly moved

He only is my rock and my salvation; he is my defence; I shall not be greatly moved.Psalm 62:2

We wait patiently upon His coming. For our expectation is from Him. I shall not be moved.

Psalm 62 · They delight in lies

A tottering fence

How long will ye imagine mischief against a man? ye shall be slain all of you: as a bowing wall shall ye be, and as a tottering fence. They only consult to cast him down from his excellency: they delight in lies: they bless with their mouth, but they curse inwardly. Selah.Psalm 62:3-4

The contrast of this faith in the Lord, the Messiah, the anointed One, the surety of His deliverance and steadfast faith in the promise, is held up against men who trust worldly institutions, their own vain imagination and mischief against their fellow man. Their end is death, they shall be slain and the frame they trusted will collapse like a tottering fence.

Who are these men? What do they do? They consult God only to cast Him down from His Excellency, they delight in lies, they bless with their mouth but curse Him inwardly. They trust earthly institutions of men, and political power, oppression and spoiling the riches of others.

Application • A church of men

Christ is King

Here in modern times, we see a rise of political schemers shouting "Christ is King," and belonging to a foreign institution, under a "pope," who they claim is the rock, an institution in which they place all their trust.

They held a conference this past weekend where a supposed Christian pastor welcomed a Roman Catholic propagandist to the stage, proclaiming a false gospel; that there is no salvation outside of his church.

They will fail for the same reason the hypocrites and thieves of David's day did.

Psalm 62 · Wait thou only upon God

My expectation is from him

My soul, wait thou only upon God; for my expectation is from him. He only is my rock and my salvation: he is my defence; I shall not be moved. In God is my salvation and my glory: the rock of my strength, and my refuge, is in God. Trust in him at all times; ye people, pour out your heart before him: God is a refuge for us. Selah.Psalm 62:5-8

Not a church of men. Not vain traditions and dead works. Not paying lip service to God, as these men of low degree do. The vain beliefs of men with high degree, theologians counting angels on the tip of a pin, and missing the truth of grace and mercy. David says if they were weighed in a balance, all of them would be lighter than vanity itself. Worthless. Hopeless.

Psalm 62 · Men of low and high degree

Lighter than vanity

Surely men of low degree are vanity, and men of high degree are a lie: to be laid in the balance, they are altogether lighter than vanity. Trust not in oppression, and become not vain in robbery: if riches increase, set not your heart upon them.Psalm 62:9-10

They seek power and fame, riches and accolades, to make merchandise of those foolish enough to get caught up with them, setting their hearts upon men, rather than God.

Psalm 62 · God hath spoken once

Power belongeth unto God

God hath spoken once; twice have I heard this; that power belongeth unto God.Psalm 62:11

Amen! It is His power to save. His rock of defense we trust on. His truth and His way, forever.

Psalm 62 · Unto thee belongeth mercy

According to his work

Also unto thee, O Lord, belongeth mercy: for thou renderest to every man according to his work.Psalm 62:12

Jesus will render to every man according to his works;

For the Son of man shall come in the glory of his Father with his angels; and then he shall reward every man according to his works.Matthew 16:27

And His reward is great, for those that wait patiently, serving Him. Even so Lord, come quickly.

Psalm 63

In a dry and thirsty land

✦ ✦ ✦
Psalm 63 · Two flights

In the wilderness of Judah

Psalm 63 is given us as a Psalm written when David was in the wilderness of Judah, something that happened twice in his life.

We have read of David's flight from Saul, the decade or so where the future King of Israel would be on the run from the mad King. Multiple Psalms address this period as we have seen.

But there was a second time David had to flee to the wilderness, a much older David, who had to leave Jerusalem due to the insurrection led by his son, Absalom.

Psalm 63 · The king

The king shall rejoice in God

While the superscription of Psalm 63 doesn't say which occasion this was written during, the context of verse 11 leads me to believe that it is this latter period of the King's life.

But the king shall rejoice in God; every one that sweareth by him shall glory: but the mouth of them that speak lies shall be stopped.Psalm 63:11

During the lifetime of Saul, David did not claim the title. He would not presume to even harm the Lord's anointed. This final stanza seems to indicate that David was already King, the lies of those involved in the insurrection had forced him from his home.

Psalm 63 · Early will I seek thee

A dry and thirsty land

O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee: my soul thirsteth for thee, my flesh longeth for thee in a dry and thirsty land, where no water is; To see thy power and thy glory, so as I have seen thee in the sanctuary.Psalm 63:1-2

In his old age and facing the ultimate betrayal of a son rising up against the father, David is still praising God. He is still seeking the Lord early, thirsting for Him. Longing for Him. Especially here in the wilderness, a dry and thirsty land.

Spiritually he is as he is physically. And like a man in a desert seeks an oasis, David's heart seeks God.

David writes that he longs to see the power and glory of God as he has seen in the sanctuary. The Tabernacle. Given that longing, we may see what a man of faith he was.

Application • The sanctuary

In the place God had chosen

At a time where the King could use the power of the crown to bring the religious leaders and the Mercy Seat of God with him, depriving the Kingdom of the spiritual presence of God and His ministers, David chose rather to keep them in the place God had chosen to place His name. To allow others in Israel to avail themselves of His presence. To trust in God to deliver him back to the Tabernacle, the sanctuary again, to see His power and to sing His holy praise.

David was willing to sacrifice the carnal impulse to assert control of the state religion, to the spirit of peace he had in faith on the Lord.

Psalm 63 · Thy lovingkindness

Better than life

Because thy lovingkindness is better than life, my lips shall praise thee. Thus will I bless thee while I live: I will lift up my hands in thy name.Psalm 63:3-4

David is as sure of his salvation in life, fleeing, in peril, as he was when he dwelt peacefully in Jerusalem. And he vows to offer praise to God while he lives, regardless of the circumstances.

God's grace is sufficient for him, and better to be absent from the body and be present with the Lord. We should value Him more than our very life.

Psalm 63 · Satisfied

In the shadow of thy wings

My soul shall be satisfied as with marrow and fatness; and my mouth shall praise thee with joyful lips: When I remember thee upon my bed, and meditate on thee in the night watches. Because thou hast been my help, therefore in the shadow of thy wings will I rejoice. My soul followeth hard after thee: thy right hand upholdeth me.Psalm 63:5-8

Be satisfied in the Spirit more than in the flesh. Praise Him for His lovingkindness, being full with His mercy, more than the marrow and fatness of succulent and abundant meats.

When we sleep and when we awake, when we are up in the middle of the night, sing His praises. Because He has been our help, our shelter, our Provider, under the shadow of His wings we have found peace and security. Rejoice!

Psalm 63 · Those that seek my soul

A portion for foxes

But those that seek my soul, to destroy it, shall go into the lower parts of the earth. They shall fall by the sword: they shall be a portion for foxes.Psalm 63:9-10

Part of the peace that passes all understanding is the knowledge that God's judgments and vengeance will fall on the wicked. They will be made portions for foxes, fed to the creatures of the wilderness when they fall to the sword they sought to use on us. That in the end, even the king shall rejoice in God, all who swear by Him, trusting in Christ shall glorify His holy name. And the mouths of those that speak lies shall be stopped. Amen
