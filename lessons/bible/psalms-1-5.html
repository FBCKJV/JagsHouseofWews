Psalm 1

The blessed man and the ungodly

✦ ✦ ✦
Psalm 1 · The Psalms

Songs of the called out assembly

The book of Psalms, songs sung by the congregation, musicians and singers, in the called out assembly.

Most are attributed to King David, whose playing of the harp soothed his predecessor, King Saul, and while Psalm 1 isn't clearly introduced as David's, it follows his patterns of praise and doctrine, and generally is believed to be his. But more importantly, they are of the Spirit, like all scripture, and profitable for learning and reproof.

And much like the poetic wisdom of Solomon, we find the gospel of Christ throughout.

Psalm 1 · The blessed man

Blessed is the man

Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly, nor standeth in the way of sinners, nor sitteth in the seat of the scornful.Psalm 1:1

Verse 1 opens with a standard refrain found in Psalms; the blessedness of the man, and the understanding that it is by God's grace and mercy all blessings flow, Whose promises are sure.

Here, the promise to bless those who do not listen to the ungodly, the wisdom of this world. Blessing those who do not go the way of sinners, or the scorners.

In many ways, the Psalms reflect the wisdom of the Lord found in Proverbs, and give an insight to the mind and heart of the King, a man after God's own heart.

Psalm 1 · Day and night

His delight is in the law of the Lord

But his delight is in the law of the Lord; and in his law doth he meditate day and night.Psalm 1:2

David contrasts the ungodly sinners and scorners with the man who delights in the law of God. Who meditates upon the word day and night. This too is a frequent theme found in Psalms, and it shows the importance of scripture, and its role in the Christian life.

Application • The word of God

Yea, hath God said?

Too often we see those opposed to Christ attacking the inerrant, inspired and preserved word of God. As Satan tempted Eve in the garden, saying "Yea, hath God said?" enemies of God attack His word, because it is the easiest way to cast doubt, or to put false doctrine, vain traditions and damnable heresy in place of the rock which Jesus tells us to build upon; these sayings of His.

Even so-called professing Christian sects and denominations, that make their history, mythology and traditions the focal point of their faith will downplay and minimize the importance of scripture, mocking Bible believing Christians for making it the sole authority in our faith and practices.

Psalm 1 · The tree

A tree planted by the rivers

And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season; his leaf also shall not wither; and whatsoever he doeth shall prosper.Psalm 1:3

But what sayeth the scripture? Those that walk in the Spirit, seeking the blessings of God, meditate in His word day and night. By doing so, we become as a tree planted by the river, constantly fed and nourished. Bearing fruit for God. Not withering and being cut off, as the unfruitful. Prospering in all our ways.

Psalm 1 · The chaff

Like the chaff

The ungodly are not so: but are like the chaff which the wind driveth away.Psalm 1:4

The ungodly have no fruit. They are compared to the chaff of wheat, that blows away in the wind on the threshing floor. Insubstantial. Unfruitful. Hollow husks with no purpose other than to be burned up in fire.

Psalm 1 · The judgment

The congregation of the righteous

Therefore the ungodly shall not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.Psalm 1:5

The distinction between the two types of men, the saved believers, the righteous by faith and the ungodly, who do not walk in faith, is made clear.

We will sit in judgment with Christ. We shall be the church of the righteous, the congregation of the elect in Jesus. But the ungodly will not.

Psalm 1 · The two ways

The way of the ungodly shall perish

For the Lord knoweth the way of the righteous: but the way of the ungodly shall perish.Psalm 1:6

Just as Jesus asks Martha after the death of her brother, Lazarus:

And whosoever liveth and believeth in me shall never die. Believest thou this?John 11:26

David clearly did. The sinners perish. But the righteous in Christ are with the Lord, always.

Psalm 2 · The heathen rage

Why do the heathen rage?

Why do the heathen rage, and the people imagine a vain thing? The kings of the earth set themselves, and the rulers take counsel together, against the Lord, and against his anointed, saying,Psalm 2:1-2

Why do the heathen rage? Why do people think they can go against God? Kings and rulers, presidents and prime ministers, popes and priests, all seek to be freed from the ways of the Lord, to do what they will.

Especially the religious and political leaders in the time of Jesus.

For of a truth against thy holy child Jesus, whom thou hast anointed, both Herod, and Pontius Pilate, with the Gentiles, and the people of Israel, were gathered together,Acts 4:27
Psalm 2 · The bands

Let us break their bands asunder

Let us break their bands asunder, and cast away their cords from us.Psalm 2:3

They believed if they killed the Son, they would break the bands, their bonds, and be free of God.

Jesus addressed this in a parable, about a Lord who planted a vineyard and lent it out to husbandmen to dress and keep it. And when the Lord sent servants to gather the harvest, the stewards beat and killed them. So finally, the Lord sends his son;

Psalm 2 · The bands

Come, let us kill him

But when the husbandmen saw the son, they said among themselves, This is the heir; come, let us kill him, and let us seize on his inheritance.Matthew 21:38

David prophetically writes of this here in Psalm 2.

Psalm 2 · The Lord shall have them in derision

He that sitteth in the heavens shall laugh

He that sitteth in the heavens shall laugh: the Lord shall have them in derision. Then shall he speak unto them in his wrath, and vex them in his sore displeasure.Psalm 2:4-5

But, it is folly. The Lord holds them in derision. He laughs at their vain efforts, because Christ, His anointed, rose again.

Psalm 2 · My holy hill of Zion

Thou art my Son

Yet have I set my king upon my holy hill of Zion. I will declare the decree: the Lord hath said unto me, Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee.Psalm 2:6-7

He set His Son upon Zion, and upon the throne.

And we declare unto you glad tidings, how that the promise which was made unto the fathers, God hath fulfilled the same unto us their children, in that he hath raised up Jesus again; as it is also written in the second psalm, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee.Acts 13:32-33
Psalm 2 · The inheritance

A rod of iron

Ask of me, and I shall give thee the heathen for thine inheritance, and the uttermost parts of the earth for thy possession. Thou shalt break them with a rod of iron; thou shalt dash them in pieces like a potter’s vessel.Psalm 2:8-9

Jesus' inheritance are all the nations of the world, and possession of the uttermost parts of the world. And as Lord of lords and King of kings, He will rule them.

And he that overcometh, and keepeth my works unto the end, to him will I give power over the nations: And he shall rule them with a rod of iron; as the vessels of a potter shall they be broken to shivers: even as I received of my Father.Revelation 2:26-27
Psalm 2 · A warning to the rulers

Be wise now therefore, O ye kings

Be wise now therefore, O ye kings: be instructed, ye judges of the earth.Psalm 2:10

This prophetic Psalm gives warning to the rulers of this world, reminding them of their place. That they serve at the pleasure of the Lord, and He will deliver vengeance.

It's an amazing and consistent message, across the entire Bible; The Christ is God's anointed, and Jesus is the Christ. All who believe on Christ Jesus shall be saved. His grace and mercy giving us the chance to serve Him in righteousness.

Psalm 2 · Kiss the Son

Kiss the Son

Serve the Lord with fear, and rejoice with trembling. Kiss the Son, lest he be angry, and ye perish from the way, when his wrath is kindled but a little. Blessed are all they that put their trust in him.Psalm 2:11-12

Amen

The Lord my shield

Psalm 3 · Many rise up against me

When the world seems against you

Lord, how are they increased that trouble me! many are they that rise up against me. Many there be which say of my soul, There is no help for him in God. Selah.Psalm 3:1-2

When the world seems against you, and the wicked seem to prosper, and scoffers mock your trust in the Lord, we can grow weary and discouraged.

Knowing there are others who share this feeling, and have experienced similar despair, but found comfort and solace in prayer, as David does here, is such a blessing. And it is part of what makes the Bible so different from other religious texts.

Psalm 3 · Thou art a shield for me

The lifter up of mine head

But thou, O Lord, art a shield for me; my glory, and the lifter up of mine head. I cried unto the Lord with my voice, and he heard me out of his holy hill. Selah.Psalm 3:3-4

The Lord is our shield. He does lift up our heads when we are feeling down. When everything feels lost, and we don't have the strength to continue we can cry out to the Lord, and He will hear us.

Psalm 3 · I awaked

The Lord sustained me

I laid me down and slept; I awaked; for the Lord sustained me. I will not be afraid of ten thousands of people, that have set themselves against me round about.Psalm 3:5-6

The same Lord over all that gives us life, and a new day and another chance, is the same Lord Who protects us from all evil.

Psalm 3 · Arise, O Lord

Vengeance is His

Arise, O Lord; save me, O my God: for thou hast smitten all mine enemies upon the cheek bone; thou hast broken the teeth of the ungodly.Psalm 3:7

Vengeance is His. And He is faithful to answer our plea for it. Turn to the Lord rather than wrath or despair, and see His faithful love for us.

Psalm 3 · Thy blessing is upon thy people

Salvation belongeth unto the Lord

Knowing that

Salvation belongeth unto the Lord: thy blessing is upon thy people. Selah.Psalm 3:8

Psalm 4

Prayer at night

✦ ✦ ✦
Psalm 4 · Prayer

Psalms of prayer

Psalms of prayer are the most common. The cries to the Lord to save us from affliction and thanksgiving for having delivered us.

We often don't know how to pray, let alone in the midst of trouble. We often don't take our troubles directly to God. And when we do, we are at a loss for what to say.

Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought: but the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered.Romans 8:26

But the Psalms are given as a foundation of prayer, to know we have a strong deliverer, a Lord that hears us, Who has given us help in our distress and Who grants mercy to His people.

Psalm 4 · Hear me when I call

O God of my righteousness

Hear me when I call, O God of my righteousness: thou hast enlarged me when I was in distress; have mercy upon me, and hear my prayer.Psalm 4:1

David calls out to the Lord, O God of my righteousness, not telling God of his own righteousness, but the knowledge that whatever righteousness we have, is His.

David acknowledges that the Lord has delivered him in the past, and is asking to be heard to do it again. Praise and gratitude. Humility. These are the foundations of an effective prayer.

Psalm 4 · The sons of men

They seek after leasing

O ye sons of men, how long will ye turn my glory into shame? how long will ye love vanity, and seek after leasing? Selah.Psalm 4:2

Lamenting the ways of those who trouble us, the sons of men who vex the righteous with their filthy conversation. They scoff at the faithful. They love vain things, worldly ideas that profit nothing. They seek after leasing, which means to pursue lies, falsehoods, or deceit. But we are not like they are.

Or at least we shouldn't be.

Psalm 4 · Set apart

Him that is godly for himself

But know that the Lord hath set apart him that is godly for himself: the Lord will hear when I call unto him.Psalm 4:3

The Lord has set us apart, His holy people. And He will hear when we reach out to Him.

Psalm 4 · Upon your bed

Stand in awe, and sin not

Stand in awe, and sin not: commune with your own heart upon your bed, and be still. Selah.Psalm 4:4

How often do we lay awake in prayer. Pondering our own sins, and seeking the Lord? How often do we wake in the morning, and praise God for His mercy, asking to be used by Him today?

Psalm 4 · Put your trust in the Lord

The sacrifices of righteousness

Offer the sacrifices of righteousness, and put your trust in the Lord.Psalm 4:5

We have the power given us by Christ, to be sons of God. To boldly go before the throne. Stand in awe, and sin not. Make yourselves a living sacrifice, in all you do. Put your trust in the Lord. Comfort yourself with this relationship with Him, that He has mercifully granted to us.

Psalm 4 · Who will shew us any good?

Lift thou up the light of thy countenance

There be many that say, Who will shew us any good? Lord, lift thou up the light of thy countenance upon us.Psalm 4:6

David writes of the many who have no hope, who don't know the Lord is good to those that love Him. Don't despair as they do. Ask Him for His grace, to shine His light upon us. To look your way. Going to Him in confidence that whatever is His will, it shall be done. Those that trust in the Lord shall not be ashamed.

Psalm 4 · Gladness

Gladness in my heart

Thou hast put gladness in my heart, more than in the time that their corn and their wine increased.Psalm 4:7

Let His light fill you, rejoice. We are more blessed than those of the world. More blessed than those in time of bounty. He has made us His children. He has given us eternal life. Rejoice in that. Be still and know He is faithful.

Psalm 4 · In peace, and sleep

I will both lay me down in peace

I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, Lord, only makest me dwell in safety.Psalm 4:8

Amen!

And in the dark night, when you call out to Him in prayer, know He is with you, even His Spirit that dwells within you.

Prayer in the morning

✦ ✦ ✦
Psalm 5 · Give ear to my words

My King, and my God

Give ear to my words, O Lord, consider my meditation. Hearken unto the voice of my cry, my King, and my God: for unto thee will I pray.Psalm 5:1-2

The song of Psalm 5 opens again with prayer, asking the Lord to hear and harken to the singer. David, the psalmist, King of Israel calls out to his King and his God.

Many of the Psalms open with this plea, and serve as a model for prayer. "Lord, my God, hear me" is an excellent start any time you go boldly before the Throne. Unto Thee will I pray.

Psalm 5 · In the morning

In the morning

My voice shalt thou hear in the morning, O Lord; in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up.Psalm 5:3

And as Psalm 4 was about praying in the night before sleeping, here in Psalm 5 it is prayer to start the morning. Day and night, we should be in prayer to our Father. Directing our prayer to Him.

After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name. Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven.Matthew 6:9-10
Psalm 5 · Hallowed be His name

Thou hatest all workers of iniquity

For thou art not a God that hath pleasure in wickedness: neither shall evil dwell with thee. The foolish shall not stand in thy sight: thou hatest all workers of iniquity.Psalm 5:4-5

Hallowed be His name. He is holy, and hates the wicked, the foolish, the workers of iniquity.

Psalm 5 · Into thy house

In thy fear will I worship

Thou shalt destroy them that speak leasing: the Lord will abhor the bloody and deceitful man. But as for me, I will come into thy house in the multitude of thy mercy: and in thy fear will I worship toward thy holy temple.Psalm 5:6-7

We see imprecatory cursing of the liars and the bloody deceitful men, followed by the pledge to submit to God's mercy, and in fear worship Him in His house.

Psalm 5 · Lead me

Make thy way straight

Lead me, O Lord, in thy righteousness because of mine enemies; make thy way straight before my face.Psalm 5:8

Then, the plea to show us His will, that the Lord's paths be made known to us, by His righteousness and in the face of our enemies seeking to destroy us, asking that God's will be done.

Psalm 5 · Their throat

An open sepulchre

For there is no faithfulness in their mouth; their inward part is very wickedness; their throat is an open sepulchre; they flatter with their tongue.Psalm 5:9

The enemies of God are wicked, and their throat, an open sepulchre, an open grave, giving off the stench of their own corruption.

Jesus uses this term so effectively in Matthew 23 against the Pharisees.

Cross references • Psalm 5:9

Whited sepulchres

Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness. Even so ye also outwardly appear righteous unto men, but within ye are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.Matthew 23:27-28

He curses these workers of iniquity, in terms they know from scripture. The death their words bring forth to those that follow them and their lies, Jesus will say to them, begone, I never knew you.

Psalm 5 · Cast them out

Destroy thou them, O God

Destroy thou them, O God; let them fall by their own counsels; cast them out in the multitude of their transgressions; for they have rebelled against thee.Psalm 5:10

The Psalmist asks God to destroy these people that rise against the Lord and against truth. To bring them down by their own wicked plans, to cast them out in their rebellion against God.

Psalm 5 · Rejoice

Let them ever shout for joy

But let all those that put their trust in thee rejoice: let them ever shout for joy, because thou defendest them: let them also that love thy name be joyful in thee.Psalm 5:11

But, for those that trust in the Lord, rejoice. Shout for joy! Let those that love His precious name, who love Jesus, forever shout for joy, because the Lord defends us from the wicked. And redeemed us from our sin in His mercy and grace.

Psalm 5 · Thou wilt bless the righteous

As with a shield

For thou, Lord, wilt bless the righteous; with favour wilt thou compass him as with a shield.Psalm 5:12

Amen.
