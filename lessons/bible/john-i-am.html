The Seven “I Am” Figures

And God said unto Moses, I AM THAT I AM: and he said, Thus shalt thou say unto the children of Israel, I AM hath sent me unto you.Exodus 3:14

Jesus uses the misunderstanding motif to declare His divinity in a picture, and each carries the gospel inside it: believe on Him.

The seven “I am” figures

1. The Bread of Life

And Jesus said unto them, I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger; and he that believeth on me shall never thirst.John 6:35

The literal hearer

The Jews therefore strove among themselves, saying, How can this man give us his flesh to eat?John 6:52

Coming is compared to eating; believing to drinking.

The seven “I am” figures

2. The Light of the World

Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.John 8:12

Restated

I am come a light into the world, that whosoever believeth on me should not abide in darkness.John 12:46

The literal hearer

And some of the Pharisees which were with him heard these words, and said unto him, Are we blind also?John 9:40
The seven “I am” figures

3. The Door

I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in and out, and find pasture.John 10:9

The hearers

This parable spake Jesus unto them: but they understood not what things they were which he spake unto them.John 10:6

One way into the fold. They understood not, so He said it again a different way.

The seven “I am” figures

4. The Good Shepherd

I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep.John 10:11

The figure points to the cross

Therefore doth my Father love me, because I lay down my life, that I might take it again. No man taketh it from me, but I lay it down of myself. I have power to lay it down, and I have power to take it again. This commandment have I received of my Father.John 10:17-18
The seven “I am” figures

5. The Resurrection, and the Life

Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live: And whosoever liveth and believeth in me shall never die. Believest thou this?John 11:25-26

The literal hearer, Martha

Martha saith unto him, I know that he shall rise again in the resurrection at the last day.John 11:24

Martha thought of the last day. Jesus pointed her to Himself, the Life standing before her.

The seven “I am” figures

6. The Way, the Truth, and the Life

Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.John 14:6

The literal hearer, Thomas

Thomas saith unto him, Lord, we know not whither thou goest; and how can we know the way?John 14:5

Thomas thought of a road. The way is a Person.

The seven “I am” figures

7. The True Vine

I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman.John 15:1

Joined to Him

I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing.John 15:5

Life and fruit come only by being joined to Him.

The five “I am he” sayings · John 4

1. I That Speak unto Thee Am He

The hearer

The woman saith unto him, I know that Messias cometh, which is called Christ: when he is come, he will tell us all things.John 4:25

His words

Jesus saith unto her, I that speak unto thee am he.John 4:26

The meaning

The woman then left her waterpot, and went her way into the city, and saith to the men, Come, see a man, which told me all things that ever I did: is not this the Christ?John 4:28-29

She looked for a Messiah someday. He was standing in front of her.

The five “I am he” sayings · John 8

2. Ye Shall Die in Your Sins

His words

I said therefore unto you, that ye shall die in your sins: for if ye believe not that I am he, ye shall die in your sins.John 8:24

The hearers, the Jews

Then said they unto him, Who art thou? And Jesus saith unto them, Even the same that I said unto you from the beginning.John 8:25

The meaning

They understood not that he spake to them of the Father.John 8:27

They asked for a name; He had already given it. Believing that He is “I am” is life or death.

The five “I am he” sayings · John 8

3. When Ye Have Lifted Up the Son of Man

His words

Then said Jesus unto them, When ye have lifted up the Son of man, then shall ye know that I am he, and that I do nothing of myself; but as my Father hath taught me, I speak these things.John 8:28

The hearers

As he spake these words, many believed on him.John 8:30

The meaning

And I, if I be lifted up from the earth, will draw all men unto me. This he said, signifying what death he should die.John 12:32-33

The cross would prove who He is.

The five “I am he” sayings · John 13

4. That Ye May Believe That I Am He

The figure

I speak not of you all: I know whom I have chosen: but that the scripture may be fulfilled, He that eateth bread with me hath lifted up his heel against me.John 13:18

His words

Now I tell you before it come, that, when it is come to pass, ye may believe that I am he.John 13:19
The five “I am he” sayings · John 13

4. That Ye May Believe That I Am He, continued

The hearers

Then the disciples looked one on another, doubting of whom he spake.John 13:22

The hearers again

Now no man at the table knew for what intent he spake this unto him.John 13:28

The disciples missed it at the table. Once the betrayal came to pass, the fulfilled word would prove He is “I am.”

The five “I am he” sayings · John 18

5. I Am He

His question

Jesus therefore, knowing all things that should come upon him, went forth, and said unto them, Whom seek ye?John 18:4

The hearers

They answered him, Jesus of Nazareth. Jesus saith unto them, I am he. And Judas also, which betrayed him, stood with them.John 18:5

Their response

As soon then as he had said unto them, I am he, they went backward, and fell to the ground.John 18:6
The five “I am he” sayings · John 18

5. I Am He, continued

The meaning

Jesus answered, I have told you that I am he: if therefore ye seek me, let these go their way: That the saying might be fulfilled, which he spake, Of them which thou gavest me have I lost none.John 18:8-9

They came for a man from Nazareth. At the name of God they fell, and He gave Himself up to keep His own.

John 8

Before Abraham Was, I Am

The final declaratory “I AM” to the Pharisees. Abraham rejoiced to see His day (); the Jews answered, “Thou art not yet fifty years old” ().

Jesus said unto them, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Before Abraham was, I am.John 8:58

Their response

Then took they up stones to cast at him: but Jesus hid himself, and went out of the temple, going through the midst of them, and so passed by.John 8:59

They counted His age in years. He spoke as the eternal God.
