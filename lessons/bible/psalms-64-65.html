Psalm 64 · Hear my voice

Prayer in troubling times

Hear my voice, O God, in my prayer: preserve my life from fear of the enemy. Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked; from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity:Psalm 64:1-2

Psalm 64, a Psalm of David, another entry in the songbook of prayer to God in troubling times.

When we consider the life of David, we see that these two things are true:

Cross references • Psalm 64:2

All that will live godly

Yea, and all that will live godly in Christ Jesus shall suffer persecution. But evil men and seducers shall wax worse and worse, deceiving, and being deceived.2 Timothy 3:12-13

David, the apple of God's eye, a man after God's own heart, had a life of constant tribulation. He had hosts of enemies, foreign and domestic. He sought the will of God most of his life.

Psalm 64 · The life of David

Constant trouble

It wasn't perfect. There are none righteous, no not one. He made mistakes and suffered consequences for them. But largely we find David being sought by the wicked for no reason, under threat, the target of arrows, both literal and the figurative arrows of lies, with and without cause.

He gained the Kingdom he was anointed to, only after years of flight and fighting. Once established, he saw war and invasion, palace intrigue, the loss of his first son with Bathsheba, the betrayal by his son Absalom, the contention as he approached death and appointed the younger son Solomon to the crown...David saw constant trouble while remaining faithful to the end.

Psalm 64 · Hide me

The insurrection of the workers of iniquity

He trusted the Lord God to preserve him in the face of his enemies. To hide him, protecting him from their schemes.

While he maintained his respect for Saul, refusing to lay a hand on God's anointed, leaving judgment in God's hands, he was beset by the insurrection of the workers of iniquity who rose up against him as King.

Psalm 64 · Bitter words

They encourage themselves in an evil matter

Who whet their tongue like a sword, and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words: That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not. They encourage themselves in an evil matter: they commune of laying snares privily; they say, Who shall see them? They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search: both the inward thought of every one of them, and the heart, is deep.Psalm 64:3-6

He was often vexed that the evil men and seducers waxed worse and worse, deceiving, and being deceived, but he was never moved. They would encourage themselves in their evil. They would search out iniquity, diligently. Their hearts deep with the evil they sought for him.

But his faith never wavered.

Psalm 64 · But God shall shoot at them

Their own tongue to fall upon themselves

But God shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly shall they be wounded. So they shall make their own tongue to fall upon themselves: all that see them shall flee away.Psalm 64:7-8

He trusted God to deliver him. To destroy his enemies as He destroyed Goliath, Saul, the Philistines, Amalekites, Ammonites, Arameans/Syrians and Edom. His son's insurrection, the betrayal of Ahithophel, Sheba, son of Bichri who led a rebellion by the Benjaminites, his trusted general Joab and High Priest Abiathar, who both backed David's eldest son, Adonijah over Solomon, even lowly Shimei, son of Gera who cursed David as he fled the revolution of Absalom.

Psalm 64 · Their own end

Each fall by their own devices

These men who whetted their tongues like a sword, sharpening their words like a blade, would fall upon themselves. Their slander and schemes against David would be their own end.

David faced endless betrayal and danger, and through it all sought the face of God, and saw each fall by their own devices while David remained protected by the Lord he called upon.

Psalm 64 · Trust in Him

Surely as He delivered David

And all men shall fear, and shall declare the work of God; for they shall wisely consider of his doing. The righteous shall be glad in the Lord, and shall trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory.Psalm 64:9-10

Psalms like this remind us to place our faith in the Lord. That our eternal soul is already given to Him, bought with a price paid for by Jesus, sealed unto redemption by the Spirit, the earnest of our inheritance. Our spirit crying out to the Father, "Abba!"

If we have trusted God for eternity, how can we fear the temporal slings and arrows of life's outrageous fortune? The vanity of men who seek our destruction? Surely as He delivered David, He will deliver us. We can place our trust in His strong tower, to be gathered under His wing. To be still and know He has us in His hand.

Amen

Psalm 65 · Praise waiteth for thee

Unto thee shall all flesh come

Praise waiteth for thee, O God, in Sion: and unto thee shall the vow be performed. O thou that hearest prayer, unto thee shall all flesh come.Psalm 65:1-2

David's Psalm of praise and glory to God contains doctrine and prophecy. Acknowledging the power of the Lord, the weakness of man, the need for salvation and the blessings on His people, in this life and in eternity.

The praise of Christ, coming into His own in Zion, the promises fulfilled. O thou that hearest prayer, unto thee shall all flesh come. Every knee shall bow and tongue confess, that Jesus is Christ.

Psalm 65 · Iniquities prevail against me

Thou shalt purge them away

Iniquities prevail against me: as for our transgressions, thou shalt purge them away.Psalm 65:3

Iniquities may prevail in our flesh, but our sins will be purged away.

Cross references • Psalm 65:3

Sin that dwelleth in me

For that which I do I allow not: for what I would, that do I not; but what I hate, that do I. If then I do that which I would not, I consent unto the law that it is good. Now then it is no more I that do it, but sin that dwelleth in me. For I know that in me (that is, in my flesh,) dwelleth no good thing: for to will is present with me; but how to perform that which is good I find not. For the good that I would I do not: but the evil which I would not, that I do. Now if I do that I would not, it is no more I that do it, but sin that dwelleth in me.Romans 7:15-20

The flesh at war with the Spirit, gives way to the purging that comes in Christ.

Cross references • Psalm 65:3

O wretched man that I am

O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me from the body of this death? I thank God through Jesus Christ our Lord. So then with the mind I myself serve the law of God; but with the flesh the law of sin.Romans 7:24-25
Psalm 65 · Thy holy temple

Blessed is the man whom thou choosest

Blessed is the man whom thou choosest, and causest to approach unto thee, that he may dwell in thy courts: we shall be satisfied with the goodness of thy house, even of thy holy temple.Psalm 65:4

But blessed is the man who through Jesus Christ our Lord is made to come before Him, into His courts to dwell forever in the goodness of His house, even of His holy temple. His body, given for us.

Psalm 65 · The power of Creation

Which stilleth the noise of the seas

Which by his strength setteth fast the mountains; being girded with power: Which stilleth the noise of the seas, the noise of their waves, and the tumult of the people. They also that dwell in the uttermost parts are afraid at thy tokens: thou makest the outgoings of the morning and evening to rejoice.Psalm 65:6-8

The power of Creation, the Lord of the raging seas. Who calms our hearts as He does the tumult of the storm.

Psalm 65 · Thou visitest the earth

The river of God

Thou visitest the earth, and waterest it: thou greatly enrichest it with the river of God, which is full of water: thou preparest them corn, when thou hast so provided for it. Thou waterest the ridges thereof abundantly: thou settlest the furrows thereof: thou makest it soft with showers: thou blessest the springing thereof.Psalm 65:9-10

The Lord and Savior visited the earth, and streams of living water pour out from Him, the Spirit inside us, ever flowing, the harvest ready.

Cross references • Psalm 65:9

Rivers of living water

He that believeth on me, as the scripture hath said, out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water. (But this spake he of the Spirit, which they that believe on him should receive: for the Holy Ghost was not yet given; because that Jesus was not yet glorified.)John 7:38-39

He is glorified in the resurrection, and made to sit in heavenly places, making the ground fertile and the furrows of man's heart, soft, able to receive the seed of the word, and be made to spring forth from the ground as the fruit of corn.

Psalm 65 · Thou crownest the year

They shout for joy, they also sing

Thou crownest the year with thy goodness; and thy paths drop fatness. They drop upon the pastures of the wilderness: and the little hills rejoice on every side. The pastures are clothed with flocks; the valleys also are covered over with corn; they shout for joy, they also sing.Psalm 65:11-13

He makes the flocks lay down in green pastures, the hills themselves rejoice, the corn is abundant, white for the harvest. That we may glorify God, shouting for joy and singing of the mercies of the Lord forever. Amen
