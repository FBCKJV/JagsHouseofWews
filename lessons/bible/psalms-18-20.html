Psalm 18

My rock, my fortress, and my deliverer

✦ ✦ ✦
Psalm 18 · My strength

I will love thee, O Lord

I will love thee, O Lord, my strength.Psalm 18:1

Psalm 18 is one of the first long-form songs of David, and it reflects his own experience with the Lord, his trust in God, and deliverance from his enemies, while weaving together prophetic views of Jesus, the doctrines of salvation, the end times wrath and Kingdom of God.

Ultimately, the Psalm gives us understanding of Who the Lord is and why He is worthy of our trust, today.

Psalm 18 · Who the Lord is

My rock, my fortress

The Lord is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my God, my strength, in whom I will trust; my buckler, and the horn of my salvation, and my high tower. I will call upon the Lord, who is worthy to be praised: so shall I be saved from mine enemies.Psalm 18:2-3
With the merciful thou wilt shew thyself merciful; with an upright man thou wilt shew thyself upright; With the pure thou wilt shew thyself pure; and with the froward thou wilt shew thyself froward.Psalm 18:25-26

The Lord is our strength. Our rock. Our fortress. Our deliverer. Our strength. Trustworthy. Our buckler, the horn of our salvation, our high tower. He is worthy to be praised. He is merciful to those that are merciful. Upright in all His ways to those that walk in them. Pure to the pure and froward to the froward.

Psalm 18 · The word of the Lord is tried

His way is perfect

As for God, his way is perfect: the word of the Lord is tried: he is a buckler to all those that trust in him.Psalm 18:30

His way is perfect, His word is tried and trusted, and the strength and shield of those that place that trust in Him.

Psalm 18 · No other rock

Who is a rock save our God?

For who is God save the Lord? or who is a rock save our God?Psalm 18:31

There is no other rock than the Lord. There is no other name given but Jesus. He is the fulfillment of the word, the word made flesh, the promise of God come down from Heaven. The Christ. And He liveth.

Psalm 18 · Thy right hand

The shield of thy salvation

Thou hast also given me the shield of thy salvation: and thy right hand hath holden me up, and thy gentleness hath made me great.Psalm 18:35

The shield of salvation is given, by the right-hand of God. His gentleness has made those who trust Him great.

Psalm 18 · My rock

The Lord liveth

The Lord liveth; and blessed be my rock; and let the God of my salvation be exalted.Psalm 18:46
Psalm 18 · A people I have not known

The head of the heathen

Thou hast delivered me from the strivings of the people; and thou hast made me the head of the heathen: a people whom I have not known shall serve me. As soon as they hear of me, they shall obey me: the strangers shall submit themselves unto me.Psalm 18:43-44

The Lord is praised among the heathen. The strangers hear and obey. The people who were not a people are come, and are subdued under the throne of David, to his Seed. To His anointed, evermore.

Psalm 18 · The enemies destroyed

Hail stones and coals of fire

The Lord also thundered in the heavens, and the Highest gave his voice; hail stones and coals of fire. Yea, he sent out his arrows, and scattered them; and he shot out lightnings, and discomfited them.Psalm 18:13-14
They cried, but there was none to save them: even unto the Lord, but he answered them not.Psalm 18:41

The enemies of David, like the enemies of Christ, are crushed before them by the fiery and fierce judgment of God. Rains of fire and brimstone, hail and lightnings, leading the wicked to cry out, and hide themselves from His fury. And ultimately be destroyed by His coming.

Psalm 18 · A rewarder

According to my righteousness

The Lord rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.Psalm 18:20
Therefore hath the Lord recompensed me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in his eyesight.Psalm 18:24

The Lord then is a rewarder of those that trust in Him, who diligently seek Him. Who follow His ways and obey His statutes. He will reward us for the righteousness we have shown, and the recompense for our cleanliness in His sight. The righteousness of Christ imputed on His children. The gold, silver and precious stones we build upon that foundation.

Psalm 18 · He lifts us up

Thou wilt light my candle

For thou wilt light my candle: the Lord my God will enlighten my darkness. For by thee I have run through a troop; and by my God have I leaped over a wall.Psalm 18:28-29

He lifts us up, strengthening us to do great deeds in His name. He fills us with His light.

Psalm 18 · In my distress

He heard my voice

In my distress I called upon the Lord, and cried unto my God: he heard my voice out of his temple, and my cry came before him, even into his ears. Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations also of the hills moved and were shaken, because he was wroth. There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, and fire out of his mouth devoured: coals were kindled by it.Psalm 18:6-8

He hears our cries. He comes to our aid. He destroys the wicked for our sake, in the anger kindled by their sin against Him and their treatment of His children.

Psalm 18 · Thanks

Among the heathen

Therefore will I give thanks unto thee, O Lord, among the heathen, and sing praises unto thy name.Psalm 18:49

Amen

Psalm 18 · Songs of deliverance

The complete picture

Great deliverance giveth he to his king; and sheweth mercy to his anointed, to David, and to his seed for evermore.Psalm 18:50

Songs of deliverance and praise, with the appearance of the Lord as a consuming fire, yet displaying tender mercies to His beloved, those that place their trust in Him, and who call upon the Lord, are found throughout the book of Psalms.

The reassurance to believers that what God has done for David will be done for us, His name and word are worthy, and are to be trusted. The picture of salvation and judgment. All are detailed repeatedly, just as much as songs of despair and pleas for God's intervention. But both styles of song contain thanksgiving and praise for His grace and mercy, and all the blessings He has already provided those that love Him.

Taken as a whole, Psalms gives us the complete picture of the Christian life. They give us a model of prayer and praise that fits every stage of our growth and development, while also dripping with doctrine, teaching us the truth of God.

Psalm 19

The heavens declare • The law of the LORD is perfect

Psalm 19 · The testimony of creation

Without excuse

Psalm 19 is fairly short, yet packed with doctrine, teaching the purpose of creation, the testimony of God to all creatures, the picture of Christ, and the establishment of the law of the Lord in nature itself, that there is no excuse, while the believer is given constant reminders of His judgements.

For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men, who hold the truth in unrighteousness; Because that which may be known of God is manifest in them; for God hath shewed it unto them. For the invisible things of him from the creation of the world are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made, even his eternal power and Godhead; so that they are without excuse:Romans 1:18-20
Psalm 19 · Day unto day

The heavens declare the glory of God

The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament sheweth his handywork. Day unto day uttereth speech, and night unto night sheweth knowledge.Psalm 19:1-2

The heavens declare the glory of God. Looking upon the night sky we see His precision and power. And as the day follows day and night follows night, each with the passing of the sun and the stars, we are given evidence of His might.

Psalm 19 · Their line is gone out

No speech nor language

There is no speech nor language, where their voice is not heard. Their line is gone out through all the earth, and their words to the end of the world. In them hath he set a tabernacle for the sun,Psalm 19:3-4

The things of creation speak without words, that all man may understand. They don't need a language. They don't need to be explained. Man sees the passage of time, seasons, years, decades even, in heavenly bodies.

The vain hypothetical of a savage on a remote island, never hearing the gospel, is undone by the understanding that all nature is a parable and a testament of God, showing men truth.

Psalm 19 · The sun

As a bridegroom

Which is as a bridegroom coming out of his chamber, and rejoiceth as a strong man to run a race. His going forth is from the end of the heaven, and his circuit unto the ends of it: and there is nothing hid from the heat thereof.Psalm 19:5-6

The boldness of the sun breaking forth in the morning is as the risen Christ, returning in glory, a picture of the Creator Who is blessed forever, amen.

Nature teaches us God's law. His ways.

Doth not even nature itself teach you, that, if a man have long hair, it is a shame unto him?1 Corinthians 11:14
Cross references • Psalm 19:1–6

Written in their hearts

His wrath is revealed against those who sin, even without the oracles of God documenting His commandments. The Spirit showing mankind the results of their transgressions. The understanding that murder, adultery, lying, stealing, all of these things that do harm to your neighbor and are a shame before God, are known.

For there is no respect of persons with God. For as many as have sinned without law shall also perish without law: and as many as have sinned in the law shall be judged by the law; (For not the hearers of the law are just before God, but the doers of the law shall be justified. For when the Gentiles, which have not the law, do by nature the things contained in the law, these, having not the law, are a law unto themselves: Which shew the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience also bearing witness, and their thoughts the mean while accusing or else excusing one another;) In the day when God shall judge the secrets of men by Jesus Christ according to my gospel.Romans 2:11-16
Cross references • Psalm 19:7

The oracles of God

Man can see the law of God, know it written in their hearts, watch the outcome of their behavior and the wrath upon those who sin, and know they are guilty before the consuming fire which is God.

How much more are we who have His statutes and commandments by the oracles of God?

What advantage then hath the Jew? or what profit is there of circumcision? Much every way: chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God.Romans 3:1-2

The advantage given Israel, and held by the Jews in Paul's day, is now given to the world. That everyone may know His word.

Psalm 19 · Converting the soul

The law of the Lord is perfect

The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.Psalm 19:7

If the day declares His righteousness and the night His knowledge, and the very nature of things speaks to His wrath, then the oracles of God give us the hope of His mercy. God is a rewarder to those that diligently seek Him.

Psalm 19 · The statutes of the Lord

The fear of the Lord is clean

The statutes of the Lord are right, rejoicing the heart: the commandment of the Lord is pure, enlightening the eyes. The fear of the Lord is clean, enduring for ever: the judgments of the Lord are true and righteous altogether.Psalm 19:8-9

The fear of the Lord is clean, and known to all men. The fool may say in their heart there is no God, but the wise fear Him, even if they don't know scripture.

Psalm 19 · Great reward

Sweeter than honey

More to be desired are they than gold, yea, than much fine gold: sweeter also than honey and the honeycomb. Moreover by them is thy servant warned: and in keeping of them there is great reward.Psalm 19:10-11

The truth is more desirable than gold, sweeter than honey. Its pursuit is its own reward, spurring us to seek Him. The warnings and rewards, motivation to learn His ways.

And, in that knowledge, we find His word, and from that word, salvation.

Psalm 19 · Presumptuous sins

Cleanse thou me

Who can understand his errors? cleanse thou me from secret faults. Keep back thy servant also from presumptuous sins; let them not have dominion over me: then shall I be upright, and I shall be innocent from the great transgression.Psalm 19:12-13

In Him we are cleansed from our iniquity. In Him we are conformed to His image, kept back from presumptuous sin. Made upright. His righteousness imputed on us that we may be transformed. To be our strength and the Lord our Redeemer.

Application • Creation

All creation is a parable

We are blessed to have the knowledge of the truth before us. His inspired and preserved word, that we may know Him. And God does not leave those without the oracles lacking.

But for the children of God, creation gives us reassurance and opportunities to worship Him for His might and glory. To have reminders that He loves us.

To every seed we sow that comes to fruition. To every shooting star, or bird in flight, or wave crashing on the shore, we are able to look at the Lord in awe. Babies born to the death of the elderly, spring following winter, the setting sun and darkest night...all creation is a parable to those with ears to hear and eyes to see.

Application • Creation

New creatures in Christ

To know God loves us. And His mercy upon us is evidence to others seeking truth. The light in us shines that others may see and glorify God.

We are to be as the firmament, as the rising sun and the crashing wave. His children are meant to teach the heathen who seeks the Lord, as much as anything in creation. Because we are new creatures in Christ, brought forth as a testimony to God's greatness.

Psalm 19 · The words of my mouth

My strength, and my redeemer

Let the words of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be acceptable in thy sight, O Lord, my strength, and my redeemer.Psalm 19:14

Amen

The name of the LORD our God

Psalm 20 · Out of Zion

The name of the God of Jacob

The Lord hear thee in the day of trouble; the name of the God of Jacob defend thee; Send thee help from the sanctuary, and strengthen thee out of Zion; Remember all thy offerings, and accept thy burnt sacrifice; Selah.Psalm 20:1-3

Psalm 20 is yet another song of praise and worship for the salvation that is in Christ Jesus.

The name of the God of Jacob. The Lord that strengthens us out of Zion. The one offering forever, the burnt sacrifice. Christ Jesus.

Psalm 20 · Thy counsel

Fulfil all thy counsel

Grant thee according to thine own heart, and fulfil all thy counsel.Psalm 20:4

The love of God has been granted to us in his grace, and his counsel is wisdom; trust in the Lord.

Psalm 20 · In the name of our God

We will set up our banners

We will rejoice in thy salvation, and in the name of our God we will set up our banners: the Lord fulfil all thy petitions.Psalm 20:5

We will rejoice in the salvation given to us in the name of our God. We will set up banners in His holy name. He is Christ Jesus, and God hears all our petitions, lifted up in His name.

Psalm 20 · His right hand

The Lord saveth his anointed

Now know I that the Lord saveth his anointed; he will hear him from his holy heaven with the saving strength of his right hand.Psalm 20:6

The Lord saves His anointed. He did not leave his soul to see corruption. His anointed was not left in hell. The saving strength of His right hand. Christ Jesus.

Psalm 20 · We will remember the name

Some trust in chariots

Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will remember the name of the Lord our God. They are brought down and fallen: but we are risen, and stand upright.Psalm 20:7-8

Some trust in the power of this world, their own strength, their own works. And they will be brought down low. He will say depart from me. I never knew you.

But to those of us who trust in the name of the Lord, our God, we are risen and made to stand upright. In Christ Jesus.

Psalm 20 · Save, Lord

Save, Lord

Save, Lord: let the king hear us when we call.Psalm 20:9

Save, Lord. In the name of the King of Kings and the Lord of lords, Christ Jesus.

Psalm 20 · Some trust in chariots

Horses and chariots

Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will remember the name of the Lord our God.Psalm 20:7

This verse is often quoted, and has inspired generations of believers. But in practice it can be difficult. We often seek reassurance from our material, wealth and position.

David, writing this Psalm with dual purpose (at least), the prophetic imagery of Christ Jesus and the surety of his salvation, but also as an intercessional prayer before a battle against a well-armed and equipped foe.

Application • Trust

We have already overcome

David's showing us that it is better to trust in the Lord than all the horses and chariots in the world. That in our battles, even if we are seemingly overmatched by our enemy, we are still victorious in God.

What a comfort. What a blessing. That we can stand fast, and be sure of our deliverance. That we can trust in the Lord in all things, because we've first trusted in Him, our eternal security.

When we are weak we are made strong. God uses us to confound the wise. We have already overcome in Christ. And we should rejoice in our salvation.
