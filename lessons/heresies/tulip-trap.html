Lesson 1

The Wrong Question

Why the Limited vs. Unlimited Debate Misses the Point

  • Both Calvinism and Arminianism confine the atonement to the question of individual election/salvation.
  • Scripture presents a cosmic, triumphant reconciliation already accomplished (; ), rendering the debate moot.

Key: Christ’s work is bigger than either system allows.

Already accomplished

And, having made peace through the blood of his cross, by him to reconcile all things unto himself; by him, I say, whether they be things in earth, or things in heaven.Colossians 1:20
To wit, that God was in Christ, reconciling the world unto himself, not imputing their trespasses unto them; and hath committed unto us the word of reconciliation.2 Corinthians 5:19
Lesson 2

Total Depravity

Biblical Truth Without Calvinistic Inability

Affirm

Man is dead in sins, none seek God, totally affected by sin (; ).

Reject the premise

This does not mean moral inability to believe the gospel when convicted by the Spirit through the Word (; ; ; ; ).

The gospel is the power of God unto salvation—the Word quickens, the Spirit convicts all who hear, and whosoever will may come.

The power of God unto salvation

So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.Romans 10:17
For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.Romans 1:16
And when he is come, he will reprove the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment:John 16:8
Lesson 3

Unconditional Election

The Biblical Doctrine of Election is Not About Individual Salvation

  • Election in Scripture is primarily corporate (in Christ), to service, holiness, and inheritance (; understood as conformity to Christ).
  • Reject the premise that God arbitrarily chooses some for salvation and passes over others before creation.
  • God desires all to be saved (; ); the gospel is a genuine offer to all.

God desires all to be saved

Who will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth.1 Timothy 2:4
The Lord is not slack concerning his promise, as some men count slackness; but is longsuffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.2 Peter 3:9
Lesson 4

Irresistible Grace

vs. the Resistible Drawing of the Spirit Through the Word

  • The Spirit convicts the world (), but men can resist (; ).
  • Grace is not an internal overpowering but the external provision of Christ and the gospel call—effective when believed, resisted when rejected.

Faith comes by hearing; the power is in the message, not a secret inward work only on the elect.

Men can resist

And when he is come, he will reprove the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment:John 16:8
Ye stiffnecked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, ye do always resist the Holy Ghost: as your fathers did, so do ye.Acts 7:51
Lesson 5

Perseverance of the Saints

The Biblical Truth vs. the Calvinist/Arminian Counterfeit

Calvinist "P"

Teaches that true believers will persevere in holiness/works as evidence of election—often leading to doubt and introspection.

Arminians

Accept the same premise (real saints must endure in faith/works) but conclude salvation can be lost—also breeding fear.

Lesson 5

Biblical truth

Biblical truth: Once a person believes, God keeps them eternally secure by His power (; ; ; ).

And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand. My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father’s hand.John 10:28-29
Lesson 5

Enduring to the end

Passages about "enduring to the end" (; Rev. 2–3) refer to physical deliverance through tribulation/persecution for kingdom inheritance, not eternal salvation.

True believers are sealed and kept forever; fruit follows naturally but is not the root of assurance. No back-door works salvation.

In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise, Which is the earnest of our inheritance until the redemption of the purchased possession, unto the praise of his glory.Ephesians 1:13-14
Conclusion lesson

Once Saved, Always Saved

The Simple Biblical Gospel

  • Salvation is by grace through faith alone, not of works ().
  • Eternal life is a present possession the moment one believes (; ).
  • God finishes what He starts (); Christ ever lives to intercede.

Not of works

For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.Ephesians 2:8-9
Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation; but is passed from death unto life.John 5:24
Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ:Philippians 1:6

Rest in the finished work
