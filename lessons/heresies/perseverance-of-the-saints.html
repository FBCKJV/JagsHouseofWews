How do you know you're saved?

When asked "How do you know you're saved?", many religious systems answer with uncertainty:

  • "If I persevere to the end..."
  • "If I continue in faith and good works..."
  • "If I keep the sacraments and don't fall away..."

Their assurance rests on what they have done or will do—always looking inward at performance.

He did it all

But the Bible-believing Christian answers differently:

  • "Because Jesus died for my sins and rose again—He did it all!"
  • "Because God, who cannot lie, promised everlasting life the moment I believed."
In hope of eternal life, which God, that cannot lie, promised before the world began;Titus 1:2
Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation; but is passed from death unto life.John 5:24

Ye may know

Today we'll expose the false doctrine of "perseverance" taught in many theologies, contrast it with biblical Once Saved, Always Saved in context, and rejoice in the objective, finished work of Christ that ye MAY KNOW we have eternal life by faith alone.

What Other Theologies Teach About "Perseverance of the Saints"

Calvinism/Reformed Theology

True saints will persevere in holiness and good works as inevitable evidence of election. If you don't endure in faithfulness, you were never truly saved. (Tied to their "P" in TULIP)

Arminianism/Wesleyan Theology

Believers can lose salvation if they don't persevere in faith and obedience; "enduring to the end" is required to be finally saved.

Rome and Luther

Roman Catholicism

Salvation is maintained through sacraments, cooperation with grace, and avoiding mortal sin—no full assurance until death. In fact, Catholicism officially teaches that claiming to know with 100% certainty that you are in a state of grace (and will persevere to the end) is the sin of presumption against the Holy Spirit.

Lutheranism (varying views)

Some emphasize perseverance through Word and Sacrament; assurance is hoped for but often tied to ongoing faith/repentance.

Common thread

Common thread: Assurance is subjective—based on personal endurance, fruit, or continued performance. No one can fully know until the end.

What "Perseverance" Actually Means in Context

These phrases are not about eternal salvation but physical deliverance/safety through tribulation and persecution for kingdom inheritance and rewards.

But he that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved.Matthew 24:13

The Olivet Discourse

Context: Olivet Discourse about the Great Tribulation (). "Saved" here means delivered physically/safely from tribulation, the persecution of believers in this world (as in ).

For then shall be great tribulation, such as was not since the beginning of the world to this time, no, nor ever shall be. And except those days should be shortened, there should no flesh be saved: but for the elect’s sake those days shall be shortened.Matthew 24:21-22

Revelation 2–3 (letters to churches, not individual people!): "Overcometh" and hold fast for rewards, crowns, and ruling with Christ—not gaining or losing salvation.

Biblical perseverance

Biblical perseverance for believers today: Standing firm in faith amid trials, but eternal security is guaranteed by God's power, not ours.

Blessed is the man that endureth temptation: for when he is tried, he shall receive the crown of life, which the Lord hath promised to them that love him.James 1:12
Whom resist stedfast in the faith, knowing that the same afflictions are accomplished in your brethren that are in the world.1 Peter 5:9

Guaranteed by God's power

And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand. My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father’s hand.John 10:28-29
Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ:Philippians 1:6

Probation, not justification

The modern demand to "repent of your sins" (turn from all sin to be saved or stay saved) confuses biblical repentance (a change of mind) with works—it turns the gospel into probation (ongoing performance required) instead of instant justification by faith alone.

But to him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, his faith is counted for righteousness.Romans 4:5
For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.Ephesians 2:8-9

God Himself "repents"

God Himself "repents" multiple times in Scripture (over a dozen), yet He cannot sin—proving repentance means a change of mind or course, not turning from sin.

Who can tell if God will turn and repent, and turn away from his fierce anger, that we perish not? And God saw their works, that they turned from their evil way; and God repented of the evil, that he had said that he would do unto them; and he did it not.Jonah 3:9-10
And it repented the Lord that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him at his heart.Genesis 6:6
And the Lord repented of the evil which he thought to do unto his people.Exodus 32:14

Sinners repent about Christ

Sinners repent (change mind) about Christ—believing the gospel—not promising to clean up their life first or maintain it afterward.

The leaven of the Pharisees

It breeds fruit inspectors and holier-than-thou attitudes—the leaven of the Pharisees, which Jesus directly calls hypocrisy.

Then Jesus said unto them, Take heed and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees.Matthew 16:6
Then understood they how that he bade them not beware of the leaven of bread, but of the doctrine of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees.Matthew 16:12

(The leaven is hypocrisy and false doctrine—)

I am not as other men are

The Pharisee stood and prayed thus with himself, God, I thank thee, that I am not as other men are, extortioners, unjust, adulterers, or even as this publican. I fast twice in the week, I give tithes of all that I possess.Luke 18:11-12

It's hypocritical back-door works salvation:

  • Claims "grace alone" but ties assurance to ongoing effort.
  • Makes perseverance the root instead of the fruit—turning the gospel upside down.

Good works aren't automatic

Good works aren't automatic—if you don't walk in the Spirit, don't expect to do the works God has appointed for you ().

  • True fruit is primarily the others we bring to Christ with the gospel (; ).
  • Works will be rewarded at the Judgment Seat, but even if they are wood, hay, stubble—you yourself are still saved.
If any man’s work shall be burned, he shall suffer loss: but he himself shall be saved; yet so as by fire.1 Corinthians 3:15

But not before God

Men may justify us by outward works, but not before God.

Ye see then how that by works a man is justified, and not by faith only.James 2:24

(Context: justified before men, —"shew me thy faith")

What shall we say then that Abraham our father, as pertaining to the flesh, hath found? For if Abraham were justified by works, he hath whereof to glory; but not before God.Romans 4:1-2

How It Creates Doubt

  • For the seeking sinner: "I must turn from sins, make Jesus Lord, and persevere" becomes vague, hopeless, and impossible—leading to trust in self instead of Christ.
  • Lordship Salvation / "repent of your sins" / "make Jesus Lord of your life" is subjective and undefined—what sin level disqualifies? How much commitment is enough?
  • Keeps people from simple trust: They never rest because they're always inspecting fruit or fearing failure.

Robs Believers of Joy

For the believer: Constant doubt—"Have I endured enough? Is my faith real?"—steals peace and assurance God intended.

Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ:Romans 5:1
That their hearts might be comforted, being knit together in love, and unto all riches of the full assurance of understanding, to the acknowledgement of the mystery of God, and of the Father, and of Christ;Colossians 2:2

A present possession

Salvation is received the moment you believe—eternal life is a present possession.

Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation; but is passed from death unto life.John 5:24
Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that believeth on me hath everlasting life.John 6:47

Sealed

Assurance is objective: Anchored in God's unbreakable promises and Christ's resurrection, not our performance.

In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise, Which is the earnest of our inheritance until the redemption of the purchased possession, unto the praise of his glory.Ephesians 1:13-14
And grieve not the holy Spirit of God, whereby ye are sealed unto the day of redemption.Ephesians 4:30

Kept by the power of God

Wherefore he is able also to save them to the uttermost that come unto God by him, seeing he ever liveth to make intercession for them.Hebrews 7:25
Who are kept by the power of God through faith unto salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.1 Peter 1:5
Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy,Jude 24

Commanded to know

We are commanded to know we have eternal life—not hope or examine fruit.

These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life, and that ye may believe on the name of the Son of God.1 John 5:13

Fruit follows naturally from abiding in Christ (John 15 ↗), but it's never the basis of assurance.

Rest in the Sure Promises of God

  • Reject vague, hopeless demands ("make Jesus Lord," "repent of sins," "persevere in works").
  • Trust the simple, objective gospel: Christ died for your sins, rose again, and gives eternal life freely to whosoever believeth.
For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures:1 Corinthians 15:3-4

Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ

And they said, Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house.Acts 16:31
For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come, Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.Romans 8:38-39

How do you know you're saved? Because God said it, and He cannot lie.

Jesus' Unbreakable Promise of Eternal Security

All that the Father giveth me shall come to me; and him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out. For I came down from heaven, not to do mine own will, but the will of him that sent me. And this is the Father’s will which hath sent me, that of all which he hath given me I should lose nothing, but should raise it up again at the last day. And this is the will of him that sent me, that every one which seeth the Son, and believeth on him, may have everlasting life: and I will raise him up at the last day.John 6:37-40
No man can come to me, except the Father which hath sent me draw him: and I will raise him up at the last day.John 6:44
Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that believeth on me hath everlasting life.John 6:47

In no wise cast out

  1. Raised up at the last dayJesus promises four times in this chapter that He will raise up every believer at the last day—it's the Father's will, and Christ guarantees He will lose nothing.
  2. Never rejected"In no wise cast out" means once you come to Him by faith, you're never rejected or lost—eternal security from the Lord's own mouth.
  3. Do it todayIf you've never trusted Christ alone—do it today. Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved.

Believer—stop doubting.

Don’t let the hypocrites and the holier-than-thous make you doubt your salvation. Rejoice in your eternal security and live for the One who saved you completely!
