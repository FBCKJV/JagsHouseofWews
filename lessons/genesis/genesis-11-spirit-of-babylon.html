Genesis 11 • Revelation 17

The Spirit of Babel

Adultery. Fornication. Whoredoms.

The study of Revelation 17 and the Whore of Babylon, and the fall of that wicked city, has a lot of context coming from scripture: concepts regarding the faithfulness of God, the betrothal to His people and the eventual marriage of the Lamb. There are many chapters and verses, but these should give you some ideas of this major theme throughout the Bible.

The spirit of Babel, which is Babylon, and the empires and kingdoms that followed it, is one of adultery. Fornication. Whoredoms.

A Symbol of All Works-Based False Religion

The Tower, Its Destruction, and the End Times

The Tower of Babel is a symbol of all works-based false religion, its destruction a picture of the end times. Just as Babylon the empire, and Nebuchadnezzar and his idol, are a picture of the beast and his abomination. Harlots, taking from God what is His. Leading man to fornication.

This spirit of Babylon and its whore of self-righteous works religion, bowing to idols, worshipping the created more than the Creator, is fornication.
