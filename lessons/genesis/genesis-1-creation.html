vv. 1–2 · The Foundation

In the Beginning

In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth. And the earth was without form, and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep. And the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters.Genesis 1:1-2
Cross references · Genesis 1:1–2

The Word & Spirit at Creation

  • The eternal Son, the Word, was present at and the active agent of all creation. “Without him was not any thing made” leaves no exception.
  • Christ created all things and presently holds all things together. He is not just Creator, but Sustainer.
  • The Son is the exact image of God and the instrument through whom the worlds were made.
Cross references · Genesis 1:1–2

The Word & Spirit at Creation

  • Word and Breath (Spirit), both active in creation. Both present in Genesis 1:1–2 before a single creative act takes place.

Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, all three present in the opening two verses of Scripture. The Bible does not begin with monotheism and slowly develop a Trinity. The Triune God is there from the very first word.

Cross references · Genesis 1:26

Jesus in Genesis 1 — “Let Us Make Man in Our Image”

  • The Plural. “Us / Our”: God speaks in the plural within Himself. Man is made in God’s image, not angels’. This cannot be God addressing angels. This is the Triune God deliberating within Himself.
  • John 1:1, 14. The One who said “Let us make man in our image” later became the Man, taking on the very nature He designed.
  • Man was created by Christ and for Christ. The Creator later became the Redeemer of what He made.
Cross references · Genesis 1:26

Jesus in Genesis 1 — “Let Us Make Man in Our Image”

  • Wisdom personified, present with God at creation, rejoicing. A picture of the pre-incarnate Son (cf. , ).
  • Quoting . The writer of Hebrews quotes Psalm 102 and applies it directly to the Son. The Father calls Christ the Creator of heaven and earth.
  • “By whom are all things, and we by him.” Paul includes our very existence as coming through Christ the Creator.
Cross references · Genesis 1:26

Creation and Redemption

“Let us make man” is not a detail to pass over. The Son who spoke those words in eternity past is the same Son who took on that image in Bethlehem (), died for those who bore it, and will one day present a redeemed humanity perfectly conformed to His own glorious image (). Creation and redemption are one unbroken story.

And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested from all his work which God created and made.Genesis 2:1-3
