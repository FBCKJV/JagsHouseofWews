vv. 1–3

God Remembered Noah

The Fountains of the Deep and the Windows of Heaven Stopped

And God remembered Noah, and every living thing, and all the cattle that was with him in the ark: and God made a wind to pass over the earth, and the waters asswaged; The fountains also of the deep and the windows of heaven were stopped, and the rain from heaven was restrained; And the waters returned from off the earth continually: and after the end of the hundred and fifty days the waters were abated.Genesis 8:1-3

Noah and his ark of survivors ride out the flood, and the deluge abates, as God closes up the windows of heaven and the fountains of the deep.

vv. 4–5

The Ark Rested

Upon the Mountains of Ararat

And the ark rested in the seventh month, on the seventeenth day of the month, upon the mountains of Ararat. And the waters decreased continually until the tenth month: in the tenth month, on the first day of the month, were the tops of the mountains seen.Genesis 8:4-5

The final resting place for the ark, in Turkey: Mt. Ararat. So many “In Search Of”s and History channel specials claim to have found the ark, but we know everything we need to from scripture.

vv. 6–9

The Raven and the Dove

The Dove Found No Rest for the Sole of Her Foot

And it came to pass at the end of forty days, that Noah opened the window of the ark which he had made: And he sent forth a raven, which went forth to and fro, until the waters were dried up from off the earth. Also he sent forth a dove from him, to see if the waters were abated from off the face of the ground; But the dove found no rest for the sole of her foot, and she returned unto him into the ark, for the waters were on the face of the whole earth: then he put forth his hand, and took her, and pulled her in unto him into the ark.Genesis 8:6-9
vv. 10–12

An Olive Leaf Pluckt Off

Nothing Is Incidental in Scripture

And he stayed yet other seven days; and again he sent forth the dove out of the ark; And the dove came in to him in the evening; and, lo, in her mouth was an olive leaf pluckt off: so Noah knew that the waters were abated from off the earth. And he stayed yet other seven days; and sent forth the dove; which returned not again unto him any more.Genesis 8:10-12

The raven and the doves sent by Noah, and the olive branch the dove returns with, are taught in every Sunday school: that God offered an olive branch of peace to mankind. It reads a bit more into it than is there, but maybe. Nothing is incidental, accidental or coincidental in scripture.

vv. 13–14

The Six Hundredth and First Year

A Hard Record of Events

And it came to pass in the six hundredth and first year, in the first month, the first day of the month, the waters were dried up from off the earth: and Noah removed the covering of the ark, and looked, and, behold, the face of the ground was dry. And in the second month, on the seven and twentieth day of the month, was the earth dried.Genesis 8:13-14

Noah’s 601st birthday is marked while waiting to get God’s blessing to disembark. The times given are details that mark this account as historical rather than symbolic or allegory. God rarely gives precise measures of time or place unless it is to create a hard record of events in scripture. So we know the days and the weeks and the months of the flood and the aftermath.

vv. 15–19

Go Forth of the Ark

Be Fruitful, and Multiply upon the Earth

And God spake unto Noah, saying, Go forth of the ark, thou, and thy wife, and thy sons, and thy sons’ wives with thee. Bring forth with thee every living thing that is with thee, of all flesh, both of fowl, and of cattle, and of every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth; that they may breed abundantly in the earth, and be fruitful, and multiply upon the earth. And Noah went forth, and his sons, and his wife, and his sons’ wives with him: Every beast, every creeping thing, and every fowl, and whatsoever creepeth upon the earth, after their kinds, went forth out of the ark.Genesis 8:15-19
v. 20

Noah Builded an Altar

Of Every Clean Beast, and of Every Clean Fowl

And Noah builded an altar unto the Lord; and took of every clean beast, and of every clean fowl, and offered burnt offerings on the altar.Genesis 8:20

Noah settled in, the ark unloaded, makes sacrifice to God. An act of incredible faith, given the limited number of animals to survive the flood, and it is pleasing unto God.

vv. 21–22

The Lord Smelled a Sweet Savour

While the Earth Remaineth

And the Lord smelled a sweet savour; and the Lord said in his heart, I will not again curse the ground any more for man’s sake; for the imagination of man’s heart is evil from his youth; neither will I again smite any more every thing living, as I have done. While the earth remaineth, seedtime and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease.Genesis 8:21-22

God gives us insight to His thought process, recognizing the evil imagination within man’s heart, and vows never to destroy every living thing because of it. Rather than curse the ground and total destruction of life, God vows to preserve the world as is until the end.

Application • Genesis 8:22

Environmentalist Panic

Seedtime and Harvest Shall Not Cease

This is why environmentalist panic is unbiblical nonsense. We are not going to see cataclysmic climate change of any type before the day of the Lord.

I wonder sometimes if the entire point of climate panic is to make those alive during the tribulation and into the wrath of God doubt His hand in it. I think it’s foolish cope, but there probably will be some deluded souls, even as the locusts of Hell bite them in the darkness, blaming CO₂ emissions.

While the Earth Remaineth

Noah gave thanks on dry ground, and God promised: while the earth remaineth, seedtime and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease. God will preserve the world as is until the end.
