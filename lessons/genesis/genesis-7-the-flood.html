“Come thou and all thy house”

And the Lord said unto Noah, Come thou and all thy house into the ark; for thee have I seen righteous before me in this generation. Of every clean beast thou shalt take to thee by sevens, the male and his female: and of beasts that are not clean by two, the male and his female. Of fowls also of the air by sevens, the male and the female; to keep seed alive upon the face of all the earth. For yet seven days, and I will cause it to rain upon the earth forty days and forty nights; and every living substance that I have made will I destroy from off the face of the earth. And Noah did according unto all that the Lord commanded him.Genesis 7:1-5
  • God calls Noah in: “for thee have I seen righteous before me in this generation.” Just Noah, who found grace in the eyes of the Lord.
  • Seven days of warning, then forty days and forty nights of rain.
  • “And Noah did according unto all that the Lord commanded him.” Faith obeys.

And they went in unto Noah into the ark, two and two of all flesh, wherein is the breath of life.Genesis 7:13-15

And the Lord shut him in

Noah built the ark, but God closed the door. Those inside were kept by the Lord, and those outside were not let in.

Windows of heaven, fountains of the deep

In the six hundredth year of Noah’s life, in the second month, the seventeenth day of the month, the same day were all the fountains of the great deep broken up, and the windows of heaven were opened. And the rain was upon the earth forty days and forty nights.Genesis 7:11-12

Waters pouring in from the skies like through open windows, and up from the deep like a fountain.

The waters prevailed

And the flood was forty days upon the earth; and the waters increased, and bare up the ark, and it was lift up above the earth. And the waters prevailed, and were increased greatly upon the earth; and the ark went upon the face of the waters. And the waters prevailed exceedingly upon the earth; and all the high hills, that were under the whole heaven, were covered. Fifteen cubits upward did the waters prevail; and the mountains were covered.Genesis 7:17-20

“All the high hills, that were under the whole heaven, were covered.” A global flood.

All flesh died

And all flesh died that moved upon the earth, both of fowl, and of cattle, and of beast, and of every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth, and every man: All in whose nostrils was the breath of life, of all that was in the dry land, died. And every living substance was destroyed which was upon the face of the ground, both man, and cattle, and the creeping things, and the fowl of the heaven; and they were destroyed from the earth: and Noah only remained alive, and they that were with him in the ark.Genesis 7:21-23

God’s judgment came upon the wicked and the scoffers, and the corrupt earth tainted with the blood of violence and sin. All flesh died, and just Noah was saved, with his sons and their wives, along with the animals of the earth.

The Bible says it’s true

There are archeologies and secular histories that all confirm a global flood, relegated to “myth” status but wondered about by natural men, seeking to confirm the story by natural means.

The face of the earth

Fossils of sea life found on top of Everest, and deposits of salt in high plateaus. The deluge changing the face of the earth in grand canyons and fiords and river beds.

One family of man

The common descent of every nation of men, traced back to the sons of Noah.

But the Bible says it’s true, and that’s enough. The science of man comes around to confirming the accounts of scripture, not the other way around.

Willingly ignorant

Knowing this first, that there shall come in the last days scoffers, walking after their own lusts, And saying, Where is the promise of his coming? for since the fathers fell asleep, all things continue as they were from the beginning of the creation. For this they willingly are ignorant of, that by the word of God the heavens were of old, and the earth standing out of the water and in the water: Whereby the world that then was, being overflowed with water, perished:2 Peter 3:3-6

Scoffers laugh at the idea of Biblical fundamentalism, and some believers take it personally and are embarrassed by the word of God. We shouldn’t be moved. Rather, those scoffers should be embarrassed by themselves and their own foolish hearts.

Ready always to give an answer

And who is he that will harm you, if ye be followers of that which is good? But and if ye suffer for righteousness’ sake, happy are ye: and be not afraid of their terror, neither be troubled; But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts: and be ready always to give an answer to every man that asketh you a reason of the hope that is in you with meekness and fear: Having a good conscience; that, whereas they speak evil of you, as of evildoers, they may be ashamed that falsely accuse your good conversation in Christ. For it is better, if the will of God be so, that ye suffer for well doing, than for evil doing.1 Peter 3:13-17
  • Be not afraid of their terror, neither be troubled.
  • Sanctify the Lord God in your hearts, and answer with meekness and fear.
  • Keep a good conscience, that they who speak evil of you may be ashamed.

Eight souls were saved by water

For Christ also hath once suffered for sins, the just for the unjust, that he might bring us to God, being put to death in the flesh, but quickened by the Spirit: By which also he went and preached unto the spirits in prison; Which sometime were disobedient, when once the longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah, while the ark was a preparing, wherein few, that is, eight souls were saved by water. The like figure whereunto even baptism doth also now save us (not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good conscience toward God,) by the resurrection of Jesus Christ:1 Peter 3:18-21

Christ in triumph

Jesus was triumphantly shown in Hell before those that didn’t believe, preaching His glory, leading captivity captive.

The disobedient

The scoffers and unbelievers who mocked God, while the longsuffering of God waited, were swallowed up in the flood.

The Lord knoweth how to deliver

For if God spared not the angels that sinned, but cast them down to hell, and delivered them into chains of darkness, to be reserved unto judgment; And spared not the old world, but saved Noah the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly; And turning the cities of Sodom and Gomorrha into ashes condemned them with an overthrow, making them an ensample unto those that after should live ungodly; And delivered just Lot, vexed with the filthy conversation of the wicked: (For that righteous man dwelling among them, in seeing and hearing, vexed his righteous soul from day to day with their unlawful deeds;) The Lord knoweth how to deliver the godly out of temptations, and to reserve the unjust unto the day of judgment to be punished:2 Peter 2:4-9

Noah

A preacher of righteousness, saved with his house while the world of the ungodly was destroyed.

Lot

Just Lot, vexed by the filthy conversation of the wicked, delivered before the fire fell on Sodom and Gomorrha.

The faith of Noah

By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house; by the which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith.Hebrews 11:7

We are called to have the faith of Noah, who, having the grace of God extended to him, and faith in the Christ to come, believed God’s promise of salvation. Building the ark, diligently and meekly, obeying the Lord. Vexed, like Lot, by the sinful fallen world around him. Yet Noah stayed true, and God delivered him and destroyed the wicked, just as He will in the great day of the Lord.
