A study in Genesis 6

Sons of God

Who took the daughters of men to wife, and why it matters that God judged man.

A disagreement among brethren

Who the sons of God were in Genesis 6 is a tertiary doctrine. It does not touch salvation, and faithful believers who love the King James Bible hold both of these views:

Sons are believers

The sons of God were men of Seth’s believing line who married unbelieving women of the world.

Sons are angels

The sons of God were fallen angels who took human women as wives.

We will show our work from the King James Bible alone, state each view as its holders would, and let the text decide.

  1. Two linesCain’s family and Seth’s family, from Genesis 4 and 5

The line of Cain

And Cain went out from the presence of the Lord, and dwelt in the land of Nod, on the east of Eden.Genesis 4:16
  • Cain builds a city and names it after his son Enoch.
  • Lamech takes two wives, the first man recorded to do so.
  • Lamech boasts to his wives that he has slain a man, and claims a vengeance greater than Cain’s.

A line that walks away from the presence of the Lord and grows in cities, crafts, and violence. Not once in Genesis 4 does anyone in this line call upon God.

The line of Seth

And to Seth, to him also there was born a son; and he called his name Enos: then began men to call upon the name of the Lord.Genesis 4:26

God appoints “another seed instead of Abel,” and from that seed men begin to call on the Lord. Genesis 5 then traces that line by name, ten generations from Adam to Noah.

Two families now live side by side on the earth: one that went out from the Lord’s presence, and one that calls on His name.

Two Enochs, two Lamechs

Moses gives both lines a man named Enoch and a man named Lamech, as if to set them side by side so we cannot miss the contrast.

Cain’s line
Seth’s line
Enoch. A city is named for him.
Enoch. “Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him.”
Lamech. Takes two wives and boasts of slaying a man.
Lamech. Names his son Noah, hoping for comfort from the ground the Lord cursed.

Same names, opposite hearts. When Genesis 6 speaks of “sons of God” and “daughters of men,” these two families are the ones the reader has just met.

Noah, the last son before the Flood

But Noah found grace in the eyes of the Lord. These are the generations of Noah: Noah was a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah walked with God.Genesis 6:8-9

Ten generations after Adam, the line that called on the Lord comes down to one man and his house. The sons of God had married into the world around them; Noah walked with God.

“Perfect in his generations” means that among the men of his own time, Noah was a believer, mature in his faith. God calls Job “perfect” in the same way, and asks it of Abram. The verse explains itself: “Noah walked with God.”

How anyone becomes a son of God

But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:John 1:12
For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.Romans 8:14
For ye are all the children of God by faith in Christ Jesus.Galatians 3:26

Scripture never makes sonship a matter of nature, rank, or power. It is received by faith in the Son.

Sons before the cross

Christ is “the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world.” His work stands outside of time, and Old Testament saints believed God’s promise of Him.

They looked to Christ

The Lamb slain from the foundation of the world. Abraham rejoiced to see Christ’s day. Moses esteemed the reproach of Christ. Seth’s line called on the name of the Lord.

God called them His own

“Ye are the children of the Lord your God.” “Bring my sons from far, and my daughters.” “Ye are the sons of the living God.”

The Old Testament already uses the language of sons for God’s believing people. Genesis 6 does not need a new definition.

Read it slowly

And it came to pass, when men began to multiply on the face of the earth, and daughters were born unto them, That the sons of God saw the daughters of men that they were fair; and they took them wives of all which they chose. And the Lord said, My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that he also is flesh: yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty years. God’s Spirit strives with man, “for that he also is flesh.” The flesh in view is man’s. The wickedness God sees is man’s. The creature God repents of making is man.

The order of events

  1. The giants are already there“There were giants in the earth in those days.” They are present before the text describes any union.
  2. The sons of God take wivesThey choose by sight, “of all which they chose,” not by faith.
  3. Their children become famous men“Mighty men which were of old, men of renown.” Famous men. The text never calls the children giants, angels, or anything but men.
  4. God sees the wickedness of manEvery imagination of man’s heart was only evil continually.
  5. God repents that He made man, and will destroy man
  6. But Noah found grace

The judgment falls on the godly line’s compromise and the world’s wickedness alike. Nowhere does God name a hybrid race as the cause.

Job 1 and 2: the sons of God in heaven

Now there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the Lord, and Satan came also among them.Job 1:6

Who stands before God

The heavenly assembly includes “the spirits of just men made perfect.” Redeemed men stand in God’s presence.

Among them, not of them

Satan comes “among them” as the accuser, there to charge Job. He is never counted as one of the sons.

Repeating the scene in chapter 2 adds nothing new: the same sons, and the same accuser in their midst.

Job 38:7: who is the corner stone?

The strongest text for Sons are angels, and the answer rests on one question.

Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth? declare, if thou hast understanding. Who hath laid the measures thereof, if thou knowest? or who hath stretched the line upon it? Whereupon are the foundations thereof fastened? or who laid the corner stone thereof; When the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God shouted for joy?Job 38:4-7

Who are the morning stars? Angels. Scripture pictures angels as stars: Lucifer is the “son of the morning,” and the dragon’s tail drew “the third part of the stars of heaven.” The plural morning stars are not to be confused with Christ, who alone is “the bright and morning star.”

So verse 7 names two companies, angels singing and the sons of God shouting. And God names two works: the foundations of the earth, and the corner stone. The sons of God shout when the corner stone is laid. So when was Christ laid as the corner stone?

  1. Before the foundation of the worldThe Lamb was foreordained before the foundation of the world, and is the Lamb slain from its foundation.
  2. At creation, He is the Creator“In the beginning was the Word… and the Word was God. All things were made by him.” At creation Christ is the Builder, not a stone laid in His own building. If the corner stone were laid at creation, the Creator would become part of the creation.
  3. In time, God lays the corner stoneGod promised to lay it “in Zion” (). It was laid when He came in the flesh, was crucified and set at nought by the builders, and was raised from the dead. At the resurrection God fulfilled “Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee.”

The question: is laying the corner stone the same event as founding the earth? Sons are angels reads verses 4–7 as one creation scene, and on a first reading that is natural. Sons are believers sees two works, linked but separate: the earth founded by Christ the Creator, and Christ Himself laid as the corner stone in His triumph over death. At that, the morning stars (the angels) sing, and the sons of God, the redeemed, shout for joy, as Abraham rejoiced to see His day.

Angels are not sons, and do not marry

For unto which of the angels said he at any time, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee? And again, I will be to him a Father, and he shall be to me a Son?Hebrews 1:5
Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of salvation?Hebrews 1:14
For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as the angels of God in heaven.Matthew 22:30
For when they shall rise from the dead, they neither marry, nor are given in marriage; but are as the angels which are in heaven.Mark 12:25

Angels serve the heirs of salvation; they are not the heirs. And the Lord Jesus uses angels as the very picture of beings who do not marry.

Sons are angels, stated fairly

Many faithful believers hold that the sons of God were fallen angels who took human wives. Most reject the Book of Enoch and build the case from the King James Bible. Stated as its holders would state it:

  • In Job, the sons of God stand before the Lord in heaven, so Genesis 6 should be read the same way.
  • Job 38:7 places the sons of God at the founding of the earth, before man was made.
  • Setting “sons of God” against “daughters of men” marks two different kinds of being.
  • Angels have taken bodies, eaten, and been taken for men.
  • Matthew 22:30 describes holy angels in heaven, not the ones who “left their own habitation.”
  • Jude, 2 Peter, and 1 Peter tie angelic sin to “strange flesh” and to the days of Noah.
  • The children became “mighty men… of renown,” something beyond the ordinary.

We grant that this is a real case for angels, built from Scripture by people who love the Book. Notice, though, which angels it needs: not holy ones, but angels who had fallen or were falling in that very act. We will come back to that.

Jude 6, honestly

And the angels which kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation, he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day.Jude 6

What it says

Some angels did not keep their first estate. They left their own habitation. They are held in chains for the judgment.

What it does not say

When they left. Anything about women, marriage, or children. Anything about the Flood.

The Sons are believers reading, held with an open hand: unclean spirits seek a habitation in men. The wickedness of Noah’s day may well have included spirits possessing men, as they still do, with no bodies, wives, or children of their own. Where Scripture is not plain, we should not build a doctrine.

Angels in the form of men

What Sons are angels rightly sees: angels have appeared as men, eaten a meal, and taken men by the hand. Some have entertained angels unawares.

What Scripture also shows

  • An angel is a messenger sent from God, and appears in whatever form God sends him: as men at Abraham’s tent, as six-winged seraphims in Isaiah’s vision.
  • Hebrews calls them “ministering spirits, sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of salvation.” They are spirits who serve the sons of God, not mankind in the flesh.
  • At Sodom, the angels are the ones defended, not the ones defiled. The lust belongs to the men of the city, and the angels strike them blind.
  • No angel who appears as a man ever weds a woman or fathers a child.

Appearing as a man for a message is not becoming a man for a marriage.

The Flood settled it either way

Whatever those unions were, God destroyed them. “Noah only remained alive, and they that were with him in the ark.”

How Jesus told it

In Noah’s days they were eating, drinking, marrying, and giving in marriage. Ordinary marriages of ordinary men.

Never again

After the Flood, Scripture never records another union of angels and women. If it happened once, it left no trace in the rest of the Book.

Still today

Possession by unclean spirits appears again and again, and missionaries still meet it.

One explanation fits what Scripture keeps showing us. The other requires an event Scripture never shows again.

Giants are men

Genesis 6:4 says there were giants “also after that.” The rest of the Bible shows exactly that: tall men, after the Flood, with no angels involved.

  • The sons of Anak, “which come of the giants.”
  • The Emims, “a people great, and many, and tall.”
  • Og of Bashan, whose iron bed was nine cubits long, about thirteen and a half feet.
  • Goliath of Gath, six cubits and a span, nearly ten feet.
  • A man of great stature with six fingers and six toes, “born to the giant,” slain by David’s men.

In living memory, Robert Wadlow (1918–1940) stood 8 feet 11 inches tall. Great stature is a human trait, then and now. It needs no angelic father.

Do not shift the blame

And the man said, The woman whom thou gavest to be with me, she gave me of the tree, and I did eat. And the Lord God said unto the woman, What is this that thou hast done? And the woman said, The serpent beguiled me, and I did eat.Genesis 3:12-13

Since the garden, man has pointed somewhere else. God answered each one for his own sin.

But every man is tempted, when he is drawn away of his own lust, and enticed.James 1:14

In Genesis 6, God names one guilty party: man. Even if spirits were at work in that day, His verdict is “the wickedness of man.” A teaching that moves the weight of Genesis 6 onto a demonic host moves it where God did not put it, under either view.

Grant that they were angels

Suppose the case for angels is right. Then they were not holy angels, who keep their estate. They were fallen angels, rebels against God in the very act of Genesis 6. So the question becomes: would God call those His sons?

What Scripture says of fallen angels

They share the devil’s final destination: hell, “prepared for the devil and his angels.” Even wicked men are called “children of the devil” and “children of disobedience.” God never calls an angel His son, holy or fallen.

What Scripture says of sons who sin

They are still sons. Just Lot was a righteous man while he vexed his soul in Sodom. God’s sons are warned to come out and be separate, not yoked with unbelievers. Israel was not to marry the nations, “for they will turn away thy son from following me.”

Sons are made in His image

So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.Genesis 1:27

Man

Made in the image and likeness of God. Christ took on Him the seed of Abraham, so that men might become sons by faith.

Angels

Spirits, created by Him and for His purpose, not made in His likeness. Salvation is never offered to them: “he took not on him the nature of angels.”

James, answering what “a man may say,” notes that “the devils also believe, and tremble.” Even so, that is not belief unto salvation, because saving grace was never extended to them. Sonship by faith is offered only to the creature made in God’s image.

A note on the fringe

The Book of Enoch, the “Watchers,” and giants thousands of feet tall are not found in the Bible, and they have no place in this study. Most who hold Sons are angels reject them as well.

Jude’s quoting one prophecy of Enoch no more makes that book Scripture than Paul’s quoting pagan poets makes their writings Scripture.

Romans 14:5
