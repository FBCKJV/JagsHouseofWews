vv. 1–3

The LORD Visited Sarah

At the Set Time

And the Lord visited Sarah as he had said, and the Lord did unto Sarah as he had spoken. For Sarah conceived, and bare Abraham a son in his old age, at the set time of which God had spoken to him. And Abraham called the name of his son that was born unto him, whom Sarah bare to him, Isaac.Genesis 21:1-3

Now Abraham had been sojourning in King Abimelech's land of Gerar for a while, long enough for Sarah to conceive and bear Isaac, and for him to be weaned. The birth of the son by faith, as promised to Abraham by God.

vv. 4–5

An Hundred Years Old

Eight Days Old

And Abraham circumcised his son Isaac being eight days old, as God had commanded him. And Abraham was an hundred years old, when his son Isaac was born unto him.Genesis 21:4-5

Abraham was 100, meaning Sarah was 90.

vv. 6–7

God Hath Made Me to Laugh

Who Would Have Said unto Abraham?

And Sarah said, God hath made me to laugh, so that all that hear will laugh with me. And she said, Who would have said unto Abraham, that Sarah should have given children suck? for I have born him a son in his old age.Genesis 21:6-7

If we remember back a few chapters, upon hearing the promise directly from the Lord, Sarah laughed at the idea. Who would believe it, in other words. It's a funny situation, she thinks others will share in the humor of it.

vv. 8–10

Mocking

Cast Out This Bondwoman and Her Son

And the child grew, and was weaned: and Abraham made a great feast the same day that Isaac was weaned. And Sarah saw the son of Hagar the Egyptian, which she had born unto Abraham, mocking. Wherefore she said unto Abraham, Cast out this bondwoman and her son: for the son of this bondwoman shall not be heir with my son, even with Isaac.Genesis 21:8-10

But the son of the bondwoman mocked the situation. Ishmael laughed at it.

vv. 11–13

In Isaac Shall Thy Seed Be Called

Hearken unto Her Voice

And the thing was very grievous in Abraham’s sight because of his son. And God said unto Abraham, Let it not be grievous in thy sight because of the lad, and because of thy bondwoman; in all that Sarah hath said unto thee, hearken unto her voice; for in Isaac shall thy seed be called. And also of the son of the bondwoman will I make a nation, because he is thy seed.Genesis 21:11-13

This dichotomy always struck me as more about Sarah and her own self-consciousness and regret of the sin she foisted on Abraham with Hagar. But she insisted they be cast out, and God was in agreement with her.

So with the blessing and consolation of the Lord, Abraham cast out the son of the bondwoman. A picture of the salvation to come in faith, through Jesus, Abraham's seed of the promise, and a rejection of being bond to the works of the law.

Cross references • Genesis 21:10

Which Things Are an Allegory

Galatians 4:22-24

For it is written, that Abraham had two sons, the one by a bondmaid, the other by a freewoman. But he who was of the bondwoman was born after the flesh; but he of the freewoman was by promise. Which things are an allegory: for these are the two covenants; the one from the mount Sinai, which gendereth to bondage, which is Agar.Galatians 4:22-24

Galatians 4:30-31

Nevertheless what saith the scripture? Cast out the bondwoman and her son: for the son of the bondwoman shall not be heir with the son of the freewoman. So then, brethren, we are not children of the bondwoman, but of the free.Galatians 4:30-31

Again, the New Testament is the study guide for the Old. This event, the casting out of Hagar and Ishmael, the father of Arabia is a picture of the covenant of Mt Sinai, the law of Moses. But the covenant of Jerusalem is free. Liberty from the law in Christ.

Application • Genesis 21:10

The Bondage of the Law

Return to the Bondage of the Law

Many seek to return to the bondage of the law, even in Paul's day. Paul questions the salvation of the foolish Galatians who were adding works to the Gospel of reconciliation through faith, just as we do today, when someone professing themselves to be Christians add works of the law to be saved. Be they turning from sins which is obedience to the law, requiring circumcision, baptism, rites and rituals, denominational subservience, paying alms, fasting of meats...any act of the flesh designed to give righteousness to the man added to the imputed righteousness of Christ Jesus. Return to the bondage of the law, by which no flesh be justified.

If you are trusting in your works to save you, help save you, or keep your salvation, you are perishing, in your bonds.

Cross references • Genesis 21:12

The Children of the Promise

This lesson, from Genesis and taught throughout the New Testament, made very clear here in Galatians, that the children of the promise live by faith, just as Isaac was the child of the promise of God, received in faith by Abraham.

Romans 4:3-5

For what saith the scripture? Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness. Now to him that worketh is the reward not reckoned of grace, but of debt. But to him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, his faith is counted for righteousness.Romans 4:3-5
Application • The children of the promise

Sacraments

The Same Damnable Heresy

False churches like the Roman Catholics, adding adherence to their "sacraments," the vain traditions of men of rites and rituals they claim in their catechism, are required for salvation. That they dispense God's abundant grace which is given freely from the Covenant made in Jesus on the mount in Jerusalem, by works of the law. By bondage to themselves. It's the same damnable heresy being talked about in Galatians. Be it circumcision or infant "baptism."

Baptism of believers is an outward sign of their saving faith. Not a requirement for righteousness, but a way churches can know someone professes Christ as their salvation. But the wicked sprinkling or dunking of infants, like the command under the law to circumcise on the 8th day, is explicitly taught against as judaizing; adding works to salvation.

Cross references • Genesis 21:4

Not in Circumcision

It's a false gospel. How do we know? Because Abraham was saved when he called upon the name of the Lord. In circumcision? No, decades before that OT requirement was made.

Romans 4:10-11

How was it then reckoned? when he was in circumcision, or in uncircumcision? Not in circumcision, but in uncircumcision. And he received the sign of circumcision, a seal of the righteousness of the faith which he had yet being uncircumcised: that he might be the father of all them that believe, though they be not circumcised; that righteousness might be imputed unto them also:Romans 4:10-11

Pay close attention to what Paul writes here. If you are counting on a rite or ritual, such as circumcision or baptism to be saved, you are making yourself heir to the law, faith is made void and the promises made of none effect.

You're not saved. Repent.

Cross references • Genesis 21:4

Christ Shall Profit You Nothing

Galatians 5:2-4

Behold, I Paul say unto you, that if ye be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing. For I testify again to every man that is circumcised, that he is a debtor to do the whole law. Christ is become of no effect unto you, whosoever of you are justified by the law; ye are fallen from grace.Galatians 5:2-4

The leaven of the Pharisees is the whole lump of Catholicism. Adding baptism or confession, communion, confirmation, last rites...adding any of it to the Gospel of salvation in faith through Christ Jesus makes you a debtor to the whole of the law. Christ shall profit you nothing.

Application • Liberty in Christ

Stand Fast in the Liberty

The Error of Cain

What the whore of Babylon, the Roman Catholic Church and its prot harlot daughters do is deny the liberty to the law that is in Christ Jesus, so that its adherents stay yoked to trying to justify themselves before God in their works.

But to we who are free in Christ by the free gift of salvation, we willingly become servants for righteousness, sanctifying ourselves, denying the flesh and walking in the spirit.

By denying the liberty, they damn those following their perverse false doctrine. And by relying on their vain works, they receive nothing but death and Hell, being debtors to the whole of the law.

Galatians 5:1

Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.Galatians 5:1
Application • Liberty in Christ

Wood, Hay and Stubble

God's word describes the man who seeks to be justified by dead works and adherence to the law as under bondage. Satan tells us that our feeble attempts at our own righteousness is sufficient. The error of Cain. But God says our righteousness are as filthy rags. Wood, hay and stubble.

And just as He rejected Cain's works and accepted Abel's offering of faith, so too it is for us today.

Application • The whole of scripture

No Requirement Other Than Faith

It is important to note that the whole of scripture teaches the same thing; the free gift of eternal life given by God the Father in His grace, by faith in the blood of His Son, Christ Jesus, sealed by the Holy Spirit unto redemption.

There is no scripture that contradicts that Gospel. There is no secret to salvation, the knowledge is freely given. There is no requirement other than faith. All men are called, and know the wrath of God and His law, written in their hearts, so they have no excuse. But so many choose to be heirs of the bondwoman. Debtors to the law. And end up perishing.

These who self-righteously toil in the law, seek to put others in the same chains. They make entire books of doctrines and dogmas, created by man, but ignore scripture and the doctrines of God. It's tragic.

vv. 14–16

The Water Was Spent

The Wilderness of Beersheba

And Abraham rose up early in the morning, and took bread, and a bottle of water, and gave it unto Hagar, putting it on her shoulder, and the child, and sent her away: and she departed, and wandered in the wilderness of Beersheba. And the water was spent in the bottle, and she cast the child under one of the shrubs. And she went, and sat her down over against him a good way off, as it were a bow shot: for she said, Let me not see the death of the child. And she sat over against him, and lift up her voice, and wept.Genesis 21:14-16

Like Hagar and Ishmael, they wander in the wilderness. Their water, given by man, by Abraham, runs out. They thirst.

vv. 17–19

God Heard the Voice of the Lad

She Saw a Well of Water

And God heard the voice of the lad; and the angel of God called to Hagar out of heaven, and said unto her, What aileth thee, Hagar? fear not; for God hath heard the voice of the lad where he is. Arise, lift up the lad, and hold him in thine hand; for I will make him a great nation. And God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water; and she went, and filled the bottle with water, and gave the lad drink.Genesis 21:17-19

But, just as Christ is the living water who satisfies our thirst to be sanctified by God, that we may never thirst, God provides a well that they may drink. Hagar desperately cries out to God. And He in His grace hears her;

vv. 20–21

God Was with the Lad

The Wilderness of Paran

And God was with the lad; and he grew, and dwelt in the wilderness, and became an archer. And he dwelt in the wilderness of Paran: and his mother took him a wife out of the land of Egypt.Genesis 21:20-21

This is a picture of salvation. Of Jesus. Even to the bondwoman, there is the offer of salvation by faith. Calling on the name of the Lord.

vv. 22–24

God Is with Thee

Swear unto Me Here by God

And it came to pass at that time, that Abimelech and Phichol the chief captain of his host spake unto Abraham, saying, God is with thee in all that thou doest: Now therefore swear unto me here by God that thou wilt not deal falsely with me, nor with my son, nor with my son’s son: but according to the kindness that I have done unto thee, thou shalt do unto me, and to the land wherein thou hast sojourned. And Abraham said, I will swear.Genesis 21:22-24

Now Abimelech, seeing how God has blessed Abraham, making him mighty and rich, seeks to make a treaty with him.

vv. 25–27

A Well of Water

Both of Them Made a Covenant

And Abraham reproved Abimelech because of a well of water, which Abimelech’s servants had violently taken away. And Abimelech said, I wot not who hath done this thing: neither didst thou tell me, neither yet heard I of it, but to day. And Abraham took sheep and oxen, and gave them unto Abimelech; and both of them made a covenant.Genesis 21:25-27
vv. 28–32

Seven Ewe Lambs

Beersheba

And Abraham set seven ewe lambs of the flock by themselves. And Abimelech said unto Abraham, What mean these seven ewe lambs which thou hast set by themselves? And he said, For these seven ewe lambs shalt thou take of my hand, that they may be a witness unto me, that I have digged this well. Wherefore he called that place Beersheba; because there they sware both of them. Thus they made a covenant at Beersheba: then Abimelech rose up, and Phichol the chief captain of his host, and they returned into the land of the Philistines.Genesis 21:28-32

For lambs and a well, in the place Beersheba, where their covenant was made. Abimelech swearing not only his own covenant, but those of his sons, and their sons. To the 4th generation.

vv. 33–34

The Everlasting God

In the Philistines' Land

And Abraham planted a grove in Beersheba, and called there on the name of the Lord, the everlasting God. And Abraham sojourned in the Philistines’ land many days.Genesis 21:33-34

And again, Abraham called upon the name of the Lord, to bless the place he sojourned.

The land of the Philistines, where 400+ years later, where the Hebrews, the sons of Abraham would find conflict with the sons of Abimelech.
