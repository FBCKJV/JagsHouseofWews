The Tower of Babel

One language

And the whole earth was of one language, and of one speech. And it came to pass, as they journeyed from the east, that they found a plain in the land of Shinar; and they dwelt there. And they said one to another, Go to, let us make brick, and burn them thoroughly. And they had brick for stone, and slime had they for morter. And they said, Go to, let us build us a city and a tower, whose top may reach unto heaven; and let us make us a name, lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth.Genesis 11:1-4

About 100 years after the flood, in the year of Peleg’s birth (for in his days was the earth divided), the people imagined a vain thing. A tower to Heaven. That they could work their way to Heaven and be like God.

“Let us go down”

And the Lord came down to see the city and the tower, which the children of men builded. And the Lord said, Behold, the people is one, and they have all one language; and this they begin to do: and now nothing will be restrained from them, which they have imagined to do. Go to, let us go down, and there confound their language, that they may not understand one another’s speech. So the Lord scattered them abroad from thence upon the face of all the earth: and they left off to build the city. Therefore is the name of it called Babel; because the Lord did there confound the language of all the earth: and from thence did the Lord scatter them abroad upon the face of all the earth.Genesis 11:5-9

The Lord God, the Father, says “let us go down…” And they scattered the people and confused their language.

We are meant to be in nations

We are meant to be in nations. Nations by clan. By blood.

Not one world government

Full of vanity, making a name for ourselves, lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth.

Not paper and statutes

Not even a civic nationalism of paper and statutes that erases who we are.

The unity God gives

Neither pray I for these alone, but for them also which shall believe on me through their word; That they all may be one; as thou, Father, art in me, and I in thee, that they also may be one in us: that the world may believe that thou hast sent me. And the glory which thou gavest me I have given them; that they may be one, even as we are one: I in them, and thou in me, that they may be made perfect in one; and that the world may know that thou hast sent me, and hast loved them, as thou hast loved me.John 17:20-23

Babel sought to be one by its own works, to make a name. Christ prays that those who believe on Him through the word be one in the Father and the Son.

A mighty hunter before the Lord

And Cush begat Nimrod: he began to be a mighty one in the earth. He was a mighty hunter before the Lord: wherefore it is said, Even as Nimrod the mighty hunter before the Lord. And the beginning of his kingdom was Babel, and Erech, and Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar.Genesis 10:8-10

Nimrod gets a bad rap. Having founded Babel and being a mighty man, a hunter before the Lord, it is assumed he’s the impetus for the construction of the tower.

  • Most of the assumption is based on his proximity, his greatness, being a gibbor or mighty man, and his lineage forming the great cities listed in Genesis 10: Babel, Erech, Accad and Calneh, in the land of Shinar.
  • Some translate his name as “deserter,” others “he rebelled,” and build an entire story around him, the tower, and great wickedness against God.

Yet scripture doesn’t give a negative accounting of him.

The generations of Shem

These are the generations of Shem: Shem was an hundred years old, and begat Arphaxad two years after the flood: And Shem lived after he begat Arphaxad five hundred years, and begat sons and daughters. And Arphaxad lived five and thirty years, and begat Salah: And Arphaxad lived after he begat Salah four hundred and three years, and begat sons and daughters.Genesis 11:10-13

Then we are given the generations of Shem, leading to Terah, father of Abram and grandfather of Lot.

Where and when we are

The genealogy of man after the flood is again given to establish where and when we are for the next phase of the story: the origins and chronology of the people God dealt with.

Genesis 5Adam to Noah
Genesis 11Noah to Abram
Matthew 1, Luke 3To Jesus Christ

In like manner, the Gospels have similar genealogies, that we have a lineage and time frame from Adam to Jesus’ earthly incarnation. Many overlook this as simply a history or chronological device, or try to extract much deeper meaning from it than is there, often at the expense of believing it literally, especially the timing of years.

Life spans shrinking

And Nahor lived nine and twenty years, and begat Terah: And Nahor lived after he begat Terah an hundred and nineteen years, and begat sons and daughters. And Terah lived seventy years, and begat Abram, Nahor, and Haran.Genesis 11:24-26
600years, Shem
205years, Terah
175years, Abraham

The life spans of man are shrinking as we go from Shem to Abram, approaching the hundred and twenty years God decided back in Genesis 6. A shadow of the 900 years most of the earlier patriarchs lived to.

No respecter of persons

God is not a respecter of persons, the Bible says repeatedly. He does not care if you are great or small, rich or poor, man or woman, Greek or Jew, where you’re from or who your parents are.

Then Peter opened his mouth, and said, Of a truth I perceive that God is no respecter of persons: But in every nation he that feareth him, and worketh righteousness, is accepted with him.Acts 10:34-35

He gives us all the same offer of salvation and adoption in Christ. As individuals, we have free will.

But God does judge nations

God does judge nations. He favors some over others at times, and destroys many who go too far astray.

  • He curses the land stained by the blood of violence, sodomy, fornication.
  • Of people, collectively, to the fourth generation.

We are to know the judgment of God and emulate it. Not being a respecter of persons, giving the Gospel to whomever we meet, but judging ungodly nations for what they are. Including our own.

Scattered as judgment

As at Babel, scattering is God’s judgment on a people who forsake Him.

And I will scatter you among the heathen, and will draw out a sword after you: and your land shall be desolate, and your cities waste.Leviticus 26:33
I call heaven and earth to witness against you this day, that ye shall soon utterly perish from off the land whereunto ye go over Jordan to possess it; ye shall not prolong your days upon it, but shall utterly be destroyed. And the Lord shall scatter you among the nations, and ye shall be left few in number among the heathen, whither the Lord shall lead you.Deuteronomy 4:26-27

Yet a remnant

Yet will I leave a remnant, that ye may have some that shall escape the sword among the nations, when ye shall be scattered through the countries.Ezekiel 6:8
Even so then at this present time also there is a remnant according to the election of grace. And if by grace, then is it no more of works: otherwise grace is no more grace. But if it be of works, then is it no more grace: otherwise work is no more work. What then? Israel hath not obtained that which he seeketh for; but the election hath obtained it, and the rest were blinded. (According as it is written, God hath given them the spirit of slumber, eyes that they should not see, and ears that they should not hear;) unto this day.Romans 11:5-8

The remnant is by grace, not works. The rest were blinded.

Reprobate silver

Reprobate silver shall men call them, because the Lord hath rejected them.Jeremiah 6:30

Speaking of the Jews who would reject the coming Messiah, Romans 1 applies to them as it applies to all that go the way of the Pharaoh. Because they did not like to retain God in their knowledge, “He hath blinded their eyes, and hardened their heart.” By their individual free will. Collectively judged.

Not “doing nothing”

Unsaved men often criticize born again believers as “doing nothing,” as weak, afraid or cowardly, because we aren’t running around in panic, getting sucked into reactionary spirals by the provocations of the rulers of the darkness of this world.

They rail and demand we join seditions and take vengeance for ourselves. They fail to notice that the more they do this, the worse the situation becomes for everyone. This frustration, doubt and impatience with God can strike even believing Christians.

If my people

If my people, which are called by my name, shall humble themselves, and pray, and seek my face, and turn from their wicked ways; then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin, and will heal their land.2 Chronicles 7:14

God is clear on how we save our nation and get His blessings for our people.

The hard work

  • Have faith. Obey the commandments. As much as possible, live peaceably among men.
  • Be a godly husband, raise godly children, and share the gospel, winning souls.
  • Give comfort to those mourning, be a good judge for the fatherless and true widows.

This is not “doing nothing.” It is hard work, or else it wouldn’t be so difficult to get people to do it. Like most effective things, it requires discipline, motivation and perseverance. It’s easier to express impotent outrage online.

We wrestle not against flesh and blood

For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.Ephesians 6:12

The frustrations and provocations assailing us are how Satan tempts us, so he can accuse the saints before God: to make us sin, so that we can be persecuted for those sins by the powers that be. They avoid persecuting believers for His name’s sake as much as possible, because when they clearly persecute us for our faith, directly, they lose.

As a people, if Christians turn towards God and obey Him, fearful of nothing except God, He will hear us, heal our land, and bring blessings to all our nation, believers or not.
