Gen 2:1–3 · The Sabbath, Creation Complete

God Rested & Sanctified the Seventh Day

Thus the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the host of them. And on the seventh day God ended his work which he had made; and he rested on the seventh day from all his work which he had made. And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested from all his work which God created and made.Genesis 2:1-3
Gen 2:7–8 · The Formation of Man

Man Formed from the Dust

And the Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul. And the Lord God planted a garden eastward in Eden; and there he put the man whom he had formed.Genesis 2:7-8
Cross references · Genesis 2:7

Man Formed by God, Made in His Image

  • The Image of God. Man is unique among all creation, made in God’s own image. The distinction between male and female is built into that image from the very beginning.
  • Man the Image, Woman the Glory. Paul grounds male/female roles directly in the Genesis 2 creation order, not culture, but design.
  • Fearfully and Wonderfully Made. Every person is the direct handiwork of God, personally formed, not accidentally produced.
  • The Breath of the Almighty. The breath of God in Genesis 2:7 is not merely biological, it is spiritual. Man alone among creation received this direct animating breath.

Man is not the product of time and chance. He was personally formed and animated by the living God. This dignified origin is the very reason marriage, between two image-bearers, carries such weight.

Gen 2:15–17 · Man’s Purpose & Command

Dress It and Keep It

And the Lord God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden to dress it and to keep it. And the Lord God commanded the man, saying, Of every tree of the garden thou mayest freely eat: But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it: for in the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die.Genesis 2:15-17
Gen 2:18 · The First “Not Good” · Foundation of Marriage

It Is Not Good That the Man Should Be Alone

And the Lord God said, It is not good that the man should be alone; I will make him an help meet for him.Genesis 2:18
Cross references · Genesis 2:18

“Not Good to Be Alone”: Marriage Throughout Scripture

  • Finding a Wife. Marriage is not merely permitted, it is a gift and a sign of God’s favour.
  • A Virtuous Woman. The “help meet” God promised is portrayed in Proverbs 31 as a woman of remarkable character and godliness.
  • Two Are Better Than One. Solomon’s wisdom reflects Genesis 2:18. The aloneness God called “not good” is answered by the companionship God designed in marriage.
  • Marriage as God’s Provision. Paul acknowledges both the gift of singleness and that marriage is God’s ordained provision for companionship and purity.

God Himself declared aloneness “not good,” the only time before sin that God pronounced something in creation as lacking. The help meet He provided was His answer: not a servant, but a complement perfectly fitted for the man He had made.

Gen 2:19–20 · Adam’s Authority

Adam Names the Animals

And out of the ground the Lord God formed every beast of the field, and every fowl of the air; and brought them unto Adam to see what he would call them: and whatsoever Adam called every living creature, that was the name thereof. And Adam gave names to all cattle, and to the fowl of the air, and to every beast of the field; but for Adam there was not found an help meet for him.Genesis 2:19-20
Gen 2:21–22 · Creation of Woman

God Made He a Woman

And the Lord God caused a deep sleep to fall upon Adam, and he slept: and he took one of his ribs, and closed up the flesh instead thereof; And the rib, which the Lord God had taken from man, made he a woman, and brought her unto the man.Genesis 2:21-22
Cross references · Genesis 2:21–22

Woman Created, the Help Meet

  • Woman from Man, for Man. Paul does not invent a gender hierarchy. He traces it directly to Genesis 2’s creation order.
  • Order of Creation Matters. The order of creation is cited by Paul as the basis for roles within the covenant community.
  • Husbands Love as Christ Loves. The husband’s calling is not dominance but sacrificial love, just as Christ gave Himself for the Church.
  • The Help Meet Fulfilled. The “help meet” of Genesis 2 is portrayed throughout Scripture not as lesser, but as indispensable.

Woman was not an afterthought. She was the crown of a creation that God Himself declared incomplete without her. Taken from man’s side, not his feet nor his head, she stands beside him as companion, counterpart, and co-bearer of the image of God.

Gen 2:23 · Adam’s Proclamation, First Words About Marriage

Bone of My Bones, Flesh of My Flesh

And Adam said, This is now bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh: she shall be called Woman, because she was taken out of Man.Genesis 2:23
Cross references · Genesis 2:23

“Bone of My Bones”: Union & Recognition

  • Husbands & Their Own Bodies. Paul directly echoes Adam’s words and applies the same intimacy to Christ’s relationship with the Church. Marriage is a living picture of the gospel.
  • Mutual Belonging. The Song of Solomon celebrates the joyful recognition of belonging that Adam first expressed.
  • “My Bone and My Flesh.” The phrase “bone and flesh” becomes a covenant expression of belonging throughout the Old Testament.

Adam’s first words were poetry, a joyful cry of recognition. This was no cold arrangement but a moment of wonder. The first human speech recorded in Scripture is a husband celebrating his wife.

Gen 2:24–25 · The Institution of Marriage

Leave · Cleave · One Flesh — God’s Design

Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh. And they were both naked, the man and his wife, and were not ashamed.Genesis 2:24-25
Cross references · Genesis 2:24

Leave · Cleave · One Flesh

  • Jesus Quotes Genesis 2. Jesus settles the marriage question by going back to creation: permanent, exclusive, one man and one woman.
  • From the Beginning of Creation. Marriage predates the Fall, the Law, and every culture. It is anchored in creation order itself.
  • The Great Mystery. Paul reveals the ultimate meaning: marriage has always been a picture of Christ’s union with the Church.
Cross references · Genesis 2:24

Leave · Cleave · One Flesh

  • One Flesh Is Not Casual. “One flesh” is a real and binding union. Sexual union always creates a covenant bond.
  • The Lord Witness Between Thee and the Wife. God Himself is witness to the marriage covenant.
  • Marriage Honourable. The New Testament upholds the honor of marriage as God designed it: pure, exclusive, and guarded by God’s own judgment.

Genesis 2:24 is the most-quoted verse in the New Testament on marriage, quoted by Jesus and by Paul. One man leaves his family of origin, cleaves exclusively to one wife, and the two become one new covenant unit. This is what God joined. This is what man must not divide.

Gen 3 · The Fall, Marriage Under Curse

But It Wasn’t to Last…

God had called it very good. Man had a purpose, a garden, a companion, and communion with God. Then came the serpent. Genesis 3 will show what sin does to the marriage God designed, and why redemption must restore what the Fall corrupted.

The story of marriage is not finished at Genesis 2. It runs through every page of Scripture, climaxing in Christ and the Church.
