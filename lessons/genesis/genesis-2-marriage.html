Genesis 2:24 · King James Bible
Gen 2:1–3The Sabbath — Creation Complete

God Rested & Sanctified the Seventh Day

1Thus the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the host of them.

2And on the seventh day God ended his work which he had made; and he rested on the seventh day from all his work which he had made.

3And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested from all his work which God created and made.

Gen 2:7–8The Formation of Man

Man Formed from the Dust

7And the Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.

8And the Lord God planted a garden eastward in Eden; and there he put the man whom he had formed.

Gen 2:15–17Man's Purpose & Command

Dress It and Keep It

15And the Lord God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden to dress it and to keep it.

16And the Lord God commanded the man, saying, Of every tree of the garden thou mayest freely eat:

17But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it: for in the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die.

Gen 2:18The First "Not Good" · Foundation of Marriage

It Is Not Good That the Man Should Be Alone

18And the Lord God said, It is not good that the man should be alone; I will make him an help meet for him.

Gen 2:19–20Adam's Authority

Adam Names the Animals

19And out of the ground the Lord God formed every beast of the field, and every fowl of the air; and brought them unto Adam to see what he would call them: and whatsoever Adam called every living creature, that was the name thereof.

20And Adam gave names to all cattle, and to the fowl of the air, and to every beast of the field; but for Adam there was not found an help meet for him.

Gen 2:21–22Creation of Woman

God Made He a Woman

21And the Lord God caused a deep sleep to fall upon Adam, and he slept: and he took one of his ribs, and closed up the flesh instead thereof;

22And the rib, which the Lord God had taken from man, made he a woman, and brought her unto the man.

Gen 2:23Adam's Proclamation — First Words About Marriage

Bone of My Bones, Flesh of My Flesh

23And Adam said, This is now bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh: she shall be called Woman, because she was taken out of Man.

Gen 2:24–25The Institution of Marriage

Leave · Cleave · One Flesh — God's Design

24Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh.

25And they were both naked, the man and his wife, and were not ashamed.

Coming Next
Gen 3The Fall — Marriage Under Curse

But It Wasn't to Last…

God had called it very good. Man had a purpose, a garden, a companion, and communion with God. Then came the serpent. Genesis 3 will show what sin does to the marriage God designed — and why redemption must restore what the Fall corrupted.

The story of marriage is not finished at Genesis 2. It runs through every page of Scripture, climaxing in Christ and the Church.

Cross References · Genesis 2:7

Man Formed by God · Made in His Image

Genesis 1:26–27 · The Image of God

"And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness… So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them."

↳ Man is unique among all creation — made in God's own image. The distinction between male and female is built into that image from the very beginning.

1 Corinthians 11:7–9 · Man the Image, Woman the Glory

"For a man indeed ought not to cover his head, forasmuch as he is the image and glory of God: but the woman is the glory of the man. For the man is not of the woman; but the woman of the man."

↳ Paul grounds male/female roles directly in the Genesis 2 creation order — not culture, but design.

Psalm 139:14 · Fearfully and Wonderfully Made

"I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well."

↳ Every person is the direct handiwork of God — personally formed, not accidentally produced.

Job 33:4 · The Breath of the Almighty

"The Spirit of God hath made me; and the breath of the Almighty hath given me life."

↳ The breath of God in Genesis 2:7 is not merely biological — it is spiritual. Man alone among creation received this direct animating breath.

Man is not the product of time and chance — he was personally formed and animated by the living God. This dignified origin is the very reason marriage, between two image-bearers, carries such weight.

Cross References · Genesis 2:18

"It Is Not Good That the Man Should Be Alone"

Proverbs 18:22 · Finding a Wife

"Whoso findeth a wife findeth a good thing, and obtaineth favour of the LORD."

↳ Marriage is not merely permitted — it is a gift and a sign of God's favour.

Proverbs 31:10 · A Virtuous Woman

"Who can find a virtuous woman? for her price is far above rubies."

↳ The "help meet" God promised is portrayed in Proverbs 31 as a woman of remarkable character and godliness.

Ecclesiastes 4:9–12 · Two Are Better Than One

"Two are better than one; because they have a good reward for their labour. For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow… and a threefold cord is not quickly broken."

↳ Solomon's wisdom reflects Genesis 2:18 — the aloneness God called "not good" is answered by the companionship God designed in marriage.

1 Corinthians 7:2 · Marriage as God's Provision

"Nevertheless, to avoid fornication, let every man have his own wife, and let every woman have her own husband."

↳ Paul acknowledges both the gift of singleness and that marriage is God's ordained provision for companionship and purity.

God Himself declared aloneness "not good" — the only time before sin that God pronounced something in creation as lacking. The help meet He provided was His answer: not a servant, but a complement perfectly fitted for the man He had made.

Cross References · Genesis 2:21–22

Woman Created · The Help Meet

1 Corinthians 11:8–9 · Woman from Man, for Man

"For the man is not of the woman; but the woman of the man. Neither was the man created for the woman; but the woman for the man."

↳ Paul does not invent a gender hierarchy — he traces it directly to Genesis 2's creation order.

1 Timothy 2:13 · Order of Creation Matters

"For Adam was first formed, then Eve."

↳ The order of creation is cited by Paul as the basis for roles within the covenant community.

Ephesians 5:25–27 · Husbands Love as Christ Loves

"Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it; That he might sanctify and cleanse it… that he might present it to himself a glorious church."

↳ The husband's calling is not dominance but sacrificial love — just as Christ gave Himself for the Church.

Proverbs 31:11–12 · The Help Meet Fulfilled

"The heart of her husband doth safely trust in her… She will do him good and not evil all the days of her life."

↳ The "help meet" of Genesis 2 is portrayed throughout Scripture not as lesser, but as indispensable.

Woman was not an afterthought — she was the crown of a creation that God Himself declared incomplete without her. Taken from man's side, not his feet nor his head, she stands beside him as companion, counterpart, and co-bearer of the image of God.

Cross References · Genesis 2:23

"Bone of My Bones" · Union & Recognition

Ephesians 5:28–30 · Husbands & Their Own Bodies

"So ought men to love their wives as their own bodies. He that loveth his wife loveth himself… For we are members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones."

↳ Paul directly echoes Adam's words and applies the same intimacy to Christ's relationship with the Church. Marriage is a living picture of the gospel.

Song of Solomon 2:16 · Mutual Belonging

"My beloved is mine, and I am his: he feedeth among the lilies."

↳ The Song of Solomon celebrates the joyful recognition of belonging that Adam first expressed.

Genesis 29:14 · "My Bone and My Flesh"

"And Laban said to him, Surely thou art my bone and my flesh."

↳ The phrase "bone and flesh" becomes a covenant expression of belonging throughout the Old Testament.

Adam's first words were poetry — a joyful cry of recognition. This was no cold arrangement but a moment of wonder. The first human speech recorded in Scripture is a husband celebrating his wife.

Cross References · Genesis 2:24

Leave · Cleave · One Flesh

Matthew 19:4–6 · Jesus Quotes Genesis 2

"And he answered and said unto them, Have ye not read, that he which made them at the beginning made them male and female, And said, For this cause shall a man leave father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife: and they twain shall be one flesh? Wherefore they are no more twain, but one flesh. What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder."

↳ Jesus settles the marriage question by going back to creation — permanent, exclusive, one man and one woman.

Mark 10:6–9 · From the Beginning of Creation

"But from the beginning of the creation God made them male and female. For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and cleave to his wife; And they twain shall be one flesh."

↳ Marriage predates the Fall, the Law, and every culture — it is anchored in creation order itself.

Ephesians 5:31–32 · The Great Mystery

"For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall be joined unto his wife, and they two shall be one flesh. This is a great mystery: but I speak concerning Christ and the church."

↳ Paul reveals the ultimate meaning: marriage has always been a picture of Christ's union with the Church.

1 Corinthians 6:16 · One Flesh is Not Casual

"What? know ye not that he which is joined to an harlot is one body? for two, saith he, shall be one flesh."

↳ "One flesh" is a real and binding union — sexual union always creates a covenant bond.

Malachi 2:14–15 · The Lord Witness Between Thee and the Wife

"…the LORD hath been witness between thee and the wife of thy youth… yet is she thy companion, and the wife of thy covenant."

↳ God Himself is witness to the marriage covenant.

Hebrews 13:4 · Marriage Honourable

"Marriage is honourable in all, and the bed undefiled: but whoremongers and adulterers God will judge."

↳ The New Testament upholds the honor of marriage as God designed it — pure, exclusive, and guarded by God's own judgment.

Genesis 2:24 is the most-quoted verse in the New Testament on marriage — cited by Moses, Jesus, Paul, and the writer of Hebrews. One man leaves his family of origin, cleaves exclusively to one wife, and the two become one new covenant unit. This is what God joined. This is what man must not divide.
