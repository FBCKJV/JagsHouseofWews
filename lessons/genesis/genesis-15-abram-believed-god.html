The word of the Lord came

After these things the word of the Lord came unto Abram in a vision, saying, Fear not, Abram: I am thy shield, and thy exceeding great reward.Genesis 15:1
In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. The same was in the beginning with God.John 1:1-2

The Bible tells us who the word of God is. It is Jesus.

Lord God, what wilt thou give me?

And Abram said, Lord God, what wilt thou give me, seeing I go childless, and the steward of my house is this Eliezer of Damascus? And Abram said, Behold, to me thou hast given no seed: and, lo, one born in my house is mine heir. And, behold, the word of the Lord came unto him, saying, This shall not be thine heir; but he that shall come forth out of thine own bowels shall be thine heir. And he brought him forth abroad, and said, Look now toward heaven, and tell the stars, if thou be able to number them: and he said unto him, So shall thy seed be.Genesis 15:2-5

Abram, old when he called upon the name of the Lord and received blessings and promises, now even older and without a true heir, questions the Lord. His faith tested, the word of God brings him forth to number the stars.

Counted for righteousness

And he believed in the Lord; and he counted it to him for righteousness.Genesis 15:6
✦ ✦ ✦

Not of works

What shall we say then that Abraham our father, as pertaining to the flesh, hath found? For if Abraham were justified by works, he hath whereof to glory; but not before God. For what saith the scripture? Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness. Now to him that worketh is the reward not reckoned of grace, but of debt. But to him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, his faith is counted for righteousness.Romans 4:1-5
And the scripture was fulfilled which saith, Abraham believed God, and it was imputed unto him for righteousness: and he was called the Friend of God.James 2:23

The promise was made when he believed in the Lord; and he counted it to him for righteousness. His righteousness was imputed for that faith, by a gracious God. And it was faith in that seed to be given by the Lord. Abraham and James 2 ↗

Even as Abraham believed God

Even as Abraham believed God, and it was accounted to him for righteousness. Know ye therefore that they which are of faith, the same are the children of Abraham. And the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the heathen through faith, preached before the gospel unto Abraham, saying, In thee shall all nations be blessed. So then they which be of faith are blessed with faithful Abraham.Galatians 3:6-9
Now to Abraham and his seed were the promises made. He saith not, And to seeds, as of many; but as of one, And to thy seed, which is Christ.Galatians 3:16

The law cannot disannul it

And this I say, that the covenant, that was confirmed before of God in Christ, the law, which was four hundred and thirty years after, cannot disannul, that it should make the promise of none effect. For if the inheritance be of the law, it is no more of promise: but God gave it to Abraham by promise.Galatians 3:17-18
Wherefore the law was our schoolmaster to bring us unto Christ, that we might be justified by faith. But after that faith is come, we are no longer under a schoolmaster. For ye are all the children of God by faith in Christ Jesus.Galatians 3:24-26

“And if ye be Christ’s, then are ye Abraham’s seed, and heirs according to the promise.”

As the stars of heaven

We see in scripture that this promise was kept, his seed being one, Jesus Christ. And in Him the promise of a nation innumerable, like the stars in the sky, is fulfilled in Revelation 7.

After this I beheld, and, lo, a great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues, stood before the throne, and before the Lamb, clothed with white robes, and palms in their hands; And cried with a loud voice, saying, Salvation to our God which sitteth upon the throne, and unto the Lamb.Revelation 7:9-10

Following the tribulation and the rapture, there are saints redeemed in the blood of Christ: 144,000 Old Testament saints, 12,000 from each of the tribes listed (not Jews, but Israelites), and a great multitude, which no man could number, of all nations, and kindreds, and people, and tongues.

The promise to Abraham

This is the promise to Abraham. Not for the modern Jew. Not for the apostate and wicked modern state calling itself Israel. But of Christ and Him crucified, with the power to make the elect heirs to the Kingdom of Heaven: whosoever will call upon the name of the Lord.

Old Testament. New Testament. The same dispensation. The same promise. The same faith.

They do not understand what they affirm

Dispensationalist Christians who say “God doesn’t break His promises!” don’t understand what the promises are, and don’t believe they are fulfilled in Christ. They cannot rightly divide the word of God, for whatever reason, and they allow wicked men with a Satanic agenda to tell them what it all means.

Knots

They twist themselves into knots trying to square the wicked doctrines of different paths to righteousness, one of works, one of blood, one of faith; the wicked doctrine of the pre-trib rapture; the invention that the millennial Kingdom is for the modern Jew.

Whereby shall I know?

And he said unto him, I am the Lord that brought thee out of Ur of the Chaldees, to give thee this land to inherit it. And he said, Lord God, whereby shall I know that I shall inherit it? And he said unto him, Take me an heifer of three years old, and a she goat of three years old, and a ram of three years old, and a turtledove, and a young pigeon. And he took unto him all these, and divided them in the midst, and laid each piece one against another: but the birds divided he not. And when the fowls came down upon the carcases, Abram drove them away.Genesis 15:7-11

The word of God tells him to offer an heifer, a she goat and a ram of three years old, a turtledove and a young pigeon. Each a sign or a picture of Christ, as established in the Levitical laws 400 years and more from when this is happening, to show the mercy of God in redemption by faith: the blood offerings, the sin offerings, the burnt offerings. A testament necessitates a blood offering. These were a symbol of Christ’s.

A stranger in a land not theirs

And when the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell upon Abram; and, lo, an horror of great darkness fell upon him. And he said unto Abram, Know of a surety that thy seed shall be a stranger in a land that is not theirs, and shall serve them; and they shall afflict them four hundred years; And also that nation, whom they shall serve, will I judge: and afterward shall they come out with great substance.Genesis 15:12-14

In a deep sleep, a horror of great darkness fell upon Abram, and the word of God prophesied the sojourning in Egypt and enslavement. And also that nation, whom they shall serve, will I judge: and afterward shall they come out with great substance. Moses writing in the Spirit the prophecy of Exodus given to Abraham.

The iniquity of the Amorites

And thou shalt go to thy fathers in peace; thou shalt be buried in a good old age. But in the fourth generation they shall come hither again: for the iniquity of the Amorites is not yet full.Genesis 15:15-16

Abram himself will die knowing peace, but the land will not be given unto his descendants until the wickedness of the Amorites comes in. They are going to purge the land of Canaan of the wicked, as an instrument of the Lord.

A burning lamp

And it came to pass, that, when the sun went down, and it was dark, behold a smoking furnace, and a burning lamp that passed between those pieces. In the same day the Lord made a covenant with Abram, saying, Unto thy seed have I given this land, from the river of Egypt unto the great river, the river Euphrates:Genesis 15:17-18

A furnace and a lamp passing between the pieces of the offering: a testament signed by God.

Full of iniquity

One of the most important doctrines, which touches both the spiritual nature of man and a warning to heed here on earth, is the reprobate doctrine. It is taught throughout both the Old and New Testament.

Reprobation, the final and utter rejection by God of those who have finally and utterly rejected His overtures of grace, the call to Christ and the prodding and patience of the Holy Ghost, is one of the three spiritual states of man.

The three spiritual states of man

The unregenerate sinner

Dead in spirit but with the conscience of God within, who until death could hear the call and believe on Jesus Christ and be saved. The children of disobedience.

The saved

Those who call upon the name of the Lord, believing God’s testimony, spirit quickened. Born again believers, called the sons of God.

The reprobate

Twice dead. Conscience seared. Hearts hardened, ears dulled. Unable to come to the knowledge of the truth. Rejected of God, given up. Clouds without water. The children of the devil.

Lumped in with the common sinner

Often Christians, seeking to be kind and show mercy, thinking they are doing the right thing, will completely ignore the final group, lumping them in with the common sinner. Holding out hope, even when they reveal their wickedness in ways exactly as God’s word tells us they will.

Evil often isn’t an outward appearance of malevolence, like a comic book super villain. It’s often someone who is a miserable human being. We take pity on them, and they use our good-natured inclination for mercy and grace to take advantage of us. They are the false teachers, the false prophets bringing doctrines of damnation, with the appearance of holiness, whited sepulchres.
