v. 1

Fear Not, Abram

The Word of the Lord Came unto Abram in a Vision

After these things the word of the Lord came unto Abram in a vision, saying, Fear not, Abram: I am thy shield, and thy exceeding great reward.Genesis 15:1

The word of God came to Abram in a vision. The Bible tells us who the word of God is. It is Jesus.

vv. 2–5

Lord God, What Wilt Thou Give Me?

So Shall Thy Seed Be

And Abram said, Lord God, what wilt thou give me, seeing I go childless, and the steward of my house is this Eliezer of Damascus? And Abram said, Behold, to me thou hast given no seed: and, lo, one born in my house is mine heir. And, behold, the word of the Lord came unto him, saying, This shall not be thine heir; but he that shall come forth out of thine own bowels shall be thine heir. And he brought him forth abroad, and said, Look now toward heaven, and tell the stars, if thou be able to number them: and he said unto him, So shall thy seed be.Genesis 15:2-5

Abram, old when he called upon the name of the Lord and received blessings and promises, now even older and without a true heir, questions the Lord. Abram’s faith tested, the word of God brings him forth to tell the stars.

v. 6

He Believed in the Lord

And He Counted It to Him for Righteousness

And he believed in the Lord; and he counted it to him for righteousness.Genesis 15:6

This is the promise made when he believed in the Lord; and he counted it to him for righteousness. That his righteousness was imputed for that faith, by a gracious God. And it was faith in that seed to be given by the Lord, and the blessings for that heir and the promise made in Him.

vv. 7–11

Whereby Shall I Know?

An Heifer, a She Goat, a Ram, a Turtledove and a Pigeon

And he said unto him, I am the Lord that brought thee out of Ur of the Chaldees, to give thee this land to inherit it. And he said, Lord God, whereby shall I know that I shall inherit it? And he said unto him, Take me an heifer of three years old, and a she goat of three years old, and a ram of three years old, and a turtledove, and a young pigeon. And he took unto him all these, and divided them in the midst, and laid each piece one against another: but the birds divided he not. And when the fowls came down upon the carcases, Abram drove them away.Genesis 15:7-11

Each a sign or a picture of Christ. As established in the Levitical laws, 400 and more years from when this is happening, done to show the mercy of God in redemption by faith: the blood offerings, the sin offerings, the burnt offerings. A testament necessitates a blood offering. These were a symbol of Christ’s.

vv. 12–14

A Horror of Great Darkness

Thy Seed Shall Be a Stranger in a Land That Is Not Theirs

And when the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell upon Abram; and, lo, an horror of great darkness fell upon him. And he said unto Abram, Know of a surety that thy seed shall be a stranger in a land that is not theirs, and shall serve them; and they shall afflict them four hundred years; And also that nation, whom they shall serve, will I judge: and afterward shall they come out with great substance.Genesis 15:12-14

In a deep sleep, a darkness fell upon Abram, and the word of God prophesied the time to come: the sojourning in Egypt and enslavement. Moses writing in the Spirit of the prophecy of Exodus given to Abraham.

vv. 15–16

The Iniquity of the Amorites

Is Not Yet Full

And thou shalt go to thy fathers in peace; thou shalt be buried in a good old age. But in the fourth generation they shall come hither again: for the iniquity of the Amorites is not yet full.Genesis 15:15-16

The Lord telling Abram that he himself will die knowing peace, but that the land will not be given unto his descendants until the wickedness of the Amorites comes in. They are going to purge the land of Canaan of the wicked, as an instrument of the Lord.

vv. 17–21

A Burning Lamp

From the River of Egypt unto the Great River

And it came to pass, that, when the sun went down, and it was dark, behold a smoking furnace, and a burning lamp that passed between those pieces. In the same day the Lord made a covenant with Abram, saying, Unto thy seed have I given this land, from the river of Egypt unto the great river, the river Euphrates: The Kenites, and the Kenizzites, and the Kadmonites, And the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Rephaims, And the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Girgashites, and the Jebusites.Genesis 15:17-21

A furnace and a lamp appeared on the offerings, passing between those pieces laid on an altar: a testament signed by God.

A doctrine worth knowing • Genesis 15:16

The Reprobate Doctrine

One of the Most Overlooked but Relevant Doctrines in the Bible

One of the most important doctrines, which touches on both the spiritual nature of man and a warning to heed here on earth, is the reprobate doctrine. It is taught throughout both Old and New Testament, which not only gives description of how one becomes a reprobate, but example after example of those that do, and warning after warning of how to handle them. Reprobation, the final and utter rejection by God of those who have finally and utterly rejected His overtures of grace, the call to Christ and the prodding and patience of the Holy Ghost, is one of the three spiritual states of man.
