John 8:1–11

The Woman Taken in Adultery

✦ ✦ ✦

Moses in the law commanded

Jesus went unto the mount of Olives. And early in the morning he came again into the temple, and all the people came unto him; and he sat down, and taught them. And the scribes and Pharisees brought unto him a woman taken in adultery; and when they had set her in the midst, They say unto him, Master, this woman was taken in adultery, in the very act. Now Moses in the law commanded us, that such should be stoned: but what sayest thou? This they said, tempting him, that they might have to accuse him. But Jesus stooped down, and with his finger wrote on the ground, as though he heard them not.John 8:1-6

He that is without sin

So when they continued asking him, he lifted up himself, and said unto them, He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her. And again he stooped down, and wrote on the ground. And they which heard it, being convicted by their own conscience, went out one by one, beginning at the eldest, even unto the last: and Jesus was left alone, and the woman standing in the midst. When Jesus had lifted up himself, and saw none but the woman, he said unto her, Woman, where are those thine accusers? hath no man condemned thee? She said, No man, Lord. And Jesus said unto her, Neither do I condemn thee: go, and sin no more.John 8:7-11

The Law demanded death for both parties, yet only the woman was brought. Jesus did not abolish the Law; He exposed the accusers’ own guilt. His verdict was not cheap grace, “go, and sin no more,” but mercy that upheld the standard while extending redemption. Grace does not erase the Law; it fulfills it.

The Full Picture · Genesis to Revelation

Marriage: From Eden to Eternity

The Bible opens with a wedding: one man, one woman, one flesh in the garden of God. The Bible closes with a wedding: the Lamb and His bride, the redeemed of all ages, clothed in white, gathered at the marriage supper of the Lamb.

Every earthly marriage is a shadow of that eternal union. Christ is the Bridegroom. The Church is His bride. He gave Himself for her, He is coming for her, and He will present her to Himself: holy, without blemish, in fine linen, clean and white.

This is a great mystery: but I speak concerning Christ and the church.Ephesians 5:32

What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder.
