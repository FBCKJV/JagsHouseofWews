vv. 1–3

Lot Sat in the Gate of Sodom

Two Angels at Even

And there came two angels to Sodom at even; and Lot sat in the gate of Sodom: and Lot seeing them rose up to meet them; and he bowed himself with his face toward the ground; And he said, Behold now, my lords, turn in, I pray you, into your servant’s house, and tarry all night, and wash your feet, and ye shall rise up early, and go on your ways. And they said, Nay; but we will abide in the street all night. And he pressed upon them greatly; and they turned in unto him, and entered into his house; and he made them a feast, and did bake unleavened bread, and they did eat.Genesis 19:1-3

And righteous Lot, saved by faith while in the camp of Abram, who pitched his tent towards Sodom and Gomorrah, on the plains of the Jordan, representing the saved man who is taken in by the world rather than living a life of separation. Worldly. Carnal. Vexed in the spirit by the filthy conversation around him, but mired in it.

Application • The reprobate

Sins All Too Common to Man

Of Which I Am Chief

We know from scripture that while man's heart is wicked, and left to our own devices, living in the flesh that all manner of natural sin afflicts us if we don't walk in the spirit, daily. If we don't feed our new man, as Paul calls it, but rather live carnally giving in to the old man. And for the unbelieving, this is doubly true. All forms of evil we can get up to. Lust, envyings, lying, fearfulness, adultery, fornication, whoremongering, harlotry, drunkenness, stealing, wrath and railing, greed and vengeance. Profaning what's holy, even blaspheming God or Jesus' name.

Sins all too common to man. We are all sinners, of which I am chief.

These Sins Have Their Reward

The Wages of Sin Is Death

And these sins have their reward. The wages of sin is death. And for the saved, the chastisement from God in this life, and a loss of rewards and blessings in this life and the next. Even being taken home to the Lord early, being so lost in sin despite our being saved. Many such cases.

Application • The reprobate

Given Over

A Free Will Choice

Reprobates have chosen to reject God to the point they are past saving, and God gives them what they want, and in doing so they are now without conscience, and the wickedness of their hearts turns to even unnatural things.

So many people struggle with this doctrine. Even when shown the examples throughout the Bible, and knowing the judgment of God of what they are, how they came to be, what they do, and what they deserve, people still refuse to see it.

We are given the process, the order of operations as it were, that reprobation like salvation is a free will choice. That God provides chances to accept the truth, and they reject it.

In Exodus 7 we are shown the process, as we see Pharaoh given multiple chances and he hardens his heart to the truth, until God gives him up, gives him over, and hardens his heart Himself.

Application • Genesis 19:17

Flee the Plains

Despite Knowing Better

As everyone and everything around us falls into wickedness. Sodomites glorified and rapists and murderers hailed. Every depravity known to man, even the spoiling of children. And yet we cling to the worldly and the profane, despite knowing better. Despite having the Spirit within us. Despite our being vexed constantly by the filthy conversation of the reprobates and unregenerate man around us.

God wants us to go to the safety of the mountain and flee the plains. To go where the living may be harder and perhaps more dangerous, but far away from the wicked and their vices. And under the protection of God.

It's a good lesson to learn especially if you're struggling in sin.

vv. 23–25

Brimstone and Fire from the LORD

Out of Heaven

The sun was risen upon the earth when Lot entered into Zoar. Then the Lord rained upon Sodom and upon Gomorrah brimstone and fire from the Lord out of heaven; And he overthrew those cities, and all the plain, and all the inhabitants of the cities, and that which grew upon the ground.Genesis 19:23-25

And so, knowing that the judgment of God is upon these wicked people, we see that His righteous wrath is poured out on them. Just as Sodom and Gomorrah and the surrounding cities of the plain were destroyed by fire and brimstone, called down from Heaven, so too will the earth be subject to His wrath in the time to come.

vv. 36–38

Moab and Ammon

The Father of the Moabites unto This Day

Thus were both the daughters of Lot with child by their father. And the first born bare a son, and called his name Moab: the same is the father of the Moabites unto this day. And the younger, she also bare a son, and called his name Benammi: the same is the father of the children of Ammon unto this day.Genesis 19:36-38

Having lost their mother and home and betrothed husbands, and seeing the wrath poured out on the plains and the cities of the plain, they think it is the end of them. And so this evil seems reasonable to them. And they birth the nations of the Moabites and Ammon.
