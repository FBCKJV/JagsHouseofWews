Genesis 12 • The Story of Abram

All of Genesis Was Prologue

The Promise of the Man Christ Jesus

All of Genesis, as important as the stories of Adam, Cain, Noah, the flood and Babel are, was prologue to this: the story of Abram and Sarai. Abraham and Sarah.

Up to this point, mankind who called upon the name of the Lord had the promise of His blessing and protection, of God being their Saviour, but not the specific promise of the man Christ Jesus. Born of man. Seed of Abraham. Here we see that the Lord appears unto Abram and makes him promises.

vv. 1–3

Get Thee Out of Thy Country

I Will Make of Thee a Great Nation

Now the Lord had said unto Abram, Get thee out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and from thy father’s house, unto a land that I will shew thee: And I will make of thee a great nation, and I will bless thee, and make thy name great; and thou shalt be a blessing: And I will bless them that bless thee, and curse him that curseth thee: and in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.Genesis 12:1-3

These are direct blessings and promises to Abram himself. Many try to take the personal promise to Abraham, to bless those that bless him and curse those that curse him, and claim it’s an eternal promise to the modern Jew and the apostate state calling itself Israel. This is found nowhere in scripture. This is God’s promise to protect him and his wife, as God has called Abraham to go to a different land, to Canaan, and there He would establish his legacy.

vv. 4–5

So Abram Departed

Seventy and Five Years Old

So Abram departed, as the Lord had spoken unto him; and Lot went with him: and Abram was seventy and five years old when he departed out of Haran. And Abram took Sarai his wife, and Lot his brother’s son, and all their substance that they had gathered, and the souls that they had gotten in Haran; and they went forth to go into the land of Canaan; and into the land of Canaan they came.Genesis 12:4-5

This promise was made to a 75 year old, childless Abram. And Abram took Sarai his wife, and Lot his brother’s son, and all their substance that they had gathered, and the souls that they had gotten in Haran; and they went forth to go into the land of Canaan.

vv. 6–8

Unto Thy Seed Will I Give This Land

He Called upon the Name of the Lord

And Abram passed through the land unto the place of Sichem, unto the plain of Moreh. And the Canaanite was then in the land. And the Lord appeared unto Abram, and said, Unto thy seed will I give this land: and there builded he an altar unto the Lord, who appeared unto him. And he removed from thence unto a mountain on the east of Bethel, and pitched his tent, having Bethel on the west, and Hai on the east: and there he builded an altar unto the Lord, and called upon the name of the Lord.Genesis 12:6-8

There, God appeared again to him, and gave this more specific promise, in a place where the Canaanite was then in the land. His altar built between the Mediterranean and Jordan, between Galilee and Jerusalem.

He was saved by faith through grace. Note the timing of when that was. It comes up later in scripture. Genesis 17 ↗

vv. 9–10

A Famine in the Land

Abram Went Down into Egypt

And Abram journeyed, going on still toward the south. And there was a famine in the land: and Abram went down into Egypt to sojourn there; for the famine was grievous in the land.Genesis 12:9-10

Next we are given a familiar story of a famine in the land, of going to Egypt to sojourn, of Pharaoh and plagues. This theme of finding refuge and sojourning in Egypt is a picture of young Jesus being taken there by Joseph, just as Jesus’ stepfather’s namesake will.

vv. 11–13

Say, I Pray Thee, Thou Art My Sister

A Lapse of Faith

And it came to pass, when he was come near to enter into Egypt, that he said unto Sarai his wife, Behold now, I know that thou art a fair woman to look upon: Therefore it shall come to pass, when the Egyptians shall see thee, that they shall say, This is his wife: and they will kill me, but they will save thee alive. Say, I pray thee, thou art my sister: that it may be well with me for thy sake; and my soul shall live because of thee.Genesis 12:11-13

There in Egypt, worried that Sarai, his 65 year old wife, who was still apparently very fair to look upon, would be taken from him, and that he would be killed, Abram tells her to lie and say they are siblings.

vv. 14–16

Taken into Pharaoh’s House

The Woman Was Very Fair

And it came to pass, that, when Abram was come into Egypt, the Egyptians beheld the woman that she was very fair. The princes also of Pharaoh saw her, and commended her before Pharaoh: and the woman was taken into Pharaoh’s house. And he entreated Abram well for her sake: and he had sheep, and oxen, and he asses, and menservants, and maidservants, and she asses, and camels.Genesis 12:14-16
vv. 17–20

The Lord Plagued Pharaoh

Take Her, and Go Thy Way

And the Lord plagued Pharaoh and his house with great plagues because of Sarai Abram’s wife. And Pharaoh called Abram, and said, What is this that thou hast done unto me? why didst thou not tell me that she was thy wife? Why saidst thou, She is my sister? so I might have taken her to me to wife: now therefore behold thy wife, take her, and go thy way. And Pharaoh commanded his men concerning him: and they sent him away, and his wife, and all that he had.Genesis 12:17-20

This seems to be a lack of faith by Abram, and causes enmity with Pharaoh, who indeed was going to seek to betroth Sarai, but now, subject to plagues by the Lord, angrily asks them to leave the shelter of his kingdom. God cursed those who would curse Abram, despite his lapse of faith. God made that promise to him, and if he trusted God, he wouldn’t fear for his life. As it stood, God came through anyway, but Abram and all his household were sent away, back to Canaan and the famine.

Application

Like Abram, Many of Us Have Doubts

Faithful unto Death

God makes promises to us who believe: to be blessed, to be heirs, to be a blessing to others, to be protected by Him in faith and saved from death, in the imputed righteousness of Jesus. By His grace and mercy. Unfortunately, like Abram, a man of great faith, many of us have doubts. We try to arrange matters and control circumstances out of fear. Sometimes even with convenient lies. Abram, who by faith moved his entire house to a strange land at the behest of God, was never told to go to Egypt in the first place. Let alone to lie to save his own skin.

We should not fear famines or tribulations or even persecution of unregenerate man. We should be faithful unto death, convinced of the hope that we shall be delivered. Even Abram, the father of the seed that contained Christ incarnate, doubted and was weak. But we will see, his faith grew stronger the more he walked with God.

By Faith Abraham Obeyed

Hebrews 11:8

By faith Abraham, when he was called to go out into a place which he should after receive for an inheritance, obeyed; and he went out, not knowing whither he went.Hebrews 11:8

James 2:23

And the scripture was fulfilled which saith, Abraham believed God, and it was imputed unto him for righteousness: and he was called the Friend of God.James 2:23

Abraham believed. This is the verb form of faith, the noun, the thing Abraham had. He believed, having faith in God’s word, His promise. And that faith was imputed unto him for righteousness, and he was called the Friend of God.

Cross references • Genesis 12:4

By Faith Abraham Obeyed, continued

Romans 4:3-5

For what saith the scripture? Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness. Now to him that worketh is the reward not reckoned of grace, but of debt. But to him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, his faith is counted for righteousness.Romans 4:3-5

If we believe, an action, if we have faith, the evidence of things unseen, a thing to possess, in the word of God, His promises, His testimony, we have His righteousness imputed on us. Not our own righteousness. Not our works. But our faith, which comes from believing, which comes from the word of God.

O, to be called the friend of God for our faith!

Salvation Is by Faith Alone

Salvation has always been by hearing the word of God, believing it, which gives us faith to call upon the name of the Lord, for which God grants us His grace. Salvation is by faith alone. Yesterday, today and for ever, amen.
