vv. 1–3

Be Fruitful, and Multiply

Every Moving Thing That Liveth Shall Be Meat

And God blessed Noah and his sons, and said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth. And the fear of you and the dread of you shall be upon every beast of the earth, and upon every fowl of the air, upon all that moveth upon the earth, and upon all the fishes of the sea; into your hand are they delivered. Every moving thing that liveth shall be meat for you; even as the green herb have I given you all things.Genesis 9:1-3

God blesses Noah and his sons, gives commandments and a covenant with all living things in the earth. Blessings, commandments and promises: this is the pattern of things with God when those who are faithful and saved by His grace walk with God. God tells Noah and his sons to be fruitful and multiply, birthing all nations of man. To that end, He reminds them that all animals are meat to eat, with no restrictions.

v. 4

But Flesh with the Blood

The Life Thereof Shall Ye Not Eat

But flesh with the life thereof, which is the blood thereof, shall ye not eat.Genesis 9:4

Of the clean and unclean animals they may eat, but only the clean for sacrifice to God, being they represent Christ Jesus. There is no restriction on what they can eat, provided they drain the blood, the life thereof. Of that they should not eat.

Cross references • Genesis 9:3–4

Clean and Unclean

Genesis 7:2

Of every clean beast thou shalt take to thee by sevens, the male and his female: and of beasts that are not clean by two, the male and his female.Genesis 7:2

As we saw in chapter 7, Noah had seven pair, male and female, of every clean beast, that he could give sacrifice in thanksgiving to God for saving his family in chapter 8.

Hebrews 9:10

Which stood only in meats and drinks, and divers washings, and carnal ordinances, imposed on them until the time of reformation.Hebrews 9:10

This was replaced for a time for Israel with strict dietary laws against eating unclean beasts under Mosaic law, which, along with the priestly and carnal ordinances, were done away with in Christ.

vv. 5–6

Blood for Blood

For in the Image of God Made He Man

And surely your blood of your lives will I require; at the hand of every beast will I require it, and at the hand of man; at the hand of every man’s brother will I require the life of man. Whoso sheddeth man’s blood, by man shall his blood be shed: for in the image of God made he man.Genesis 9:5-6

God institutes the death penalty, capital punishment for the shedding of blood. Before the flood, from Cain on, the requirement for blood didn’t exist, and evil propagated. Here, God established a new understanding for man: that we were to enforce His law, blood for blood, for in the image of God made he man.

v. 7

Bring Forth Abundantly

Replenish the Earth

And you, be ye fruitful, and multiply; bring forth abundantly in the earth, and multiply therein.Genesis 9:7

God commands again that man be fruitful and multiply. Replenish the earth, bringing forth life abundantly.

vv. 8–11

My Covenant with You

Neither Shall There Any More Be a Flood

And God spake unto Noah, and to his sons with him, saying, And I, behold, I establish my covenant with you, and with your seed after you; And with every living creature that is with you, of the fowl, of the cattle, and of every beast of the earth with you; from all that go out of the ark, to every beast of the earth. And I will establish my covenant with you; neither shall all flesh be cut off any more by the waters of a flood; neither shall there any more be a flood to destroy the earth.Genesis 9:8-11

God promises all life that He would never destroy the earth and all living things again by flood.

vv. 12–17

The Token of the Covenant

I Do Set My Bow in the Cloud

And God said, This is the token of the covenant which I make between me and you and every living creature that is with you, for perpetual generations: I do set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a token of a covenant between me and the earth. And it shall come to pass, when I bring a cloud over the earth, that the bow shall be seen in the cloud: And I will remember my covenant, which is between me and you and every living creature of all flesh; and the waters shall no more become a flood to destroy all flesh. And the bow shall be in the cloud; and I will look upon it, that I may remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature of all flesh that is upon the earth. And God said unto Noah, This is the token of the covenant, which I have established between me and all flesh that is upon the earth.Genesis 9:12-17

He gives the rainbow as His reminder, a token of the covenant between Him and the earth.

vv. 18–19

Shem, Ham, and Japheth

Of Them Was the Whole Earth Overspread

And the sons of Noah, that went forth of the ark, were Shem, and Ham, and Japheth: and Ham is the father of Canaan. These are the three sons of Noah: and of them was the whole earth overspread.Genesis 9:18-19

And so the Bible tells us of Shem, and Ham, and Japheth, and that Ham is the father of Canaan. Of these three was the whole earth overspread. The next chapter of Genesis goes into detail of the generations and nations of them, but for the next part of chapter 9, we need to know Ham’s son Canaan. Genesis 10 ↗

vv. 20–23

Noah Began to Be an Husbandman

He Drank of the Wine, and Was Drunken

And Noah began to be an husbandman, and he planted a vineyard: And he drank of the wine, and was drunken; and he was uncovered within his tent. And Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father, and told his two brethren without. And Shem and Japheth took a garment, and laid it upon both their shoulders, and went backward, and covered the nakedness of their father; and their faces were backward, and they saw not their father’s nakedness.Genesis 9:20-23

Noah, retired from ark building, becomes a husbandman, a grower of grapes. The Bible says he drank his own wine and became drunk, and that Ham saw his nakedness as he was passed out in his tent, and that he called his brothers to see.

vv. 24–25

What His Younger Son Had Done

Cursed Be Canaan

And Noah awoke from his wine, and knew what his younger son had done unto him. And he said, Cursed be Canaan; a servant of servants shall he be unto his brethren.Genesis 9:24-25

There is a lot of speculation as to what this means. Most agree that Ham did something unspeakable to Noah in his condition. The Bible doesn’t say that, and generally, God’s word isn’t shy about naming the acts done by the wicked. The implication comes from Noah’s reaction and punishment, which is severe.

vv. 26–27

Blessed Be the Lord God of Shem

Shem Blessed, Japheth Enlarged, Canaan a Servant

And he said, Blessed be the Lord God of Shem; and Canaan shall be his servant. God shall enlarge Japheth, and he shall dwell in the tents of Shem; and Canaan shall be his servant.Genesis 9:26-27

He curses Canaan to be servants of Shem and of Japheth, the nations that spring from each son. He blesses Shem with the blessing of the Lord God, as the oldest. This is the blessing of Christ through his seed. And he blesses Japheth with abundance, to be enlarged greatly. There is a lot of speculative theology based off this curse, and some of the conclusions drawn aren’t supported in scripture. Take secular takes on a spiritual matter with the appropriate grains of salt.

vv. 28–29

Nine Hundred and Fifty Years

The Last of the Long-Lived Men

And Noah lived after the flood three hundred and fifty years. And all the days of Noah were nine hundred and fifty years: and he died.Genesis 9:28-29

Noah lives 350 years following the flood, a total of 950, but he is the last of the antediluvian long-lived men.

Application

The Deeper Things of God

Mercy, Judgment, Grace and Salvation

There are, of course, deeper things of God: His promises and blessings, the salvation of Christ mirroring the salvation of the ark. But the predominantly secular focus on hidden meaning, esoteric knowledge, the hyperfocus on race and genealogies of man, conspiracies and Jewish fables have all but flooded out those truths in the minds of man. Even saved Christians get sucked in to endless strivings about the meaning of some of what’s revealed to us in God’s word.

What’s important to Bible believing Christians is to draw spiritual application from scripture, to understand the things of God which He revealed to us from the beginning. Mercy, judgment, grace and salvation. The fallen nature of man and the prevalence of sin while we are in the flesh. The righteousness of the living God.

Give Thanks

As we give thanks to God for our salvation and the blessings He bestows, the same way Noah gave thanks after leaving the ark, we should try to keep in mind the spiritual, looking forward to that better country and the feast of Christ Jesus, knowing that man’s relationship with God has always been, from the beginning. Amen.
