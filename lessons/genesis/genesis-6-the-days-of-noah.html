A study in Genesis 6

The Days of Noah

Man’s wickedness, God’s judgment, and grace found by faith.

Only a short while after creation

Genesis 6 is not far removed from the garden. Adding the ages given in Genesis 5, the whole world before the Flood fits inside a few overlapping lifetimes.

930Adam dies. Noah’s father Lamech is already 56.
1056Noah is born.
1656Methuselah dies, at 969.
1656The Flood, in Noah’s 600th year.

Years counted from creation by the ages in Genesis 5 and 7.

Men who had heard of Eden from Adam’s own mouth were alive when Noah’s father was born. And still, within ten generations, mankind had fallen so utterly that it corrupted the earth itself.

The pattern of salvation

The story of the patriarchs from Adam to Noah is a pattern of salvation given to us by God. Man has always been saved by grace through faith.

By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house; by the which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith.Hebrews 11:7

Noah walked as Enoch walked

“Enoch walked with God.” “Noah walked with God.” A just man, perfect in his generations, who found grace in the eyes of the Lord.

Grace, then and now

“For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God.”

When men began to multiply

1And it came to pass, when men began to multiply on the face of the earth, and daughters were born unto them, 2That the sons of God saw the daughters of men that they were fair; and they took them wives of all which they chose. 3And the Lord said, My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that he also is flesh: yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty years.Genesis 6:1–3
  • The sons of God chose wives by sight: “that they were fair.” Not by faith, but by what pleased the eye.
  • The Lord said His Spirit would not always strive with man, “for that he also is flesh.”
  • To limit the wickedness of man, God set his days at an hundred and twenty years, and man’s natural life began to shorten.

Who are the sons of God? This study reads them as believers, those who “call upon the name of the Lord.” Faithful believers differ here, and we cover both views in depth in Genesis 6 – Sons of God.

Giants in the earth

There were giants in the earth in those days; and also after that, when the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men, and they bare children to them, the same became mighty men which were of old, men of renown.Genesis 6:4

Giants were already there

“In those days,” before the text describes the unions. And “also after that.”

The children were men

Mighty men of renown. Of great stature, no doubt, as we see in Goliath and his people, and even occasionally today.

The genetic potential of man is what it is, divinely made.

Only evil continually

And God saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually.Genesis 6:5

This is God’s judgment of man. He tells us it was the wickedness of man that caused Him to repent of our creation.

It repented the Lord

6And it repented the Lord that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him at his heart. 7And the Lord said, I will destroy man whom I have created from the face of the earth; both man, and beast, and the creeping thing, and the fowls of the air; for it repenteth me that I have made them. 8But Noah found grace in the eyes of the Lord.Genesis 6:6–8
  • God repents, turning in His dealings given the outcome, and it grieves Him at His heart.
  • He resolves to destroy man, and with him beast, creeping thing, and fowl, for the earth too had become corrupt.
  • But Noah found grace in the eyes of the Lord.

The generations of Noah

9These are the generations of Noah: Noah was a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah walked with God. 10And Noah begat three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.Genesis 6:9–10

This is the prologue to the generations of Noah. God sets out to destroy His creation, but for the sake of one just man, Noah, He offers grace and gives a path to salvation, for Noah and his house: his wife, his three sons, and their wives.

The earth filled with violence

11The earth also was corrupt before God, and the earth was filled with violence. 12And God looked upon the earth, and, behold, it was corrupt; for all flesh had corrupted his way upon the earth. 13And God said unto Noah, The end of all flesh is come before me; for the earth is filled with violence through them; and, behold, I will destroy them with the earth.Genesis 6:11–13

Blood cries from the ground

As we saw in the murder of Abel, the earth itself is corrupted by our sin and the spilling of innocent blood.

Blood defiles the land

This is established Biblical doctrine. Nations fall by this very thing, and here all the earth has been made corrupt by man.

It is man that has fallen

The story of the Flood is clear throughout: it is man’s own wickedness that God is punishing. Humanity has fallen. Man is corrupt and wicked of heart, the same then as it is today.

As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one.Romans 3:10
For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God.Romans 3:23

Only Noah stayed faithful, God’s Spirit striving with him, but not so with the rest of mankind.

Evil is not something foreign

Many false doctrines place evil as something “other”: introduced by bloodlines, genetics, or ancestry, with salvation only for a certain people, chosen by nothing but their genes. Genesis 6 shows that it is man’s own wickedness that destroys us.

And hath made of one blood all nations of men for to dwell on all the face of the earth…Acts 17:26  

If man is lost by his genes

then he is condemned by a god of bloodlines, not the God of the Bible.

If man is saved by a quality given him

then he has no free will to receive or reject Christ.

If man is judged for another’s sin

then he has no soul liberty to be saved by his own faith in Christ.

We are all one blood: given to the same temptations of the flesh, the same sinful nature, the same free will, and the same need for salvation.

The oldest excuse

And the man said, The woman whom thou gavest to be with me, she gave me of the tree, and I did eat.Genesis 3:12  

Since the garden, Adam blaming his sin on Eve and on God for creating her, man has looked for an excuse to justify himself before the Lord: push it off on the devil for offering temptation, or place it on the sins of another.

Blame the flood on the demonic lineage of half-human chimeras, and we are really saying, “Maybe it’s not our fault, God.” But God knows our heart, and He says the thoughts of man’s heart were only evil continually.

Why this matters for salvation

This understanding is the basis for faith in God for salvation: the knowledge that we are wicked and sinful creatures, deserving death and Hell, and in need of saving.

For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.Romans 6:23

To walk with God, to strive with His Spirit, requires His grace and His promise of salvation. Not of ourselves, but as a gift of God.

God’s plan of salvation: the ark

14Make thee an ark of gopher wood; rooms shalt thou make in the ark, and shalt pitch it within and without with pitch. 15And this is the fashion which thou shalt make it of: The length of the ark shall be three hundred cubits, the breadth of it fifty cubits, and the height of it thirty cubits. 16A window shalt thou make to the ark, and in a cubit shalt thou finish it above; and the door of the ark shalt thou set in the side thereof; with lower, second, and third stories shalt thou make it.Genesis 6:14–16
300cubits long, about 450 feet
50cubits wide, about 75 feet
30cubits high, about 45 feet

Cubits taken as about eighteen inches. God gave Noah the size, the material, the type, and the purpose: one ark, one door.

“With thee will I establish my covenant”

17And, behold, I, even I, do bring a flood of waters upon the earth, to destroy all flesh, wherein is the breath of life, from under heaven; and every thing that is in the earth shall die. 18But with thee will I establish my covenant; and thou shalt come into the ark, thou, and thy sons, and thy wife, and thy sons’ wives with thee. 19And of every living thing of all flesh, two of every sort shalt thou bring into the ark, to keep them alive with thee; they shall be male and female. 20Of fowls after their kind, and of cattle after their kind, of every creeping thing of the earth after his kind, two of every sort shall come unto thee, to keep them alive. 21And take thou unto thee of all food that is eaten, and thou shalt gather it to thee, and it shall be for food for thee, and for them.Genesis 6:17–21

Judgment and salvation come in the same breath. The same God who brings the Flood makes a covenant to preserve the ones who come into the ark: “wherein few, that is, eight souls were saved by water.”

Genesis 6:22

Thus did Noah

“Thus did Noah; according to all that God commanded him, so did he.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Faith obeys. Noah, warned of things not seen as yet, built what God told him to build, the way God told him to build it.

A figure of baptism

The Flood cleansed the earth of its wickedness in a global baptism, itself a picture of the death, burial, and resurrection of Christ.

The like figure whereunto even baptism doth also now save us (not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the answer of a good conscience toward God,) by the resurrection of Jesus Christ.1 Peter 3:21  
Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death: that like as Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life.Romans 6:4

The waters that judged the world bore up the ark. Those in it passed through death into a new world.

As the days of Noah were

But as the days of Noe were, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be.Matthew 24:37  
  • Eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage: things continue as ever. Man is just as caught up in the things of this world.
  • Like Noah, we who are obedient to the Gospel follow God’s instructions to our salvation, mindful of the times we are in.
  • So we keep watch of the skies for His return in glory.
