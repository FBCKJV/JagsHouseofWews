v. 1

Behold, Here I Am

God Did Tempt Abraham

And it came to pass after these things, that God did tempt Abraham, and said unto him, Abraham: and he said, Behold, here I am.Genesis 22:1

Here I am. (Remember this for later)

v. 2

Thine Only Son Isaac

Get Thee into the Land of Moriah

And he said, Take now thy son, thine only son Isaac, whom thou lovest, and get thee into the land of Moriah; and offer him there for a burnt offering upon one of the mountains which I will tell thee of.Genesis 22:2

(2 Chronicles 3:1 tells us that Moriah is where Jerusalem is). And offer him there for a burnt offering (Jesus three days and nights in Hell) upon one of the mountains which I will tell thee of (Golgotha?)

Cross references • Genesis 22:2

Mount Moriah

2 Chronicles 3:1

Then Solomon began to build the house of the Lord at Jerusalem in mount Moriah, where the Lord appeared unto David his father, in the place that David had prepared in the threshingfloor of Ornan the Jebusite.2 Chronicles 3:1

Of course the sacrifice of Christ Jesus recalls Genesis 22, Abraham and Isaac; the purification sacrifice laid out in Leviticus 14, the mount in Jerusalem being where Abraham brought his son.

vv. 3–4

On the Third Day

He Saw the Place Afar Off

And Abraham rose up early in the morning, and saddled his ass, and took two of his young men with him, and Isaac his son, and clave the wood for the burnt offering, and rose up, and went unto the place of which God had told him. Then on the third day Abraham lifted up his eyes, and saw the place afar off.Genesis 22:3-4

On the third day, (obvious). (Jesus lifting up his eyes rising up from hell)

v. 5

And Come Again to You

I and the Lad Will Go Yonder and Worship

And Abraham said unto his young men, Abide ye here with the ass; and I and the lad will go yonder and worship, and come again to you.Genesis 22:5

(Abraham had no doubt that even if he slay his son that God would resurrect him).

v. 6

Laid It upon Isaac His Son

They Went Both of Them Together

And Abraham took the wood of the burnt offering, and laid it upon Isaac his son; and he took the fire in his hand, and a knife; and they went both of them together.Genesis 22:6

(Isaac the sacrifice carrying wood up the hill of Golgotha in Jerusalem)

vv. 7–8

God Will Provide Himself a Lamb

Where Is the Lamb?

And Isaac spake unto Abraham his father, and said, My father: and he said, Here am I, my son. And he said, Behold the fire and the wood: but where is the lamb for a burnt offering? And Abraham said, My son, God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering: so they went both of them together.Genesis 22:7-8

(God literally is providing himself as the lamb for the burnt offering. That's carefully phrased, don't miss it!)

vv. 9–10

Abraham Stretched Forth His Hand

Bound Isaac His Son

And they came to the place which God had told him of; and Abraham built an altar there, and laid the wood in order, and bound Isaac his son, and laid him on the altar upon the wood. And Abraham stretched forth his hand, and took the knife to slay his son.Genesis 22:9-10
v. 11

Here Am I

Abraham, Abraham

And the angel of the Lord called unto him out of heaven, and said, Abraham, Abraham: and he said, Here am I.Genesis 22:11

(Here I am is where you brought yourself. Here am I is a place where you've been brought)

v. 12

Thine Only Son

Now I Know That Thou Fearest God

And he said, Lay not thine hand upon the lad, neither do thou any thing unto him: for now I know that thou fearest God, seeing thou hast not withheld thy son, thine only son from me.Genesis 22:12

(Again. Note Isaac isn't Abraham's only son, he has an older son Ishmael, God is being precise for a reason, this is a picture of Jesus)

v. 13

A Ram Caught in a Thicket

In the Stead of His Son

And Abraham lifted up his eyes, and looked, and behold behind him a ram caught in a thicket by his horns: and Abraham went and took the ram, and offered him up for a burnt offering in the stead of his son.Genesis 22:13

(From the crown of thorns to being the burnt offering in place of Abraham's son, God placed his only begotten son)

Cross references • Genesis 22:13

Two Birds Alive and Clean

Leviticus 14:4

Then shall the priest command to take for him that is to be cleansed two birds alive and clean, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop:Leviticus 14:4

Leviticus 14:5-6

And the priest shall command that one of the birds be killed in an earthen vessel over running water: As for the living bird, he shall take it, and the cedar wood, and the scarlet, and the hyssop, and shall dip them and the living bird in the blood of the bird that was killed over the running water:Leviticus 14:5-6

Isaac who carried the wood up the hill, to be the burnt offering. God providing the ram, caught by the head in a thicket of thorns. We see God tell Moses in Leviticus that the sacrifice for him that is to be cleansed two birds alive and clean, and cedar wood, and scarlet, and hyssop.

Cross references • Genesis 22:13, continued

Everything Points to Jesus

So That the Jews Would Have No Excuse

We see the wood of the cross, the scarlet of the blood, and the hyssop, the same used by the Roman guard to give Jesus the vinegar to drink.

We see one bird placed in an earthen vessel, as Jesus was placed in flesh. We see it sacrificed and burned, its blood put on the living bird, which is then set free.

Everything points to Jesus and everything in the law was a picture of Him to come, so that the Jews would have no excuse.

Amen!
