If you died today, do you know for sure that you would go to Heaven? Many people have never even been asked. The Bible says you can know.

1 John 5:13These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life, and that ye may believe on the name of the Son of God.

The Bible tells us that we can know, today, that we have eternal life. A free gift given by the grace of the Father through faith on His Son, Christ Jesus.

To be saved, there are a few simple truths the Bible asks us to understand and believe. Each one is God’s word, not man’s.

Calling on Him

It isn’t the words of a prayer that save. It is believing in your heart, and calling out to Christ in that faith for the forgiveness of your sins and the gift of eternal life. If you believe, you can call on Him in your own words, or like this:

Lord Jesus, I know that I am a sinner, and that I deserve death and Hell. I believe that You died on the cross for my sins, that You were buried, and that You rose again the third day. I can’t save myself, and I’m not trusting in anything I do. I’m trusting You alone. Please forgive me of all my sins, and give me the gift of eternal life. Thank You for saving me. Amen.

If you have called upon His name in faith, now what?

You are saved. You have eternal life, sealed by the Holy Spirit of promise, and you shall never perish. Now:

  • Be baptized as a believer, the answer of a good conscience toward God. The local church
  • Find a church that preaches salvation by grace through faith from the King James Bible. Finding a church
  • Read your Bible every day, and tell others. Chapter studies · Doctrines
