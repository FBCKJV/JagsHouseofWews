And please don't get me wrong, James 2 is incredibly important lesson in how we as Christians should carry ourselves and use our faith to be effective for others through our works.

It's also crucial when understanding how others perceive us and that they are respecters of persons even though God isn't, and we shouldn't be.

It's also brilliant in how it's written, showing the Spirit of God in the words, framing the rhetorical question of what a man might say to a Christian to illustrate the points.

But of course we know from scripture that Abraham was saved by faith alone, and wasn't justified or made righteous by his works decades later, willing to sacrifice Isaac, long after he accepted the Lord and called upon his name for salvation.

We know this from reading the old testament, but more so we know it from the examples Paul uses regarding this in his Epistles.

Quoting Genesis 15:6 "And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness." in Romans 4:3-5:

3 For what saith the scripture? Abraham believed God, and it was counted unto him for righteousness.

4 Now to him that worketh is the reward not reckoned of grace, but of debt.

5 But to him that worketh not, but believeth on him that justifieth the ungodly, his faith is counted for righteousness.

So, if these clear contradictions of the "faith without works is dead" interpretation can't be wrong, and if the snippet of out of context lines from James 2 cannot be wrong, then the interpretation, that James is saying Abraham wasn't saved until he was justified before God by his works instead of before man, must be wrong.

This is made evident in simply reading the full James 2 epistle and understand what's being talked about,, and comparing scripture with scripture, the litany of verses explaining the Gospel is by faith alone in Christ to to justify us, and accept God's forgiveness and eternal life, given in Grace, in return for that faith.

Even he who worketh not...

Because it is a gift. “Not of works, lest any man should boast.”

Listen to me when I tell you that if your preacher priest or pastor is teaching you that you need to do works in order to be saved, they are lying to you to control you and to keep you from the knowledge of the Gospel of the Lord.

Only Jesus saves, and he doesn't need our help for that. He does need our help in winning souls to Christ that can only be done by those who are redeemed in his blood and who have accepted him as their only means of salvation without works. His work alone perfect. Complete. If he is adding to that salvation with extra steps? Run. Then fall to your knees and call upon Christ alone to redeem you.

"But though we, or an angel from heaven, preach any other gospel unto you than that which we have preached unto you, let him be accursed."
