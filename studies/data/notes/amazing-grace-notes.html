Amazing grace! How sweet the sound,

That saved a wretch like me!

I once was lost, but now am found,

Was blind, but now I see

That hymn, “Amazing Grace” (which is one of my favorites), was written by John Newton in 1772, just before 1776 and American Independence. And as Pastor Tefft has often pointed out, Newton was a truly a “wretch.”

Before his conversion to Christianity, John Newton was a wayward sailor, living a reckless and morally depraved life marked by profanity, drunkenness, debauchery, and involvement in the brutal Atlantic slave trade. And the song is about how amazing God's grace is, “To save a wretch like me.”

And that is true for all of us. What an amazing, glorious and wonderful grace, that God abundantly gives, even when we were wretches. Even as sinners, Christ died for us.

But when I sing Brother Newton's inspired hymn, I often think about the grace God shows me even now. What a wretch I continue to be, and yet God's love is constantly shown to me. The Lord is still with me, and shall not forsake me. His grace for me is not only sufficient, but an amazement. That He loves wretch like me, even me.

How amazing is His grace.
