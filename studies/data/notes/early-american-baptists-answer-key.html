Think it through

A. Establishment (one church, or all Christian churches taxed alike); the middle way (no tax-funded church, but laws and oaths that keep the state friendly to Protestant Christianity); and Baptist soul liberty. Henry’s bill is a general assessment, in the first camp. Jay is the middle way.

B. Even an equal tax makes the state the judge of what counts as a church, and puts the magistrate between a man and God. Romans 14:4 asks who judges another man’s servant, and John 18:36 says Christ’s kingdom is not of this world.

C. Madison: a state that uses religion as an “engine of civil policy” corrupts religion and invites bigotry and persecution. The Baptists: the Gospel “wants not the feeble arm of man for its support,” and compulsion destroys liberty of conscience.
