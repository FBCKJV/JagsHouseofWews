The Names of God

  • Genesis 1:1 – Elohim
  • In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth. (A plural name, showing God's majesty and power)
  • Exodus 3:14 – I AM THAT I AM (YHWH, known as the Tetragrammaton)
  • And God said unto Moses, I AM THAT I AM: and he said, Thus shalt thou say unto the children of Israel, I AM hath sent me unto you. (God's self-existence and eternal nature)
  • Genesis 17:1 – Almighty God [El Shaddai]
  • And when Abram was ninety years old and nine, the LORD appeared to Abram, and said unto him, I am the Almighty God; walk before me, and be thou perfect. (God as the all-powerful and all-sufficient One)
  • Psalm 23:1 – The LORD [Jehovah]
  • The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want. (God as our provider, covenant keeper, and shepherd)
  • Exodus 34:14 – Jealous [Jehovah Qanna]
  • For thou shalt worship no other god: for the LORD, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God: (God's intolerance for competition and demand for exclusive loyalty)
  • Deuteronomy 4:24 – A consuming fire
  • For the LORD thy God is a consuming fire, even a jealous God. (God's holiness and judgment against sin)
  • Psalm 46:1 – God is our refuge and strength [El Ma'oz]
  • God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. (God as our strong, safe place)
  • Isaiah 7:14 – Immanuel
  • Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel. (God with us)
  • Deuteronomy 10:17 – Lord of lords
  • For the LORD your God is God of gods, and Lord of lords, a great God, a mighty, and a terrible, which regardeth not persons, nor taketh reward: (God's supreme authority over all other rulers and gods)
  • Jeremiah 10:10 – The living God
  • But the LORD is the true God, he is the living God, and an everlasting king: at his wrath the earth shall tremble, and the nations shall not be able to abide his indignation. (God as the source of all life, in contrast to dead idols)
  • Matthew 1:21 – Jesus
  • And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins. (The Savior)

The Nature of the Father

  • John 3:16: For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life
  • John 6:27: Labour not for the meat which perisheth, but for that meat which endureth unto everlasting life, which the Son of man shall give unto you: for him hath God the Father sealed. (The Father as the one who gives authority and approves of the Son's work)
  • Ephesians 4:6: One God and Father of all, who is above all, and through all, and in you all. (The Father's position as supreme over all things)
  • 1 Corinthians 8:6: But to us there is but one God, the Father, of whom are all things, and we in him; and one Lord Jesus Christ, by whom are all things, and we by him. (The Father as the ultimate source of all things)
