Expository: James 2:14-17

What doth it profit, my brethren, though a man say he hath faith, and have not works? can faith save him? If a brother or sister be naked, and destitute of daily food, And one of you say unto them, Depart in peace, be ye warmed and filled; notwithstanding ye give them not those things which are needful to the body; what doth it profit? Even so faith, if it hath not works, is dead, being alone.

  • James 2 misunderstood
  • writing to saved Christians, to encourage those of us justified by the law of liberty (James 2:12), to exercise our faith in the service of others
  • What good is our faith if we don’t also do for others?
  • Justified before men “Yea, a man may say Thou hast faith, and I have works: shew me thy faith without thy works”

Knowing this, that God so loved the world He gave His only begotten Son, that Jesus so loved the brethren that He gave His life for us that we may by faith receive the gift of eternal life, to be made sons of God and heirs to the Kingdom and the promises and blessings we have in this life and the next, sealed by the Spirit unto redemption…The least we can do in return is be a blessing to others. To show them the same love as Jesus showed us.
