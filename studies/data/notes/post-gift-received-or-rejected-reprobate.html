For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life...

...The Lord is not slack concerning his promise, as some men count slackness; but is longsuffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.

...For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.

...And to Seth, to him also there was born a son; and he called his name Enos: then began men to call upon the name of the Lord.

...And he believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness

...For had ye believed Moses, ye would have believed me: for he wrote of me

...God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, Hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds;

...the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world.

...Behold, I am laying in Zion a stone, a tested stone,
A costly cornerstone for the foundation, firmly placed.
He who believes in it will not be disturbed.

...Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it.

Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you: For every one that asketh receiveth; and he that seeketh findeth; and to him that knocketh it shall be opened.

...Just as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these men also oppose the truth, men of depraved mind, rejected in regard to the faith.

...And for this cause God shall send them strong delusion, that they should believe a lie: That they all might be damned who believed not the truth, but had pleasure in unrighteousness.

...Because that, when they knew God, they glorified him not as God, neither were thankful; but became vain in their imaginations, and their foolish heart was darkened.
Professing themselves to be wise, they became fools,
And changed the glory of the uncorruptible God into an image made like to corruptible man, and to birds, and fourfooted beasts, and creeping things.Wherefore God also gave them up to uncleanness through the lusts of their own hearts, to dishonour their own bodies between themselves: Who changed the truth of God into a lie, and worshipped and served the creature more than the Creator, who is blessed for ever. Amen.
For this cause God gave them up unto vile affections: for even their women did change the natural use into that which is against nature:

God would all choose the gift of righteousness through the sacrifice of Christ, washed in His blood, the sole propitiation for our sin.

The gift of eternal salvation has been received by faith, calling upon the name of the Lord, since Seth. Those who willingly come to accept salvation are written in the book of life of the Lamb, slain from the foundation of the World. Those who don't before they die, erased.

Those who, while living willfully reject the gift, eventually are made reprobate, cast off, turned over and rejected by God and given what they want; to no longer retain God in their knowledge. Their conscience seared. Sons of Belial. Dogs, twice dead. Filled with all unrighteousness and burning with unnatural lusts, for sins not common to mankind. Enemies of God. Antichrists. Deserving death.
