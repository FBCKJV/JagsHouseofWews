How God Reveals Himself to Man: Answer Key (Week 2)

Instructions: This answer key provides the correct answers for the review worksheet, with filled-in blanks underlined for teacher use. The reflection question is not answered, as it is student-specific.

Part 1: General vs. Specific Revelation

  1. General Revelation is God revealing Himself through creation and our conscience, accessible to all people (Romans 1:18-20).
  2. Specific Revelation is God revealing Himself through direct means like Scripture, prophets, and Jesus Christ (Hebrews 1:1-2).
  3. Psalm 19:1 says that creation, such as the heavens, declares God’s glory, acting like a parable for us to know God.
  4. According to Romans 2:14-15, the law written in people’s hearts shows General Revelation through their moral conscience.

Part 2: God’s Revelation Through Creation

  1. Romans 1:20 teaches that creation reveals God’s invisible qualities, so people are “without excuse.”
  2. Job 12:7-9 says that beasts, birds, and the earth teach us that the hand of the Lord made all things.
  3. The miracle of birth or a bird’s migration shows God’s design in creation.

Part 3: God’s Revelation Through Wrath

  1. Psalm 7:11 describes God as a righteous judge, angry with the wicked every day.
  2. Jeremiah 25:31-32 says God has a controversy with the nations and judges them by their works.
  3. Hebrews 10:30 declares that the Lord shall judge His people, showing His authority over them.
  4. Romans 1:18 reveals God’s wrath against all ungodliness, showing His holiness and justice.

Part 4: God’s Revelation Through Mercy

  1. Psalm 103:8 says God is merciful and gracious, slow to anger.
  2. Romans 11:30-31 teaches that God’s mercy on believers reveals His nature to unbelievers.
  3. A believer’s changed life or answered prayers can show God’s mercy to unbelievers.

Part 5: God’s Revelation Through Scripture

  1. Hebrews 1:1-2 says God now speaks through His Son.
  2. John 1:1 calls Jesus “The Word,” showing He is fully God.
  3. 2 Timothy 3:16-17 says all Scripture is inspired by God for doctrine, reproof, correction, and instruction in righteousness.
  4. 2 Peter 1:20-21 confirms that prophecy came not by man’s will but by holy men moved by the Holy Spirit.

Reflection Question:
Choose one way God reveals Himself (creation, wrath, mercy, or Scripture) and briefly explain how it has helped you know Him better. Use a personal example or a Bible verse to support your answer.
