Most people conflate preference for principle.

Most people wish-cast reality away for the thing that lets them behave the way they want, now.

Most people put their lusts and desires in the same mental space as needs.

Very few will advocate for the right thing if it doesn't directly favor them.

This tendency to rationalize and project is why Satan promotes the false idea that all it takes to be righteous is to be a "good person." To try to be "moral." They can manipulate and shift the window on what makes one moral, fooling even the best of us into adopting wickedness as good. This false belief in our own works, and ability to be self-righteous, is what damns so many to hell. It's why the majority are so susceptible to manipulation. It's why it's so hard for those with the humility to accept the free gift of salvation to be deceived by a lie.

As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one. For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God; .Romans 3:10;23
And for this cause God shall send them strong delusion, that they should believe a lie: That they all might be damned who believed not the truth, but had pleasure in unrighteousness.- 2 Thessalonians 2:11-12
