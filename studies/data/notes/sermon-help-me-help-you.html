Help me, help you.

1 Corinthians 12: 27-28 Now ye are the body of Christ, and members in particular. And God hath set some in the church, first apostles, secondarily prophets, thirdly teachers, after that miracles, then gifts of healings, helps, governments, diversities of tongues.

The title of my sermon tonight; Help me, help you.

Paul is writing to the church in Corinth concerning Spiritual gifts and the contentions among them over the distribution of those gifts where some envied and coveted the more attractive

  • The church are all members of the same body, Jesus the head over all
  • Least comely- being a ministering servant is just as important as the more flashy, miracles and healings

Paul expounds on a more excellent way. He writes that even if you have the gift of speaking every language, see all prophecies, give away all our possessions, understand all mysteries and have all knowledge…if you don’t have charity, the gift and desire to help others out of love, it profits us nothing. As Paul writes, of all the talents we have as Christians, the greatest of these is charity.

Charity is love in action, and being a help to others as the expression of the Love God has shown us. The Bible says we should Follow after charity, and desire spiritual gifts, pray to be a better helper, and for the opportunity to be used by God

Not just money, BUT Time and God given talent. Doing for others out of love for God AND To show our love, helping one another by exercising this gift of God

But more than a gift, being a helper of others is also a commandment of God:

We all know The Golden Rule:

  • Matthew 7:12 Therefore all things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them: for this is the law and the prophets.
  • Matthew 26:36-37 Jesus says - Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.This is the first and great commandment. And the second is like unto it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself
  • James 1:27 Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father is this, To visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep himself unspotted from the world.
  • James 4:17 Therefore to him that knoweth to do good, and doeth it not, to him it is sin.

Helping one another. Doing unto others. Knowing to do good, being doers of the law…visiting those in need, being a help to all… In the Gospels Jesus calls us again and again to be a helper, to do unto the least of our brothers, and that by doing so, we are doing it unto Him.

His burden is truly light. It really is the least we can do, helping others is our reasonable service..our duty as Christians, and WE SHOULD help others with meekness and humility:

Luke 17:10 So likewise ye, when ye shall have done all those things which are commanded you, say, We are unprofitable servants: we have done that which was our duty to do.

And so in lowliness of mind let each esteem others better than themselves. Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others…Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus (Philippians 2:3-5)

The ability and opportunity to help others is a gift from God.

Helping others is a commandment of God

And being a helper is a blessing for you, the giver.

This is a promise from God

Helping others helps you: Receive blessings in this life:

  • Proverbs 19:17 He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the LORD; and that which he hath given will he pay him again.
  • Proverbs 22:9 The liberal soul shall be made fat, and he that watereth shall be watered also himself
  • Acts 20:35 In all things I have shewed you, that so labouring ye ought to support the weak, and to remember the words of the Lord Jesus, how he said, It is more blessed to give than to receive

These are rewards promised of God, for all we do for others. It’s never in vain. Even if it isn’t appreciated by anyone else. God knows, and He promises us Rewards in the next life:

  • Hebrews 6:10 God is not unrighteous to forget your work and labour of love, which ye have shewed toward his name, and how ye have ministered to the saints, and do minister
  • Matthew 16:27 For the Son of man shall come…and he shall reward every man according to his works.
  • Matthew 6:20-21 Lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven… For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.
  • Hebrews 13:16 And do not forget to do good and to share with others, for with such sacrifices God is pleased

By helping others, you help yourself and please God. What a wonderful blessing it is to be used by the Lord Who has done so much for us!

Helpers have a gift from God, are obeying a commandment from God, that contains a promise from God.

** Practical encouragement **

I tell you all this Because as Paul wrote to the Phlipians… I desire fruit that may abound to your account…(Philippians 4:17)

Helping me, helps you. Helping others, with your love, time, wealth, talents, by teaching, preaching, giving the gospel, ministering to the brethren, the brokenhearted…helping at home - helping your spouse, children helping your parents, helping at church, helping at work, school, or helping total strangers you meet in the world

…This is the more excellent way Paul writes about. Also, knowing that when we help a stranger, thereby some have entertained angels unawares. (Hebrews 13:2)

Pray for the ability and opportunity to be used by God, to be a blessing and a help to others

…IT WILL CHANGE YOUR LIFE…

And finally, by helping others, we GIVE GLORY TO GOD

  • 1 Corinthians 10:31 Whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God.
  • Matthew 5:16 Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven

Let us help others, ministering them as the ability and opportunity that God gives us, a GIFT FROM GOD

Let us obey the Lord in helping others, as servants, For His yoke is easy, and His burden is light…. following the COMMANDMENT FROM GOD

Let us remember that God is faithful to reward us for our service to others, which is a PROMISE OF GOD

Because, when we help others, when we exercise our gift of helps, we GIVE GLORY TO GOD

1 Peter 4:11 If any man speak, let him speak as the oracles of God; if any man minister, let him do it as of the ability which God giveth: that God in all things may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom be praise and dominion for ever and ever. Amen
