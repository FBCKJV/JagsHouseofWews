List of the Epistles placed in the context of Acts chapters where they were most likely written (based on a conservative, fundamentalist timeline aligning with Acts events).

- Acts 1–12

→ Read James (written ~AD 44–49 from Jerusalem, addressing early persecution and trials among dispersed Jewish believers)

- Acts 13–14

→ Read Galatians (written ~AD 48–49 from Antioch, shortly after the first missionary journey to the Galatian churches)

- Acts 15–18

→ Read 1 Thessalonians and 2 Thessalonians (written ~AD 50–51 from Corinth, to encourage the new church planted in Thessalonica during the second journey)

- Acts 19

→ Read 1 Corinthians (written ~AD 55 from Ephesus, addressing issues in the Corinthian church)

- Acts 20:1–2

→ Read 2 Corinthians (written ~AD 56 from Macedonia, as a follow-up to 1 Corinthians)

- Acts 20:3–38

→ Read Romans (written ~AD 57 from Corinth, as Paul planned his trip to Rome)

- Acts 21–28 (Paul's arrest, trials, and journey to Rome)

→ After Acts 28, read the Prison Epistles:

Ephesians, Colossians, Philemon, and Philippians (written ~AD 60–62 from house arrest in Rome)

Post-Acts Epistles (after Acts ends ~AD 62):

- 1 Timothy and Titus (~AD 63–65)

- 2 Timothy (~AD 66–67)

- Hebrews (~AD 60–68, possibly from Italy)

- 1 Peter (~AD 62–63, from Rome/"Babylon")

- 2 Peter (~AD 65–68)

- Jude (~AD 65–80)

- 1 John, 2 John, 3 John, Revelation (~AD 85–95)
