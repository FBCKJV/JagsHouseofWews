Posted 2023-09-30

This story has so many applications. In the lives of saved believers, we often are mocked and ridiculed for preaching the word of God and telling those in sin that they're wrong. And when these unsaved come to the supposed leaders of churches and denominations, and say "tell this Christian to shut up," essentially, they comply.

They say that we have to be loving. We can't preach against sin. Against the wicked. Against sodomites and Jews. That we can't tell people the judgment of God against those who sin and those who are haters of God. That we may offend them. They will even say things like Billy Graham or various Popes; "Jews, Muslims and Hindus who are sincere will find Heaven," knowing the Bible says the only path to the Father is through the Son.

And so much of it comes from fear of the Jews. How much dispensationalism and Zionism is preached by pastors who know better, for fear of the Jews? Fear of lost funding. Fear of being called names by the ADL? Fear of being persecuted?

Even in our own lives, we may have relatives or people in our church who tell us to cool it with the antisemitism. Stop speaking out against abominations. To rein in our hatred of every false way.

These same types will also wet-blanket soul winning and evangelizing the Gospel. They may even call giving the Gospel to Jews a hate crime.

For fear of the Jews.

But we know that by following the word of God and fearing only Him, we will be delivered. Even in death, we are delivered. So we speak boldly. We give the Gospel to every creature. We stand against evil and we defend that which is good. Even if it means we are mocked. Because we know the mockers will get their reward.

2 Peter 3:3-4

Knowing this first, that there shall come in the last days scoffers, walking after their own lusts, And saying, Where is the promise of his coming? for since the fathers fell asleep, all things continue as they were from the beginning of the creation.

We know the Lord is not slack concerning his promise. We know the mockers will be destroyed. That we shall overcome and be delivered by our Lord Jesus Christ.
