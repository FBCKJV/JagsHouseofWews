The Doctrine of Christ: Match the Verse

Instructions: Match each scripture verse on the left with the truth it teaches about Jesus on the right. Draw a line to connect them!

Scripture Verses

  1. John 1:1 - In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.
  2. Colossians 1:16 - For by him were all things created, that are in heaven, and that are in earth... all things were created by him, and for him.
  3. Hebrews 4:15 - For we have not an high priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities; but was in all points tempted like as we are, yet without sin.
  4. Acts 4:12 - Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.
  5. Daniel 3:25 - ...and the form of the fourth is like the Son of God.
  6. John 14:6 - Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.
  7. Genesis 14:18 - And Melchizedek king of Salem brought forth bread and wine: and he was the priest of the most high God.
  8. Philippians 2:7 - But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men.
  9. Isaiah 7:14 - ...Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.

A. He appeared in the Old Testament as a pre-incarnate figure (theophanies).

B. He is our merciful and perfect High Priest.

C. He is the only way to salvation.

D. He is the Son of God, who came through the lineage of Abraham and David.

E. He is the Messiah who was promised from the beginning.

F. He is fully God.

G. He is the Creator of all things.

H. He is the sole mediator to the Father.

I. He is fully man
