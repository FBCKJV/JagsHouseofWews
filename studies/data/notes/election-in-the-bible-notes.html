Election in Scripture means being singled out by God for a special purpose, privilege, or service.

It is never presented as God choosing who will believe or be caused to be saved.

It is always accompanied by conditions, responsibilities and, in many cases, warnings of disqualification, and real consequences for failure

Israel as a nation Deuteronomy 7:6–8

To be a special people unto the LORD, a holy people, above all people upon the face of the earth; to receive the covenant promises and the land

The LORD’s love and the oath He sware unto the fathers (v. 8); expressly not because of their numbers or righteousness (v. 7; cf. Deut 9:4–6)

Keep His commandments, love Him, serve Him with all heart and soul, walk in His ways (Deut 10:12–13; 11:22)

Breaking the covenant, idolatry, disobedience → curses, removal from the land, being cut off (Deut 28:15–68; 29:25–28; 30:17–18)

Angels 1 Timothy 5:21

To remain in their holy office and serve as witnesses before God

Did not join the rebellion (implied by contrast with fallen angels in 2 Pet 2:4; Jude 6)

Continue in obedience and holiness

Rebellion → cast down, chained in darkness (2 Pet 2:4; Jude 6)

Christ Himself Isaiah 42:1–4 (Matt 12:18)

To bring forth judgment to the Gentiles, establish justice upon earth, be a light and covenant to the people

God’s delight in Him; Spirit placed upon Him (Isa 42:1)

Perfect faithfulness and obedience (He shall not fail nor be discouraged, v. 4)

None possible—He is perfect

Believers Romans 8:28–33

To be conformed to the image of His Son; to be justified and glorified

Those who love God and are the called according to His purpose (v. 28)

Love God (v. 28); continue in the process leading to glorification

None explicitly stated in this passage

Believers Ephesians 1:4–5

To be holy and without blame before Him in love; unto the adoption of children (full heirship at the redemption of the body)

Chosen in Him before the foundation of the world; according to the good pleasure of His will (v. 5)

Be holy and without blame in love; walk worthy of the vocation wherewith ye are called (Eph 4:1; broader letter)

Grieve not the holy Spirit of God, whereby ye are sealed unto the day of redemption (Eph 4:30)

Believers Colossians 3:12

To put on mercies, kindness, humbleness, meekness, longsuffering, forbearing, forgiving, and charity (love)

Already addressed as “elect of God, holy and beloved” (v. 12)

Put on the listed virtues; let the peace of God rule in hearts; be thankful (vv. 12–15)

Context warns against being beguiled by false teaching and not holding the Head (Col 2:8, 18–19)

Believers 1 Thessalonians 1:4

To receive the gospel in power and serve the living and true God; to be ensamples to others

Evident by their work of faith, labour of love, patience of hope; gospel came in much assurance and joy of the Holy Ghost (vv. 3–6)

Wait for His Son from heaven; continue as ensamples (vv. 7–10)

None explicit in immediate context; broader letter warns against uncleanness (4:3–7)

Believers 2 Thessalonians 2:13

To the full obtaining of salvation through sanctification of the Spirit and belief of the truth; to obtain the glory of our Lord Jesus Christ

Already believing the truth; through ongoing sanctification of the Spirit (v. 13)

Stand fast and hold the traditions taught (v. 15)

Broader context warns of not loving the truth leading to strong delusion (vv. 10–12)

Believers 2 Timothy 2:10

To the full obtaining of salvation which is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory

Those for whom Paul endures—all the elect who will obtain the completion of salvation through the preached gospel

Endure suffering, continue in the things learned (2 Tim 3:14); if we suffer we shall reign, if we deny Him He will deny us (2:11–13)

Denying Him → He will deny us (v. 12)

Believers Titus 1:1

Acknowledging of the truth which is after godliness; in hope of eternal life

Already possess the faith common to God’s elect and knowledge of the truth (v. 1)

Live godly lives consistent with the truth; rebuke false professors (vv. 9–16)

Professing to know God but denying Him in works → reprobate (v. 16)

Believers 1 Peter 1:1–2

Unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ

According to the foreknowledge of God the Father, through sanctification of the Spirit (v. 2)

Be holy in all manner of conversation (1:15); purify souls by obeying the truth (1:22); gird up minds, be sober, hope to the end (1:13)

None explicitly in vv. 1–2, but broader letter warns against returning to former lusts and false teachers

Believers/Church 1 Peter 2:9

That ye should shew forth the praises of Him who hath called you out of darkness into His marvellous light

Mercy obtained (v. 10); contrast with those who stumble at the word being disobedient (vv. 7–8)

Abstain from fleshly lusts, have honest conversation among the Gentiles (2:11–12); submit to ordinances (2:13ff)

Disobedience leads to stumbling and appointment to judgment (v. 8, by contrast)

Believers Matthew 24:22,24,31 (parallels Mark 13:20,22,27)

Days of tribulation shortened for their sakes; to be gathered from the four winds at Christ’s return

None additional stated; simply “the elect” whom He hath chosen

Watch, pray always, endure unto the end (broader discourse, Matt 24:13,42–44; Mark 13:33–37)

Warned of possible deception by false christs and prophets (if it were possible, they shall deceive the very elect, v. 24)

Elect lady & sister 2 John 1,13

To walk in truth and love; abide in the doctrine of Christ

Some of her children walking in truth (v. 4)

Walk in love, keep His commandments (vv. 5–6); abide in the doctrine of Christ (v. 9); refuse to receive false teachers (vv. 10–11)

Transgressing and not abiding in the doctrine of Christ → hath not God (v. 9); receiving false teachers → partaker in their evil deeds (v. 11)
