The Doctrine of Scripture: Answer Key (Week 3)

Part 1: Inspiration of Scripture

  1. According to 2 Timothy 3:16-17, all Scripture is given by inspiration of God and is profitable for doctrine, reproof, correction, and instruction in righteousness.
  2. 2 Peter 1:20-21 says no prophecy of Scripture comes from private interpretation, but holy men were moved by the Holy Ghost.
  3. In Jeremiah 36:2, God commanded Jeremiah to write all His words, showing Scripture’s divine origin.

Part 2: Preservation of Scripture

  1. Psalm 12:6-7 declares the words of the Lord are pure and that God will preserve them forever.
  2. Matthew 24:35 says heaven and earth shall pass away, but God’s words shall not pass away.
  3. Isaiah 40:8 contrasts the fading grass with the enduring word of God that stands forever.

Part 3: Inerrancy of Scripture

  1. John 17:17 says God’s word is truth, which sanctifies believers through His truth.
  2. Psalm 119:89 says God’s word is settled in heaven, showing its unchanging nature.
  3. Matthew 24:35 affirms that God’s words are so true they outlast creation itself.

Part 4: Implications of Scripture

  1. Man does not live by bread alone, but by every word of God.
  2. Scripture makes the man of God perfect and thoroughly furnished unto all good works (2 Timothy 3:16-17).
  3. Revelation 20:12 says the unsaved will be judged by the books according to their works.
