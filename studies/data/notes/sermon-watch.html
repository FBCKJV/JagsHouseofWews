Luke 2:25-33

And, behold, there was a man in Jerusalem, whose name was Simeon; and the same man was just and devout, waiting for the consolation of Israel: and the Holy Ghost was upon him. And it was revealed unto him by the Holy Ghost, that he should not see death, before he had seen the Lord's Christ. Simeon was there when Mary and Joseph brought in the baby Jesus, and he took the child up in his arms, embracing Him, and blessed God, and said, Lord, now lettest thou thy servant depart in peace, according to thy word: For mine eyes have seen thy salvation, Which thou hast prepared before the face of all people; A light to lighten the Gentiles, and the glory of thy people Israel.

The title of my sermon tonight is Watch.

Simeon is an example of an OT saint, faithful and waiting patiently for the coming of Christ and was rewarded to see his faith fulfilled. Embracing the hope he had watched for. A light to lighten the Gentiles, quoting Isaiah 49, the prophets all gave witness

Looking down to verse 36; we meet Anna, of the tribe of Asher, a prophetess of great age, who departed not from the temple, but served God with fastings and prayers night and day. And there, seeing the Christ child, she gave thanks to the Lord, and spake of Him to all them that looked for redemption in Jerusalem.

The OT is full of examples of those who watched and waited for that redemption, in Christ.

We should look at our forerunners in faith, and to be like them:

To show the same diligence to the full assurance of hope unto the end: That ye be not slothful, but followers of them who through faith and patience inherit the promises. Those who watched and waited:

Hebrews 11 - The Hall of Faith, tells us of OT saints who watched, waiting for the promises of the Lord:

  • By faith Abel offered unto God a more excellent sacrifice than Cain (blood of the lamb vs works)
  • Noah by faith found grace in the eyes of God,being warned of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark, watching for the promised rain
  • Abraham believed God and it was counted for righteousness.

God made a promise to Abraham… Saying, Surely blessing I will bless thee, and multiplying I will multiply thee. And so, after he had patiently endured, he obtained the promise. (Heb 6) For he looked for a city which hath foundations, whose builder and maker is God.

Knowing It is impossible for God to lie, We like Abraham can have a strong consolation, to lay hold upon the hope set before us, Like Simeon, who embraced his hope, Jesus Christ. Looking forward to the cross.

Hebrews 11:1 Faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen. For by it the elders obtained a good report. Hope is a tangible thing, though we have not seen it or touched it, we know that it IS.

By faith the elders Watched patiently: Abel, Enoch, Noah, Abraham, Sarah, Issac, Jacob, Moses, Rahab, the judges, the prophets and kings…

Hebrew 11:13

These all died in faith, not having received the promises, but having seen them afar off, and were persuaded of them, and embraced them….16 But now they desire a better country, that is, an heavenly: wherefore God is not ashamed to be called their God: for he hath prepared for them a city.

The Bible says these are examples, written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the ages have come. That we should take heed in these last days, of their trust in God, and watch patiently for the promise of redemption in Jesus. And so we watch

The apostle Andrew waited watching by faith, for the messiah, preached of by John the Baptist, and hearing of Jesus, Andrew ran to tell his brother Simon, Peter, saying “We have found Him, the Messias.” the Christ, and brought his brother to Jesus. By faith they were blessed to witness the death, burial and resurrection of the Christ that they had long hoped for. The gospel on which we who are saved now stand (1 Cor 15)

But, without faith it is impossible to please God. Those who have not accepted the free gift of salvation, they have nothing to watch for, having no hope at all. Paul, writing to the Corinthians - if the dead rise not? let us eat and drink; for tomorrow we die. Those who believe not are condemned already. Their only promises are the wages of sin; death and Hell. To have the promise of the reward, eternal life, you must accept that free gift, given by the grace of the Father, paid for by the precious blood of our saviour. Sealed by the Spirit unto redemption - Knowing THAT Jesus paid it all. All to Him I owe. Sin had left a crimson stain, He washed it white as snow!

Jesus - In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: …ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise…the earnest of our inheritance…. (Ephesians 1:13-14) We are bought with a price. The Spirit indwelling us is the downpayment towards that day. Knowing this we WATCH

Just as the apostles watched as Jesus ascend into Heaven on Pentecost, 50 days after the resurrection, and they were told by angels - to watch, for He shall so come in like manner as ye have seen him go - We who are saved by that same faith in Jesus are called to likewise to WATCH & WAIT for that return

Many think it may even be in our lifetime. That like Simeon, Anna,and the Apostles, we may be blessed to be alive for the rapture, the gathering of the elect, those saved in Christ.

BUT the Bible says that there shall come in the last days scoffers, walking after their own lusts, And saying, Where is the promise of his coming? That evil men shall wax worse and worse as that time approaches. But one day is with the Lord as a thousand years, The Lord is not slack concerning his promise, But is longsuffering..not willing that any should perish So be diligent

DILIGENTLY WATCHING. Waiting. Warning others. For the time grows short and our life here is but a vapour, that appears briefly, and then vanishes away. Watching. Blessed to know that no matter how bad things get, no matter what tribulations befall us, that it will all work together for good because we love God.

SO WE SHOULD Walk in wisdom toward them that are without, redeeming the time Sharing the Gospel, warning and winning souls…Redeeming the time, because the days are evil (Collosians 4:5,Ephesians 5:16)

WATCH, knowing we look toward a better country, a heavenly one, that city promised by God, and eternal life in glorified bodies: In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump…When our corruptible flesh becomes incorruptible. (1 Corinthians 15:52)

Knowing that the dead in Christ shall rise first: Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds, The Rapture - to meet the Lord in the air: and so shall we ever be with the Lord. (1 Thessalonians 4:16-17)

Jesus said Blessed are those servants, whom the lord when he cometh shall find watching …Watch therefore, for ye know neither the day nor the hour wherein the Son of man cometh. (Luke 12:37-40, Matthew 25:13)

Scripture encourages us to Be patient…unto the coming of the Lord.(James 5:7)

Looking for that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ. (Titus 2:13)

As Jesus told His disciples What I say unto you, I say unto all, Watch.(Mark 13:37)

Finally, As the apostle John writes, with a longing and joy that thrills my spirit:

Beloved, now are we the sons of God, and it doth not yet appear what we shall be: but we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is. And every man that hath this hope in him purifieth himself, even as he is pure. (1 John 3:2-3)
