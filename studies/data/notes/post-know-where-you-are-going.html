You want to know where you are going when your body dies, the Bible says you can know, with certainty.

That believing on the perfect work of Jesus Christ as the sole sacrifice, the sole propitiation for our sins, accepting God's free gift of eternal life, is all you need. That by doing so and trusting in Him, your dead spirit is quickened, your inner man awakened, becoming heir to the promise, a son of God. Whom Jesus is not ashamed to call brethren. Sealed unto judgement. Who no man may pluck from His hand. That our faith is counted on for righteousness.

1 John 5:13-15

13 These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life, and that ye may believe on the name of the Son of God.

14 And this is the confidence that we have in him, that, if we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us:
