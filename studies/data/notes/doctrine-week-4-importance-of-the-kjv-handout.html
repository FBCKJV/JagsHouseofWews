Fun Bible Facts

  • Epic Timespan: The Bible, from creation in Genesis to John’s writing of Revelation (c. AD 95–97), covers approximately 4,284 years. The writing itself spans over 1,500 years, from Moses (c. 1400 BC) penning the first 5 books to Revelation (c. AD 95).
  • 40+ Authors, One God: Written by over 40 diverse authors—kings, shepherds, prophets, fishermen—inspired by the Holy Spirit.
  • Prophecy Power: Over 1,800 prophecies, with 300+ about Jesus fulfilled perfectly, like His Bethlehem birth (Micah 5:2).
  • Self-Referencing Scripture: The Bible cites itself over 1,000 times, with phrases like “It is written” affirming its authority.
  • The KJV is its own Dictionary!: You can generally find the meaning of a word by looking for its first appearance and seeing the context.
  • Year-Long Read: 1,189 chapters—read 3.3 chapters daily to finish in a year, or read 30 min. daily and finish in under 5 months!
  • 66 Books, One Story: 39 Old Testament + 27 New Testament books weave God’s redemptive plan from Genesis to Revelation.
  • Verses Galore: 31,102 verses in the KJV, packed with wisdom, history, and God’s Word for all.
  • No Contradictions: Despite centuries and authors, the Bible’s message stays unified, a testament to divine inspiration.
  • Three Tongues: Written in Hebrew (Old Testament), Aramaic (parts of Daniel, Ezra), and Greek (New Testament).
  • Shortest & Longest Chapters: Psalm 117 (2 verses) is the shortest; Psalm 119 (176 verses) is the longest, a meditation on God’s Word.
  • Shortest Verse: John 11:35—“Jesus wept”—shows Christ’s humanity and mercy in just two words.

How the KJV Bible Is Broken Down

The KJV’s 66 books are grouped by purpose and style, telling God’s story from creation to eternity. Here’s the breakdown:

  • Pentateuch (5 Books): God’s creation, salvation from Egypt, giving of the law and Israel’s origins.
  • Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy.
  • Historical Books (12 Books): Israel’s journey from conquest to exile, and return from captivity.
  • Joshua, Judges, Ruth, 1 & 2 Samuel, 1 & 2 Kings, 1 & 2 Chronicles, Ezra, Nehemiah, Esther.
  • Poetic/Wisdom Books (5 Books): Songs, prayers, wisdom.
  • Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Solomon.
  • Major Prophets (5 Books): God’s word through bold prophets (longer books).
  • Isaiah, Jeremiah/Lamentations, Ezekiel, Daniel.
  • Minor Prophets (12 Books): Short, powerful prophetic books.
  • Hosea, Joel, Amos, Obadiah, Jonah, Micah, Nahum, Habakkuk, Zephaniah, Haggai, Zechariah, Malachi.
  • Gospels (4 Books): Jesus’s life, death, and resurrection.
  • Matthew, Mark, Luke, John.
  • History (1 Book): The early church’s growth.
  • Acts.
  • Pauline Epistles (14 Books): Paul’s letters to churches and leaders.
  • Romans, 1 & 2 Corinthians, Galatians, Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians, 1 & 2 Thessalonians, 1 & 2 Timothy, Titus, Philemon, and Hebrews.
  • General Epistles (7 Books): Letters by other Apostles and saints, written for all believers.
  • James, 1 & 2 Peter, 1, 2 & 3 John, Jude.
  • Prophecy (1 Book): Christ’s return and final victory.
  • Revelation.
