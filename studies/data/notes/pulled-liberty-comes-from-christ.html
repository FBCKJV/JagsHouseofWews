Posted 2024-04-25

One of the takeaways to the story of Israel, from the patriarchs, Moses and Joshua, the judges and kings, is that the means of government is incidental to the hearts of the people.

That how they are ruled is not as important as their faith in God and their not turning aside to vanity, to the gods of this world. Not falling into Idolatry. Not rejecting His promise or the inheritance He has established for us. Obeying the commandments and law He has given us that it be well with us and we live long in the land He has appointed for us.

The same is true for us today. For all the political discourse on the proper means of government, the economic system that's used, the means of deciding leadership, the false binary choices we are given...none of it matters if we are not a godly people. And if we are, it will not be a terror for us, but against those who would do evil.

We need men of God who stand on His word, preaching against sin. Who say; God forbid that I should sin against the LORD in ceasing to pray for you: but I will teach you the good and the right way, preaching the whole word of God. In season or out of season. And the people who call themselves by His name to follow Him in all things.

But the solutions offered by so many boil down to calling for a king. For bringing the ark to battle without the Lord being present. For offering our own sacrifices and not accepting His. Not repenting to the Lord that He may deliver us.

We have the government we deserve. The evil done to us is the result of turning away from the straight paths, the old paths that God has made for us. Turning to the left or to the right.

If my people, which are called by my name, shall humble themselves, and pray, and seek my face, and turn from their wicked ways; then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin, and will heal their land.

Our judging the fatherless and taking care of our widows indeed. Bringing hope to the hopeless. Staying spotless from the sins of the world.

Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father is this, To visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep himself unspotted from the world.

There is no political solution to a spiritual problem.
