  1. Ezekiel 40:4 And the man said unto me, Son of man, behold with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears, and set thine heart upon all that I shall shew thee; for to the intent that I might shew them unto thee art thou brought hither: declare all that thou seest to the house of Israel.
    • Relevance: God commands Ezekiel to observe, hear, internalize, and declare His truth, mirroring the commitment of groups like the Waldensians and Tyndale to share vernacular Scriptures despite opposition.
  2. Deuteronomy 11:18 Therefore shall ye lay up these my words in your heart and in your soul, and bind them for a sign upon your hand, that they may be as frontlets between your eyes.
    • Relevance: Emphasizes internalizing God’s Word in the heart and keeping it before the eyes, reflecting the Baptist focus on biblical purity and personal faith.
  3. Isaiah 6:9-10 And he said, Go, and tell this people, Hear ye indeed, but understand not; and see ye indeed, but perceive not.     • Relevance: Highlights the call to declare God’s Word boldly, even when opposed, as early Baptists and Anabaptists did under persecution.
  4. Proverbs 4:20-21 My son, attend to my words; incline thine ear unto my sayings. Let them not depart from thine eyes; keep them in the midst of thine heart.
    • Relevance: Encourages hearing, seeing, and keeping God’s Word in the heart, aligning with the study sheet’s emphasis on preserving Scripture (e.g., King James Bible).
  5. Acts 28:27 For the heart of this people is waxed gross, and their ears are dull of hearing, and their eyes have they closed; lest they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and should be converted, and I should heal them.
    • Relevance: Paul’s preaching reflects the call to share God’s truth, as Baptist-like groups did, urging others to see, hear, and turn to God’s Word.
