The title of my sermon tonight is Faith over fear. To have faith in God over the fears of the world.

Nowadays we live in a society that loves to throw fear in our face and make us afraid, whether it’s the news or social media. And If you've spent any time on Facebook or Instagram doomscrolling, you may have seen the posts claiming that the Bible says “there are 365 verses from God telling us to not be afraid.” Very inspirational. But, like most internet theology it turns out it isn’t true, it is more like 110. Still, the idea of believers not having fear is a powerful message found throughout scripture. And tonight I'd like to encourage us as believers to put our faith in the Lord to have Faith over fear.

The Bible says in 2 Timothy 1:7

…God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind

That spirit of fear being mentioned is not in reference to god, it's the spirit of fear towards the world, and worldly things

As it says in Proverbs 1:7

The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge

Now of course I'm preaching on having faith over fear, but first it needs to be understood that It’s good to fear the Lord, that’s how we gain knowledge, and grow as christians and it’s also how many gain faith. Fearing the Lord and His wrath, fear of Hell, brings many people to believe on Christ.

As Jude writes And others save with fear, pulling them out of the fire.

We should fear god, have faith in the lord, but not fear the world

Having a healthy fear of the Lord can build us as obedient followers and bring us closer to God. But fear of the world is irrational, and god doesn't want us to fear of things out of our control, in fact those things should bring us to just put our faith in the Lord. Trusting Him to handle it. That in Christ, we have nothing to fear.

As Jesus says in Matthew 10:28

Fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear Him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell

Being unafraid is a commandment of God:

Joshua 1:9

Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the Lord thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.

Revelation 21:8

But the fearful, and unbelieving, ..and all liars, shall have their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone

It is a powerful thing to know that God is with us at all times. In my last sermon I preached on eternal life and how once we are saved we are in God's hand and He will never let go of us. Like he says “No man can pluck them from my hand”. This understanding that God’s got me really helped me to actually deliver that sermon. Boy, was I nervous.

Fear of public speaking, also known as glossophobia, is frequently cited as the most common phobia people have. It can be terrifying to get up before a room of people and speak. Let alone to try and preach the word of God! But the actual content of what I was preaching, reading verses in preparation about how God is always with us, actually helped me get past that. Especially when I have faith that what I’m preaching is the truth

We have the most high God watching over us, we are his children so what is there to fear?

When we started to go soul winning, you would have thought that we would be nervous or scared - Afraid about what people might think or how they’d react, and there’s definitely crazy people in this world! Also not to mention some large dogs, but for that I let brother Ron know he’s got that one. But I found I have no fear knowing I have the Lord watching over me.

As the Bible says Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit…that utterance may be given unto me, that I may open my mouth boldly, to make known the mystery of the gospel. It became easier than I ever thought possible.

The Spirit helping you to overcome fear, the power of God in our lives protecting us, showing us there is no need to fear, increases our faith. And the more faith we are given, the less fear we have.

Isaiah 41:10

Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.

Fear thou not! We have the power of God with us and there’s nothing more powerful than God. Our faith in Him should trump any fears we may have. No matter what trouble or temptations come into our lives.

As it says in Psalm 46:1-3

God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea; Though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with the swelling thereof.

In life we are all going to have storms come through and trouble us, Therefore will not we fear, I'm sure there's multiple things in each of our lives we are currently scared of or afraid of the results of certain things, but when you put all your faith in God and lean on Christ, no matter what happens He will strengthen you, and through the Spirit give you the strength to conquer all fears that come your way. Knowing I can do all things through Christ which strengthens me. Without the spirit of fear. But in faith. Amen
