Title: Are you persuaded?

Text: Acts 26:28 - Then Agrippa said unto Paul, Almost thou persuadest me to be a Christian.

Intro: Context - Paul was defending his faith before King Agrippa II and Festus in Caesarea. He gives his testimony and the gospel truth of Jesus

  • Throughout Acts we see the Apostles like Paul sought to persuade men of the truth of Christ
  • Paul is being led in chains from Jerusalem to Rome for proclaiming his faith, persuading others, turning the world upside down with his doctrine.

Transition: What does it take to persuade you of something? To know it is true?

  • Seeing is believing - What if it is something that can’t be seen?
  • Thomas “My Lord and my God” - Thomas, because thou hast seen me, thou hast believed: blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed.
  • Transfiguration of Christ,Peter, who with John and James witnessed the Son of Man, Jesus, in His Glory, heard the Father’s voice from heaven, saw the appearance of Moses and Elijah on the Mount of Olives, yet Peter says that despite all this, We have also a more sure word of prophecy with scripture
  • Just the facts, ma’am - Kid ourselves we are swayed only by facts - emotion/heart, bias all influence us
  • Subliminal, subconscious
  • Paid Influencers - media, politicians, advertising, seeking to persuade
  • If it were only by facts, they wouldn’t need to mislead and play psychological games.
  • Persuaded to do evil
  • Matthew 27:20 But the chief priests and elders persuaded the multitude that they should ask Barabbas, and destroy Jesus.
  • Reliable witness? - What if you don’t know them or their character?
  • Jesus appealed to scripture and figures they claimed to trust and they rejected Him
  • If they hear not Moses and the prophets, neither will they be persuaded, though one rose from the dead.
  • Moses and the prophets, the Gospels, the Apostles all give witness.
  • 1 Corinthians 15: Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures: And that he was seen of Cephas, then of the twelve: After that, he was seen of above five hundred brethren at once; of whom the greater part remain unto this present, but some are fallen asleep. After that, he was seen of James; then of all the apostles. And last of all he was seen of me also, as of one born out of due time.
  • Jesus doesn’t testify of Himself alone: If I bear witness of myself, my witness is not true.

If we receive the witness of men, the witness of God is greater: for this is the witness of God which he hath testified of his Son.
  • Power of the Gospel - For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth;

Persuaded to believe: Not just salvation, but all the promises of God

  • Answering prayer: If we ask anything according to His will, He heareth us
  • The truth of His word - Psalm 119:160 Thy word is true from the beginning: and every one of thy righteous judgments endureth for ever
  • The Holy Spirit brings all wisdom - John 16:13 Howbeit when he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will guide you into all truth
  • Divine wisdom: James 1:5 If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.
  • Direction in life: Proverbs 3:5-6 In all thy ways acknowledge Him and He shall direct thy paths
  • Chastening of sons: God promises to discipline and correct His children out of love Hebrews 12:6 For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth, and scourgeth every son whom he receiveth
  • Constant presence: God promises never to leave or forsake His people (Hebrews 13:5).
  • Provision of daily needs: God promises to supply every physical and material need, giving us our daily bread, raiment, shelter, (Philippians 4:19).
  • Comfort in affliction: 2 Corinthians 1:3-4 Blessed be God, even the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies, and the God of all comfort; Who comforteth us in all our tribulation, that we may be able to comfort them which are in any trouble, by the comfort wherewith we ourselves are comforted of God.
  • Peace that passes all understanding
  • A way out of temptation - There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able
  • Working all things to good - Blessings even in tragedy - And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.
  • Walking in the Spirit, not fulfilling the lusts of the flesh - Being freed from the burden of sin by the power of the blood, Galatians 5:16: This I say then, Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh.
  • Rest in Christ - Yoke is light - Matthew 11:28 Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.

Acts tells us of those who were Persuaded:

  • The 3,000 at Pentecost, heard the preaching of Chris, miracle of tongues, pricked of the heart for the death of Jesus, Peter used OT scripture to persuade them
  • Acts 2:41 Then they that gladly received his word were baptized: and the same day there were added unto them about three thousand souls.
  • 5,000 in Jerusalem Acts 4:4 Howbeit many of them which heard the word believed
  • The Ethiopian Eunuch Acts 8 Persuaded by OT prophecy of Jesus, explained by Phillip - I believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.
  • Cornelius and his household, Lydia and her household, Philippian Jailer and his household -Acts 16:30-34 Sirs, what must I do to be saved? And they said, Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house...And they spake unto him the word of the Lord, and to all that were in his house and they were persuaded
  • Athenians, Corinthians, Crispus and his house and many other Gentiles were persuaded to repent of what they had believed, to accept the truth of Christ
  • Jews and religious proselytes who followed Paul and Barnabas: who, speaking to them, persuaded them to continue in the grace of God.
  • Most famously, Saul of Tartius, Apostle Paul on the road to Damascus, and as we saw at the beginning, his testimony almost persuaded Agrippa.

Martyrs - All these were persuaded

  • Stephen, who was stoned to death preaching Christ to the Jews from scripture, seeking to persuade them. Paul present. Stephen was persuaded, falling to his knees, asking God to forgive them, even as they slew him. And he saw Jesus at the right hand of the Father as he died, He was persuaded
  • Apostle James. Preaching Christ at the Temple, seeking to persuade others - Herod the king stretched forth his hands to vex certain of the church. And he killed James the brother of John with the sword. He was persuaded
  • Paul and Peter and all the other apostles, save John, and all the martyrs persecuted throughout the New Testament, and the long trail of blood that followed, all were persuaded unto death!
  • Hebrews hall of faith Noah, Abraham, Moses, the prophets…These all died in faith, not having received the promises, but having seen them afar off, and were persuaded of them
  • OT saints Who through faith subdued kingdoms, wrought righteousness, obtained promises, stopped the mouths of lions. Quenched the violence of fire, escaped the edge of the sword, out of weakness were made strong, waxed valiant in fight, turned to flight the armies of the aliens. Women received their dead raised to life again: and others were tortured, not accepting deliverance; that they might obtain a better resurrection: And others had trial of cruel mockings and scourgings, yea, moreover of bonds and imprisonment: They were stoned, they were sawn asunder, were tempted, were slain with the sword: they wandered about in sheepskins and goatskins; being destitute, afflicted, tormented; (Of whom the world was not worthy:) they wandered in deserts, and in mountains, and in dens and caves of the earth All were persuaded to suffer and glory in that suffering for the cause of Christ!
