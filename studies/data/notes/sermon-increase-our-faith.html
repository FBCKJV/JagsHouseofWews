Luke 17:3-10

Take heed to yourselves: If thy brother trespass against thee, rebuke him; and if he repent, forgive him. And if he trespass against thee seven times in a day, and seven times in a day turn again to thee, saying, I repent; thou shalt forgive him.

And the apostles said unto the Lord, Increase our faith.

Jesus is teaching the apostles that it is not enough to forgive a brother one time or even 3. But, He says, even if it’s 7 times in a day, forgive. And the command isn’t merely 7 times Matthew 18:21-22: "Then came Peter to him, and said, Lord, how oft shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? till seven times? Jesus saith unto him, I say not unto thee, Until seven times: but, Until seventy times seven

490 times! Jesus is using 7, God's number of perfection or completion, to say “every single time! Forgive him!” It's easy to do once. We may even, out of the charity of our regenerate Christian heart, forgive ‘em twice. But the command is to forgive them every time! Jesus’ call to forgive shows radical expectation of God’s limitless grace, to forgive others completely, just as He forgave us.

The Apostles react to this saying “We don't have the faith to do it! We hear you Lord. We get what you're saying but ..common! SEVEN times? We are gonna need more faith than we have to do what you command us to do!”

And the Lord said, If ye had faith as a grain of mustard seed, ye might say unto this sycamine tree, Be thou plucked up by the root, and be thou planted in the sea; and it should obey you.

Jesus says even the tiniest amount of faith could defy physics! Warp reality!! Work Miracles! Forgiving each other is nothing compared to that.

Jesus then explains that if you want to increase your faith, you must first obey His commands. Because Obedience comes before faith!

To demonstrate it, Jesus then gives us this parable about a servant and a master. The servant works all day, plowing the field, feeding the cattle and comes in at the end of the day. Imagine how exhausted he'd be! And his master says, make me dinner. Serve me, and after that you can eat. The servant doesn't say “I would, but you gotta increase my faith first” he just does it.

Jesus asks Doth he thank that servant because he did the things that were commanded him? I trow not! He says No, I don’t think so!. Because at the end of the day, when we do what we are commanded to do, we are unprofitable servants: we have done that which was our duty to do.

As the great modern theologian, Chris Rock said about doing what you're supposed to do, “What do ya want? A cookie?”

Jesus isn't suggesting we forgive others. He is commanding it. He doesn't care that you're too faithless to understand why, or to discipline yourself into obeying. He says, like Nike “Just do it.” Obedience to His command comes before the faith to do it!

AND The payoff for obeying will show you the truth behind the commands. The results of forgiving a brother or sister, demonstrating the mercy of God, develops the fruit of the Spirit in us, longsuffering, patience...Joy. Love. The mercy we show others is shown to us. And you will see the promise of God fulfilled, and that will increase your faith. Because Obedience comes before faith!

Jesus says If you love Me, keep my commandments. “Lord, I hear you. But…”

HE SAID If you love Me keep my commandments! They are not grievous. His burden is light! Pick up your cross.

The word of God convicts us. It's why we need to walk in His word every day, to know what God thinks. To hear His commands and learn His ways, for His ways are not our ways. What makes sense to God might not make sense to us. But you do what God says and He will give you the faith to understand and continue doing it. EVERYTIME! But first you gotta know what He says, and then do it. Obedience comes before faith

The Bible says His word is a light unto our feet. It directs us where to go. God's word is like the flashlight app on your phone. Comes in very handy when you're in a dark place and you don't know where to turn. We should reach for the Bible when we can’t see where He wants us to go. And when we step out in obedience on the path God directs us towards, without the faith to do it, when we do what we don't think we are able to, He increases our faith to continue. We learn that Obedience comes before faith.

You may say “I don't know Lord… not sure I can do that, I don’t think I can bear this burden. I don’t have the faith.” But you take the step anyway. Because Obedience comes before faith

Some examples - God says children honor your parents. Fear them, that is have a healthy respect for them. It's the first commandment with promise! That you'll live long upon the earth. “But they are mean! They aren't fair! They are the worst!” Note God doesn't say a thing about how good or bad they are, He just says to honor them. No matter how old you get BTW And it pays off! A good relationship with your parents is a blessing! You gain their trust. You grow in grace. You live long upon this earth. God blesses you. Showing you that Obedience comes before faith

Husbands, love your wives the way Jesus loves the church! “But she doesn't always listen!! She nags me…” The Bible doesn't say “if she was perfect.” Jesus loves His church despite how imperfect we are, while we were yet sinners Christ died for us! If things aren't going the way you think they should at home, step out in obedience, LOVE YOUR WIVES!…and God will give you the faith to continue. Watch it work! Watch her love for you grow, and your appreciation to God for such a wonderful spouse. And you will see that your Obedience comes before faith

Wives, the Bible says to submit to your husband. And just as a Parent or Wife doesn't need to be perfect first for us to obey God, neither does he. “But he doesn't remember the anniversary! He can’t get his dirty socks in the hamper! He leaves the seat up!” God didn't tell you to submit to a perfect husband, but to the one ya got. And by your chaste conversation, your godly behavior, he’ll come around. But Obedience comes before faith

When we step out in obedience to God, and He will increase your faith to continue doing the thing you trusted Him to do. But we must obey first. Because Obedience comes before faith!

The Bible says to serve your boss, Working for them or your company or teacher or whoever has God given authority over you, as though you're working directly for God. “But the boss is a jerk!” the Bible says servants obey your masters even if they are froward. Even if they're jerks! Christians are to obey the higher power. That includes the government God gives us. To render unto Caesar, so pay your taxes! Without complaining…“Oh Lord, increase my faith!” …but Obedience comes before faith!

The Bible says to go to church, you're all here and God bless you for it, but even when you don't feel like it, don't forsake the assembly! You obey and you see the blessing it is in your life. You learn you can’t live without it. And so God increases your faith in Him and His word. Obedience comes before faith!

This sermon isn’t a lecture on being more forgiving or more obedient to your parents or being more loving to your spouse, although those are good things…It’s about how to grow your faith.

I am using examples of commandments we may struggle with for a reason. Like the Apostles struggled with the idea of forgiving someone an absurd amount of times. To show that Obedience comes first. Increased faith in God and trust in His word and His ways follows that obedience. Obedience comes before faith!

Especially when it’s hard. Especially when God’s commands bring us outside our comfort zone. When they challenge us to do hard things….God commands us - Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature. Go soul winning!

“But I'm afraid! I won’t know what to say!”

Go ye! He didn't say “Go some of ya! Go everyone but the socially awkward!” We obey, and as Ethan will tell you, you knock that door, you ring that bell…and suddenly the Spirit will fill you with exactly the measure of confidence and love that you need. And it becomes easy to talk with others about how much Jesus loves them. Your faith will increase having seen the Spirit in action. But Obedience comes before faith!

Sometimes it might feel like you're stepping out onto shaky ground. Jesus says whosoever heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them is established, as if standing upon a rock.

You step out in obedience and you think to yourself this isn't so bad. I can do this. And God blesses you and God rewards you for your obedience and you see things start to happen in your life. The Spirit moves. You see that jerk of a boss start to come around. You see your husband put the toilet seat down once in a while! You see your mom and dad trust you to do that thing you wanted to do. You step out again and again. You obey. You grow in faith. Because Obedience comes before faith…

Love God with all your heart, Love your neighbors as yourselves, go to church,… don't steal or kill…, don't lie… We obey more and our faith grows…with every step we take.… We forgive and put others’ profit above our own, and we pray and see results, and read our bible every day and develop wisdom, understanding…. We grow in faith…Growing to trust in the Lord as we go…This is your Christian walk…You hesitate less and less, you step out in obedience more confidently with each step…now you are running…

This is the race we run that’s set before us. As Paul writes, This is our course, God’s given a race for each and every one of us, not a competition between one another but with ourselves, pressing for that mark set before us… Knowing It's a marathon not a sprint. The Christian life is measured in decades not years. With each step our faith grows comparable to our obedience. Knowing Obedience comes before faith

Yes there are stumbling stones and snares in our way….Temptations. Things that trigger us: God says Love thy enemies..woah.. AND Pray for them. WHAT? Lord increase my faith. It’s hard. God knows it. God's word gives us encouragement and so step out without the faith to do it and trust Him.. Obedience comes before faith!

But why? Why should you want to do the hard things, to obey? Why should we seek to increase our faith?

Because, when hardship comes, when the troubles of this life weigh you down, when God gives you burdens you don’t think you can bear…when your unshakable faith is shaken..When you read “I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.” AND “we know that all things work together for good to them that love God,” we have the faith to trust Him and know that it’s so.

So you take that step, and realize it is a true saying, "God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble" You'll have the faith to trust Him, to know it's true. That it will work out in the end to His will and His glory and our salvation. And you receive that peace that passes all understanding.

No matter how heavy the burden is…sickness, financial trouble, deaths of loved ones, disasters and tragedies, When we are hardly able to see the path God directs us towards through the tears…Then we will have the faith to trust the Lord. Even in our darkest hours we can know, like Job. Though he slay me, yet will I trust in him. Even in death, trusting that to be absent from the body is to be present with the Lord.

And once you start obeying and stepping out on your course, and your faith increases, don’t be surprised at the heights the Lord will take you to.

Pressing on the upward way, New heights I'm gaining every day;

Still praying as I onward bound - Lord, plant my feet on higher ground.

That higher ground ultimately leads to Heaven. However, like all journeys, it starts with the first step. And that first step is accepting that free gift of eternal life. Jesus says that we are to obey the will of the Father. And this is the will of him that sent me, that every one which seeth the Son, and believeth on him, may have everlasting life: and I will raise him up at the last day.

Friends if you're here tonight and you haven't made that first step, if you're afraid you don't have enough faith to trust Jesus as your savior… the Bible says God gives to every man a measure of faith enough to not just hear the gospel, but to respond to it. To hear about that free gift of Salvation given by the grace of the Father paid for by the blood of the Son and sealed unto the day of redemption by the Spirit. If you haven't trusted in Christ as your savior if you haven't asked for and received that free gift calling upon the name of the Lord. Take that step. “Lord save me cuz I can't save myself.”
