1 Corinthians 4:15 For though ye have ten thousand instructers in Christ, yet have ye not many fathers: for in Christ Jesus I have begotten you through the gospel.

Father’s day -

  • We have our physical fathers. Loved my dad. Blessed to have wonderful children
  • But Every Christian has a spiritual father - Those who led us to the truth of Christ
  • Today I want to honor mine, Pastor Harry Fletcher.
  • I was raised Catholic, twelve years of parochial school, full catechism, confirmed — I had all the head knowledge and none of the assurance.
  • College, Navy, married with young children. - Playing the role of “father”
  • We were living with my MIL Nancy - who for sure was an instructor in Christ who was instrumental in leading my oldest children to Christ - She convinced me that we should attend church with her.
  • Pastor Fletcher was a great and gifted preacher. Wonderful sermons, kind.
  • Testimony - 6 months went by, hearing the invitation every Sunday - TUNED IT OUT
  • One beautiful spring day, approaching Easter, 1994. I remember where I was sitting. End seat on the left hand side.
  • Romans Road Pastor Fletcher, began the invitation, as he always did:
  • “With all heads bowed and eyes closed, are there any here this morning who do not know for sure they have eternal life in Christ” - Something about it caught my attention this time. It struck me that I actually did not know for sure. I thought I did but I had to admit to myself I didn’t
  • He said something like “First, we are all sinners. You’ve sinned, I’ve sinned..”
  • Romans 3:10 As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one
  • Romans 3:23: For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God."
  • I didn’t need any persuading on this. I was struggling with sin every day.
  • “And sin has a price. Death and Hell” I was raised Catholic, of course I knew there was a Hell! The guilt trips convinced me of that.
  • Romans 6:23 For the wages of sin is death… Hebrews 9:27 And as it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment:
  • But Pastor Fletcher continued the verse …but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.
  • Again, this struck me. Salvation IS a gift. How often I had heard about a gift, and God’s grace… but I was taught my whole life that ultimately salvation was on me. How could it be a gift if I had to pay for it? That contradiction had always bothered and discouraged me.
  • Romans 5:8 But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.
  • I was struck by this truth. Something I had NEVER heard before. This was entirely new information. I was taught my whole life that we had to cleanse ourselves with baptism, penance and all these things we must do to be “worthy of grace”
  • He continued: Romans 10:9 That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved.
  • He said “It is the promise of God Who cannot lie!” That Romans 10:13 said Whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.
  • I knew John 3:16 For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth on Him should not perish, but HAVE everlasting life - I KNEW it but never fully understood it.
  • Pastor Fletcher finished the invitation, stressing that once you are saved, born again in Christ, you can never lose that precious gift.
  • John 10:28-29 And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand. My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father's hand.
  • I had been taught my whole life that it was a “sin of presumption” to claim to know you will make it to Heaven, but now I saw that’s not what the Bible says.
  • Raised hand - “If you want me to pray for you, or to be shown from the Bible that you can know for sure you're saved, just slip your hand up”
  • He recited the “Sinners prayer,” encouraging those who raised their hands to call upon the name of the Lord. And so I did
  • After the service, as I stood kind of in shock, a man approached me. I knew he was an usher or a deacon, I don’t think I ever learned his name.
  • “You raised your hand during the invitation…do you have questions?” Boy did I!
  • He proceeded to go over it with me again, asking at each step if I believed what he was saying was true. He led me in a prayer, again (just to be sure!).
  • I still remember the joy and relief I felt. So many contradictions in my head were cleared up. So many questions were suddenly answered.
  • I felt like the Ethiopian eunuch -after hearing the gospel and believing, and being baptized by Philip, his father in faith - he went on his way rejoicing. I certainly did.
  • I think about Pastor Fletcher as my “spiritual father.” One sows the seeds, another waters, another harvests - But God gets the increase.
  • Blessed to be the spiritual father to many, some here tonight. And that they have been used by God to lead others - Spiritual Grandfather!
  • Tonight I want to honor my spiritual father and encourage you to as well

3 John 1:4 I have no greater joy than to hear that my children walk in truth.
