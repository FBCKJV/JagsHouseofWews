Many cultural Christians, seeking to justify the faith put their trust in science, and history, in the words and works of men to try to find reason to justify their faith.

Rather they should put their faith in Christ first, and see through that faith and the outpouring of Grace from God the Creator, that that is all the evidence needed.

1  Corinthians 1:22-24

22 For the Jews require a sign, and the Greeks seek after wisdom:

23 But we preach Christ crucified, unto the Jews a stumblingblock, and unto the Greeks foolishness;

24 But unto them which are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God, and the wisdom of God.

Hebrews 11:1 Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen
