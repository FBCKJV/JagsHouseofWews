Biblical support and notes

Salvation by Faith Alone

Ephesians 2:8–9: “For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works...”

Fundamental and Particular Baptists affirm sola fide. Arminian-leaning Baptists may imply works via front- or back-loaded conditions.

Eternal Security

John 10:28–29: “Neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand”; Ephesians 1:13–14: “Sealed with that holy Spirit of promise”

Fundamental and Particular Baptists affirm eternal security. Arminian-leaning Baptists teach salvation can be lost, contradicting literal assurance.

Free Will in Salvation

Romans 10:13: “Whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved”; 1 Timothy 2:4: “Who will have all men to be saved”

Fundamental and Arminian-leaning Baptists affirm free will. Particular Baptists’ limited atonement limits gospel scope, less literal.

Apostasy Indicates Never Saved

1 John 2:19: “They went out from us, but they were not of us”; Hebrews 6:4–6: “Tasted of the heavenly gift”

Fundamental and Particular Baptists view apostasy as non-saving faith. Arminian-leaning Baptists see it as losing salvation, misinterpreting Hebrews 6.

Avoids Works-Based Salvation

Romans 3:28: “A man is justified by faith without the deeds of the law”; Galatians 2:16: “Not justified by the works of the law”

Fundamental Baptists reject works-based conditions. Particular Baptists’ perseverance may imply works. Arminian-leaning Baptists tie salvation to obedience.

Contextual Use of Matthew 24:13

Matthew 24:13: “He that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved” (tribulation context)

Fundamental Baptists interpret as physical deliverance. Particular and Arminian-leaning Baptists misuse for perseverance or conditional security.

Mercy Toward Differing Maturity

Romans 14:1: “Him that is weak in the faith receive ye”; Galatians 6:1: “Restore such an one in the spirit of meekness”
