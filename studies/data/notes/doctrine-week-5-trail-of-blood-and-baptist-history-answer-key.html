The New Testament refers to “churches,” plural, 49 times. Acts 9:31 ("Then had the churches rest throughout all Judaea and Galilee and Samaria...") and Revelation 1:4 ("John to the seven churches which are in Asia...").

The Pastoral Epistles (1, 2 Timothy, Titus) gives the qualifications for Pastors and Deacons.

Bishop, Elder, Overseer & Shepherd are used in the Bible synonymously for “Pastor.”

The church offices aren’t "priests," ALL believers are.

An Ordinance is a special practice commanded by God, but it is not a “sacrament.”

“Anabaptist” means to baptize again. Catholics used the term as an insult, because Baptists were usually formerly Catholic converts who had been baptized as babies.
