KJV Bible Review Sheet Answer Key

  1. The Bible covers approximately 4,284 years from Genesis to Revelation.
  2. Over 1,800 prophecies are in the Bible, with 300+ about Jesus fulfilled perfectly.
  3. The KJV has 66 books, split into 39 Old Testament and 27 New Testament.
  4. The shortest verse, “Jesus wept,” is found in John 11:35.
  5. The longest chapter in the Bible is Psalm 119 with 176 verses.
  6. The Pentateuch, the first five books of the Old Testament, starts with Genesis.
  7. The Major Prophets, like Isaiah and Jeremiah, have longer books than the Minor Prophets.
  8. The book of Revelation is the only New Testament book focused on prophecy.
  9. The Bible was written in three languages: Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek.
  10. Reading 3.3 chapters daily lets you finish the Bible in a year.
  11. If you read one chapter a day, you can finish the book of Proverbs in 31 days!
  12. The most quoted book in the Bible is a song book! The Book of Psalms.
  13. The KJV was first released in what year? 1611
  14. Bonus: Name the four Gospels: Matthew, Mark, Luke, John
