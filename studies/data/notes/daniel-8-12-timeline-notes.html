Timeline of Days in Daniel 8 and 12

I. Daniel 8:13-14 – Vision of the Sanctuary Cleansing

Daniel 8:13-14

Then I heard one saint speaking, and another saint said unto that certain saint which spake, How long shall be the vision concerning the daily sacrifice, and the transgression of desolation, to give both the sanctuary and the host to be trodden under foot? And he said unto me, Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed.

II. Daniel 12:11-12 – Specific Time Periods

Daniel 12:11-12

And from the time that the daily sacrifice shall be taken away, and the abomination that maketh desolate set up, there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days. Blessed is he that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days.

III. Timeframe Breakdown

Total Period: 7 Years

Daniel 9:27

And he shall confirm the covenant with many for one week: and in the midst of the week he shall cause the sacrifice and the oblation to cease…

  • The "week" equals 7 prophetic years, calculated as 2,520 days (7 x 360-day lunar year+a “leap month" of 30 days).

First Half: 3.5 Years (Tribulation of the Saints, 1,260 Days)

Daniel 7:25

And he shall speak great words against the most High, and shall wear out the saints of the most High, and think to change times and laws: and they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time.

  • This period of 1,260 days (3.5 x 360) represents the tribulation where the saints face persecution.

Midpoint: Abomination of Desolation

Daniel 9:27

…and in the midst of the week he shall cause the sacrifice and the oblation to cease, and for the overspreading of abominations he shall make it desolate…

  • Occurs at the 1,260-day mark, splitting the 7 years.

Second Half: 3.5 Years (Great Tribulation, 1,260 Days + 30 Days)

Daniel 7:25

…a time and times and the dividing of time.

  • Extends to 1,290 days: Daniel 12:11 And from the time that the daily sacrifice shall be taken away, and the abomination that maketh desolate set up, there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days.
  • Extra 30 Days: The 1,290 days include the 1,260 days of the second half plus an additional 30 days, likely an intercalary month in the Jewish lunar calendar to align the 360-day prophetic years with the solar calendar, ensuring proper timing of events.

Additional 45 Days (Great Tribulation Concludes)

Daniel 12:12

Blessed is he that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days.

  • The 1,335 days extend 45 days beyond the 1,290 days (1,335 - 1,290 = 45).
  • Total 75 Days: The 30 days (1,290 - 1,260) plus 45 days (1,335 - 1,290) form a 75-day period of intensified great tribulation, overlapping the second half’s end, concluding key events.

Wrath of God: 3.5 Years (Post-1,335 Days)

The remaining time after the 1,335 days (approximately 1,260 days, calculated as 2,520 total days minus 1,260 tribulation days and 75 great tribulation days) aligns with the period of God’s wrath, following the conclusion of the great tribulation.
