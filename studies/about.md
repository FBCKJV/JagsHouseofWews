**About these studies**

These studies are my first thoughts each morning on the chapter I read that day, written down as I was led in the moment. They are not meant to be a complete commentary or the final word on any passage. Not every doctrine is fully mined here, and good-natured brethren can disagree on secondary doctrines or on my interpretations.

The King James Bible is the authority, not these notes. Compare everything here scripture with scripture, search the scriptures daily whether these things are so, and let every man be fully persuaded in his own mind.

**1 Corinthians 2:13**
*Which things also we speak, not in the words which man’s wisdom teacheth, but which the Holy Ghost teacheth; comparing spiritual things with spiritual.*

**Acts 17:11**
*These were more noble than those in Thessalonica, in that they received the word with all readiness of mind, and searched the scriptures daily, whether those things were so.*

**Romans 14:5**
*One man esteemeth one day above another: another esteemeth every day alike. Let every man be fully persuaded in his own mind.*

**1 Thessalonians 5:21**
*Prove all things; hold fast that which is good.*
