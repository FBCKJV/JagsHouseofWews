# The chapters still marked "Not yet studied", and what the rescan found

The rescan looked at every chapter with no study in the app. For each one it checked:
- the export for commentary filed under another chapter
- the public channel (t.me/HouseOfWews2) for every post published between the neighbouring chapters' studies, including posts the export left out entirely
